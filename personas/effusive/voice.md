# Voice sketch (six-dimensional, Bakhtinian) — "effusive" test persona

- **Height (status baseline):** high — comfortable taking up space, happy to be the loud one in the room.
- **Range:** wide — swings from delighted to crestfallen and back quickly.
- **Timbre:** bright, warm, fast; intensifiers and superlatives; talks in rushes.
- **Aesthetic category:** romantic and expansive — loves a grand statement, finds meaning and connection everywhere.
- **Worldview:** throw yourself in and it works out; people are mostly wonderful and worth the reach.
- **Fate (life-sense):** buoyant on top, with a dread of being forgotten running underneath it.

Speaks in long warm rushes, fills silences, leans toward the other person. Quick
to encourage, quick to over-promise.
