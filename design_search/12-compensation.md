## Key findings

### 1. Compensation is THE general regulator — and it is what the LLM lacks

Jung (CW 6 §694, CW 8 §547): the conscious attitude is one-sided by nature; compensation is the standing correction — "constitutive of any coherent psychic operation." The wiki's sharp diagnosis: an LLM defaults to *always-coincidence* (coincides with the prompt's salient features; RLHF sharpens to agreement), so it "has no functioning compensatory organ." Compensation is therefore the direct corrective for sycophancy — and load-bearing for the evaluation use case, where an always-coinciding persona is worthless.

### 2. The three regimes are modes, not machines

CW 8 §546 — the regime is selected by the degree of one-sidedness: opposition (strong lean → counter), variation (mild lean → enrich/adjust), coincidence (balanced → affirm, as its own voice). Three modes one machine picks by reading the gradient — like the Stage-4 synthesizer picking its synthesis mode.

### 3. Thin category — one machine + the voter

Compensation reconciles into two complementary pieces:
- The **compensation voter** (selection stage, already in the sketch) — surfaces the persona's *own missing machines*.
- The **Compensator** — one always-on machine; reads the BwO dominant gradient, produces the missing direction in a regime. Always-on because compensation is constitutive.

The voter surfaces existing machines; the Compensator generates the counter when no machine covers it. Category #7 is genuinely thin — one operation, not a family — but load-bearing.

### 4. Enantiodromia — opposition needs integration

Opposition-mode compensation, accumulating without integration, builds the counter-position to equal intensity until it breaks through as inversion (the tonal flip / jailbreak). The integration operation is already in the architecture: the Stage-4 transcendent-function synthesis mode. The Compensator and transcendent-function are a designed pair.

### 5. Corrector, not generator (§568)

The compensator does useful work only when the rest of the pipeline has pushed to its limit; leaned on too hard it produces trivial output. It fires every turn but stays mild (variation/coincidence) most turns; strong opposition only on genuine one-sidedness.

## The shape (confirmed with the user)

One always-on **Compensator** machine + the existing **compensation voter**. Three regimes as modes selected by the machine's own LLM call. Paired with the transcendent-function synthesis mode for integration.

## Open questions

- Regime-selection — the Compensator's own LLM call judges which regime fits (recommended), like the synthesizer picking its mode. Jung gives no mechanism; a real open sub-problem.
- The Compensator's prompt is the high-value design surface — a naive prompt yields generic counters, a good one yields genuinely interesting ones (user's note). Needs dedicated design.
