## Key findings

### 1. Three accounts of desire, one fault line

The category sits on a real disagreement:
- **D&G** (`desire-as-production.md`): desire is production, not lack. Names a "priest's triple curse" — three betrayals of desire: desire-as-lack, desire-ends-in-pleasure, and impossible-jouissance. Fantasy is named as the third curse (theater, not factory).
- **Lacan** (`need-demand-desire.md`, `fantasy-formula.md`): desire IS constituted by lack — desire = demand − need, the remainder that slides metonymically and never alights. Fantasy ($◇a) is the structural support of desire.
- **Spinoza** (`conatus.md`): desire is the conatus, the actual essence, striving-to-persist. "We judge a thing good because we desire it." The pre-D&G ground of desire-as-production.

### 2. Fantasy sits on the fault line

D&G's foundational frame is anti-fantasy (fantasy = the third curse). Lacan makes fantasy desire's support. The user wants fantasy central. Decision: the tension is noted, not resolved. The persona system simulates a human persona; humans fantasize constantly; a persona that doesn't is less believable. D&G's critique becomes an *angle* (fantasy can be a trap — a reterritorialization, a theater the persona gets stuck in — something the ghostwriter watches for) rather than a veto. Per the deep-end discipline: the Fantasy machine generates imagined scenes; the theory explains why fantasy is interesting and risky, not how it runs.

### 3. Affect already owns bare striving

The sketch's Affect category has Cupiditas-baseline (forward-reach / striving), and "longing"/"hope" are in the affect catalog. Desire machines therefore do NOT redo bare striving. The Desire category's distinct contribution is the *content* of wanting — imagined scenes, objects, projections. This also settles "distinct from Preference": a preference is a stable surface like/dislike (carried in the BwO and biographical detail); Desire machines produce wanting as a live process.

### 4. Memory and Fantasy are siblings — differentiated by frame, not grounding

The Fantasy machine and the Memory generator both hallucinate showing-mode scenes; neither is grounded in anything real. They are differentiated by three concrete things:
- **Stance** — memory = held as having happened; fantasy = held as imagined.
- **Constraint** — memory fits biography; fantasy fits desire (and can be biographically impossible).
- **Trigger** — memory is summoned by the present gap and reaches back; fantasy is propelled by a wanting and reaches toward.

In the BwO, memory grounds and fantasy pulls. The boundary is passively permeable (prose texture is the only marker; the final machine cannot always tell) — left as-is because human memory is reconstructive and wish-laden. Actively-exploited confabulation (the ghostwriter deliberately promoting fantasies into the memory register) was considered and **declined** by the user — passive bleed only.

## The corpus — 5 Desire machines, organized around fantasy

1. **Fantasy** — imagined wished-for or dreaded scene; showing-mode.
2. **Wish** — bare articulated want, often counterfactual.
3. **Rehearsal** — pre-played imagined next move.
4. **Fixation** — wanting circling one object/detail.
5. **Want-of-the-other** — imagined model of what the interlocutor wants from the persona (analysis-shaped).

Four of the five are fantasy-modes; Fixation is the drive-circling one. Mostly proposal-shaped.

## Open questions

- Wish and Fantasy are close — a wish is roughly a fantasy compressed to a sentence. Possible fold into one machine with variable output length.
- Fixation is adjacent to Perception's Repetition noticer (input-recurrence vs the persona's own circling).
- Fantasy's input structure — what feeds it the persona's desire — is less settled than Memory's (biographical detail + random anchors).
