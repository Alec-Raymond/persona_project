## Key findings

### 1. The stub conflates two things

The stub — "binary flow-producer + flow-interrupter pairs; relational machinery; how the persona engages with others" — is two distinct things:
- **D&G flow/break coupling** — every machine produces a flow and interrupts another. The universal operating principle of *all* machines, already realized in the pipeline (the connective synthesis mode, the grouping). NOT a category of machines.
- **Interpersonal relating** — how the persona engages the other person. This is the real category #8.

### 2. The mechanism — coupled prediction

`mutual-prediction-loops.md`: conversation is coupled generative machinery; each participant runs their own production-system as a prediction of the other's; the persona-user pair aligns over turns. Bakhtin: interiority is itself relational ("no internal sovereign territory"). Two constraints — the persona connects through the verbal channel only (intercorporeity / bodily relating is unavailable, a no-body limit); the persona-user bond is structurally projective (love-of-the-same).

### 3. Connection is thin — relating is already distributed

Voice has sideward-glance and loophole; Desire has Want-of-the-other; Trauma has defended relating; Perception's noticers read the other. What remains genuinely distinct for Connection is the relational *moves* — making, sustaining, breaking the relational line.

### 4. Status — a candidate new source (Johnstone, *Impro*)

User association: the relational pairs resemble improv status play — "holding your status slightly above or below the other." The wiki has *no* status-as-transaction material; Johnstone / *Impro* is not ingested. Voice's six-dimensional sketch "absorbs Status" — but that is *structural* status (the persona's preferred, habitual level), not the per-turn transaction. Johnstone's *Impro* (1979) is a candidate new source — concrete, craft-grounded, recognizable (a virtue, given the deep-end-discipline concern). Added to `cited-sources.md` for ingest.

### 5. The three-axes structure

The corpus reframes from "producer/interrupter" (which fit Bid/Cut but not the rest) to three axes of relational position, each pair the two poles of one axis: Presence (Bid / Cut), Sameness (Attune / Differentiate), Verticality (Raise / Lower).

## The corpus — 6 machines, 3 pairs

Bid / Cut (presence), Attune / Differentiate (sameness), Raise / Lower (verticality). Mixed shape — proposal for the contact moves, modulation for Attune and Raise/Lower. Each produces a relational *move*, never a whole-subject relational *claim* (the latter is the illegitimate connective mode).

## Open questions

- The status pair (Raise/Lower) waits on the *Impro* ingest for concrete grounding and examples.
- Connection is the most heavily-bordered category; revisit whether all three axes earn their place once per-entry wiki work begins.
