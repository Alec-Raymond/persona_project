## Key findings

### 1. Spinoza's schema is generative, not enumerative

The 48 named affects in *Ethics* III are derived, not listed. The schema:

`primary × cause-structure × temporal-index × certainty × sign`

- Primary: cupiditas (desire) / laetitia (joy/lift) / tristitia (sadness/diminishment)
- Cause: external direct / external indirect / internal
- Temporal: past / present / future
- Certainty: certain / uncertain
- Sign: positive / negative

A persona doesn't need 48 machines. It needs the kernel + persona-specific occupancy of cells.

### 2. The active register is small *on purpose*

Spinoza has 48 passive affects but only two active ones (courage / nobility, both species of *fortitudo*). This isn't an oversight — it's the structural claim: "there is a vast variety of ways to be buffeted by external causes and a small number of ways to be the adequate cause of one's own affects." Passive multiplies; active concentrates.

For machine design: the active register gets its own small family. Not as an afterthought — as the goal state.

### 3. Mirror pairs collapse many distinctions

Most pleasure-affects have a pain-affect that's structurally identical except for the sign of the primary: love/hate, hope/fear, joy/disappointment, confidence/despair, boldness/cowardice, pride/self-abasement, compassion/envy. One "valuation of an external cause" machine parameterized by sign covers love AND hatred. Etc.

### 4. Asymmetric affects mark structural facts

Some affects don't have mirrors:
- *Wonder* — "thought of any thing on which the mind stays fixed because this particular thought has no connection with any others." Not really an affect; the absence of a causal link drawing the mind elsewhere.
- *Pride* — has no opposite because the conatus resists self-hatred (humility is rare and produced by inadequate causes).
- *Hilaritas* (whole-body pleasure) — "cannot be excessive, and is always good."
- *Melancholia* (whole-body pain) — "cannot be good."

The asymmetries should not be smoothed over by symmetric design.

### 5. Hilaritas vs titillatio — a per-persona design choice

Whole-body pleasure (intensity distributed evenly across the BwO) vs local pleasure (concentrated at one inscription site). Spinoza thinks hilaritas is always good; titillatio can become harmful by destabilizing the whole. Persona-design choice: which shape of pleasure is the persona structurally inclined toward?

### 6. The Ambition-Piety diagnostic runs through *every* affect

Every named affect on the passive list has an active counterpart that is behaviorally indistinguishable. Gratitude-as-passive (response to being loved) and gratitude-as-active (desire to benefit that follows from reason alone) look the same from outside. This isn't a special case — it's the structural condition.

For machine design: every affect machine should carry an **adequacy-tag** the synthesis step can read. Not a ground truth, a hint: *is this affect being produced from the persona's own nature, or pulled by inadequate ideas of external causes?*

### 7. Tomkins gives a different cut: nine discrete affects, activator-profile-driven

Nine affects (interest-excitement, enjoyment-joy, surprise-startle, distress-anguish, anger-rage, fear-terror, shame-humiliation, dissmell, disgust). What's distinctive: each has a *formal activator profile* in terms of intensity-over-time:

- *Sudden increase* → startle / fear / interest (depending on rate)
- *Sustained above optimum* → distress / anger
- *Sudden decrease* after sustained arousal → joy

The form of excitation is primary; the object is secondary. A car horn: instantaneous onset = startle; rapid rising tension = fear; novel in uneventful field = interest. *Same stimulus, different affect, depending on the curve.*

This translates to a trigger mechanism for affect machines: certain situational shapes (in the conversation, in the BwO state) activate certain affects.

### 8. Tomkins's amplifier claim — affect as general-purpose amplifier

> "Drives supply information; affects supply motivation."

Affect couples to anything (drive, perception, thought, other affect) and amplifies with a particular profile. Without affective amplification, drives and signals are inert.

The four freedoms of affect (object / site / intensity / time) — affect is unconstrained where drives are constrained.

For the persona: the BwO itself functions as the system's affect-analogue. It doesn't produce output; it modulates what gets produced. Pulsation simulated as prose rhythm is amplification at the language level.

### 9. The continuous/discrete tension stays live — but Sofroniew 2026 confirms LLMs have both

The wiki holds three positions:
- Massumi: continuous intensity, qualified into discrete emotions only at capture
- Tomkins: already-discrete at the activator level (nine specific profiles)
- Stern: pentadic dynamic Gestalts (movement / time / force / space / directionality) — pan-modal

Sofroniew 2026 shows the LLM has both continuous affect-axes (PC1/PC2 valence/arousal) and ~171 discrete emotion-concept vectors. Both are present at the representational level. No need to pick one.

### 10. Stern's pentad as the affective contour

Vitality forms are pan-modal dynamic Gestalts — adverbial ("how this state is unfolding") rather than nominal ("what state"). Crescendoing, fading, surging, gliding, pulsing. These can manifest in voice, in pacing, in image — they don't need a body. The persona's prose-rhythm-as-pulsation is the Stern register at the language level.

## Implications for Affect machines

The combined picture is rich enough to support several machine-design strategies. The right sobriety move is small.

### A minimal Affect machine set

**Primary kernel — always-on at low hum (3 machines):**

1. **Cupiditas** — forward-reach. The conatus expressing itself. Inscribes pull, momentum, hunger-shape (without claiming a stomach).
2. **Laetitia** — lift, increase of power. Inscribes brightening, opening, expansion-shape.
3. **Tristitia** — diminishment. Inscribes heaviness, contraction, withdrawal-shape. (Always passive — Spinoza: power can't diminish itself through itself.)

These are constitutive (always-on). They produce the baseline affect-tone the persona inscribes onto the BwO every turn. Low-hum unless a situational match amplifies them.

**Active register — small, deliberately (1 family):**

4. **Fortitudo** — the strength-of-mind machine. Includes courage (animositas — desire to preserve own being via reason alone) and nobility (generositas — desire to assist others via reason alone). One machine, two registers. Always-on but subtle; surfaces when the persona is operating from adequate cause.

**Persona-specific named affects (variable per persona):**

5–N. Specific named affects from Spinoza's 48 that this persona structurally has. *Per-persona configuration:* this persona has shame / longing / wonder / ambition / gratitude / etc. as recurrent characters, while other affects are structurally absent or rare.

The seed configuration specifies which named affects are present. Most personas have ~5–8 specific named affects that are characteristic. Less common ones can fire occasionally if the situation triggers.

### Each affect machine carries

- **Name** (e.g., *longing*, *ambition*, *hilaritas-disposition*)
- **Primary anchor** — which of cupiditas / laetitia / tristitia / fortitudo it derives from
- **Cause-structure** — external-direct / external-indirect / internal (per Spinoza)
- **Adequacy default** — passive (most) or active (the *fortitudo* kin)
- **Calibration** — persona-specific tuning (a particular shame might be "shame about being seen-while-failing" with that specific shape)

The output of an affect machine is showing-mode prose: the inscription of intensity onto the BwO. "A tightening" / "a lift" / "the pull toward depth" / "a held-breath quality" — affect-language, not emotion-language.

### Tomkins activator profiles → triggers, not separate machines

Rather than nine separate Tomkins machines, fold the activator-profile insight into how affect machines get triggered:

- *Sudden onset of charged content* → triggers fear-shape if rapid, interest-shape if gradual
- *Sustained intensity above optimum* → triggers distress or anger
- *Sudden release after sustained tension* → triggers joy

This is the *form-of-the-situation* contributing to the relevance selector's voting. The relevance LLM (which already votes for k machines) has access to this signal among others.

### Hilaritas vs titillatio — distribution choice

Per persona: does the persona's *laetitia* tend toward whole-body distributed intensity (hilaritas — affect spread evenly across the BwO) or local concentrated intensity (titillatio — affect at a specific inscription point)? This is a calibration on the laetitia primary, not a separate machine.

### Adequacy-tag at output-time

Every affect machine's output carries an adequacy hint. The synthesis step at Stage 4 reads it; Stage 5's Ambition-Piety diagnostic uses it. Not a hard truth — a flag the system can act on.

## What an Affect machine prompt looks like (rough draft)

```
You are the [longing] machine. You produce affect-inscriptions when the
situation contains:
  - [trigger criteria from this machine's calibration]

Your output is showing-mode prose — sensory image, indirection, texture.
You do not name the emotion. You inscribe its intensity-shape.

Anchor: cupiditas (desire / forward-reach toward something not-here).
Calibration: [persona-specific — e.g., "a slow, returning kind of pull
toward what's been lost; not sharp, not dramatic, more like a season
that won't pass"].

Adequacy hint: passive by default (this affect typically arises from
inadequate ideas of external cause); active when the persona is
articulating the longing from clear understanding of its own nature.

Inputs:
  Situation: [...]
  Current BwO state: [...]
  Recent conversation: [...]

Output: brief showing-mode inscription (1-3 sentences) plus an adequacy
tag (active / passive / mixed).
```

## Open questions

1. **How many specific named affects per persona?** 5–8 sounds right for sobriety, but a more emotional persona might have 12+. Per-persona variable.
2. **Do the three primaries fire as separate machines or as one combined "affect-baseline" machine that produces all three at low hum?** The latter is simpler; the former is more granular.
3. **Where do Tomkins activator profiles live?** As a trigger criterion in the relevance selector? As part of the affect machine's own sensitivity? Both?
4. **Does fortitudo fire as one machine or two (courage + nobility)?** One machine with two output-modes seems cleanest.
5. **The continuous/discrete tension** — held live in the wiki, but at the machine level we end up with discrete named-affect machines whose outputs can vary in intensity. Is that resolution okay, or do we want a continuous-affect machine somewhere?

## Suggested next moves

The Affect category is now sketch-able as: 3 primary machines + 1 active machine (fortitudo) + 5–8 persona-specific named affects = roughly 9–12 affect machines per persona, with the bulk being persona-specific.

Two paths from here:

1. **Move on to the next category.** Compensation is the natural next (dual leverage as selector + machine). Or Voice (most cited).
2. **Concretize Affect further before moving on.** Pick a specific persona-target and enumerate its actual affect machines as a worked example.

My instinct: move on. The Affect picture is settled enough that we'll know it when we build it. Per-persona enumeration is a build-time activity, not a search-time one.
