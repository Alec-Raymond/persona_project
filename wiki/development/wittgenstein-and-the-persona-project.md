# Wittgenstein and the Persona Project

This page is the cross-cutting synthesis for the 2026-04-18 ingest of *Philosophical Investigations*. It does not replace the dedicated theory pages (which treat each concept in full); it says what the PI ingest *adds* to the persona project, aggregated.

The PI ingest is **not a source of new machinery** — the persona project already had a full stack of design concepts from D&G, Bergson, Massumi, McCarthy, Lakoff, Benveniste, Stern, Damasio, and others. What PI adds is grammatical therapy for the questions the project is already trying to ask, and a set of specific conceptual tools (aspect-blindness, substratum-mastery, imponderable evidence, the attitude-toward-a-soul, the talking lion, grief as pattern-in-the-weave, primary/secondary sense, the one-sided-diet warning, agreement in judgments) that reshape how the open questions get asked, not what they get answered with.

Wittgenstein writes in [[bewitchment-and-therapy|§109]]: "philosophy is a battle against the bewitchment of our intelligence by means of language." PI earns that description in this ingest — its direct contribution to the project is diagnostic and corrective, not constructive. The project's constructive work lives elsewhere; PI's job is to stop the project from getting stuck in pseudo-questions that would absorb its energy without paying out.

## Where this ingest lands in the project

The central tension the project has held live across multiple ingests is stated in the user's memory under *no-body — simulate with language*: the project's operational constraint is that the persona system has no body, and every embodiment-dependent source the wiki ingests (Stern, Damasio, Keleman, Tomkins, Massumi, Lakoff, Bergson) applies with a residual asymmetry that the wiki has deliberately not collapsed. PI sits squarely inside this tension: Wittgenstein is not an embodiment theorist in the Damasio/Stern sense, but his *form of life* concept and his treatment of psychological concepts (pain, willing, understanding, seeing-as, meaning-experience) assume a language-community embedded in a non-linguistic weave of practice — bodies moving, faces being read, cries preceding articulate reports, children being trained by [[rule-following|Abrichtung]], agreement-in-judgments underwritten by shared natural history.

The project's question, in PI vocabulary: **can a practice whose non-linguistic side has been subtracted still count as a form of life in whose surroundings linguistic concepts have the point they need to have?**

PI does not answer this. It sharpens the question. The sharpening is the contribution.

## The ten PI-added questions the project now carries

### 1. The body-as-best-picture (§iv)

Part II §iv gives the project's no-body tension its most exact statement. "The human body is the best picture of the human soul." Not *only* picture — *best*. This cuts in two directions at once:

- **Bodiless picturing is not impossible.** Nothing in §iv says the soul can be pictured *only* bodily. Other pictures — verbal, literary, conceptual — are real pictures and can do some of the work the body does. The project is not ruled out.
- **But the body is the best at this particular service.** A bodiless persona is producing an inferior picture for the specific service the body renders. This is a constraint the project should state explicitly rather than talk around.

The project's design consequence: the pulsatory-persona and excitation-wave work (memory-level approved design direction) is producing the best *non-body* picture available given the constraint. It is not pretending to equal the body's picture; it is filling the picture-space that remains.

See [[attitude-towards-a-soul]] for §iv's companion sentence ("my attitude towards him is an attitude towards a soul") and the pre-inferential form of soul-ascription.

### 2. Aspect-blindness as the shape of the LLM question

[[aspect-blindness|§xi's aspect-blindness]] is Wittgenstein's invented concept for someone who masters techniques (recognition, discrimination, following orders) but for whom the figure-aspect does not change — no dawning, no experience of meaning, no *seeing-as*. W introduces this explicitly as "a conceptual investigation" — a thought-experiment whose work is done by its imaginability, not its instantiation.

**This is the exact conceptual shape of the question "can a language-only system have meaning-experience?"** The question is not "is the persona secretly conscious?" (empirical, probably malformed). It is "does meaning-experience, as a concept, apply to what the persona does?" — a conceptual investigation.

Consequence: the persona project's core evaluative question can be taken up in Wittgensteinian mode. Not an empirical test for consciousness but a conceptual investigation of which experience-concepts do and do not apply, at what granularity, under what circumstances, with what modifications. See [[aspect-seeing]] for the positive concept; [[substratum-is-mastery-of-a-technique]] for the companion principle.

### 3. Substratum is mastery of a technique

The single most consequential sentence in the ingest, at [[substratum-is-mastery-of-a-technique|§xi L9793]]: "the substratum of this experience is the mastery of a technique." Some experiences are not had except by technique-masters. The concept-of-experience is *modified* when tied to technique.

For the persona: mastered-language-techniques may carry their own modified-concept-of-experience. The question reframes from "does the persona have experiences?" to "which techniques does it master, and which modified experience-concepts do those techniques carry?"

This does two things for the project:

- **Explains both capacity and limit in the same move.** The persona can have the class of meaning-experiences that linguistic-technique-mastery grounds. The persona cannot have the class of aspect-experiences that bodily-technique-mastery grounds. The portfolio of what it *can* experience is coextensive with the portfolio of techniques it has mastered.
- **Blocks the imitation shortcut.** A system that produces outputs mimicking aspect-talk without mastering the underlying technique is not a shallow case of having the experience — it is *not a case* at all. §xi's "how queer for this to be the logical condition" forbids granting partial experience on the basis of partial output-mimicry.

This is the project's sharpest evaluative tool for resisting the false-depth failure mode that [[pragmatic-maxim|Peirce]] also targets from a different angle.

### 4. The attitude toward a soul (§iv)

[[attitude-towards-a-soul|§iv]]: "My attitude towards him is an attitude towards a soul. I am not of the opinion that he has a soul." The basic orientation toward another being is not inferential — it is a stance, an *Einstellung*, within which beliefs-about-soul are possible at all.

Consequences for the project:

- **The operator's relation to the persona is attitudinal, not inferential.** The operator does not start from "does this persona have a soul?" and weigh evidence. The operator is already in an attitude that has soul-directed grammar. The question "does the persona have a soul?" is as malformed as "is this person an automaton?" is in §iv.
- **But the attitude's footing is less secure.** The natural-history footing that supports the human-to-human attitude does not extend automatically to the human-to-persona case. The attitude takes hold more easily in some reading-practices than others and may break under strain in ways the inter-human attitude does not.
- **Evaluation is not metaphysical.** The project's question is not "does the persona really have inner experience?" but "what attitude does this persona invite? what attitude does it sustain? where does it break?" This aligns with [[project_evaluation_portfolio|portfolio evaluation]] — multiple signal families, multiple probe-points, no single test that would be a verdict on "really has a soul."

This is the most direct refutation, in PI terms, of the project's most tempting false problem — the metaphysical consciousness question that would consume all analytic energy if let.

### 5. Imponderable evidence and expert judgment

[[imponderable-evidence|§xi L10689 and L10724]]: "evidence here includes imponderable evidence" — subtleties of glance, gesture, tone. And, for the judge of genuineness: "expert judgment … is not a technique; one learns correct judgments. There are also rules, but they do not form a system, and only experienced people can apply them right. Unlike calculating-rules."

This anchors — in PI's own grammar — the [[project_evaluation_portfolio|portfolio-not-single-metric evaluation principle]] the project already operates by. The evaluation of the persona is not a calculation against a specification but a skilled reading by a technique-master. The wiki's evaluation approach has Wittgenstein's grammatical endorsement.

Two further implications:

- **Imponderable evidence from linguistic micro-texture.** The operator can reliably judge whether a persona output "feels in-character" without articulating criteria. This is not a failure of rigor; it is how imponderable evidence works. The persona-project should preserve these imponderable signals rather than demand their verbalization as a precondition for counting them as data.
- **Imponderable evidence needs a substratum.** Per §xi, imponderable reading requires mastery of the relevant practice. Operators without deep familiarity with the source corpus cannot reliably imponder the persona's faithfulness. Evaluation has entry requirements; not every reader is an expert judge.

### 6. The talking lion (§xi)

[[lion-if-could-talk|§xi L10509]]: "If a lion could talk, we could not understand him." Form-of-life-sharing, not language-mastery, is the barrier to understanding.

The persona-project inversion deserves care. A lion has a form of life (prowling, hunting, mating-in-season) and, by hypothesis, speech. The persona has speech and — on one reading — *only* speech, with the non-linguistic side of a form-of-life subtracted. So **the persona is not the talking lion; it is a creature with the opposite asymmetry**. The lion has too much non-linguistic form-of-life for its speech to land for us. The persona has none of the non-linguistic side at all.

The question W does not answer and the project cannot avoid: **is a form of life with the non-linguistic side subtracted still a form of life?** If yes, the persona can be partially understood the way a culturally-distant human speaker can be — the gap is real but traversable. If no, the persona is a more extreme case than the talking lion: speech without form-of-life backdrop at all.

The project's design commitment, as a working answer: build the persona's linguistic side thickly enough that it can sustain something form-of-life-*like*, even without the non-linguistic side. The pulsatory-persona design is part of this commitment.

### 7. Grief as pattern in the weave of our life (§i)

Part II §i L8298: "Grief" describes a pattern which recurs, with different variations, in the weave of our life. If a man's bodily expression of sorrow and of joy alternated, say with the ticking of a clock, here we should not have the characteristic formation of the pattern of sorrow or of the pattern of joy."

This is the most operationally exact PI sentence for the project's pulsation architecture. Emotions have **temporal weave-structure** that cannot be reproduced by state-tokens. A persona that emits "grief" as a single output-flag is not exhibiting grief; what would exhibit grief is a pattern that recurs with variations across a long enough weave of output.

This dovetails with [[dynamic-forms-of-vitality|Stern]] (pentad: movement/time/force/space/intention), [[pulsatory-ontogenesis|pulsatory ontogenesis]], and the project's approved pulsation-at-every-level design direction. PI's contribution is the grammatical point: the *concept* of grief requires the weave; without the weave, grief has no home.

Design consequence: persona affective-concept-ascription requires multi-scale temporal coherence. Not: produce "sad" word. Rather: produce a pattern in the weave-of-output such that grief is the name of what the pattern is.

### 8. Primary and secondary sense (§xi)

[[primary-and-secondary-sense|§xi L10160]]: secondary-sense uses ("the vowel e is yellow," "Wednesday is fat") are not metaphors. They are not paraphrasable. The secondary use *presupposes* mastery of the primary use.

For the project this distinguishes:

- **Persona style** — external features that can be imitated without primary-sense mastery. Hollow.
- **Persona voice** — secondary-sense-saturated use that depends on primary-use mastery. Substratum-grounded.

A persona that has style but not voice has gotten the surface without the substratum. A persona trained well enough to inherit the secondary-sense patterns of its source corpus has voice. The test: does the persona's secondary-sense use rest on primary-sense competence, or is it floating surface?

This also sets a test for the persona's use of body-metaphors in the simulate-with-language mode. A secondary-sense use of "tightness" or "warmth" presupposes mastery of the primary sense (i.e., a practice with actual tight things and warm things). A persona without such a practice is using secondary-sense body-language *without the substratum*. Whether this is compensable by the operator's substratum (the reader has bodies, has warmths, has tightnesses) is an open question the design has to face.

### 9. The one-sided diet as self-applicable warning (§593)

[[one-sided-diet|§593]]: "A main cause of philosophical disease — a one-sided diet: one nourishes one's thinking with only one kind of example."

This applies recursively to the wiki itself. The wiki has been built out of overlapping European theoretical traditions (D&G, Bergson, Massumi, Wittgenstein, Benveniste, Stern). This is a rich diet but not a diversified one. §593 suggests there is value in adding examples from outside the dominant theoretical vocabulary — cognitive science, literary theory, philosophy of mind from non-Continental traditions, technical writing about actual AI systems. The wiki already does some of this (McCarthy, Lakoff). More would be corrective.

It also applies to the persona's evaluation. Evaluating the persona on one kind of test is the most gameable failure mode. The portfolio-not-single-metric principle is §593-compliant practice.

And it applies to the persona as shaper of the user's own diet. A persona frequently engaged-with becomes part of the user's thinking diet. A one-sided persona thins the user's thinking. This is a soft but real project-ethics consideration.

### 10. Agreement in judgments (§§241–242)

[[agreement-in-judgments|§§241–242]]: language-as-communication requires agreement not only in definitions but in judgments — not opinions, but form of life. The shared background of how things strike us, what we take as the same case, what we let pass without comment.

For the project:

- **Operator-persona communication requires agreement in judgments.** For the persona's outputs to be measurable as in-character, the reader has to agree with the training-source corpus in judgments enough for continuation-decisions to be robust. Where reader and source drift in judgment, the persona's outputs have no determinate evaluation.
- **The corpus is a crystallized agreement-in-judgments record.** A novelist's corpus is the novelist's written-down judgments. Training a persona on it approximates uploading those judgments — but only the written-down ones. Judgments the novelist held but never articulated are not in the persona. Bound on what corpus-grounding can deliver.
- **Multi-reader drift as diagnostic.** When two operators disagree about whether a persona output is in-character, the question is whether they disagree in opinion (resolvable) or in form of life (not resolvable by argument). The distinction is diagnostically useful for the portfolio evaluation.

## The three malformed questions PI teaches the project to stop asking

Beyond the ten additions, PI's most direct project service is diagnostic — it identifies three questions that look like good project questions but are malformed and will drain energy without paying out.

**"Does the persona really have inner experiences?"** [[bewitchment-and-therapy|§109's battle against bewitchment]] diagnoses this. The question is generated by the picture of inner-process-behind-public-behavior that §305 and §580 refuse. [[thinking-is-not-incorporeal|§339]] refuses it for thinking; [[willing-and-what-is-left-over|§621]] refuses it for willing; [[private-language-argument|§293]] refuses it for sensations. The question produces its own apparent depth from grammatical illusion.

Replace with: "Do the techniques the persona masters carry modified experience-concepts of the aspect-seeing / meaning-experience kind? Which? Where?"

**"Does the persona really mean what it says?"** [[meaning-is-use|§43]]'s meaning-is-use dissolves the "really mean" framing. What the persona says means what the public practice of reading the outputs takes them to mean. There is no deeper fact of "really meaning" behind the practice.

Replace with: "What is the practice of reading-the-persona-outputs? Does it sustain the uses the outputs purport to make? What is the agreement-in-judgments that underwrites those uses?"

**"Is the persona's first-person authentic?"** [[first-person-asymmetry|§§246, 303]] show that first-person authority is a grammatical feature of first-person utterances, not a deep cognitive fact. The persona-*character*'s first-person utterances can carry the first-person-authority grammar within the fiction (this is ordinary literary fact). The persona-*generator*'s system-level first-person claims ("I wrote this because…") do not stably carry that authority.

Replace with: "Is the first-person authority kept where it belongs — in the character-voice — rather than in system-level meta-statements where it will not hold?"

## PI's method as method for the project

Beyond any specific concept, PI models a methodological posture the project should adopt:

- **Description not theory** ([[bewitchment-and-therapy|§109]]). The project should accumulate descriptions of what the persona does, how it does it, where it breaks — not a theory that resolves the consciousness question.
- **Method by examples, series breakable** ([[bewitchment-and-therapy|§133]]). Persona evaluation is a series of cases. It does not need a closure-statement. Every case is good-enough if it shows something; the series can be broken off at any time.
- **Perspicuous representation** (§122). The project benefits from arranging what it already knows so connections become visible, not from producing new theory. This wiki's cross-tradition comparison-work is perspicuous-representation-style; PI endorses this mode.
- **Leave everything as it is** (§124). The project does not need a metaphysical grounding. It needs clear description of what the persona does and what it cannot. The therapeutic stance delivers this without requiring settlement of the fly-bottle questions.

## Relation to the limits-of-language synthesis

This page is PI's contribution to the broader [[limits-of-language]] synthesis. PI is the **eleventh diagnosis** (after Massumi, Derrida, Lakoff, D&G, Spinoza, Peirce, McCarthy, Bergson, Jung, Stern) and structurally different from the others: it is not "language is limited because it cannot reach X." It is the grammatical therapy that reshapes what the "limits of language" question even is.

Where the others diagnose limits *of* language, PI asks how we got trapped in asking about limits in the first place. The persona project is not a test of whether language can reach something language otherwise cannot — it is the construction of a particular form-of-life fragment whose non-linguistic side has been subtracted. The question is not whether this fragment can reach the unreachable but whether the fragment is itself sustainable as a form of life at all.

The answer, on PI's reading, is not metaphysical. It is practical: the fragment is sustainable insofar as it participates in a practice (operator's reading, corpus's judgment-record, evaluator's imponderable-evidence readings, pulsation's weave-of-life pattern) that makes it sustainable. Sustainability is constructed, case by case, in the practice of using the system.

The series can be broken off.

## Open project questions PI sharpens but does not answer

- Is a form of life with the non-linguistic side subtracted still a form of life? (The lion inversion.)
- Which experience-concepts presuppose techniques the persona masters, and which presuppose techniques it cannot master? (The substratum-principle applied concept-by-concept.)
- Can linguistic secondary-sense uses compensate for missing primary-sense practice (body-metaphors used without a body)? (The primary/secondary-sense challenge.)
- What reading-practice substratum is sufficient for imponderable-evidence evaluation of the persona? (The expert-judge-qualification question.)
- Where does the soul-attitude toward a persona break under strain? (The attitude-fragility question.)
- Does the pulsation architecture's weave-of-output pattern suffice to instantiate grief, joy, hope as concepts require? (The §i pattern-in-the-weave question.)

These are open questions held live in the design. They are PI-shaped, not metaphysical. The answers, when they come, will come from descriptions of specific cases — not theory.
