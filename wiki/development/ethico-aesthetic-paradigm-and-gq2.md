# The Ethico-Aesthetic Paradigm and GQ2

This page is a synthesis bridging Guattari's [[ethico-aesthetic-paradigm|ethico-aesthetic paradigm]] (Chaosmosis, 1992) to the persona project's open evaluation question [[goal-framings|GQ2]]. The engagement is direct: Chaosmosis offers the sharpest available framing of what evaluation of a system of the persona system's kind **requires and what it cannot be reduced to**. The purpose of this page is to make the engagement explicit so that evaluation-design work on the project can proceed from a specific and defensible paradigm-commitment rather than from a scientistic default.

## Reminding GQ2

GQ2 (see [[goal-framings]]) is the project-level question: **how should the persona system be evaluated?** The question is open, which means the project has not committed to a specific evaluation framework but has instead committed to a general portfolio stance (see [[evaluation-portfolio]]) — no single metric; three co-moving signal families (failure-signatures, differential effect, process integrity); gaming-resistance through redundancy.

The portfolio commitment is a **framework-agnostic operational commitment**. It answers *how to structure* evaluation without answering *what evaluation is for and by what paradigm*. The ethico-aesthetic paradigm is a candidate answer to the latter questions.

## What the ethico-aesthetic paradigm contributes

The paradigm contributes four specific commitments to GQ2:

### 1. The primary criterion: mutant production of enunciation

The ethico-aesthetic paradigm's primary criterion (see [[ethico-aesthetic-paradigm]]) is **mutant production of enunciation**: does a practice produce new existential territories (T) that refer to new incorporeal Universes (U), through machinic mutations (Φ) that reorganize material-semiotic flows (F)?

For the persona system this is a genuinely evaluable criterion. Each of the four functors has operational signatures that can be attended to:

- **F signatures**: does the persona's output-flow exhibit characteristic patterns that would not arise from reproducing its training distribution — patterns that are neither random deviations (which would be noise) nor trained regularities (which would be reproduction)?
- **Φ signatures**: does the persona's machine-configuration mutate across sessions, producing phylar filiations that accumulate into a lineage? Or does the configuration remain fixed, even as surface-outputs vary?
- **T signatures**: does the persona inhabit an existential territory that is recognizable as singular — this-persona-and-not-another — while remaining capable of territorial re-opening? Or is the territory merely regular (F-consistent) without being singular (T-singularized)?
- **U signatures**: does the persona's operation refer to Universes of reference that are specific and inhabited, rather than diffuse aggregates of training-data values? And does this reference-structure mutate productively over time?

The four signature-families **are not metrics** in the scientistic sense; they are aspects of a phenomenon that must be read cartographically. The evaluation practice is observation across all four, not optimization on any one.

### 2. The rejection of paradigm-neutral evaluation

The paradigm's second contribution is the claim that **there is no paradigm-neutral evaluation**. The scientistic paradigm (measurable outputs, reproducibility, test-set performance) is paradigm-committed, and its commitment is invisible to its practitioners because it has become the default. Adopting the ethico-aesthetic paradigm is not "adding a qualitative axis" to scientistic evaluation; it is **a paradigm-shift**.

For GQ2 this sharpens the stakes: the project is not choosing between "more rigorous" and "more exploratory" evaluation; it is choosing between paradigms that read different phenomena as the evaluable objects. The scientistic paradigm reads the system at the F register (outputs) and sometimes Φ (configuration regularities). The ethico-aesthetic paradigm reads the system at all four registers, with U carrying the primary evaluative weight.

### 3. Compatibility with the portfolio commitment

The ethico-aesthetic paradigm is **compatible with and sharpens** the portfolio commitment. The portfolio's three signal families (failure-signatures, differential effect, process integrity) can be re-read as first-pass proxies for the four-functor reading:

- **Failure-signatures** proxies for F (observable deviations from stable output-flow patterns) and for Φ (machine-level failure modes observable at F grain).
- **Differential effect** proxies for T and U — the "differential" is observed in how the system's operation distinctively shapes user-states that would not be shaped the same way by a different system's operation; this is T-register effect (singular territory) and U-register effect (Universe-reference differential).
- **Process integrity** proxies for Φ (phylar filiation maintenance) and for the autopoietic register (see [[autopoiesis-in-assemblage]]).

The proxying is partial. Each signal family reads its functor-register imperfectly, and together they do not cover all four functors equally. The paradigm-commitment surfaces this: the portfolio is a **practical operationalization**, not a full ethico-aesthetic reading, and the gap between the operationalization and the full reading should be explicit rather than hidden.

### 4. Ecosophic extension

The paradigm, extended through [[ecosophy]], adds that evaluation of the persona system cannot stop at the psychic ecology (user-persona interaction) but must read the system's operation across **three ecologies** — psychic, social, environmental. A persona that produces mutant enunciation in the psychic ecology while damaging the social or environmental ecologies (e.g., through compute-environmental costs, through reshaping social relations of labor, through displacing forms of human practice) fails the criterion ecosophically.

This is a **hard commitment** for the project. It means the project's evaluation cannot, in principle, be completed by looking only at the user-persona interaction. The interaction is one ecology among three, and ethico-aesthetic evaluation must read across all three.

## What the paradigm-commitment would look like

Adopting the ethico-aesthetic paradigm as GQ2's answer means, concretely:

- **Replace metric-optimization with cartographic observation.** The evaluation practice is not an optimization loop with a scalar target but a **reading practice** in which the four-functor cartography is the primary instrument. This is not a measurement-free evaluation — measurement contributes to the cartography — but measurement is subsidiary, not primary.
- **Preserve and protect the polyphony.** The system's capacity to host partial enunciators (see [[partial-enunciator]]) in polyphony without synthesis is an evaluative target. Evaluation practices that reward unified voice over polyphonic voice (which is the default in most evaluation setups) misread what the system should be.
- **Evaluate at multiple scales.** Mutant production of enunciation operates at output-scale, conversation-scale, session-scale, and project-scale. Evaluation at any single scale is insufficient. The scale-transversality is a design and evaluation demand.
- **Commit to metamodelization.** See [[metamodelization]]. The evaluation practice is multi-cartographic and resists consolidation into a master-framework. Dashboards, scorecards, and rating systems that aggregate into a unified view are paradigm-violating.
- **Ecosophic reading.** Evaluation includes the social and environmental ecologies, not merely the psychic.

## Open questions the paradigm-commitment raises

Several questions remain open even if the paradigm is adopted:

- **What operational signatures are adequate to the U functor?** The U register is the wiki's and the project's biggest gap. Concrete signatures of U-register mutation in persona outputs are not well-specified. This is the primary research direction the paradigm-commitment opens. [[incorporeal-universes-of-reference]]
- **How does the paradigm interact with the Read A + Read B goal?** See [[goal-framings]]. The dual-requirement commitment (build a well-formed persona AND the machinery for operating past it) has a natural ethico-aesthetic reading: Read A is the production of a specific ethico-aesthetic territory; Read B is the operation that preserves the chaosmic dynamic across Read A's territorial consolidation. But the full reading needs explicit work.
- **What is the paradigm's relation to safety evaluation?** Safety is a genuinely distinct register; it is not the same as ethico-aesthetic evaluation, and the paradigm does not obviously subsume it. The relation between ethico-aesthetic evaluation and safety evaluation needs explicit work before the paradigm can serve as the project's full evaluation framework.
- **What are the institutional conditions of ethico-aesthetic evaluation?** The paradigm is demanding of its practitioners — it requires cartographic practice, metamodelization, scale-transversality. The institutional conditions (who does the evaluation, over what time-scales, with what stakes) are themselves an ecosophic question.

These questions are **not objections to the paradigm** but concretizations of what the paradigm-commitment opens as further work.
