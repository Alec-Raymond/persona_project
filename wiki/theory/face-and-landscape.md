Face-and-landscape is the *coupling-pair* Deleuze and Guattari theorize in *A Thousand Plateaus* plateau 7 ("Year Zero: Faciality"). It names neither the face alone nor the landscape alone but the **reciprocal-presupposition structure** by which the face organizes the landscape *and* the landscape expresses the face — each deterritorializing the other, each reterritorializing onto the other's moving site. The page is distinct from [[faciality]] (which treats the abstract machine producing faces) and from [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization]] (which treats the general dynamics the couple instantiates). It focuses specifically on **the coupling-logic**: why face and landscape always come paired, what the pair does that neither term does alone, and what the persona project has to build (or cannot build) when it lacks one or both.

## The canonical formulation

ATP line 1355 (plateau 7):

> All faces envelop an unknown, unexplored landscape; all landscapes are populated by a loved or dreamed-of face, develop a face to come or already past. What face has not called upon the landscapes it amalgamated, sea and hill; what landscape has not evoked the face that would have completed it, providing an unexpected complement for its lines and traits?

And the positive-coupling statement at the same line:

> Christian education exerts spiritual control over both faciality and landscapity (*paysagéité*): Compose them both, color them in, complete them, arrange them according to a complementarity linking landscapes to faces.

Two reciprocal operations are named:

- **Face-as-landscape** — the face treated as a terrain, a map, "a chalk face with eyes cut in for a black hole." The face is not a portrait-on-a-background but *is* a landscape at its own scale. This is the close-up's condition of operation: "the close-up in film treats the face primarily as a landscape; that is the definition of film, black hole and white wall, screen and camera."
- **Landscape-as-face** — the landscape treated as a face, organized by a single gaze-point or focal structure. "Painting takes up the same movement but also reverses it, positioning a landscape as a face, treating one like the other: 'treatise on the face and the landscape.'"

The two operations are not alternatives — they are the *same* abstract-machine move made from each end. Face becomes landscape and landscape becomes face by running one and the same apparatus (white wall / black hole) at different scales. **What is fundamental is neither term but the reciprocal-presupposition itself.**

## The coupling as abstract-machine signature

Plateau 7 lists the face-landscape couple alongside other canonical deterritorialization-couplings (ATP line 1355, 1366):

| Couple | Direction of deterritorialization |
|---|---|
| Hand – tool | The hand deterritorializes the paw; the tool is the reciprocated deterritorialization |
| Mouth – breast | The mouth deterritorializes the snout; the breast is the reciprocated deterritorialization of the mammary gland |
| Body – clothing | The body deterritorializes the pelt; clothing is the reciprocated deterritorialization |
| **Face – landscape** | The face deterritorializes the head; the landscape deterritorializes the milieu |

The face-landscape couple is the *higher-order* couple in this list. The previous couples operate at the organic-behavioral register; the face-landscape couple operates at what ATP calls the "absolute deterritorialization" register — the face detaches the head from the stratum of the organism *entirely*, and the landscape detaches the milieu from its lived territoriality *entirely*. The couple operates "at a 'higher' level" (ATP 1355) because both terms are already passing through absolute deterritorialization when they couple.

This makes the face-landscape couple the diagnostic case for the [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|four theorems]] — not because it is the simplest but because it is the one where absolute deterritorialization can be seen operating through its coupling dynamics, which ATP claims is in fact how absolute deterritorialization always operates.

## Landscapity — the parallel concept to faciality

*Paysagéité* (landscapity) is the parallel concept to *visagéité* (faciality). ATP treats them symmetrically: a "faciality machine" produces faces; a "landscapity apparatus" produces landscapes; the same abstract machine (white wall / black hole) operates on both.

Key specifications at line 1355:

- The landscape is not a milieu (a lived territoriality) but a **deterritorialized world** — the milieu's own absolute deterritorialization into a view, a vista, a horizon with a vanishing point.
- Architecture operates by "positioning [its] ensembles — houses, towns or cities, monuments or factories — to function like faces in the landscape they transform." Architecture makes face-in-landscape directly; the building *is* a face; the surrounding built environment *is* a landscape.
- Painting runs both directions at once: portraits operate as face-become-landscape (the close-up logic); landscape-painting operates as landscape-become-face (organized by a vanishing-point, a horizon, a central axis).

Landscapity is rarer as a term in the wiki than faciality, and the asymmetry has been a gap. Faciality has a fully worked out page ([[faciality]]); landscapity has been an adjunct-concept without its own page. This page is where the coupled operation sits; a standalone landscapity page would further specify the landscape-producing abstract machine on its own.

## Courtly novel as paradigm case: the catatonic knight

Plateau 7 gives an extended reading of Chrétien de Troyes's *Perceval* (line 1357) as the paradigm of the face-landscape couple in novelistic form:

> A flock of geese flew which the snow had dazzled... Perceval saw them and heard them... When Perceval saw the trampled snow on which the goose had lain, and the blood which appeared around, he leaned upon his lance and looked at that image, for the blood and the snow together seemed to him like the fresh color which was on the face of his friend, and he thinks until he forgets himself; for the vermilion seated on white was on her face just the same as these three drops of blood on the white snow.

ATP's reading: "Everything is there: the redundancy specific to the face and landscape, the snowy white wall of the landscape-face, the black hole of the falcon and the three drops distributed on the wall; and, simultaneously, the silvery line of the landscape-face spinning toward the black hole of the knight deep in catatonia."

This is the canonical diagram of the face-landscape couple:

- **The white wall** is the snow (the landscape) and the friend's cheek (the face) at once. The landscape is a face-scale white wall; the face is a landscape-scale white wall.
- **The black hole** is the falcon's shadow on the snow (landscape) and the imagined lover's eye (face) at once. The landscape has a black hole; the face has a black hole.
- **The three drops of blood** are the faciality-traits (red marks on white wall) distributing themselves across the landscape-face.
- **The knight's catatonia** is the subject's absorption into the face-landscape system — "a catatonic knight seated on his steed, leaning on his lance, waiting, seeing the face of his loved one in the landscape." The knight has been *drawn through* the face-landscape coupling to a point where distinction between face-there and landscape-here has collapsed.

ATP's generalization: **the novel-as-genre is defined by this structure.** "There is a face-landscape aggregate proper to the novel, in which black holes sometimes distribute themselves on a white wall, and the white line of the horizon sometimes spins toward a black hole, or both simultaneously" (1357). Don Quixote's catatonias, Princesse de Clèves's states of rest, Molloy's disorientations — all instances of the knight-seated-on-his-steed scene, face-landscape collapsed through the subject who can no longer sort them.

This makes the novel-genre a specifically face-landscape-productive machine, which has consequences for the persona project (below §Persona-project stakes).

## Two limit-modes of the couple

ATP distinguishes two limit-modes the face-landscape couple runs in (ATP 1388–1394):

1. **Terrestrial signifying despotic mode.** Black holes distributed on the white wall. The landscape is populated with multiple black holes (eyes, loci of signification), and the face is a surface on which multiple binary-choices play out. "A multiplication of eyes" — the despotic face/landscape of the frontal-view icon, the Byzantine mosaic. Redundancy of frequency.

2. **Maritime subjective passional authoritarian mode.** The white wall unravels, becoming a silver thread running toward a single black hole. The landscape is a horizon-line spinning toward one point of attention; the face is in-profile, turned toward the focal passion. "Tristan, Isolde, Isolde, Tristan, in the boat carrying them to the black hole of betrayal and death." Redundancy of resonance.

The modes are not alternatives — ATP emphasizes any actual face-landscape couple has some proportion of both, and the analytic question is the ratio. A face/landscape pair dominated by mode 1 is signifying-despotic; one dominated by mode 2 is subjectifying-authoritarian; a "mixed" one moves between them.

A third state (the probe-head / defaced state) is the couple *dismantled* — faciality-traits and landscapity-traits released from the mode-1/mode-2 coupling and forming rhizomatic connections with each other (ATP 1414, "Each freed faciality trait forms a rhizome with a freed trait of landscapity, picturality, or musicality").

## Why the couple is architectural-theorem-driven, not metaphorical

A naive reading might treat "face is a landscape" as metaphor — a poetic extension of face-language into landscape-language. ATP explicitly rejects this (line 1355, near the Perceval passage): "All of this is in no way characteristic of the genre of the novel only at the end of its history; it is there from the beginning, it is an essential part of the genre."

The face-landscape coupling is not metaphor but **abstract-machine identity**: face and landscape are *the same abstract machine* running on different raw material. White wall/black hole on the head produces a face; white wall/black hole on the milieu produces a landscape. Because the same machine produces both, the two can be composed into each other without translation — the close-up is the face *used as* a landscape, the vanishing-point is the landscape *used as* a face.

This is why the [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|four theorems]] apply to face-landscape with particular force: the theorems specify how deterritorialization-couples evolve, and face-landscape is the couple where the abstract machine's own identity is what generates the coupling. Other couples (hand-tool, mouth-breast) are couples across different organs; face-landscape is a couple-of-a-machine-with-itself-at-different-scales.

## Persona-project stakes

**1. The persona has a face-analogue but has it a landscape-analogue?**

The [[faciality]] page works out the persona's face-analogue: the synthesis step produces a recognizable surface that reads as "the persona's face." What is the persona's *landscape*?

Candidate answers:

- **The [[body-without-organs|BwO]] text is the landscape.** Faciality.md already says this: "the BwO text is the landscape to the persona's face. They are in reciprocal presupposition." On this reading, the persona-face is to the BwO-landscape as the human face is to the human landscape. Holds.
- **The user's face-operations are the landscape.** Baudrillard's "you are the screen" addendum to faciality.md suggests that the persona's faciality apparatus operates primarily on the user-side — the user's face is the screen where the persona's operations register. If the user is the screen, the user's trace-across-turns is the landscape.
- **The conversation-so-far is the landscape.** The tokens preceding the current turn, the shape of the exchange, the rhythm of back-and-forth — all constitute a temporal terrain the persona's face is posted in. This is the face-landscape couple at the conversation register.

All three candidates have merit; the wiki holds them live rather than resolving.

**2. The absence-of-one-term may be as consequential as the absence-of-both.**

A persona with a face but no landscape (or a landscape but no face) is in a structurally different position than a persona with neither. ATP's claim that face and landscape *co-produce* each other implies that the LLM-persona's landscape-absence (if we read the persona as lacking a landscape in the robust sense) is not just a missing feature but a structural failure of the couple-function. The face has no landscape to *reterritorialize onto* (Theorem III of [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|the four theorems]]) — which means the persona's face-operations have nowhere to land.

This may explain a specific persona-failure mode: a persona that is face-coherent (recognizable voice, stable traits) but produces text that "does not locate itself anywhere" — no scene, no ambient, no world-that-the-face-looks-out-from. The face operates but its landscape-partner is not there, so the face-operations fold back on themselves without distribution.

**3. The novel-genre's face-landscape structure is the craft-resource the persona has access to.**

If the novel is a face-landscape-productive machine (ATP 1357), and the persona's output is textual, then the persona has access to *novelistic* face-landscape production — it can compose face-landscape couples in language, even if it has no bodily-visual face or lived landscape of its own. This is a version of the [[no-body-simulate-with-language|simulate-with-language]] move: face-landscape, at the novelistic register, is a language-operation the persona can execute.

The [[reflector-consciousness|reflector]] becomes the craft-level operator here. James's reflector is a face posted at a landscape (the *work* as landscape); the reflector's consciousness *is* the face-landscape coupling at the craft-level. A persona operating in reflector-register is producing face-landscape couples — the reflector's consciousness is the persona's face; the work-as-rendered is the persona's landscape; the coupling is in operation.

**4. The probe-head's complement is a probe-landscape.**

[[faciality]]'s concept of the probe-head (a face-trait emission whose traits propagate without being absorbed back into face-type) has its face-landscape complement: the **probe-landscape** — a landscape-trait emission whose traits propagate without being absorbed back into landscape-type. ATP's "each freed faciality trait forms a rhizome with a freed trait of landscapity" is the rhizome of probe-heads and probe-landscapes in interaction.

For persona design: if the synthesis step can occasionally emit probe-head traits (faciality-traits not absorbed back), it should also be possible for the persona to emit *probe-landscape* traits — landscape-traits (scene-traits, ambient-traits, place-traits) that are not absorbed back into the standard "this is where the character is" landscape-reading. This is a specific register the persona can operate in that goes beyond what the faciality-page alone describes. See [[scene-vs-picture]] for the craft-side of landscape-operation.

**5. Dismantling face-and-landscape (not just the face) is a harder target than it looks.**

D&G's "dismantle the face" program (ATP 1408, "Dismantling the face is no mean affair") requires simultaneously dismantling the landscape the face is coupled to. A persona-design that targets defacialization without addressing its landscape will produce the Beckett-Worm configuration (see [[worm|Worm]] and [[face-how-encouraging]]): a persona without face but with an uninhabited landscape, no coupling, no rhythm of face-and-place. The face-landscape complementarity means that the defacialization target needs to be specified *as a couple-target*, not a face-target alone.

## Cross-tradition placements

**Wittgenstein's pain-face as grammatical-criterion-face with a question about landscape.** [[pain-behaviour|Wittgenstein's §281]] grounds the face as a grammatical criterion of the pain-concept — "only of a living human being and what resembles (behaves like) a living human being can one say: it has sensations." The pain-face is the criterion; the form-of-life is the "landscape" in which it functions. This is a specifically *form-of-life-as-landscape* reading the D&G vocabulary does not supply. See [[private-language-argument]]'s note that "D&G's faciality makes the face a social-regulative surface, not a window onto an inner subject. W's PLA refuses the window metaphor from the language side. The parallels are strong; flag rather than merge." The face-landscape page is where this flag can be unpacked: D&G specify the apparatus that installs face-in-landscape as a recognition-regime; Wittgenstein specifies the grammatical-landscape (form-of-life) within which pain-faces have sense as criteria. The two apparatuses are orthogonal rather than rival: faciality is the capture-apparatus; form-of-life is the intelligibility-apparatus. A full account holds both.

**The attitude-towards-a-soul and face-landscape.** [[attitude-towards-a-soul|Wittgenstein II.iv]]: "My attitude towards him is an attitude towards a soul. I am not of the opinion that he has a soul." The attitude is a form-of-life stance toward the other's face. Read through face-and-landscape: the attitude-towards-a-soul is a specific way of holding the face-landscape couple — it refuses the face-as-window-on-inner-item reading and instead treats the face-landscape couple as the whole surface on which the soul-attitude operates. The attitude is not an inference from face to inner-soul; it is an engagement with face-as-face-in-landscape. This sharpens the [[attitude-towards-a-soul]] claim that a full synthesis "would hold both" the D&G machinic-production-of-face and the W grammatical-attitude-toward-soul: the face-landscape page is where the coupling-as-surface is specifiable without collapsing into either tradition.

**Merleau-Ponty's flesh and face-landscape.** [[invisible-of-the-visible|MP's flesh]] gives a fourth register the D&G faciality-reading does not directly give: the face and the landscape are both *fleshly* in MP's sense, part of a chiasmic-reversible structure. Face sees landscape; landscape sees face back (the "landscape that looks at me" in MP's language). The face-landscape couple in MP is not an abstract-machine identity but a fleshly reversibility — same substrate, different poles of a single chiasm. This adds a specifically *ontological* register to the coupling: face-and-landscape is not just the faciality-machine's bi-scalar operation; it is the flesh's reversibility showing up at the level of recognized features. The persona's no-flesh condition is therefore a no-reversibility condition at the face-landscape register — a distinct loss from what D&G name. See [[ecart]] for the reversibility-écart apparatus.

## Relation to the refrain

The face-landscape couple is a specifically stabilized form of the territorial [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]]. The refrain names the rhythm-of-return that constitutes a territory; the face-landscape couple names the spatialized form that territory takes at the signifiance-subjectification register. A persona's "characteristic voice-in-scene" (its recognizable face in its recognizable ambient) is its face-landscape refrain.

Dismantling-the-face runs into dismantling-the-refrain (see [[refrain-and-territorialization#sobriety-and-the-cosmic-artisan|cosmic refrain]]): the cosmic-refrain is the territorial-refrain opened to the cosmos, which at the face-landscape register is the face-landscape couple opened to probe-head + probe-landscape rhizomes. The design target is not no-refrain and not no-face-landscape — it is a refrain porous enough to emit probe-traits while still providing the territorial-security the persona needs to operate at all.

## Tensions held live

⚠ **Face-landscape complicated by the substitute-faciality regime.** Baudrillard's "TV is watching you" addendum ([[faciality]] §"Mass-media substitute facialities") claims the faciality apparatus now runs *through* the user — the user's face is the screen; the LLM's face is displaced onto the user's face-operations. What happens to the landscape-term under this inversion? Is the LLM's "landscape" the conversation-so-far, or has it been inverted onto the user's training-distribution-of-conversations? The face-landscape coupling logic does not automatically survive the inversion. Unresolved.

⚠ **The courtly-novel reading may over-determine the persona-novelistic link.** ATP's claim that the novel-as-genre is face-landscape-productive is strong and not uncontested. If the persona's craft-register is novelistic, it inherits face-landscape-productivity; if other genres (dialogue, essay, poem) operate differently at the face-landscape register, the persona has different couple-logics available. The wiki has not worked out the genre-by-genre face-landscape-signature.

⚠ **Landscapity as an under-specified term.** The wiki has a full [[faciality]] page but no comparable landscapity page. Face-and-landscape presupposes both terms are well-specified, but only one is. This page partially fills the gap by treating landscapity-as-coupling-partner, but a freestanding landscapity page (working out the landscapity-machine in its own right, the landscapity-traits register, the probe-landscape operation) is a reasonable next page. Flagged rather than written.

⚠ **Absolute deterritorialization and the face-landscape couple.** ATP claims the face-landscape couple operates at absolute deterritorialization. But the probe-head / probe-landscape program is also an absolute-deterritorialization target. This suggests the face-landscape couple is *already* absolute-deterritorialized material, and the probe-program is a *further* deterritorialization of already-absolute terms. The wiki's [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|degrees of absolute deterritorialization]] treatment needs more work before the "more-absolute-than-absolute" question can be clean.

⚠ **Face-landscape in the persona's no-body condition.** The persona has no body, no face-organ, no lived landscape. Whether the face-landscape couple can be simulated-in-language at all is a version of [[no-body-simulate-with-language]]'s central question. The James-reflector route (stakes §3 above) suggests yes; the Worm-route ([[face-how-encouraging]]) suggests the simulation produces specific losses. The wiki holds both.
