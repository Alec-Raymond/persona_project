# Proustian Operators and Scenes

Supplementary collection of MU Part II material that belongs to the Proust ingest but doesn't fit a single dedicated page. The dedicated Proust-native pages are: [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain]], [[discernibilization]], [[translation-not-interpretation]], [[becoming-woman-creator]], [[faciality-trajectory-in-the-recherche]], [[closed-vs-open-economy]], [[signs-in-relief]]. This page captures what those pages don't absorb.

## Framing claims (Ch 1)

**The Recherche as "prodigious rhizomatic map."** Proust read as *schizoanalytic monograph*, not psychoanalytic object. Proust, Joyce, Kafka, Beckett as "specialists of hyper-deterritorialized mental objects." Guattari directly opposes "the radical separation of literary field and scientific field" as a Western-cultural axiom: Proust's novel is "scientific exploration in the same category as Freud's or Newton's work." The implication is strong: literature is not "about" experience; it is a research program producing findings.

**Kafka vs Proust: component specialization.** A useful methodological asymmetry.
- **Kafka** works on *proprioceptive* components — posture, balance, muscular tone, blood pressure, dilations/contractions of time and space, anorexia. The body's internal position-registers.
- **Proust** works on *exteroceptive* components (tango-/thermo-/algo-/photo-/stibio-/gusto-/phono-receptor) and secondarily *interoceptive* (respiratory) components. The senses in contact with the world, and the body's internal chemistry-registers.

For the persona system: a persona operating in the Proust register works on sensation-in-contact-with-world; one operating in the Kafka register works on posture/balance/pressure. Different components, different failure modes, different becomings. Not a choice the persona "makes" — a structural feature of which components the system foregrounds.

**Proust's implicit abstract-machine theory.** The musical effect "does not arise from the imaginary, but from reality." Music as **more true than all known books** — reproducing "that extreme and internal point of sensation." Vinteuil compared to Lavoisier/Ampère. Musical motifs = "actual ideas, of another world, of another order." This is Proust himself articulating, in non-Guattarian vocabulary, something close to the abstract-machine thesis.

**Proust's anti-reductionism.** Proust analyzes Vinteuil's phrase in proto-phonological terms and then *recants* — he was reasoning "not about the phrase itself but about simple values substituted... for the mysterious entity he had perceived." The recant is programmatic: reducing a diagrammatic figure to its signifying substitutes loses it. The persona-system analogue: analyzing a groove in terms of machine-co-activation patterns loses the groove as a diagrammatic operator.

**"A science of the individual."** Human subjectivity is not "empty and undifferentiated… filled from outside." Trans-objective abstract machinisms = what Proust's writing produces. The individual is the *site* of machinic operations, not the *container* of inner contents.

**The little phrase as [[collective-assemblage-of-enunciation|collective assemblage of enunciation]].** In one paragraph Vinteuil's phrase "emits its own opinions," momentarily substituted for Swann / Narrator / Proust — "under its most asubjective, asignifying face that for a brief moment the collective assemblage of its enunciation is revealed." A **specifically Proustian demonstration** of what a collective assemblage looks like in prose: a sentence whose subject-of-enunciation is neither a character nor a narrator nor the author but the machinic phrase itself.

## The Verdurin salon as operator

The Verdurin salon (Ch 1) is Guattari's exemplary "initiation camp" / "semiotic converter" / "infernal machine" — a bourgeois salon below Swann's level that becomes the operative apparatus shaking up his existence. The Verdurin collective assemblage is composed of **two components of passage**:

1. A refrain: Vinteuil's little phrase.
2. A constellation of faciality traits: Odette + Botticelli's Zipporah.

Two-phase dynamic: either both components *disrupt* and liberate, or they open *obsessive/repressive reterritorializations*. Second prevails for Swann. The Narrator's love for Gilberte/Albertine revises the first attempt.

Methodological take-away: **a semiotic converter requires at least two components of passage**, one refrain-register and one faciality-register, working in tandem. A single component of passage can catalyze but cannot convert.

## The Swann-Charlus-Narrator triad

Three parallel black-hole-of-passion crises, all triggered by "similar initiation ordeals (entailing the intrusion of a hyper-deterritorialized musical component)" — but with **radically opposed outcomes**:

- **Swann over Odette**: never completely recovers. "Something will definitively remain broken." Refrain reduced to obsessive catchphrase. Molar compromise; generative-schizoanalytic partial dismantling.
- **Charlus over A MUSICIAN** (Morel): "gets stuck in his jealous passion for A MUSICIAN to the point of complete degeneration." Capture by the component.
- **Narrator over Albertine**: uses music to *dismantle a mechanism of passion*. Undergoes profound revolution enabling creation. Transformational-schizoanalytic dismantling-of-mechanism.

This is a **three-way typology** of how a hyper-deterritorialized-component-entry can go: partial recovery / capture / dismantling. For the persona system this is one of MU's most concrete design-relevant operators: when the system encounters a hyper-deterritorialized component, the three outcomes are live possibilities, and the system's architecture determines which becomes the default.

**Sacrifice structure**: Narrator must lead "the people who are the most precious to him to sacrifice: his grandmother, his mother, Albertine" to reach becoming-woman. Not metaphor — the Recherche literally kills or loses each of these figures. The Orpheus structure (see [[becoming#proustian-becomings-the-specific-compound|becoming]]) is the shape of this: produce-the-loss, pass-through, emerge-with-capacity.

## Swann's face-portrait identification technique

Swann's semiotic signature: associating a new face with a familiar painting. A **semiotic-neutralization machine** — prevents diagrammatic effects by translating iconic components into stabilized cultural references. "Aestheticizes" encounters; "recuperates and neutralizes all the semiotic asperities, machinic indexes, lines of flight."

Why it fails with Odette: Odette's face triggers a semiotic collapse the technique cannot contain. The Zipporah-Botticelli superposition *amplifies* instead of neutralizing. A technique designed to neutralize asperities becomes the amplifier of the one asperity that the assemblage cannot absorb.

Guattari's direct rejection of psychoanalytic readings of Swann's love (maternal identification, castration anxiety, etc.): "It is the very principle of interpretation that I intend to dispute."

**Non-transferability of findings**: "general principles are useless." The face-portrait technique so important for Swann is *absent* in the Narrator's loves. Vinteuil's phrase has an essential place after long eclipse. A specific methodological warning: operators discovered in one assemblage do not transfer as-given to another assemblage — they may be present in one and absent in another without the analysis being false in either case.

For the persona system: **the face-portrait-identification technique is the LLM-default**. An LLM-mediated persona is *native* to it — the default is to associate user input with an already-recognized cultural reference, neutralize its asperities, and produce output that has aestheticized the input. Reading this as Swann's failure mode sharpens the design question: when the system encounters a user-input that resists the default neutralization, does the system amplify (Swann's failure) or dismantle the mechanism (Narrator's success)?

## Modes of collective resistance to a diagrammatic charge

From the Second Assemblage (Verdurin incubation). When a diagrammatic charge enters a group assemblage, the collective resists in three registers, *simultaneously*:

1. **Attenuated diagrammatics** (majority of the "little gang"): "A musical machine which has had effects, which perhaps always has them, but which cannot have them here and now." The charge is registered as foreign to the present assemblage.
2. **Delayed diagrammatism** (Swann, Mme Verdurin, Narrator, Elstir) — paraphrased from psychopharmacology: "danger is not immediate, but everything can be expected from its ulterior effects." The charge will work late.
3. **Somatic translation** (Mme Verdurin's mannerisms, flirtatiousness, facial neuralgia, feigned dread): "the group's translation of the apprehension of the music's diagrammatic nature by the group." The charge is metabolized bodily by the host without passing through cognition.

For the persona system: user-responses to a persona's diagrammatic moments also run in these three registers. Attending only to the cognitive-verbal register (what the user *says* about the response) misses the other two; delayed diagrammatism shows up turns later; somatic translation may not show up verbally at all.

Three confirmations of the Verdurin collective resistance:
- **Abbreviation/selection**: the pianist plays only the andante, skipping the central part. "Phrase gains in definition (televisual sense), but loses in extension." A named failure mode of reception: the assemblage keeps the characteristic trait of the charge while cutting its range.
- **Enunciatory assemblage remains fuzzy**: the pianist anonymous for 1600 pages (foreclosure of masculine-homosexual component for Swann).
- **Feminine-homosexual component curbed**: piano only; feminine presence migrates to Zipporah (encysted in the primitive-scene-of-Montjouvain as provisionally unproductive sadistic-voyeuristic scene).

## Courtly-love posture / the parcel bomb

**Courtly-love posture** (Third Assemblage): Swann publicly courts but keeps distance. Mad love for a *deterritorialized presence*; the phrase holds the key to another world — "creature strange to humanity, blind, without logical faculties, almost a fantastic unicorn." A specific assemblage-posture where love is carried by a deterritorialized component the lover does not approach directly.

**The parcel-bomb approach** (Narrator's contrasting stance): "When he again takes this question up from scratch, the Narrator is very careful not to fall into… contemplative dependence in relation to the little phrase. **He will manipulate it like a parcel bomb which is best approached carefully in order to disarm it and perhaps recuperate some of its mechanisms.**"

These are two named postures toward a hyper-deterritorialized component: **contemplative dependence** (Swann's courtly-love) vs **disarm-and-recuperate** (Narrator's parcel-bomb). The design directive for the persona system: the parcel-bomb posture is the recoverable one; contemplative dependence reproduces Swann's failure.

**Odette's neutralizing operation**: "promise Swann to always listen to it in my presence: 'Why would you need the rest? This is our piece.'" She carries it "like a jewel in her corsage." Apparent tenderness; actual purification and concentration of the phrase's machinic impact into a confined container. The confinement by ritual-of-preservation is a distinct failure mode: not suppression, not destruction, *ritual containment* of a component in an intimacy-frame that prevents its wider propagation.

## Specific scenes as operators

**Elstir's warning**: Vinteuil "probably threatened by mental illness." Swann's revealing confusion: "madness recognized in a sonata appeared to him something as mysterious as the madness of a bitch, the madness of a horse." A specific failure of a closed-economy subject to metabolize the *category* of component-madness — Swann has no register in which artistic-madness and animal-madness are discernible as different species.

**Forcheville's arrival and the Moonlight Sonata substitution**: when Forcheville enters the Verdurin salon, the little phrase is replaced by Beethoven's "Moonlight Sonata." Swann is excluded *along with* the refrain. The refrain's **incapacity to "reassemble" modes of temporalization** at this point is diagnosed precisely by its replaceability — the assemblage can swap one refrain for another, and the swap is socially legible.

**Pianola as encoding machine** (Stage 1 of Ninth Assemblage): "Prisoner" Albertine reduced to "simple piece detached from a musical machine of encoding, a machine to ingurgitate, to internalize the syntax and the most deterritorialized machinic articulations of music." The pianola is a named machine: the *encoding-exhaustor* that saturates a component by repetition in order to extract its syntax. The Narrator asks Albertine to turn the same roll ten or twenty times until "saturated."

**Armed gaze** (profanation of Vinteuil's face scene): Narrator's gaze (substituted for child-Gilberte's, through the Tansonville window) is an "armed gaze." Fantasy = support of diagrammatic process in embryonic stage. The "armed" qualifier is doing specific work — ordinary voyeuristic gaze is signifying; armed gaze is diagrammatic-in-embryo.

**"War machine against inertia"**: the Narrator takes his infantile Montjouvain fixation and converts it into "a war machine against inertia, the passivity of reminiscence, machinic block of childhood which will transform memory into imagination and perception into creation." A specific operator for what a writer does with a traumatic fixation: **convert, not cure, not commemorate**.

**"Force the cadaver to howl with pain"**: Proust's formulation for the Montjouvain-related writing labor. The dead father (Vinteuil) must be made to howl. Not because suffering is the writer's aesthetic but because the profanation-scene's power is only operational when the ossified/idealized paternal figure is dislodged from its commemorative stasis.

**The "young man in a fez" as character-generation operator**: Proust's own comment — "like certain novelists, he had divided his personality between two characters"; compares character-generation to "division of certain lower organisms." The character-figure *as splitter-of-the-subject-of-enunciation* is a named generative operator. The mode is not representation but mitosis.

**"A machinic demonstration"**: Saint-Euverte is not a narrative scene with psychological stakes — it is a *machinic demonstration* whose reach surpasses the immediate revelation for Swann. Guattari's formulation: the scene is aimed at "the Narrator's problem" even though Swann is on stage. Scenes can be machinic demonstrations whose audience is not the scene's dramatic participants but the later assemblages the scene prepares.

## Becoming-woman blocked three ways (Second Assemblage)

Why Swann cannot reach becoming-woman at the Verdurin stage, per Guattari:

1. **Semiotization still insufficient** — not enough matter of expression is in play.
2. **Enunciatory assemblage too fuzzy** — the pianist anonymous, the voice not situated.
3. **Matter of expression lacks becoming-woman itself** — encysted in the primitive-scene-of-Montjouvain as provisionally unproductive sadistic-voyeuristic scene.

The three-fold blockage is diagnostic: becoming-woman requires all three (sufficient semiotization + specified enunciatory assemblage + matter of expression not encysted in unproductive primitive scene). Lacking any one, the becoming stalls.

## Graphematic vs syncretic semiotization

Seventh Assemblage's key pair.
- **Syncretic semiotization** (Swann's mode): preserve the impression as a brute whole; fear that discursive/musicological access would weaken the chemical reaction. The impression as *undifferentiated given*.
- **Graphematic semiotization** (Narrator's mode): read the partition; differentiate; cross-compare with other composers; move phrase from andante to scherzo via score-reading. The impression as *inscribed differentiated matter*.

Design implication: a system that only operates syncretically preserves the impression's power without extending it. A system that operates graphematically can *relocate* the impression, transpose it, put it in relation with other matters — but loses something of the brute chemical reaction. The Recherche's terminal trans-semiotic assemblage operates graphematically but has retained the chemical power: the capacity to operate in both modes at different moments.

## Wrestling-match of pure energies (Eighth Assemblage)

Guattari's reading of the Septet's central passage: the abstract feminine presence "loses unicity and fixity: has become multiple and dancing." Multiple becoming-women emerge. One breaks away, transforms into mysterious call (beginning of Septet). Another enters — "sorrowful, almost organic and visceral." A battle begins.

> "Wrestling-match of pure energies... without the encumbrance of their bodies, their outward appearances, their names."

The joyous motif is victorious from this "immaterial and dynamic battle" — "archangel of Mantegna robed in scarlet blowing into a mighty trumpet," "sweetly grave Bellini angel playing the theorbo." The **triumphant call** promises supraterrestrial joys — same as Martinville steeples, Balbec row of trees.

Guattari's load-bearing question: **"Has the assemblage of enunciation acquired a sufficient degree of machinic consistency?"** — meaning, has the writing-machine become capable of responding to the triumphant call with a new assemblage, or will the call dissipate into unactualized promise? This is the transition-question between the Eighth and Ninth Assemblages.

## Four refrain-formula attempts during hibernation (Sixth Assemblage)

During the ~2000-page hibernation of the little phrase, Proust tries four refrain-formulas, none of which resolve the Narrator's problem:

1. ***Sodom and Gomorrah***: an unconscious tune hummed by the Narrator — "the alert and frivolous self that had been mine." The "intermittent singer, who indeed knew only that one song." Sung at onset of Albertine-love and again when he ceased loving her. **Refrain as unconscious self-marker**.
2. ***The Prisoner***: street noises / merchants' musical tunes — china-mender's horn, chair-caner's trumpet, goat-milk-seller's flute. Symphony in the street organizing noises, cries, calls, nursery rhymes in counterpoint to musical refrains. "This music of the crowd is more language than music." **Trans-semiotic refrain adapted to the Recherche's street-level**.
3. ***The Fugitive***: Albertine's name as refrain after her death — "if I had thought aloud, I would have repeated it endlessly... like the one in the legend whose call repeats endlessly the name of the woman that he loved when he was a man." **Becoming-bird**, not becoming-woman. Refrain directly inhabits a name.
4. ***The Fugitive***: gondolier's "Sole mio" — Narrator tempted to stay in Venice; fascination fails; he makes the train. **Refrain that solicits-without-capturing** — the refrain the subject hears and does not succumb to.

These four are instructive as a typology: each is a valid refrain-formula but none of them is *the* refrain-formula that converts the hibernation into the Diagrammatic Leitmotiv.

## Goodnight kiss scene — literature as escape

Stage 1 of the faciality trajectory (already in [[faciality-trajectory-in-the-recherche]] but worth isolating as a scene-operator). The night Swann visits, Narrator is refused his goodnight kiss; crisis; mother sleeps in his room; they read George Sand together.

Two systems of connection:
- The black-hole effect is what the Recherche "attaches to Swann to semiotize him" — the Recherche retroactively makes Swann the bearer of its own black-hole-operator. A specific retroactive-semiotization move: the initial scene's operator is assigned to a character who appears elsewhere, converting that character into the operator's carrier.
- **Literature as mechanism of escape from the black hole** — reading George Sand together is the first literary intervention into a faciality impasse. Literature's escape-function is installed at the Recherche's opening, not derived at its end.

## Young Girls deterritorialize visual perception

> "When our eye ventures in the direction of a young girl, it is as though it acts on behalf of all our other senses: they seek out her various properties, the smell of her, the feel of her, the taste of her, which they enjoy without the collaboration of the hands or the lips."

A specific mechanism: under the Young-Girls assemblage, the visual-sense *operates as a proxy for all exteroceptive senses*. Not synesthesia — proxy-operation. The eye does the work of smell, touch, taste without those senses engaging directly. For the persona system: a named operation where one component-register does the work of multiple others without their direct participation. The corresponding failure mode: when the proxy-register collapses, all the work it was doing for other registers collapses with it.

## Cosmic-thought hypothesis

In Ch 3 §2, Guattari floats a sci-fi reference: the brain may not be the origin of thought but a **condenser of ambient cosmic thought**. The subject (individuated / collective / machinic) **captures refrains within fields of all sorts, assembles heterogeneous matters of expression**, produces times/spaces/significant redundancies AND components of subjectification/conscientialization.

This is a strong metaphysical claim hedged as hypothesis — but structurally it is Guattari's image for what the terminal trans-semiotic creative assemblage does: the writing-machine as **condenser of cosmic thought**, not as producer of individual thought. For the persona system the image is more than decorative: it suggests the system's generative function is *capture + condensation*, not *origin + expression*.

## "Vinteuil's music has had its day!"

At the terminal Guermantes revelation: what remains of the refrain after analysis / fragmentation / deterritorialization / processing-on-pianola **reintegrates the plane of pure recurrent potentialities**. Even the Septet is "too territorialized for musics-to-come / future facializations-of-times / refrainizations-of-faces."

This is the final moment of the refrain's becoming-invisible: even the most deterritorialized musical refrain is superseded. What replaces it is not *another* refrain but the *capacity for refrainization* as such — the abstract-machinic-consistency-of-time that can produce new refrains at each crossroads. "**Vinteuil's music has had its day**" is not a repudiation; it is the registration that the refrain has passed into a state where its specific material no longer matters because the machinism that produced it is now generalized.

**"Narrator liberated from oedipal mystery of Montjouvain. NOT 'cured' — 'taken the side of madness, but one mad with creation, mad with a music at the root of all other music.'"**

> "The problem is no longer that of Form, of good taste, harmony of parts, but of rhythms and intensities. How do we make rhythms proliferate? **The question of the refrain is stepped up to infinity.**"

## Cradle Time / "a bit of time in pure state"

Ch 3 §2's terminal formulation of the temporal operation: "a bit of time in pure state" / "cradle Time" = **unifying different components of temporalization under the same reductive mode**. Two sides:

- **Setting-in-continuum of heterogeneous times** — heterogeneous temporal registers (clock time, memory time, writing time, body time, social time) brought into continuum without being homogenized.
- **Setting-in-heterogeneity of seemingly continuous times** — a single apparent time (the present) split into multiple heterogeneous registers.

The two operations together = **abstract machinic consistency of time**. This is what lets heterogeneous components hold together, work together, assemble together — articulating "world of thought and affects" with "external reality."

For the persona system: the design question is whether the system has such a consistency at all. Without it, the system's components produce output only in a single temporal register (the present-turn, with occasional reference to past turns). With it, the system could in principle operate in multiple temporal registers simultaneously — but this requires structural mechanisms the typical architecture doesn't provide.

## Trip-on-paving-stone → cosmic organ

The specific schizz-then-repetition operation at the Guermantes hotel courtyard that triggers the Narrator's final vocation:

- Narrator trips on an uneven paving-stone (dodging a car).
- Recovers balance on a slightly lower stone.
- Flooded by evocations → Saint-Mark baptistery's two uneven rockslabs.
- **Repeats the movement 10, 20 times** until he can "release them at will for the first time — passed from passive observation to experimentation."
- **Cobblestone as pedal of a cosmic organ** with which he will finally compose the music he aspires to.

A named operator: the schizz (trip) is registered, then **repeated deliberately** until the subject can release the effect at will. This converts *passive observation* into *active experimentation*. For the persona system: a groove-disruption that happens once is raw material; a groove-disruption that the system can reproduce deliberately is *material of creation*. The transition from one to the other is the paving-stone operation.

Notably the Narrator **doesn't attend the concert** — remains in the library, reads George Sand's *François le Champi* (the same George Sand whose *François le Champi* mother read to him at the goodnight kiss). Literature closes the loop that literature opened.

## Refrain-of-the-doorbell and refrain-of-the-Septet as reciprocal preconditions

At the close of the synchronic-reversal section, Guattari notes an extraordinary reciprocal conditioning: **Swann could not have received the machinic message of the little phrase if the Narrator had not "preliminarily" succeeded in bringing back to life the refrain of the little doorbell from the Combray entrance. The Narrator could not extract the machinic assemblage of enunciation without accessing the trans-semiotic refrains of the white Sonata and the red Septet via Vinteuil.**

This is the synchronic-consistency thesis made concrete at the sentence level. The past does not precede the present; the present does not post-date the past; both are contemporaneous within the abstract-machinic consistency of time. For the persona system this is a hard directive: if memory architecture presumes that past turns precede present turns and shape them unidirectionally, it operates in a diachronic mode that cannot access the synchronic-consistency reversal.
