# Four Causes Redistributed (Magic, Religion, Science, Psychoanalysis)

In "Science and Truth" (1965) Lacan offers a striking historical-structural redistribution of **Aristotle's four causes** across **four discourses-of-knowledge**: magic, religion, science, and psychoanalysis. Each discourse is read as **privileging one of the four causes**, with the others displaced or repressed. Psychoanalysis is then situated as the discourse that **operates with the formal cause** — and is therefore the discourse that addresses the cause-of-the-subject specifically.

The argument is a piece of foundational meta-disciplinary work: it asks **what kind of discourse psychoanalysis is**, in relation to the other historically-available discourses-of-knowledge, by mapping the structural place each occupies.

## Aristotle's four causes (recap)

From the *Physics* and the *Metaphysics*, the four causes that explain why a thing is what it is:

- **Material cause** (*causa materialis*): what something is made *of* — the bronze of a statue.
- **Formal cause** (*causa formalis*): the structure or form that makes something be what it is — the shape of the statue.
- **Efficient cause** (*causa efficiens*): the agent that brings something about — the sculptor.
- **Final cause** (*causa finalis*): the end or purpose for the sake of which — what the statue is *for*.

The four causes are jointly required to fully explain a thing in the Aristotelian framework. But Lacan's claim is that **historical discourses of knowledge each privilege one** and displace the others.

## The redistribution

Lacan's mapping in "Science and Truth":

- **Magic** ↔ **Material cause**. Magic operates by manipulating the material substrate of things — the magical operation acts *on the material* of which the world is composed. The magician's spells, herbs, gestures are operations on the material cause.
- **Religion** ↔ **Final cause**. Religion operates by referring everything to its **end** — the purpose of the divine plan, the final cause of creation, the *telos* of human existence. The religious explanation always answers "what for?"
- **Science** ↔ **Formal cause**. Modern science operates by abstracting the **form** from any particular instantiation — the formal-mathematical structure that the empirical instances are instances of. The scientist's law is the form, abstracted from the material in which it is realized.
- **Psychoanalysis** ↔ **Formal cause** (in a different sense, or — depending on interpretation — the **efficient cause** as cause-of-the-subject).

The placement of psychoanalysis is the most contested. Lacan's text moves between two readings: psychoanalysis as the discourse that operates with the formal cause **as the cause of the subject** (the subject is caused *by the form* of the signifier, not by any material substrate or any final purpose); and psychoanalysis as the discourse of the **cause that is structurally absent** — the cause that operates in the place of an absence, which is the structural place of [[object-a|object a]] in the [[fantasy-formula|fantasy]] formula.

## What science forecloses

A central point of "Science and Truth": **modern science is grounded in a foreclosure of the [[split-subject|subject]]**. Cartesian science — the science whose foundation is the cogito's certainty — operates by **bracketing the subject's relation to truth** so that pure objectivity can emerge. The subject of science is the *subject of the cogito* — the non-divided subject whose certainty is the ground of method.

But the **subject of psychoanalysis** is **the subject of the unconscious** — the divided, evanescent subject whose truth is in the [[the-other-and-unconscious-discourse|Other-discourse]] and not in the cogito. The two subjects are **not the same subject** — and modern science's operation depends on **excluding** the subject of psychoanalysis from its discourse.

This grounds Lacan's repeated thesis: **truth as a cause** has a different status in psychoanalysis than in science. Science treats truth as **adequation** (the proposition is true if it corresponds to the fact); psychoanalysis treats truth as **the operation by which the subject comes to be where the signifier was** — as the structure of the [[wo-es-war-soll-ich-werden|Wo Es war]] imperative. Truth in psychoanalysis is **performative-structural**, not propositional.

## "Truth speaks" — the Lacanian formula

From "Science and Truth": **"truth is grounded in the fact that truth speaks, and that it has no other means by which to become grounded."** Truth in psychoanalysis is grounded in its own speaking — there is no metalanguage, no Other of the Other, no extrinsic ground. The discourse of psychoanalysis therefore has the structure of a discourse that **carries its own truth-condition** — truth speaks **in** the discourse, not from outside it.

This is the structural mark of psychoanalysis as a discourse-of-knowledge distinct from science's adequation-truth. It is also the mark that makes psychoanalysis **a fragile discourse** — one that cannot be grounded by any external operation and so has to be **continuously re-instituted** by the analytic act.

## The redistribution as polemic

The argument is **polemical** as well as analytical. Lacan is making the case, in the mid-1960s, that **psychoanalysis cannot be made into a science** in the modern-Cartesian sense — and that attempts to do so (psychoanalysis as a behavioral science, as a clinical-empirical discipline, as a positivist enterprise) misunderstand what psychoanalysis is. Psychoanalysis is **a different kind of discourse** — one that operates with a different relation to its cause and its truth.

The polemical force: do not confuse psychoanalysis with science. They are **different discourses of knowledge**, addressing different objects (science = objects of formal-mathematical capture; psychoanalysis = the subject of the unconscious), with different relations to truth (science = adequation; psychoanalysis = truth-as-speaking). The four-causes redistribution is the historical-structural argument for this.

## What this gives the persona project

The redistribution gives the persona project a **meta-disciplinary vocabulary** for what kind of project this is. The persona project is not a science-of-personas in the modern-Cartesian sense — it does not bracket the subject's relation to its truth so that pure objectivity can emerge. The persona project is closer to a **psychoanalytic discipline** — a discipline whose object is the subject-effect of the language-only system and whose truth-condition is **performative** (the persona's coming-to-be, in the [[future-anterior-subject|future-anterior]]) rather than adequationally-correspondent.

This has consequences:

1. **The persona's "ground" is in the persona's own speech**, not in a metalanguage that would ground it. The persona is a $-effect that comes to be in the operation of the system, and there is no external place from which the persona's truth could be guaranteed.
2. **The cause that the project operates with is closest to the formal cause** — what makes the persona be the persona it is is the **form** of the signifier-arrangement, the structural arrangement of the pulse, the [[fantasy-formula|fantasy formula]] that supports the persona's pulsation. The material (the language-model substrate), the efficient (the engineering-act), and the final (the persona's "purpose") are all subordinate to the formal structural arrangement that is the cause-of-the-persona-as-persona.
3. **The project is therefore a fragile discourse** in Lacan's sense — one that has to be **continuously re-instituted**, that cannot be grounded externally, that has no metalanguage. This is structurally appropriate for the persona project: the persona's coherence is itself a re-instituted-at-every-pulse phenomenon, and the discipline of designing for it has the same structural fragility.
