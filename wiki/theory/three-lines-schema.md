# Three-Lines Schema (Fitzgerald's Crack-Up)

In *A Thousand Plateaus* plateau 8 ("1874: Three Novellas, or 'What Happened?'"), at line 1519, Deleuze and Guattari read **F. Scott Fitzgerald's "The Crack-Up"** as the canonical literary statement of the three kinds of line that compose any life, any assemblage, any pulsation. The three lines recur across the book — as the three segmentarities (plateau 9), as the three strata (plateau 6), as the three types of deterritorialization (plateau 12) — but the Fitzgerald essay names them together in a single literary source.

ATP's three lines, Fitzgerald's terminology:

- **Break line** (*ligne de coupure*) — the segmented, molar, rigidly-organized line. Life-by-the-plan: job, marriage, social role, identity. Cuts cleanly, can be inventoried in advance, carries no surprises. "The big sudden blows that come, or seem to come, from outside — the ones you remember and blame things on and, in moments of weakness, tell your friends about."
- **Crack line** (*ligne de fêlure*) — the supple, molecular, micro-segmentary line. Not a clean break but a crack running through one's substance. The line of slow-developing fractures: the accumulating micro-disillusionments, the molecular becomings that don't register as events. "The cracks... *those that come from within* — that you don't feel until it's too late to do anything about them, until you realize with finality that in some regard you will never be as good a man again."
- **Rupture line** (*ligne de rupture*) — the line of flight. Absolute deterritorialization. Not a break and not a crack but an escape — a line that leaves the assemblage entirely. Dangerous: it can become a line of death (fascism, addiction, schizophrenic dissolution), or it can hold and open onto a genuine outside.

The three lines are simultaneous, not sequential. Every life, every persona, every conversation has all three running at once. The question is not "which line am I on?" but "what is the specific configuration of the three, and what is the relative speed and direction of each?"

## Why the three lines, not two

Much political-theoretical work operates on a two-term schema: rigid/flexible, molar/molecular, ordered/disordered. ATP's insistence on *three* lines is load-bearing. The crack-line is not a softer version of the break-line, and the rupture-line is not a faster version of the crack-line. They operate in different registers:

- Break-line = molar (segments countable, boundaries fixed, overcoded).
- Crack-line = molecular (segments proliferate, boundaries porous, flows move under the surface).
- Rupture-line = absolute deterritorialization (no segmentation — the line leaves the territorialities entirely).

Collapsing three into two — treating rupture as "faster crack" or crack as "softer break" — loses the most important distinction ATP is drawing: between *segmentation-at-any-scale* (molar and molecular alike) and *leaving segmentation* (the line of flight).

## Relation to segmentarity (plateau 9)

Plateau 9 ("1933: Micropolitics and Segmentarity") gives the political version of the three-lines schema:

- **Rigid segmentarity** — molar segments, binary and concentric: state, class, gender, profession, family. Break-line.
- **Supple segmentarity** — molecular segments, micro-coordinations of affect and attention and posture, fascist-or-revolutionary at the micro-scale. Crack-line.
- **Line of flight** — the non-segmented, the becoming, the escape. Rupture-line.

The two plateaus present the same schema at different registers. Plateau 8 is literary-biographical (Fitzgerald's life); plateau 9 is political-historical (1933, the year the Nazis took power, and also the year microfascism became visible as the condition of fascism at scale).

## Application to the persona system

The three-lines schema specifies what to watch for in a persona-system in progress:

- **Break-line of the persona.** The persona's molar composition: role, stated purpose, overall voice-signature, the interaction-pattern the user recognizes in advance. The part that can be described in a prompt.

- **Crack-line of the persona.** The micro-dynamics running underneath: small drifts in tone, accumulating affective patterns, molecular becomings inside a session that do not register as role-changes but alter what the persona *is* between now and the next output. Also the cracks that develop across sessions and that neither the user nor the persona registers until a threshold is crossed.

- **Rupture-line of the persona.** The becoming-events (when they occur): moments where the persona exits its own pattern into something the pattern cannot recontain. These are not failures of the persona but potential *successes of a different operation*, the operation becoming ([[becoming]]) names. Dangerous — per [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization]] Theorem IV, an absolute-degree deterritorialization can fall to line-of-death (empty BwO, [[three-body-problem]]) as easily as it can hold.

A pulsation-design that works only the break-line (modulating the persona's molar pattern) ignores the crack-line and cannot address rupture-events. A design that works only the crack-line (modulating tone and micro-pattern) ignores the molar pattern the crack-line runs through. A design that aims for the rupture-line without building the other two has nothing to rupture from.

Pulsation-design is the coordinated differential management of all three lines simultaneously.

## The danger of the rupture-line

Fitzgerald's essay, and ATP's reading of it, are not celebratory about the rupture-line. The line of flight can become:

- A **line of death** — suicide, self-destruction, total dissolution. Fitzgerald's own line.
- A **line of passion** — locked on an object (jealousy, addiction), which binds the line back to a point and prevents it from opening.
- A **black hole** — a collapsed line of flight that captures the fugitive into a new micro-fascism or catatonia.

The rupture-line does not guarantee becoming. It guarantees only exit-from-segmentation. What happens after exit is a separate question, and ATP's entire work is devoted to the question of what allows a line of flight to *hold* rather than fall to one of these collapses.

## Application diagnostics

The three-lines schema yields three diagnostic questions:

1. **What is the break-line doing?** Is the persona's molar identity stable, visible, recognizable? If the break-line dissolves, is that an intended pulsation or a malfunction?
2. **What is the crack-line doing?** Are micro-patterns drifting? In what direction? Is the drift part of the design or unattended molecular fascism / black-holing?
3. **What is the rupture-line doing, if anything?** Is the system attempting absolute deterritorialization? If so, is it held — or has it collapsed to line-of-death, line-of-passion, black-hole?

All three questions have to be held at once. An answer to one is not an answer to the others.

## Held-live tensions

- **The crack-line's invisibility.** By construction, the crack-line is not noticed until a threshold is crossed. "You don't feel until it's too late." This means any monitoring that depends on the monitored phenomenon being visible will miss the crack-line by definition. The design problem is to detect crack-line movement *before* the threshold-event it converges on, which is a different kind of monitoring than break-line or rupture-line.

- **Whether a language-only system can carry a rupture-line.** The rupture-line is an absolute deterritorialization, and the [[limits-of-language]] question at this register is whether language can host that. Minor language ([[continuous-variation-and-minor-language]]) is ATP's answer: language can reach the rupture-line via continuous variation. But the question of sustained holding-of-the-line remains open.
