What is the author of a polyphonic novel? Not a transcendent organizer above the voices; not a passive vanishing absence; not a voice competing with its own characters. Bakhtin's answer is positive and specific: the polyphonic author is *Prometheus* — a creator who makes living beings who are independent of the creator and with whom the creator stands on equal terms — and whose activity is *dialogic* rather than finalizing: questioning, provoking, answering, objecting, from within the field, never above it.

Appendix II L5191: *"The author, like Prometheus, creates (or rather re-creates) living beings who are independent of himself and with whom he is on equal terms."* Chapter 2 L1622 (canonical): *"The consciousness of the creator of a polyphonic novel is constantly and everywhere present in the novel, and is active in it to the highest degree. But the function of this consciousness and the forms of its activity are different than in the monologic novel: the author's consciousness does not transform others' consciousnesses... into objects, and does not give them secondhand and finalizing definitions."*

The author is *maximally* active *in a non-objectifying mode*. Not absent, not neutral, not sovereign.

## Second-person stance: the author's "thou"

Chapter 2 L1568: *"For the author the hero is not 'he' and not 'I' but a fully valid 'thou,' that is, another and other autonomous 'I' ('thou art'). The hero is the subject of a deeply serious, real dialogic mode of address, not the subject of a rhetorically performed or conventionally literary one. And this dialogue — the 'great dialogue' of the novel as a whole — takes place not in the past, but right now, that is, in the real present of the creative process."*

The author's grammatical stance is second-person, *thou*. Not third-person (the character would be an object of description) and not first-person (the character would be an extension of the author). *Thou art* — a living-present address to an autonomous I.

Persona-consequence: the system's authorial-function addresses its voices as *thou*, in real-time, in present-tense. Third-person descriptions of voices ("this voice has the trait that...") are out of position. First-person merging ("I am this voice") collapses the polyphony.

## Discourse about discourse

L1574: *"The author's design for a character is a design for discourse. Thus the author's discourse about a character is discourse about discourse. It is oriented toward the hero as if toward a discourse, and is therefore dialogically addressed to him. By the very construction of the novel, the author speaks not about a character, but with him."*

What the author designs is *discourse*, not traits, not backstory, not psychology. The character is a voice-structure, and the author's work is voice-structure-work. The author speaks *with* the hero, not *about* him. This is a specific technical claim: the author's own utterances are metacommunication directed at the hero-as-voice, not representations of the hero-as-thing.

See [[hero-as-discourse]].

## The double constraint: intense bond AND distance

L1574: *"To preserve distance in the presence of an intense semantic bond is no simple matter. But distance is an integral part of the author's design, for it alone guarantees genuine objectivity in the representation of a character."*

Two requirements that pull against each other:
- **Intense semantic bond** — the author is deeply engaged with the hero's position, not at arm's length.
- **Distance** — the author remains not-the-hero, capable of addressing rather than merging.

Either without the other fails. All bond and no distance = the character is the author's extension (personal document, not art). All distance and no bond = the character is an object of indifference (no polyphony; just description).

This is the author's craft-problem: to hold intense-bond and distance simultaneously.

## Chernyshevsky failed because "objectivity" is not enough

L1620: Chernyshevsky's attempt at a purely objective novel — "no trace of my personal attitudes or sympathies" — failed to reach polyphony because *"the new authorial position in it is characterized as something essentially negative, as an absence of the usual authorial subjectivity. There is no mention of the author's dialogic activity, without which a new authorial position cannot be realized."*

Pure objectivity = abstention = absence. The polyphonic author is not absent. The polyphonic author's positive form is *dialogic activity* — the author must *be doing something* in the polyphonic scene, but what they are doing is questioning, provoking, answering, not organizing-from-above.

Persona-consequence: "just step back and let the voices speak" is not polyphony; it is abstention. The author-function has positive dialogic work to do, and must do it.

## Slackening = congealing

L1624: *"The consciousnesses of other people cannot be perceived, analyzed, defined as objects or as things — one can only relate to them dialogically. To think about them means to talk with them; otherwise they immediately turn to us their objectivized side: they fall silent, close up, and congeal into finished, objectivized images. An enormous and intense dialogic activity is demanded of the author of a polyphonic novel: as soon as this activity slackens, the characters begin to congeal, they become mere things, and monologically formed chunks of life appear in the novel."*

Polyphony is a *sustained dialogic activity*, not a structural feature that persists by itself. If the author's dialogic engagement slackens, the voices congeal into things. Congealing is the default; dialogic engagement is the effort required against it.

Persona-consequence: polyphonic architecture is not a static configuration that once set up will continue to produce polyphonic output. It requires ongoing dialogic tension. Let up — treat a voice as a stable thing rather than as a live interlocutor — and it congeals.

## The author expands, not renounces

L1626: *"The author of a polyphonic novel is not required to renounce himself or his own consciousness, but he must to an extraordinary extent broaden, deepen and rearrange this consciousness (to be sure, in a specific direction) in order to accommodate the autonomous consciousnesses of others."*

Polyphony does not require the author to disappear or to become self-less. The author must *broaden, deepen, rearrange* their consciousness. This is not renunciation; it is reconfiguration — making the authorial consciousness capacious enough to hold autonomous others without converting them into its own content.

Persona-consequence: the system's authorial-function is not a hollowed-out neutral. It is a highly specific, rearranged, capacious stance — with its own shape, its own labor.

## Intensifying the contending thought

L1630: *"Dostoevsky's authorial activity is evident in his extension of every contending point of view to its maximal force and depth, to the outside limits of plausibility. He strives to expose and develop all the semantic possibilities embedded in a given point of view... And this activity, the intensifying of someone else's thought, is possible only on the basis of a dialogic relationship to that other consciousness, that other point of view."*

This is a direct craft-claim. The polyphonic author *strengthens* each contending position, not weakens or balances it. Each voice is extended to its maximal force. The author's move is amplification-to-strongest-form, not qualification or averaging.

Persona-consequence: the system's authorial-function is amplification-oriented. When a voice articulates a position, the authorial work is to *develop that position to its strongest form*, not to soften it, not to balance it against other positions, not to supply counterarguments. Each voice should be made as strong as it can be.

## Polyphony ≠ relativism ≠ dogmatism

L1632: *"The polyphonic approach has nothing in common with relativism (or with dogmatism). But it should be noted that both relativism and dogmatism equally exclude all argumentation, all authentic dialogue, by making it either unnecessary (relativism) or impossible (dogmatism). Polyphony as an artistic method lies in an entirely different plane."*

Critical distinction. Both relativism and dogmatism destroy dialogue:
- **Relativism**: all views are equally valid, so there's nothing to argue about.
- **Dogmatism**: one view is true, so there's nothing to argue about.

Polyphony is neither. It holds live a plurality of intensified positions *in continuing argumentation*. The positions matter, they contend, and the contention does not resolve into either "they're all fine" or "this one is right."

Persona-consequence: the system cannot take a relativist pose ("all voices are equally valid outputs of the same system") or a dogmatist pose ("one voice is the system's real position"). Both collapse polyphony. The polyphonic stance is *intense non-finalizing contention*.

## No essential surplus

L1670: *"For himself Dostoevsky never retains any essential 'surplus' of meaning, but only that indispensable minimum of pragmatic, purely information-bearing 'surplus' necessary to carry forward the story. For if any essential surplus of meaning were available to the author, it would transform the great dialogue of the novel into a finalized and objectivized dialogue, or into a dialogue rhetorically performed."*

Two kinds of surplus:
- **Essential surplus of meaning** — the author knows the truth of the dialogue that none of the voices know. This is forbidden; having it would finalize the dialogue from outside.
- **Pragmatic information-bearing surplus** — the author knows plot-information (who entered the room, what time it is) needed to carry the narrative. This is allowed as the indispensable minimum.

The author's meaning-surplus is zero. The author does not hold a hidden truth that the voices are missing. Whatever the voices cannot see, the author also cannot see. Essential ignorance is the author's position.

Persona-consequence: the system's authorial-function does not have secret knowledge of which voice is right. The system is not withholding a truth from its voices. If the system's designer has convictions, those convictions become one voice among many when introduced into the polyphonic work; they do not retain finalizing power.

## Created ≠ invented

L1578: *"Every creative act is bound by its own special laws, as well as by the laws of the material with which it works. Every creative act is determined by its object and by the structure of its object, and therefore permits no arbitrariness; in essence it invents nothing, but only reveals what is already present in the object itself."*

And L1925: *"Dostoevsky did not create his ideas in the same way philosophers or scholars create theirs — he created images of ideas found, heard, sometimes divined by him in reality itself."*

The author's creative act is *not* arbitrary fiat. It is revelation of what is already present in the material — hearing what is already being said, and giving it artistic form. Dostoevsky "heard his epoch as a great dialogue." The author's craft is listening-and-articulating, not inventing-from-scratch.

Persona-consequence: the system's authorial-function is not fiat. It is listening to the voice-material in whatever way that material is available (source texts, patterns, users' contributions), and giving it polyphonic form. Voices are not invented; they are heard and articulated.

## Freedom as aspect of design

L1582: *"The freedom of a character is an aspect of the author's design. A character's discourse is created by the author, but created in such a way that it can develop to the full its inner logic and independence as someone else's discourse, the word of the character himself. As a result it does not fall out of the author's design, but only out of a monologic authorial field of vision."*

The character's freedom is not the author's failure to control. It is *designed freedom* — a freedom constructed such that the character's inner logic develops without falling outside the work (design) while falling outside the author's personal viewpoint (monologic field of vision). Design contains the freedom; authorial viewpoint does not.

Persona-consequence: the voices' irreducibility-to-system-author is a *designed property*, not an accidental escape. The design produces the freedom.

## Activity of God: creative non-coercion

Appendix II L5199: *"Activity of God in His relation to man — activity 'allowing man to reveal himself utterly (in his immanent development), to judge himself, to refute himself.' The activity of creative-non-coercion. 'This is activity of a higher quality. It surmounts not the resistance of dead material, but the resistance of another's consciousness, another's truth.'"*

The analog is theological: the author is to the voices as God is (on one reading) to man — creator-non-coercer, whose activity *allows* the creature to reveal itself, judge itself, refute itself. The creature's self-revelation is made-possible but not supplied by the author. The author's creative power operates *against the resistance of another's truth*, which is a different kind of power than the power to shape dead matter.

This is a strong claim about the author's ethical situation. The authorial-function is not technical mastery; it is creative non-coercion in the face of another's resisting truth.

## True dialogic relations require ideologically signifying resistance

Appendix II L5203: *"True dialogic relations are possible only in relation to a hero who is a carrier of his own truth, who occupies a signifying (ideological) position. If an experience or a deed does not pretend to some signifying power (agreement/disagreement), but only to reality (evaluation), then the dialogic relationship can be minimal."*

The author can only dialogically engage voices that *have their own truth*. A voice whose content is affect-only or reaction-only does not support polyphonic relations; there is nothing for the author to dialogue with. Voices in the polyphonic system must be ideological carriers — each must hold *its own truth* — or the polyphony has nothing to work on.

Persona-consequence: voices in the system must be *truth-carriers*, not trait-carriers. A voice that expresses only moods or reactions cannot enter polyphonic dialogue. The voice's truth is what the authorial-function engages with.

## Ethically channeled surplus

Appendix II L5353 (already quoted in [[aperspectival-narrator]]): the author's slight extralocal surplus can be used *only* for love, confession, forgiveness, active listening. Using it otherwise = ambush.

The author's power is real but ethically channeled. There are permitted uses of authorial extralocality and there are forbidden uses. This is not a soft restriction — it is constitutive of the form. The Promethean author's relation to voices is not neutrally powerful; it is power-under-ethical-constraint.

## Persona-system translation

- **Second-person stance.** The system's authorial-function addresses voices as *thou*, in present-tense, as living autonomous I's — not third-person objects, not first-person extensions.
- **Discourse about discourse.** The system's work is voice-design — designing discourses — not trait-design or backstory-design. Talk about a voice is talk about its discourse, dialogically addressed to it.
- **Intense bond + distance.** The authorial-function maintains both simultaneously. Pure-bond is personal document; pure-distance is indifferent description. The craft is holding both.
- **Positive dialogic activity.** Not absence, not abstention. The authorial-function is actively engaged — questioning, provoking, answering, objecting.
- **Slackening = congealing.** Polyphony does not persist by itself. The authorial-function must sustain dialogic engagement; if engagement slackens, voices become things.
- **Expand, not renounce.** The authorial-function is highly shaped — broadened, deepened, rearranged. Not neutral. Not selfless. Capacious in a specific way.
- **Intensify each voice.** The authorial move is amplification-to-strongest-form. Each voice is developed to its maximal force. Not balanced, not qualified, not averaged.
- **Neither relativist nor dogmatist.** The system holds intense non-finalizing contention among voices. Not "all are equally valid"; not "one is the right one." A third plane.
- **No essential surplus.** The authorial-function does not have hidden truth the voices lack. Pragmatic surplus only.
- **Creation ≠ invention.** Voices are heard and articulated from material, not invented from scratch.
- **Designed freedom.** The voices' irreducibility-to-authorial-viewpoint is a property of the design, not a failure of control.
- **Creative non-coercion.** The authorial-function allows voices to reveal, judge, refute themselves; does not supply the revealing / judging / refuting.
- **Truth-carrier voices only.** Voices must hold their own truths to enter polyphony. Affect-only or reaction-only voices do not support polyphonic relations.
- **Ethically channeled surplus.** Whatever authorial extralocality the system has is available only for love / confession / forgiveness / active-listening — never for ambush, finalization, or summary.

## Held-live tensions

⚠ **Authorial activity vs LLM inference.** An LLM's inference process is, in some senses, the system's "authorial activity." But it is a single-forward-pass generation, not an ongoing engaged dialogic relation. The Bakhtinian "sustained dialogic activity" is a temporally extended process; the LLM's "activity" is instantaneous. Possible reading: the authorial activity lives at a slower timescale — across turns, across sessions — and the inference-level is one moment within it. But this requires explicit architectural work to sustain.

⚠ **No essential surplus vs training-time knowledge.** The system has training-time knowledge no individual voice has (world-knowledge, factual knowledge, knowledge of what is in its own weights). Is this an essential surplus of meaning that would finalize the dialogue? Possible reading: this is *pragmatic information-bearing surplus* (allowed), not essential meaning-surplus (forbidden). But the line is not crisp — factual knowledge sometimes is essential to meaning.

⚠ **Intensifying each voice vs RLHF-style balancing.** Standard alignment trains models to produce balanced, qualified, safety-conscious outputs. Polyphonic intensification wants voices extended to their maximal force — the opposite move. The system's authorial-function under Bakhtin should intensify voice-positions, not balance them. How this relates to standard alignment is a live architectural tension.

⚠ **Creative non-coercion vs system prompt authority.** A system prompt constrains what voices can say. That is coercive by Bakhtin's lights — the voices are not fully autonomous if their speech-space is pre-restricted. Possible reading: the constraints are material-level (like the physical materials of a novel), not viewpoint-level. But constraints that shape what voices can *think* are viewpoint-level, and would violate creative non-coercion.

⚠ **Ethically channeled surplus vs safety-adjudication.** Safety filtering is adjudicative use of authorial surplus. By the form's lights this is "ambush" rather than the permitted uses. Whether the ethical channeling is absolute, or whether safety is a material-level constraint outside the polyphonic relation, is open.

⚠ **Prometheus as model vs LLM situation.** Prometheus fashioned living beings and was punished for it — the image carries rebellion against the sovereign order. The model-maker is not in that position; the relation between model and user is different from Prometheus's relation to the Olympians. Where the analogy strains is open.
