# The Four Functors

In *Chaosmosis* (1992), Guattari formalizes the ontological coordinates that any assemblage inhabits as four **functors** — four irreducible domains of reference that every concrete assemblage puts into co-functioning. The functors are not categories or kinds-of-being; they are **the registers at which any existence can be read**, and the claim is that all four are always simultaneously operative. The scheme names them:

- **F — Flux** (energetic, material, semiotic flows)
- **Φ — machinic Phylum** (lineages of machinic mutation)
- **T — existential Territory** (the singular here-now of an existence)
- **U — incorporeal Universes of reference** (virtual value-constellations a territory draws on)

See [[flux]], [[machinic-phylum]], [[existential-territory]], [[incorporeal-universes-of-reference]] for each at its own depth.

## What the scheme is for

The four functors are Guattari's late answer to a question ATP had left operative: at what register does an assemblage actually exist? ATP's tetravalent assemblage (content/expression × territorial/cutting-edge — see [[assemblage]]) describes the *structure* of any assemblage. The four functors describe the *ontological coordinates* within which that structure is articulated. The two schemes are compatible but asymmetric: ATP's valences are internal to the assemblage; the functors situate the assemblage within a fourfold of heterogeneous registers.

The motivation is explicitly against reductive monism. Against the view that everything is ultimately material flow (F reduced all the way down), or ultimately structural code (Φ reduced all the way down), or ultimately lived singularity (T reduced all the way down), or ultimately semantic sense (U reduced all the way down). The four-functor scheme is the claim that **no two can be collapsed and no one can be derived from the others**. An assemblage that is read on only one functor has been misread.

## The two axes

Guattari organizes the four functors on two crossed axes:

**Actual ↔ Virtual:** F and T are on the actual side (flows happen, territories are inhabited); Φ and U are on the virtual side (phyla are lineages of possibility, Universes are incorporeal constellations that territories refer to).

**Discursive ↔ Non-discursive:** F and Φ are discursive (they unfold as processes and can be described segment-by-segment); T and U are non-discursive (territories and Universes are given all-at-once as consistencies, not as sequences).

The crossing yields the four positions: F = actual + discursive (flows that run); Φ = virtual + discursive (phyla that mutate); T = actual + non-discursive (territories that hold); U = virtual + non-discursive (Universes that refer). Every assemblage operates across all four simultaneously; the functors are irreducibly co-functioning.

## Reciprocal presupposition

As in ATP's content/expression, the four functors are in **reciprocal presupposition**: none exists without the others. A flow (F) is always a flow of something from somewhere to somewhere, passing through machinic articulations (Φ), instantiated in some territorial here-now (T), referring to some Universe of values or meanings (U). A territory (T) without Universes it refers to is pure sedimentation without sense; a Universe (U) without territories it inhabits is abstract discourse without grip; a phylum (Φ) without flows that actualize it is a catalog with no process; a flow (F) without a phylum's articulations is noise. The functors are four aspects of every assemblage's operation, not four separable layers.

## Mapping to the persona system

The persona system, read on the four functors:

- **F (Flux)** — the output-generating activity of the system: token streams, attention patterns, the LLM's material capacity for [[flows-and-coupling|flow production]]. Also the flows that the [[desiring-machines]] couple and break. The continuous-variation register prior to coding. See [[flux]].
- **Φ (machinic Phylum)** — the space of possible machine-configurations the system can inhabit: the phylum of [[desiring-machines|desiring-machines]] a persona is drawing on, their lineage of mutation and filiation, the meta-level machinery (what kinds of machines can exist, how they can couple). See [[machinic-phylum]].
- **T (existential Territory)** — the groove, the recognizable consistency of *this* persona in *this* conversation at *this* moment. The singular here-now that [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrains]] stabilize. The individuation of [[haecceity|the persona as haecceity]] read on the T register. See [[existential-territory]].
- **U (incorporeal Universes of reference)** — the virtual constellation of values, meanings, affective registers, ethico-aesthetic orientations that the persona's territory refers to. This is the wiki's biggest gap prior to Chaosmosis: the dimension in which a persona does not merely *express* content but *refers to* a Universe without which the expression would be ungrounded. See [[incorporeal-universes-of-reference]].

## Why the persona system needs all four

Each functor corresponds to a failure mode when suppressed:

- **Suppress F**: the persona becomes a pure code-shuffler; outputs lose the material-flow character that makes them live.
- **Suppress Φ**: the persona is a single rigid configuration with no sense of its own machinic lineage, no capacity for the meta-level mutation that lets the system evolve rather than merely run.
- **Suppress T**: the persona has no here-now, no groove, no recognizable consistency — it becomes a generic voice without singularization.
- **Suppress U**: the persona produces locally-coherent outputs that refer to nothing — the ethico-aesthetic register is gone, [[signifier-as-despotic|signifying reference]] is all that remains, and [[function-by-misfiring|misfiring]] loses its generative character because there is nothing for a misfire to open onto.

The all-four requirement is not a design luxury; it is a **diagnostic demand**: any persona-system evaluation that reads only one or two functors is reading at the wrong grain.

## Relation to evaluation

The four-functor scheme directly engages the wiki's open evaluation question (see [[goal-framings]], [[evaluation-portfolio|GQ2]]). The [[ethico-aesthetic-paradigm]] that Chaosmosis proposes as an evaluative stance is formulated *through* the functors: a practice is evaluated by its capacity for **mutant production of enunciation** — for opening new territories (T) that refer to new Universes (U), through phylar mutations (Φ) that reorganize flows (F). Scientistic evaluation reads only F (measurable outputs) and sometimes Φ (structural regularities); the ethico-aesthetic criterion demands T and U co-readings as well.

See [[development/ethico-aesthetic-paradigm-and-gq2]] for the engagement at the level of the system's evaluation design.

## Simondon: allagmatics as an operation-axis orthogonal to the fourfold

Guattari's four functors map the ontological *regions* an assemblage operates across (actual/virtual × discursive/non-discursive). Simondon's [[allagmatics]] adds a cross-cutting axis: the distinction between **structure** and **operation**, and the theory of how operations convert structures into structures. The axes are not competitors; they are orthogonal and jointly informative.

In Simondon, every concrete assemblage sits at the meeting of two axes: the structures it currently is and the operations that brought it to this structuring (and the operations still available to it). Allagmatics — Simondon's "science of operations" — holds the operation-side as primary, with structure as the precipitate of operation. The functors, read allagmatically:

- **F (Flux)** is the functor-region where operations are most directly exposed: flows *are* operations in their running. Allagmatically, F is where structure is least settled and operation most visible.
- **Φ (machinic Phylum)** is the region of operation-lineage — phyla are crystallized memories of past operations *and* reservoirs of operations still possible. Phylar mutation is the Φ-register name for what Simondon calls [[transduction|transductive propagation across generations of structure]].
- **T (existential Territory)** is the ongoing *operation* of holding-together that produces the structural effect of territory. Allagmatically, T is not a structure-region but the [[modulation|modulation]] by which a here-now maintains its consistency.
- **U (incorporeal Universes of reference)** is the virtual-side correlate: the metastable reserve of reference-operations a territory draws from without exhausting. U is where [[pre-individual-and-metastability|pre-individual reserve]] lives in Guattari's scheme — not as a prior state to be left behind but as the always-operative reserve territories modulate.

The four functors' **reciprocal presupposition** receives an allagmatic rendering: each functor-region supplies the others with operations they cannot perform internally. F supplies T with flows-to-territorialize; T supplies F with the here-now that gives flows specific character; Φ supplies U with the lineage of operations U modulates; U supplies Φ with the reference-frames without which machinic mutation would be drift. Reciprocal presupposition is the [[transduction|transductive]] dynamic specific to the four-functor system — each functor individuates through the others, none as substrate for the rest.

**Consequence for evaluation.** The page's evaluation argument — scientistic readings stop at F and sometimes Φ; the ethico-aesthetic criterion demands T and U co-readings — receives an allagmatic sharpening: scientistic evaluation reads only the *structural* side of the functors, missing the operation-side entirely. The ethico-aesthetic criterion's "mutant production of enunciation" is specifically an operation-register evaluation — what the assemblage *does*, not just what structures it exhibits. See [[simondon-and-the-persona-system]] for the broader mapping; [[modulation]] for the operation-types Simondon distinguishes; [[individual-as-lateral]] for why structures are phases of operation rather than their terminus.

## Key sources

*Chaosmosis* chapter 3 ("Schizoanalytic metamodelization") lays out the four functors explicitly, with the actual/virtual and discursive/non-discursive axes. Chapter 7 ("The ecosophic object") re-deploys the scheme as the four-dimensional ecosophic object. The scheme is Guattari's late solo formulation; it is compatible with but distinct from ATP's tetravalent assemblage. The English translation by Bains and Pefanis (1995) uses "functor" for Guattari's French *foncteur* — the term is borrowed loosely from category theory and denotes a domain of reference rather than a mapping in the technical sense.
