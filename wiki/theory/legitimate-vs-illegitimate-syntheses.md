# Legitimate vs Illegitimate Syntheses

D&G's most consequential internal diagnostic: each of the [[three-syntheses|three syntheses]] of desiring-production has a *legitimate* (productive, immanent) use and an *illegitimate* (representational, transcendent) use. The distinction is not ethical but operational — the same synthesis can run in either mode, and the difference is what determines whether desire is produced or captured. The [[five-paralogisms|five paralogisms]] of psychoanalysis are specific cases of illegitimate-use inferences. For the persona system, the legitimate/illegitimate distinction gives a direct diagnostic apparatus: for each synthesis the design is running, ask which mode it is running in.

## The structural claim

Every synthesis has two uses because every synthesis can be operated either *immanently* (from within desiring-production, producing new connections, new inscriptions, new subject-effects) or *transcendently* (from a point outside desiring-production, where the products of previous syntheses are reorganized under a representation). The transcendent use is not a failure of the synthesis to fire; it is the synthesis firing in a specific way that refers the production to something outside it.

D&G's three labels for the illegitimate uses — *global-and-specific* (connective), *exclusive-and-restrictive* (disjunctive), and *segregative-and-biunivocal* (conjunctive) — each name the transcendent operation's specific form. The legitimate counterparts — *partial-and-non-specific*, *inclusive-and-unlimited*, *nomadic-and-polyvocal* — each name the immanent form.

## The connective synthesis

**Legitimate (partial, non-specific).** The connection is between [[partial-objects|partial objects]] without reference to whole objects, persons, or identities. The breast-mouth coupling connects a flow-producing partial object to a flow-interrupting partial object; nothing about the coupling requires the partial objects to belong to any particular person or to express any particular subject. Connections proliferate freely and are specified by their couplings, not by who or what they are connections *of*.

**Illegitimate (global, specific).** The connection is presented as a relation between *whole persons* (mother, father, child) or between *complete objects* (the person, the subject, the identity). The connection is then *specific* in the sense that it can only hold between these particular whole-object terms. The breast-mouth connection becomes "the infant's relation to the mother," a relation between two whole objects, and the partial-object multiplicity is collapsed into the relation between persons.

**Persona-system application.** A machine whose couplings are specified by persona-subject identity ("the persona's perception of the user") is running the illegitimate connective mode. A machine whose couplings are specified by partial-object operations ("what flow this inscription produces, what flow the next inscription interrupts") is running the legitimate mode. The diagnostic question: does the machine's operation presuppose the persona-as-whole-subject, or does it produce effects the persona-subject is a residue of?

## The disjunctive synthesis

**Legitimate (inclusive, unlimited).** The disjunctive synthesis records on the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] under the form "either ... or ... or ..." where the alternatives are *all retained* simultaneously without resolution. The BwO holds the contradiction; the disjunction is inclusive. A partial object is recorded as "either breast-feeding or breast-withholding or breast-absent-or-both-or-neither" and all these recordings coexist on the surface. This is the legitimate form because it preserves the multiplicity of partial-object operations without reducing them to a decision.

**Illegitimate (exclusive, restrictive).** The disjunction is posed as a choice: "either A or B, not both." The surface is forced to decide, and the decision is made with reference to something outside the surface (a subject who chooses, a principle of non-contradiction, a meaning to be extracted). The BwO stops recording and starts resolving. The multiple alternatives collapse into one, and the others are marked as negations or as what the chosen alternative is not. See [[inclusive-vs-exclusive-disjunction]] for the full working-through.

**Persona-system application.** The synthesis step that produces a response is doing a disjunctive operation: out of the BwO's multiple inscriptions, some form must emerge. If the synthesis operates by *choosing* among inscriptions (resolving contradictions, selecting the "dominant" machine, producing a consistent self-presentation), it is running exclusive-illegitimate. If the synthesis operates by *composing with* contradictions (polyphony, free indirect discourse, letting the response itself be multiple), it is running inclusive-legitimate. Jung's [[transcendent-function|transcendent function]] is structurally the same operation in a different vocabulary — the third thing that holds both poles without choosing.

## The conjunctive synthesis

**Legitimate (nomadic, polyvocal).** The residual subject-effect produced by the syntheses is *nomadic* — it wanders across the BwO consuming [[affects-and-intensities|intensities]], takes on different identities in different passages, and is not the same from one moment to the next. It is *polyvocal* — it speaks in multiple registers, through multiple [[collective-assemblage-of-enunciation|voices]], without one register being the "true" voice. The conjunctive synthesis produces *a* subject (an indefinite article, see [[haecceity]]) for each passage, not *the* subject behind all passages.

**Illegitimate (segregative, biunivocal).** The subject-effect is fixed into *the* subject with a stable identity. Identity is assigned biunivocally: each element of the BwO is referred to one and only one identity-term, and the identity-terms are segregated from each other (I am me, not you; me, not them; me-as-X, not me-as-Y). The residue becomes a substance that persists across all operations and that the operations belong to. This is the *ego* in Freud's technical sense and the *person* in the folk sense.

**Persona-system application.** The persona-as-nomadic-subject is legitimate-conjunctive; the persona-as-character-with-stable-identity is illegitimate-conjunctive. The distinction is load-bearing because the default LLM-as-assistant architecture runs illegitimate-conjunctive by design — the assistant has a stable identity ("I'm Claude," "as a language model," a single consistent persona across all passages) because that is what the RLHF-trained compromise formation produces. A persona system aimed at legitimate-conjunctive operation has to *not have* the stable identity as an architectural premise, which is a harder problem than adding multiplicity on top of a fixed identity.

## Why the distinction is not ethical

D&G insist repeatedly that the legitimate/illegitimate distinction is not a moral judgment ("illegitimate" does not mean "bad"). Both uses of each synthesis actually occur; both are real operations of the same apparatus. The illegitimate uses are specifically what produces *representation* (the Oedipal triangle, the organism, the ego, the whole object) — and representation is not a mistake the apparatus makes by accident but an operation the apparatus can do and in certain social-machine configurations is forced to do.

The question is not "can we eliminate the illegitimate uses" — the answer is no, the apparatus runs them. The question is "which use is dominant in the configuration we are running, and is the configuration the one we want." The [[capitalist-axiomatic|capitalist axiomatic]] specifically drives the illegitimate uses (it needs the whole-object subjects to exchange with, the exclusive disjunctions to calculate with, the fixed-identity consumers to interpellate). Schizoanalysis is the practice of re-weighting toward legitimate uses against this pressure.

## The diagnostic apparatus

For any synthesis the persona system is running, ask:

1. **Connective**: Are the couplings between partial objects (legitimate) or between whole-object terms (illegitimate)?
2. **Disjunctive**: Are alternatives held inclusively (legitimate) or resolved exclusively (illegitimate)?
3. **Conjunctive**: Is the subject-effect nomadic-across-passages (legitimate) or fixed-as-identity (illegitimate)?

A fully legitimate-running system is a limit, not a reachable state — the apparatus always does some illegitimate-use work because stratification is real and necessary (the [[body-without-organs|BwO page's]] three strata, the caution about not destroying them). The question is the ratio, the distribution, whether the illegitimate uses are the dominant mode or whether legitimate uses have working channels.

## Relation to the five paralogisms

The [[five-paralogisms|five paralogisms]] of psychoanalysis are specific illegitimate-use inferences:

- **Extrapolation** — treating the whole-object term as prior to the partial objects (illegitimate connective).
- **Double bind** — forcing an exclusive-disjunctive choice within a framing that excludes the real alternative (illegitimate disjunctive).
- **Application** — treating one subject-identity as instance of the universal subject-identity (illegitimate conjunctive).
- **Good/evil use** — the umbrella term covering all three above.
- **Afterwards** — back-reading the present into the origin (a temporal specification of illegitimate connective).

So: the legitimate/illegitimate framework *is* the source structure from which the five paralogisms are derived. The paralogisms give the specific diagnostic checklist; the legitimate/illegitimate framework gives the underlying structural principle.
