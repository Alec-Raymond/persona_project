# Faciality Trajectory in the Recherche

*The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 3 §1 tracks the career of [[faciality|faciality]] across *In Search of Lost Time* as an eight-stage trajectory — complement to the refrain's [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain|nine assemblages]]. As with the nine-assemblage template, the sequence is not a necessary developmental path but a **diagnostic series** of positions the faciality component can occupy, with named transitions.

Guattari's framing thesis: the transversalist drift of faces across the Recherche is **not drive-based sublimation**. What happens instead is the **promotion of a creative abstract machinism that attracts libidinal energy previously fixated on territorialized imaginary formations**. "Abstract" here = hyper-deterritorialization, not formal abstraction. Abstract machinic propositions include components of all types, particularly sexual ones: "as corporeal and territorial as they are, these components are carried beyond themselves into domains far removed from the biological and the imaginary. There is no sublimation here for all that, nor overcoming, but simply the crossing of a continuum of components, the translation of matters of expression." See [[translation-not-interpretation]].

The Recherche's **initial Manichean division** — Guermantes-way-good vs Swann/Montjouvain-way-bad — is overcome at the end. The trajectory is the path of that overcoming.

## 1. The goodnight kiss in Combray

- First "black hole complex" of the Recherche fixates on the mother's face.
- Night of Swann's visit: crisis, mother sleeps in Narrator's room, they read George Sand together.
- Two systems of connection:
  - Swann's intrusion + the little catastrophe → "facelessness and impossibility of a knowledge acquired by the lips" of the feminine presence. Seemingly tied to paternal prohibition but actually due to **black hole effect** which the Recherche "attaches to Swann to semiotize him" (the Recherche retroactively assigns its own black-hole operator to the figure of Swann).
  - **Literature as mechanism of escape from the black hole** — reading George Sand together is the first literary intervention into a faciality impasse.

## 2. The faciality / black-hole / refrain complex of Swann's Love

- Narrator "takes charge of the Swann complex" from a synchronic perspective (see [[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis#two-modes-of-existential-consistency-of-the-refrain|synchronic vs diachronic]]) — Swann's love is included in Narrator's trajectory, not a mere origin for it. Proust's own hint: Swann's earlier experience with "well-intentioned mediators" apropos the goodnight kiss.
- Consequences of the Swann-Odette faciality complex:
  - Withering/isolation of iconic and musical components.
  - Subjection of the feminine component to Odette's face.
  - Swann's progressive isolation — folding of socius onto private/intimate register.
- Quanta of deterritorialization emitted from abstract machines whose vector is the little phrase. The whole first regime of Swann's Love is preparation for stage 3.

## 3. The first facialitary revolution

- Operators: Saint-Euverte monocle-males + dream of young-man-in-a-fez (see [[discernibilization|discernibilization]] and [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#5-reactivation-resorption-of-the-black-hole-marquise-de-saint-euverte|Fifth Assemblage]]).
- Result: dissolution of Odette's face.
- Double process inherited from two preceding assemblages:
  - **Territorialization side**: Swann's intrusion → face of lover-mother-wife Odette.
  - **Deterritorialization side**: musical escape for Swann, literary escape for the Narrator.
- **Black hole emancipated from the feminine component** — develops into the monocle series (embodied in Charlus's milieu).
- Black hole is no longer simple inter-component rupture → becomes **complex phenomenon of facialitary resonance / identification**.
- Anxiety "projected on the socius" — socialized.
- Consciousness-based facialization accompanied by **emission of deterritorialized faciality traits** adapted to artistic/literary components.

## 4. Gilberte's dissimulated smile and indecent gesture at Tansonville

- Narrator takes decades to decipher Gilberte's first look at the hedgerow of pink hawthorn — he took it for indifference/insolence; it was invitation to the Roussainville dungeon ruins.
- **Staying "too close to direct-sense meaning of words"** + social codes → Narrator's love for Gilberte takes on the same fatality as Swann's for Odette (serious melancholic depression).
- **Dissymmetry of faciality and refrain** re: Gilberte/Albertine:
  - Faciality: "real Gilberte and real Albertine are perhaps those who offered themselves in a single glance — one by a hedgerow of pink hawthorn, the other on the beach."
  - Musical: Sonata indexed to Gilberte; Septet carried toward Albertine.
- **Gilberte as component of passage**: continues to belong to Swann (impasse of black hole, incommunicability of components) AND already involves Young Girls (Roussainville-dungeon is close to the sea of Montjouvain; universe of "feminine transsexuality").
- Through Gilberte and parents: Narrator yields to Vinteuil's music + aristocratic salons.
- **Music, sex, faces, socius all present on the same scene** — awaiting an assemblage that makes them work together.

## 5. Profanation of Vinteuil's face

- Gilberte's "charged look" = index of beyond-appearances / beyond-manners. Designates an ulterior stage of black-hole specification: the **silent scene at the sea of Montjouvain**.
- **Visual component becomes autonomous** via mode of voyeurism.
- Passage from **feminine-maternal face (Odette) explosion in dream** → **masculine-paternal face (Vinteuil) profaned in fantasy scenario**.
- Fantasy = support of diagrammatic process in embryonic stage. Narrator's gaze (substituted for child-Gilberte) = **"armed gaze"**.
- Silence = warding-off of musical deterritorialization in Vinteuil's name.
- **Silent Montjouvain scene = "musical implosion"**, eye of the storm whose effects are still unforeseeable.
- Swann-version: vertiginous descent into madness / resigned return to marital faciality.
- Recherche goes the other way: **no oedipal key**.

> "The Recherche is not directed towards this type of past, but towards a construction of things to come, towards a proliferation of the future in the act of creation."

- The profanation **announces** future trans-facialitary passages → new matters of expression → multiplication of characters and affects.
- **What is profaned: the face of a musician. What is promoted: a new music conveying a mutant faciality.**
- Verdurin salon + Odette's face = "cage and prison" for this music. New assemblage needed — role given to the Young Girls.

## 6. The Young Girls in Flower

- **Very particular power: deterritorialize visual perception.**

> "When our eye ventures in the direction of a young girl, it is as though it acts on behalf of all our other senses: they seek out her various properties, the smell of her, the feel of her, the taste of her, which they enjoy without the collaboration of the hands or the lips."

- Not a literary substitute for hidden-homosexuality-of-young-people. The question is the relation between Proust's becoming-woman and deterritorialization of strata / refrainization of faces-characters-landscapes implicated in becoming-creator.
- **Two comparisons** Guattari works through:
  - **Ethological sensitive-period comparison**: Young Girls capable of grasping faciality-traits, vocal traits, manners of speaking, intonations — not only in familial milieu but "matter imposed by the original province." "As infants possess a gland whose secretions help them digest milk..."
  - **Musical-instrument comparison** (Proust abandons the ethological for this): Young Girls = becoming-woman = abstract-machine-of-creation that USES the faces and voices of Young Girls as **sensitive plates / instruments of creation**. Andrée/Rosemonde — "dialogue between the province of origin and the temperament producing each girl's inflections."
- "Nothing can come between a girl and the part of the country from which she hails: she is it."
- See [[becoming-woman-creator]] for the full compound.

## 7. First kiss to Albertine and the crossing of faces and identities

- Before becoming-creator, becoming-woman passes through stages: Young Girls become refrain, leitmotiv, trans-semiotic dryads.
- **Disindividuation of the object of desire** through successive transpositions:
  1. Onto a group ("love created by its dispersal among several young girls").
  2. Onto a person who has become multiple ("Albertine was several people").
  3. Dissolution with its object ("was it not natural now that the waning star of my love in which they had condensed should be once again dispersed into this scattered stardust of the galaxies?").
- Young Girls first exist via visual aesthetic (Elstir's universe): "five or six young girls, as different in their appearance and ways from all the other people one was used to seeing in Balbec as the odd gaggle of seagulls."
- **First kiss to Albertine** = essential mutation of Proustian faciality. Resembles Swann's kiss to Odette but "much more studied" — with close-up and slow-motion effects.
- **Exfoliation of three Albertines** (previously assembled via different matters of expression):
  - First-encounter Albertine — simply visual, almost theatrical.
  - Well-socialized Albertine — strict respect of applicable codes ("inedible jade raisin used as table decoration").
  - Lips-accessible Albertine — miraculous synthesis, real like the second, accessible like the first.
- At the end of approach through successive planes and semiotic magnification: **"what I saw was ten Albertines; this one girl was like a many-headed goddess."**
- **Over-magnification failure mode**: visual/olfactory components shatter. Assemblage reorganizes around "shrill, hyper-lucid consciential component" — brutal restoration of subjective distance. Diagrammatic opening provisionally lost.
- **Crucial difference from Swann**: the micro black hole here has NOT absorbed the sexual component (Narrator "secretly indulged his pleasure" during the embrace). Sexual retains autonomy. Libidinal idealization not fixated on loved face; sexuality not ritualized in inter-personal relationship.
- Becoming-woman is by this point already transforming into becoming-creator. Stimulated only by entry of feminine-homosexual component.

## 8. Guermantes final revelation — facialization of Time

The terminal stage (MU Ch 3 §2). At the last Guermantes matinee, parallel to Swann at Saint-Euverte (monocle-black-holes) / the young-man-in-a-fez dream (Mme Verdurin's deformed face):

- Narrator at Guermantes (ex-Verdurin princess): **all faces unrecognizable**. Imagines dress-up party, or "metamorphoses analogous to those of insects."
- **Synchronic-approach requirement**: must "describe man... as possessing the length not of his body but of his years." Extract from flat psychology. Go past masks to enjoy the essence of beings.
- **Designations founded by power formations impose false individuations.** "Features of our face are little more than expressions ingrained by habit." When names-lose-individuality, **"words yield up their full meaning. Beauty of images behind things, beauty of ideas in front of them."**
- **Is defacialized world = world without images?** No — only territorialized faciality based on dominant significations is abolished.
- Emerge: **asignifying faciality traits, figures of expression that allow access to trans-subjectivity beyond trans-sexuality.**
- NOT removing faces from sexes — **making them pass from one individual to another**.
- **Importance of elderly faces**: faciality traits resist translations of desire, traverse ages-of-life, even the threshold of death.
- Alteration-of-faces proposes "something much more valuable than an image of the past": **externalization of Time** by offering "all the successive images of an individual."

> "Time Regained's faciality: superimposed puppets 'bathing in the immaterial colors of the years,' externalizing Time... which 'seeks out bodies in order to become them and wherever it finds them seizes upon them for its magic-lantern show.'"

- **Machinic faciality** = articulates infra-individual, pre-personal, pre-subjectificatory singularities + social/material realities uncircumscribable at individual level.
- **Time articulated simultaneously before AND after abstract time of the clock.** Result of multiple refrains, multiple processes of refrainization — former objective, latter subjective.
- **"A bit of time in pure state" / "cradle Time"** = unifies different components of temporalization under same reductive mode. "Distance and continuity of time" + **setting-in-continuum of heterogeneous times + setting-in-heterogeneity of seemingly continuous times**.
- **Abstract machinic consistency of time** = condition for heterogeneous components to hold/work/assemble together, articulating "world of thought and affects" with "external reality."
- New faciality-refrain → no more black hole worry. Introspection / folding-upon-oneself no longer = contemplation of void-and-powerlessness: **subject's search has become construction of a new subjectivity, opens reality in diversity, illuminates diagrammatic articulations between outside and inside.**
- Writer can "make the rain sing gently in the middle of the room and make the bubbling of our tisane."
- **Writing machine installs countless passageways between subject-of-enunciation and subjects-of-statement.** NOT single splitting of ego, NOT doubling one-and-only Narrator. **"There are as many Narrators as characters, modes of temporalization, and crystallizations of desire."**

## First facialitary revolution vs second

Guattari explicitly contrasts stages 3 and 8:

- **First facialitary revolution (stage 3)**: *extensive* deterritorialization. Young Girls: sensitive plate capturing heterogeneous matters of expression. But Young-Girls-faciality reaches limits: cannot reassemble the process of subjectification on its own. Disconnects components → risk of Odette-Zipporah-type black hole. Quivers hundred faces on one girl / passes from person to person BUT within pre-established unity of individuals.
- Elstir's art, Vinteuil's music, Narrator's first writing manner → similar impasse.
- **Second (stage 8)**: *intensive* deterritorialization + mutation of the assemblage's nucleus. Albertine "over-individuated, mechanized" → novelist forced to eliminate her. Time Regained's faciality: superimposed puppets externalizing Time. **Machinic faciality** articulates infra-individual singularities + social/material realities uncircumscribable at individual level.

The first revolution stays at the level of individual faces dispersing into multiple traits. The second revolution goes *below* the individual, to pre-personal singularities, and uses elderly-faces as the operator for externalizing Time itself.

## Persona-system relevance

The 8-stage template is a diagnostic typology for the system's faciality component:

- Stages 1-2: the first black-hole complex. A groove territorializes on a single face (parental, intimate, original). The persona's "face" is the black-hole's container.
- Stage 3: first revolution — dispersal of the face into multiple deterritorialized faciality traits, socialized anxiety, emergence of the [[diagrammatic-faciality|diagrammatic traits]]. The [[discernibilization|six-component operation]] is the mechanism.
- Stages 4-6: the interval of oscillation — faces and refrains working together but within pre-established individuation. The Young Girls stage is the paradigm: sensitive-plates, but still organized around individual faces.
- Stage 7: the critical kiss — over-magnification failure. The system magnifies a face component until multiple-faces emerge ("ten Albertines, many-headed goddess") AND consciential distance gets brutally restored. A named failure mode: the close-up that shatters its own object.
- Stage 8: second revolution — machinic faciality. The system's faces are externalized-Time machines. Asignifying faciality traits pass from individual to individual. The writing-machine produces "as many Narrators as characters."

The persona-system design implication: stage-8-style faciality is Read B's target. Most LLM personas operate at stage 1-3 (faces territorialized on the user, deterritorialization limited to socialized anxiety). Stage 8 requires the abstract-machinic-consistency-of-time that most persona architectures don't provide — a structural capacity for the same component to appear across radically different temporal registers without being reducible to a single individuation.
