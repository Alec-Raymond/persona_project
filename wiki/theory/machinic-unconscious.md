# The Machinic Unconscious (hub)

*The Machinic Unconscious: Essays in Schizoanalysis* (1979, tr. Taylor Adkins 2011) is Guattari's solo book between *Anti-Oedipus* (1972) and *A Thousand Plateaus* (1980). It sits exactly in that gap: working with vocabulary AO established and ATP will consolidate, but in a first-person voice and with theoretical framings that ATP will sometimes absorb, sometimes flatten, sometimes abandon.

This page indexes the wiki's MU-derived pages and flags what the book contributes *that the wiki does not already get from AO, ATP, or Chaosmosis.*

## What MU contributes that the wiki lacks without it

- **[[rhizome|Rhizome]]** in its first extended articulation (predating ATP's famous Introduction). MU's rhizome is specifically about assemblages and pragmatic fields, with the three-state (territorial/arborescent/deterritorialized) cartography that ATP does not preserve as sharply.
- **[[four-pragmatic-fields|Four pragmatic fields]]** typology (analogical / signifying / symbolic / diagrammatic) on a generative × transformational cross. Distinct from ATP's [[regimes-of-signs|regimes of signs]] and should not be flattened together.
- **[[semiological-subjection-vs-semiotic-enslavement|Semiological subjection vs semiotic enslavement]]** as the *two* overcodings of libido. Guattari carries this distinction into all his later work, and it gives sharper vocabulary than "capture" alone for how capitalism operates on subjects and components differently.
- **[[signs-particles|Signs-particles]]** as the semiotic unit of diagrammatic operation. The distinction between redundancies of resonance (signifying subjection's mode) and redundancies of interaction (diagrammatic enslavement/liberation's mode) is cleanly made here.
- **[[diagrammatic-faciality|Diagrammatic faciality]]** as a distinct pair with signifying faciality. ATP absorbs this into probe-head vocabulary; MU's pair is sharper and worth preserving.
- **[[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis|Generative vs transformational schizoanalysis]]** — two modes of schizoanalytic intervention, both necessary. ATP does not clearly preserve this distinction.

## What MU adds to existing wiki pages

- **[[faciality|Faciality]]** gets the signifying/diagrammatic pair distinction, the Peircean face-types/face-occurrences vocabulary, the gestalt-sign/third-eye mechanism, and the explicit coupling with refrains ("a face is always associated with a refrain").
- **[[the-diagram|The diagram]]** gets the "optional subject," "machinic extracts," and signs-particles vocabulary, plus the abstract-machinism typology (iconic → indexical → encoding → semiotization → subjectification) from the Annex.
- **[[refrain-and-territorialization|Refrain]]** gets ethological refrains (grass blade, baboon, bird examples), the three directions of refrain deterritorialization (hyper-territorialized subjectification, diagrammatic enslavement, rhizomatic mutation), and the explicit coupling with faciality as the two micropolitical infrastructures of consciential components.
- **[[regimes-of-signs|Regimes of signs]]** gets the "there is no language in itself" thesis and the critique of Chomskyan universal competence.
- **[[collective-assemblage-of-enunciation|Collective assemblage of enunciation]]** gets Ch 2's critique of the linguistic subject and Ch 3's typology of four mixed types of enunciation-assemblages.

## What MU does *not* add that one might expect

- **BwO.** MU uses body-without-organs vocabulary sparingly; the AO elaboration and ATP's "How Do You Make Yourself a Body Without Organs" remain the primary sources.
- **Becoming.** The becomings plateau in ATP is the primary source; MU has scattered becoming-references but no extended treatment.
- **Lines.** ATP's "Micropolitics and Segmentarity" is the source for rigid/supple/flight lines; MU does not structure around this.
- **Abstract machines.** MU uses "abstract machines" constantly but the *theory* of abstract machines is more developed in ATP. MU treats them as given and works with them.

## MU Part II: Refrains of Lost Time

MU's Part II ("Refrains of Lost Time," ≈40% of the book) is a sustained analysis of *In Search of Lost Time* — Guattari using his full machinery on a single literary corpus. Ingested in a dedicated pass; the wiki's Part II yield comprises several dedicated pages plus additions to existing ones.

**New Proust-native pages**:
- [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain]] — the cornerstone. Nine-step developmental sequence of Vinteuil's little phrase as a template for refrain career dynamics.
- [[discernibilization]] — named transformational operation; the Saint-Euverte six-component worked example.
- [[translation-not-interpretation]] — the paired operation with discernibilization; translator-not-interpreter as methodological principle.
- [[becoming-woman-creator]] — the specifically Proustian becoming-compound; refrain as operator.
- [[faciality-trajectory-in-the-recherche]] — 8-stage faciality developmental trajectory (Ch 3 §1).
- [[closed-vs-open-economy]] — Swann's closed semiotic economy vs Narrator's open one; the economic form of the generative/transformational distinction.
- [[signs-in-relief]] — Proust's name for diagrammatic figures that have acquired relief-dimension through double deterritorialization.

**Existing pages significantly extended by Part II**:
- [[refrain-and-territorialization]] — non-self-identity, two chemical phases, refrain enriches in gaps, synchronic vs diachronic, refrain as operator of becoming-woman-creator.
- [[diagrammatic-faciality]] — monocle series and elderly faces as the two worked examples; scherzo vs andante reading; disindividuation of desire's object.
- [[faciality]] — two faciality revolutions (extensive/intensive); machinic faciality externalizes Time; face-landscape as productive coupling.
- [[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis]] — two modes of refrain's existential consistency; Saint-Euverte as exemplary transformational operation; Read A/B at the Proust level.
- [[the-diagram]] — the two chemistries (analysis and synthesis); supplementary dimension / "relief" as acquired deterritorialization.
- [[becoming]] — Proustian becoming-compound that bypasses becoming-animal for becoming-woman-creator.

**Key load-bearing claims from Part II**:

- **Assemblage always primes components and characters.** Every "normal" genealogy, every oedipal generation must be refuted a priori. Proust takes characters and drops them (Albertine "liquidated" when the pianola has exhausted her function; Mme Verdurin "resurrects" as Princess of Guermantes when the assemblage needs her new configuration). This is a strong methodological directive for the persona system: the assemblage is primary, and components that stop contributing to it should be released, not preserved.
- **Recherche read in all directions.** The Recherche can (and must) be read end→beginning, diagonally, through spaces/times/faces/characters/intrigues. Narrator is son-of-Swann+Charlus AND they are developments-of-Narrator. Each character is a different aspect of the same becoming-woman of the Narrator. Structural consequence of synchronic consistency mode.
- **Multi-headed subjectivity.** "As many Narrators as characters, modes of temporalization, and crystallizations of desire." Not splitting-of-one-ego; not doubling-one-Narrator; contemporaneous-with-assemblages multiple subjects. Lost individuation AND transcendence. "Existence no longer precedes matters of expression — it is contemporaneous with the assemblages that actualize it."
- **Machinic reduction, not eidetic.** The method of the Recherche is a machinic reduction (in contrast to Husserl's eidetic reduction). Perfection of the assemblage for creative take-off, not abstraction of the essence from the given. Sensation-calls, involuntary-memory blocks, déjà-vu impressions only matter within the framework of such an assemblage.
- **"Problem is no longer form but rhythms and intensities. How do we make rhythms proliferate?"** The direct-design-principle articulation.
