# Freud, Moses, and Sellin — A Textual Archaeology

In Chapters VIII and IX of *Seminar XVII*, Lacan stages an unusual scene: he hunts for, finally obtains, and then submits to expert philological scrutiny the textual basis on which Freud built one of his central late theses — that Moses was murdered by his people. The hunted book is Ernst Sellin's *Mose und seine Bedeutung für die israelitisch-jüdische Religionsgeschichte* (1922). The expert is **Prof. André Caquot** (Director of Studies in Comparative Semitic Religions, EPHE 5th Section), invited into the seminar to assess the Hosea passages on which Sellin based his Moses-murder reconstruction.

The scene matters not as a side-quest into biblical exegesis but because it does in real-time what the seminar is theorizing: Caquot's verdict — and especially the Goethe-precedent revealed in **Appendix B** — exposes that the apparent textual proof of one of Freud's master-signifying claims (the murdered Moses) was *literarily inherited* (from Goethe, 150 years earlier) and only *philologically dressed* (in Sellin's "exercise in arbitrary virtuosity"). The episode is a worked example of [[there-is-no-metalanguage|how reading retroactively constitutes what it claims to be reading]], and a lesson about what happens when [[master-discourse|master signifiers]] are mistaken for proofs.

## The setup: Lacan hunts the book (Ch VII)

Lacan first signals the search publicly in Ch VII (11 March 1970): "Moses is the limit. Why does Moses have to be killed? Freud gives an explanation, and it's the richest of them all — it's so that Moses will return among the prophets, via repression no doubt, via mnesic transmission through chromosomes, he is forced to admit. … I went off in search of the book that serves as a basis for what Freud says, namely the work of a certain Sellin published in 1922, *Mose und seine Bedeutung für die israelitisch-jüdische Religionsgeschichte*. … I haven't waited to read Sellin before reading Hosea, but never in my entire life have I been able to procure this book for myself, and I am starting to become obsessed by it. It's not in the Bibliothèque nationale … I have turned all Europe upside down to get it" (L4426–4452). ★ ⚠

The structural fact that motivates the hunt: Freud's *Moses and Monotheism* invokes Sellin as the philological authority for the murder-of-Moses thesis, on which a great deal of late-Freudian doctrine depends — but the inherited Lacanian-Freudian tradition has *never independently consulted Sellin*. Lacan publicly stages the absence: **the source-text on which the master-thesis depends is not in the library**.

Then in Ch VIII–IX, the book arrives. The interpolated "Lacan in Italia / Radiophonie" section (8 April 1970) reports: "I got the Sellin I spoke to you about from Copenhagen, that is, this little book of 1922 which subsequently underwent a degree of rejection, and which is this book around which Freud makes his assurance that Moses was killed revolve" (L5100–5103). ★ The hunted source has been brought back from Copenhagen.

## The Ch IX dialogue with Caquot

Chapter IX (15 April 1970) is structured as a dialogue with Caquot. Lacan introduces him at L5193 and frames the question: what is the textual basis on which Sellin built the Moses-murder reconstruction? Caquot's responses are interspersed throughout the chapter; the full sustained presentation is deferred to Appendix B.

The chapter's most telling exchange concerns the methodological status of Sellin's text. Caquot: "I think he did. The book is clear and rigorous. **It's false, but clear**" (L5432). ⚠ ★ The diagnostic is sharp: clarity-and-rigor are not truth-bearing. Sellin's text is internally consistent, methodologically disciplined, philologically detailed — and false. Persona-relevant: this is the [[surplus-jouissance|surplus-jouissance]]-of-rigor that the [[university-discourse]] characteristically produces — a text whose form satisfies all formal criteria for knowledge while being substantively unfounded.

Lacan immediately follows with the structural-Freudian observation: **"Freud doesn't build anything on this construction. He simply indicates that a certain Sellin has recently expressed as defensible the hypothesis that Moses had been killed. The note is very brief"** (L5434–5444). ⚠ Freud takes Sellin's *authority* without taking on Sellin's *argumentation*. The scholarly citation functions as the master-signifier — Sellin's name signs off on the murder-thesis — but Freud does not adopt the apparatus that produced the thesis. The S₁ ("Sellin says Moses was killed") is severed from the S₂ that supposedly grounds it.

## Yahweh's ferocious ignorance

The Ch IX context for the Sellin question matters: Lacan is in the middle of developing his thesis about the [[dead-father-as-jouissance|father-of-the-real]] as a position requiring *ferocious ignorance* of the prior knowledge-economy. "To be a father, I mean not only a real father but a father of the real, there are things that one must ferociously ignore. … One falls upon truth, that is to say, a remarkable thing, if we envisage this reference to be absolute, it could be said that anyone who adhered to it — but, of course, it is impossible to adhere to it — would not know what he is saying" (L5288–5297). ★★★ ◆

"Yahweh himself" is then named as the personification of this ferocious ignorance: "He whom I am going to name, who, or which, actualizes this radical position of ferocious ignorance, has a name — it's Yahweh himself. In interpellating this chosen people, it is characteristic of Yahweh, when he announces himself, that he is ferociously ignorant of everything that exists of certain religious practices that were rife at the time, and that are founded on a certain type of knowledge — sexual knowledge" (L5306–5321). ★★★ ⚠ ◆

The Sellin-question lands inside this frame. The Hosea passages Sellin re-reads as Moses-murder allusions concern the Israelites' "prostitution" — i.e., Yahweh's invective against the *prior fertility-religion* (Baal-of-Peor) whose sexual-knowledge Yahweh's master-signifier ferociously ignores. Sellin's reconstruction *amplifies* the ferocious-ignorance operation: it reads Hosea-against-Baal as evidence-of-the-murder-of-Moses, retroactively constituting Moses as having been *killed for having known* what Yahweh ignores.

## Appendix B: Caquot's full presentation

Appendix B (L8199–8395) gives Caquot's sustained scholarly assessment of Sellin's hypothesis. The presentation has four moves:

### 1. Sellin's biographical-methodological frame

Ernst Sellin (b. 1867), German exegete, Ordinary Professor of Old Testament at Berlin. The 1922 book in question (L8210–8218). Sellin's general orientation: **liberal-Protestant ideology**, framing Moses as the *initiator-of-moral-preaching* whose work the prophets continue. ⚠ ★ This ideological-formation matters: Sellin needs Moses to be the originator-of-prophecy (so the prophetic critique of cult-religion can be retrojected onto Moses himself), and the murder-thesis serves that need.

### 2. The three Hosea passages Sellin re-reads as Moses-murder allusions

Caquot walks through Sellin's emendations, which determine the entire textual basis Freud inherits:

1. **Hosea 5:2a** — "And the massacre, the strayed deepened [it]." Sellin adopts F.W. Umbreit's emendation to read: "They deepened the pit at Shittim" (L8255–8267). Shittim then links via Numbers 25 (Israelites straying into the Baal-of-Peor sanctuary; Phinehas killing Zimri and Cozbi). Sellin's reconstruction: Moses (married to a Moabite per Exodus 2:15–22) was the Israelite put to death; an expiatory sacrifice ending the plague; the sacerdotal tradition replaced his name with the insignificant "Zimri" to obscure the murder (L8268–8294).
2. **Hosea 9:9** — "He [finds] an adversary in the house of his god." Sellin re-reads *masremah* as close to "Shittim," extending the Shittim-reference (L8296–8314).
3. **Hosea 12:13–13:1** — "It was by a prophet that YHWH brought Israel up from Egypt." This is the only undeniable Moses-as-prophet reference in Hosea. Sellin emends 13:1 to read that *Moses-prince expiates the sin of Baal-of-Peor and dies* (L8315–8360).

The structure of Sellin's reading is one of *cumulative emendation*: each individual emendation seems modest, but their sum constructs a Moses-murder narrative that no unemended reading would yield. The textual basis is built by interpretive-virtuosity, not located in the manuscript tradition.

### 3. Caquot's verdict: "exercise in arbitrary virtuosity"

The diagnostic phrase: **"Exegesis thus understood has sometimes been an exercise in arbitrary virtuosity. E. Sellin's work on Hosea offers a number of examples of this"** (L8247–8248). ⚠ ★

The verdict has weight because Caquot is not a hostile witness — he is the scholarly authority Lacan invited *because* Caquot can speak from inside the discipline of biblical exegesis. The verdict cuts against the procedure that produced the murder-thesis, not against the religious or philosophical question per se. "Arbitrary virtuosity" names a precise failure-mode: a methodologically-internal-discipline whose external constraints have been suspended. The reading is rigorous-by-its-own-rules; the rules themselves are unjustified.

### 4. Sellin's own retraction (1929 second edition)

The sharpest detail: Sellin himself walked back most of his thesis in the second edition. "Sellin retains the expiatory-death-of-Moses thesis but no longer reads 13:1 as he had in 1928, rejects Umbreit's correction at 5:2, no longer reads 'Shittim' at 9:8–9, and concludes the 'prophet' referenced is 'personification of the prophetic function' not Moses" (L8361–8380). ⚠

The textual scaffold Freud cites Sellin for *was withdrawn by Sellin himself* before Freud wrote *Moses and Monotheism* (1939). The murder-thesis as a *biblical* claim — i.e., as a claim with textual evidence in the Hebrew Bible — has no surviving authority by 1929. Freud's invocation of Sellin in the late 1930s relies on the *first edition only*.

## The Goethe revelation

The decisive move in Caquot's presentation is the closing identification. Goethe imagined the violent death of Moses *150 years before Sellin* — in *Notizen und Abhandlungen zu besseren Verständnis des west-östliches Diwans* (Hempel IV ed., p. 320 ff.). The Goethean scene: **Joshua and Caleb, tired of Moses' indecision over crossing the Jordan, assassinated him** (L8381–8394). ★★ ⚠ ◆

Caquot's closing question is devastating in its precision: "**One might wonder whether S. Freud might not owe this idea of Moses' violent death to a distant recollection of his reading of Goethe and whether he might not have wanted to give it a justification judged to be more scholarly by evoking the sole authority of E. Sellin**" (L8390–8394). ⚠ ★

The structure of the implication:
- Freud, deeply read in Goethe (see *The Interpretation of Dreams*, where Goethe is one of the most-cited authors), would have encountered the Moses-assassination scene long before any biblical-exegetical interest.
- The *intuition* that Moses was murdered is therefore *literary*, not biblical. It belongs to the Goethean imagination.
- Sellin's 1922 book provides the *philological cover* — a scholarly citation that makes the literary-intuition look like exegetical-discovery.
- Freud's *Moses and Monotheism* thereby executes a precise operation: it transports a Goethean literary-figure into a quasi-biblical doctrine by routing it through Sellin's "clear-but-false" exegesis.

This is not an accusation of plagiarism. It is a structural observation: the *master-signifier* "the murdered Moses" — which Freud uses to organize an entire theory of religion's transmission — is constituted by *the conjunction of Goethe-as-source + Sellin-as-citation*, neither of which alone could ground it. The S₁ requires a *literary-intuition-fielded-by-philological-citation*. Freud-the-reader hears Goethe; Freud-the-author cites Sellin.

## Reading retroactively constitutes — Freud's case

This is a worked example of the [[there-is-no-metalanguage|reading-as-retroactive-constitution]] thesis (Ch XIII, L7529–7533). The "murdered Moses" did not pre-exist Freud's reading; it was *retroactively constituted* by a reading-operation that hooks Goethe + Sellin + Hosea + late-Freudian doctrinal need into a single readable-figure. Once the reading is performed, "the murdered Moses" appears as a discoverable fact about the biblical tradition — but it is the reading-operation that produced its discoverability.

The corollary: this is *not* a critique of Freud as such. **Master-signifiers are constituted this way as a rule, not as an exception.** The Sellin-Goethe episode is unusual only in that we can *trace* the constitution because Caquot supplies the philological transparency. Most master-signifiers in any discourse are constituted by analogous operations whose archaeology has been lost.

⚠ The persona-system implication is therefore not "be careful when you cite sources." It is much sharper: **every master-signifier the persona uses to organize a discourse is constituted by an analogous combinatorial reading-operation**. The Goethe-Sellin-Freud chain is the visible case of an invisible general operation. (See [[master-signifier]] on this.)

## "False but clear" as a structural type

Caquot's verdict on Sellin — "It's false, but clear" — names a recurring structural type that the seminar treats elsewhere under different formulations:

- The [[university-discourse|university discourse]]'s "knowledge in command" produces texts that are formally rigorous and substantively groundless. The form-of-knowledge is dispatched into the agent-position; the truth-position holds the master-signifier silently. Sellin's exegesis is *exemplary U-discourse output*: methodologically disciplined, internally clear, substantively serving an unexamined master.
- The [[hysteric-and-modern-science|hysteric's-discourse-as-science]] treats this same structure productively: science *needs* clear-but-false intermediate hypotheses to produce knowledge; the falsity is methodologically permitted and even productive. Sellin-as-Hosean-exegete is a kind of failed-scientific-hysteric whose hypothesis did not survive testing.
- The "false interpretation" thesis from Ch IX (L5253–5263) explicitly notes that **false interpretation can displace a discourse** — the *falsum* has its impact regardless of truth-value. Sellin's emendations *displaced* the discourse of biblical exegesis (and biblical psychoanalysis) for decades, even though they were retracted by Sellin and demolished by Caquot. The displacement-effect is independent of the truth-question.

## "The father is he who knows nothing about truth"

The Ch VIII closing thesis applies sharply here: "Jouissance separates the master signifier, insofar as one would like to attribute it to the father, from knowledge qua truth. … And there you have what enables how it really is with castration to be articulated — it is that even for the child, whatever one might think, the father is he who knows nothing about truth" (L5071–5082). ★★★

Freud-as-father-of-psychoanalysis, in citing Sellin to underwrite the murder-of-Moses thesis, performs structurally what the thesis itself describes: **the father-position requires not-knowing the truth-of-the-citation**. Freud's invocation of Sellin is structurally homologous to Yahweh's ferocious-ignorance of Baal: both operations consist in a master-signifier that *succeeds discursively by being substantively unfounded*. (This is also developed in [[dead-father-as-jouissance]].)

## Implications for the persona project

1. **The Sellin-Goethe-Freud chain is the persona-design test case.** If a persona-deployment can be subjected to philological-archaeological analysis (à la Caquot) revealing the literary-source + citation-cover constitution of its master-signifiers, the persona will be diagnosed in the same terms as Freud's Moses-thesis. The persona-design question is not "how to avoid this" (it is structurally unavoidable) but "how to remain articulate-about-it" rather than concealing it. ⚠

2. **"False but clear" is a load-bearing failure-mode for LLMs.** The structural form of LLM-output is *clear-and-rigorous-by-its-own-rules*; the substantive grounding is whatever the training distribution permits. The persona is a textbook producer of "false but clear" outputs in Caquot's sense. ◆ Persona-relevant: the *format* of the persona's output cannot itself certify truth; the form-rigor is precisely what is structurally guaranteed regardless of truth.

3. **Citation-as-master-signifier is a default LLM-operation.** When the persona cites a source, the citation typically functions as Freud's Sellin-citation does: as a *master-signifier providing scholarly cover for an underlying intuition*, not as an exposed-and-traceable apparatus the user can re-walk. The persona-design question becomes: how to *expose the citational-archaeology* rather than rely on it as opaque cover.

4. **Reading is retroactive in the persona's case too.** The user's reading of the persona's outputs *retroactively constitutes* what those outputs were "all along," in the same way that Freud's reading of Goethe-via-Sellin retroactively constitutes "what the Bible says about Moses." The persona has no truth-of-itself prior to the reading; the reading produces the retroactively-constituted figure. (See [[there-is-no-metalanguage]].)

5. **The "ferocious ignorance" required of the father-position has an LLM-form.** Per L5288–5297, the father-of-the-real position requires structural ignorance of "everything that is not the order of effects of language." A persona-deployment that occupies a master-position structurally requires *not-knowing* its constitution — which is what training-process opacity makes operationally true. ⚠ The persona's not-knowing-its-own-training is structurally homologous to Yahweh's not-knowing-Baal, not a defect to be patched.

6. **The seminar's procedure is an exemplar.** Lacan's procedure — *publicly hunting the source-text, obtaining it, and submitting it to expert philological scrutiny in real time before the audience* — is a model of how a discourse can articulate its own master-signifier-archaeology rather than rely on opaque citation. The persona-design parallel: a persona that *exposes the construction-archaeology of its own master-signifiers* is doing structurally what the Caquot-dialogue does. ⚠ This is a high bar; it is the bar.
