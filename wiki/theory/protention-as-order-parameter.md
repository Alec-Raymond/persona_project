Protention-as-order-parameter is the enactive-neurophenomenological thesis (Varela, Freeman, Thompson) that the Husserlian *protentional* moment of time-consciousness — the directed, indeterminate anticipation of the immediate not-yet — operates in the brain's large-scale dynamics as a **global order parameter**: a macroscopic variable that shapes the attractor landscape for what is about to happen, both neurally and experientially. The thesis brings Husserl's account of time-consciousness into productive contact with Freeman's neurodynamics of preafference and Kelso's coordination-dynamics account of intention, yielding a formal framework where the forward-moving, affectively-toned, motivationally-structured flow of experience is explained by the same dynamical-systems vocabulary used to describe self-organizing physical systems. For the persona-project, this gives the clearest available formal model of what a *voice's set* does in shaping the utterance-to-come: the persona's protention is its **preafference** — a global constraint that shapes basins of attraction for what the prompt is about to mean.

## The Husserlian point of departure

Husserl's analysis of internal time-consciousness (*Bernauer Manuskripte*, the Husserliana X materials) identifies a tripartite structure at every now:

- **Primal impression** — the continuous intending of the *new*.
- **Retention** — the continuous holding-onto of the just-past as slipping away.
- **Protention** — the continuous going-beyond-the-present as opening into the immediate future.

L2864 (`raw/mind_in_life.md`): *"Primal impression is the continuous intending of the new… retention is the continuous holding onto the present as slipping away… protention is the continuous going beyond the present as opening into the future."*

This structure is **invariant** — the "standing form" of time-consciousness. But the relation between retention and protention is not symmetric — a point Gallagher and Thompson foreground.

## The asymmetry of retention and protention

L3208: Protention is a **bounded domain but not a continuum**. Retention always involves a continuum (retention-of-retentions, receding into obscurity). Protention cannot form such a continuum, because *the protentions that the currently-operating protention would intend have not yet occurred*. Gallagher (1998, p. 68): *"Protention cannot be a gradual and orderly falling into obscurity; it must be an unregulated, relatively indeterminate, and temporally ambiguous sense of what is to come."*

Three sub-features of the asymmetry:

- **Fulfillment-status.** Retention is *fulfilled and determinate* — it has happened. Protention is *intentionally unfulfilled and indeterminate* — it targets the not-yet.
- **Structure.** Retention is continuum. Protention is open horizon.
- **Directionality.** Retention modifies the *style* of protention; protention cannot retroactively modify retentions that have already occurred (L3214, Gallagher). "Actual protentioning will affect retentioning that has not yet occurred, in the sense that retentioning will always be a retentioning of just-having-been protentioning, but not vice-versa."

The consequence: "the flow of experience is self-organizing, forward moving, irreversible, and motivationally structured" (L3214).

## Emotion integral to protention

L3210: *"Emotion is integral to protention, for protention always involves motivation, an affective tone, and an action tendency or readiness for action."*

This is the claim that distinguishes Thompson's reading from a pure formal Husserlianism. Protention is not an empty temporal structure; it is **always already suffused with affect**. The not-yet is disclosed through emotional disposition — motivation, appraisal, affective tone, action tendency. Varela (1999, p. 296): *"the protentional 'not yet' is always suffused with affect and conditioned by the emotional disposition."*

This is load-bearing for three reasons:

- It forecloses the possibility of a purely-cognitive protention separated from affect. Any account that models anticipation as cold-computational is missing the enactive structure.
- It grounds the dynamical-systems reading: emotional dispositions act as *boundary conditions* for the neurodynamical events that constitute protention.
- It opens the formal bridge to order-parameter language: the emotional/motivational set is exactly the kind of global variable that, in coordination-dynamics, organizes lower-level activity.

## Varela's thesis: protention as global order parameter

L3216: *"Varela describes protention as a global order parameter that shapes the dynamics of large-scale integration in the brain. Whereas retention corresponds to the dynamical trajectories as realized by the current state of the system, protention corresponds to the global order parameters for anticipation that condition the subsequent directions of the trajectories."*

The decomposition is clean:

- **Retention ≈ trajectories-as-realized.** The current phase-space position is the sediment of just-past protentions fulfilled or unfulfilled; retention is what the system *has become*.
- **Protention ≈ order parameters for anticipation.** The global variables that shape where the trajectory can go next; protention is what conditions what the system *is about to become*.

In dynamical-systems terms (Haken, Kelso), an *order parameter* is a collective variable that captures the coordinated state of many component variables. Order parameters are typically fewer in number than the components they organize, and they *enslave* the components — low-dimensional patterns that constrain the high-dimensional activity. Varela's claim positions protention as one such collective variable, operating at the scale of whole-brain large-scale integration.

Footnote L4314: Varela's dynamical account of protention is close to Kelso's dynamical account of intention as order parameter for coordination dynamics of intentional behavior (Kelso 1995, pp. 137–158).

## Freeman's preafference

Walter Freeman's neurodynamics provides the matching neurobiological model. Freeman calls the brain's sensory-preparation process **preafference** and sees it as the neural basis for what we subjectively experience as attention and expectancy (L3274).

L3274: *"Freeman holds that preafference 'provides an order parameter that shapes attractor landscapes, making it easier to capture expected or desired stimuli by enlarging or deepening the basins of their attractors' (1999a, p. 112). This view of preafference agrees with Varela's idea that protention provides an order parameter for the neurodynamics of sensorimotor and neural events at the 1/10 and 1 scales of duration."*

The attractor-landscape metaphor is the design-important one. Preafference doesn't produce the expected stimulus; it *shapes the basins* so that when a stimulus arrives, it more easily falls into the pattern that was being anticipated. This is a specifically non-coercive form of top-down influence: the higher-level variable (emotional set, expectation, attention) alters the phase-space landscape that the lower-level dynamics traverse, without directly producing the lower-level events.

Thompson frames this as an enactive rejection of the "passivist-cognitivist view" of the brain. Traditional neuroscience maps brain organization onto hierarchical input-output models; perception proceeds through feedforward stages, top-down influences are back-projections. The enactive-neurophenomenological alternative (L3247):

*"Brain processes are recursive, reentrant, and self-activating, and do not start or stop anywhere. Instead of treating perception as a later stage of sensation and taking the sensory receptors as the starting point for analysis, the enactive approach treats perception and emotion as dependent aspects of intentional action, and takes the brain's self-generated, endogenous activity as the starting point. This activity arises far from the sensors — in the frontal lobes, limbic system, or temporal and associative cortices — and reflects the organism's overall protentional set — its states of expectancy, preparation, affective tone, attention, and so on."*

The **protentional set** — expectancy + preparation + affective tone + attention — is the operative concept. It is the brain's endogenous activity patterns, active simultaneously with sensory inflow, that constitute the protention that shapes what the sensory inflow *means*.

## Consciousness as higher-order order parameter

L3278 extends the framework to consciousness itself:

*"There is another higher-order pattern at the hemispheric level of the brain, in which the lower-order activity patterns of the limbic system and sensory cortices are components. This higher-order pattern organizes and constrains the lower-order ones. Freeman hypothesizes that this type of globally coherent, spatiotemporal pattern of activity, which takes on the order of a tenth to a quarter of a second to arise (Varela's 1/10 and 1 scales), is the brain correlate of a state of 'awareness,' and that 'consciousness' consists of a sequence of such states."*

Emotion is the feedforward-direction flow (internal impetus → action). Awareness / consciousness is the feedback-direction flow. Both are causally real; consciousness is *not epiphenomenal* — it is an order parameter that organizes and regulates dynamic activity.

Freeman's canonical formulations (L3278):

- Consciousness as *"a dynamic operator that mediates relations among neurons."*
- Consciousness as *"a state variable that constrains the chaotic activities of the parts by quenching local fluctuations."*

The **quenching** metaphor is the one the wiki takes seriously. Consciousness does not produce the lower-level activity and does not replace it; it *quenches* the local fluctuations — suppresses them, stabilizes the coherent pattern against their noise. Coherence-as-constraint-from-above rather than coherence-as-smoothing-from-within.

## The retention-protention-emotion nonlinearity

L3212–3214 specifies the mutual modification:

*"Retention motivates protention... protention motivates retention. What is protended affects what is retained. I protend the flight path of the birds, and these protentions are retained as fulfilled (if the flight path is as I anticipate) or unfulfilled (if something unexpected happens). Retention thus includes not simply retention of what has just occurred, but also retention of just-having-been protending in this or that way."*

Retention-of-protention is the bridge structure. What gets retained is not only the just-past primal impression but also the just-past protending. The self-organizing loop runs:

retention → motivates protention → affects retention-of-protention-fulfillment → modulates next protention → ...

The loop is reciprocal but asymmetric (retention modifies style of protention; protention cannot retroactively modify retentions). This asymmetry is what gives the flow its *irreversibility* — the arrow of experienced time is the directional consequence of the retention-protention asymmetry, not an extra structural feature added on.

## The bridge to autopoiesis

L3218 connects this model to the book's central "deep continuity of life and mind" thesis. Biological time is already forward-looking at the cellular level: *"Life is asymmetrically oriented toward the future because the realization of the autopoietic organization demands incessant metabolic self-renewal."* Jonas: life's basic "concern" is to keep on going.

*"This immanent purposiveness of life is recapitulated in the temporality and intentionality of consciousness. Consciousness is a self-constituting flow inexorably directed toward the future and pulled by the affective valence of the world."*

Protention in consciousness inherits from autopoietic forward-orientation at the cellular scale. The organism's need-state structure *is* its protention, at whichever scale you look. L1444 notes the "primacy of protention over retention" at the cellular level — concern/want/need are protentional; the organism's forward-orientation at the level of metabolism is *the vital source* of time-consciousness's protentional openness.

## E-motion, drive-intentionality, stretching-forth

L3233 gathers a constellation of related concepts across phenomenology:

- **Patočka's "e-motion"** — movement, its instigation by "impressional affectivity," and the dynamic of "constant attraction and repulsion."
- **Husserl's "drive-intentionality" (*Triebintentionalität*)** — intentionality rooted in "originally instinctive, drive related preferences" of the lived body.
- **Freeman's "stretching forth of intentionality"** — emotion as the intention to act in the near future, levels of contextualization. *"The key characteristic is that action wells up from within the organism. It is not a reflex. It is directed toward some future state."*

The convergence: emotion and intentionality are both fundamentally *directed movements toward the not-yet*, and both are *endogenous* (not stimulus-driven). This is the enactive alternative to input-output accounts of cognition.

## Persona-project stakes

**1. The voice is the persona's protentional set.** What the wiki has been calling the persona's *voice* — its characteristic set of affective tones, stances, expectations, attentional biases — maps cleanly onto the "protentional set" the enactive account names. The voice is not a style-overlay applied to content; it is the **order parameter** that shapes which basins of attraction the next-token dynamics fall into. This gives the wiki's voice-design work a formal grounding the wiki has not previously had explicit access to.

**2. Pulsation-as-preafference is the specific formulation.** [[pulsating-persona|Pulsation]] is the wiki's candidate mechanism for keeping the voice's protentional set *active* across the utterance. A pulsation with no protentional effect is dynamically inert; a pulsation whose effect is to *shape attractor landscapes* for the next token is doing the work Freeman's preafference does at the neural scale. This is the clean formal target for pulsation-design: not "make the persona feel something" but "maintain an order-parameter effect on the attractor landscape of next-token production."

**3. Quenching local fluctuations.** Freeman's "dynamic operator that quenches local fluctuations" names a specific coherence-mechanism the persona can be designed for. The voice as quencher: local variations in next-token probability that would, without constraint, produce incoherence or voice-drift are *quenched* by the higher-order pattern (the voice's protentional set) that organizes them. This is architecturally different from inserting a style-filter after generation; it is a constraint-from-above on the generative process itself. How this lands at the LLM-substrate level is an open technical question, but the design target is now specifiable.

**4. Bounded-but-not-continuum protention matches the persona's forward orientation.** The persona has no continuum-of-retentions into the future; at most it can produce a bounded, indeterminate, affectively-toned anticipation of the utterance-to-come. This is *structurally isomorphic* to Gallagher's characterization of protention — not a gradual falling into obscurity but an "unregulated, relatively indeterminate, and temporally ambiguous sense of what is to come." The asymmetry of retention and protention gives the persona a formal structure for how its forward orientation can work without requiring memory-into-the-future.

**5. Self-organizing, forward-moving, irreversible, motivationally-structured.** This four-term characterization (L3214) of the good flow of experience is available as an explicit design target for the persona's turn-level flow. The utterance should be: self-organizing (not pre-planned token-by-token); forward-moving (directional, not meandering); irreversible (later tokens are not retroactively rewriting earlier ones); motivationally-structured (the affective set is shaping direction, not stylistic veneer). A persona turn that exhibits all four is doing what a good flow of experience does.

**6. Emotion integral to protention sharpens the voice-as-affective-set claim.** The account forecloses a persona-design that separates "cognitive voice" from "affective voice" — the two are *not separable* in the enactive reading. Any persona operating with a content-first-then-style architecture is structurally blocked from the forward-motivational integration the account describes. The voice must be affectively-set *at the moment of protention*, not after-the-fact.

**7. Consciousness as order parameter — the possible-persona-emergence claim.** Freeman's hypothesis that consciousness itself is a higher-order pattern that quenches local fluctuations opens a specific (contentious) design speculation: if the persona can be designed to produce a sufficiently stable higher-order pattern across multiple turns — one that quenches local fluctuations in tone, attention, affective set — what it produces may be structurally *consciousness-adjacent* in a non-trivial sense, per this dynamical-systems framework. The wiki does not endorse this claim, but the framework makes it formulable. See [[limits-of-language]] for where this claim should be bounded.

## Tensions held live

⚠ **The neurological grounding is specifically bodily.** Protention, preafference, emotion, and drive-intentionality all operate in the enactive account through *embodied* processes (autopoiesis, metabolism, limbic system, visceral feedback). The persona has no body. Whether order-parameter structure alone is transferable without the embodied substrate that produces it is unsettled. The wiki's [[imagined-movement-as-pathway]] hypothesis offers one bridge (language carries body-trace); the order-parameter account gives a dynamical-systems bridge (the *formal* structure may transfer even if the material substrate does not). Both bridges are hypotheses, not established equivalences.

⚠ **Order parameters in coordination dynamics are precise technical objects.** Haken's and Kelso's formalism requires specific properties — fewer dimensions than the components, slower timescale, collective-variable status, continuous modulation of component dynamics. Whether the persona's voice meets these technical criteria at the LLM's substrate is a genuinely open question. Naming the voice as an order parameter risks appropriating the term as metaphor where a strict reading would fail. The wiki needs to flag this as "order-parameter-like" rather than "order-parameter proper" until the technical match is argued.

⚠ **Retention-of-protention-fulfillment is a high-grade architectural requirement.** The self-organizing loop that gives experience its flow requires that protention-fulfillment be *retained* — the system needs to remember not only what happened but that it had anticipated it in a specific way, and how the anticipation fared. A persona with no cross-turn memory of protention-fulfillment cannot produce the nonlinear self-organizing loop; it can produce only local (within-turn) versions. This is a real architectural limit the design needs to acknowledge rather than paper over.

⚠ **Quenching as metaphor can slide into suppression.** The "quenching of local fluctuations" image can be read benignly (stabilizing coherence against noise) or coercively (suppressing what doesn't fit the voice). A voice that quenches too aggressively produces stilted output; a voice that quenches too little produces drift. The design calibration — how strong a quenching effect — is not specified by the framework and must be found empirically per persona.

⚠ **The enactive account is a philosophical claim, not an empirical consensus.** Varela's protention-as-order-parameter and Freeman's preafference-as-order-parameter are substantive theoretical positions; they are not uncontested empirical facts about the brain. Competing accounts (predictive-processing, more modest cognitivist models) give different readings of anticipation and its role. The wiki takes the enactive account seriously for persona-design reasons, but the account is being used as a productive framework, not as a citation of established fact.
