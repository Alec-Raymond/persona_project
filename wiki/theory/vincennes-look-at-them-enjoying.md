# Vincennes — Look at Them Enjoying

On 3 December 1969 — three weeks into *Seminar XVII* — Lacan went to the experimental [[university-discourse|university]] at Vincennes (Paris VIII, founded as the post-1968 reform-campus) and gave the only one of a planned four-part series called "*Analyticon*." The session is published as **Appendix A** of *Seminar XVII* (L7696–8198). It is the seminar's single most political document and contains two of Lacan's most-quoted formulations:

- **"Look at them enjoying!"** — the post-68 university as a *jouissance-display regime*, with students as its *helots* (L8186–8195).
- **"What you aspire to as revolutionaries is a master. You will get one."** — the structural certainty that revolutionary aspiration ends as [[master-discourse|master's discourse]] (L8170–8174).

The Vincennes session is not a side-event to the seminar's theoretical work. It is where the [[four-discourses|four-discourse machinery]] is *deployed* against an audience that is structurally positioned as the *a*-product of the university discourse — and is told so to its face. The political claims of the seminar take their concrete shape here.

## The setting

Vincennes was founded in 1968–69 as France's response to the May events: an experimental university built explicitly to absorb the energies of the student revolt. Its student body was constituted as the *post-68 progressive cohort*. Lacan's intervention is calibrated to that fact. He is speaking *to* a population whose existence is the post-68 institutional concession, and his entire frame is that this concession is the [[capitalist-mutation-of-master|university-discourse]]'s structural answer to the revolt: the master, having been challenged, has reconfigured as university and now produces *the revolters themselves* as its *a*-product.

The session opens with Lacan placing the four-discourse formulas on the board (L7736–7747) and saying he will work through them. His example for the master's discourse is his dog Justine: "I will be talking about my Egeria … she is the only person I know who knows what she is *speaking* — I am not saying, '*what she is saying*'" (L7715–7723). ⚠ The animal-as-discourse-illustration is set against the student-audience as the human-discourse-bearers, and the joke cuts both ways.

## The base notation, restated

Embedded in the Vincennes session is the seminar's most condensed re-statement of the foundational signifier-formula: "**A signifier is defined as representing a subject for another signifier**" (L7774–7775). Lacan calls it "an altogether fundamental notation. It can be taken as one at any rate." The Vincennes context matters: the formula is given to a non-analytic audience, *as the rule that governs the four-discourse apparatus they are about to be told they are inside*.

## "Psychoanalysis is not transmissible like other forms of knowledge"

Early in the session, Lacan responds to the obvious institutional question — Vincennes is supposed to *teach* psychoanalysis, so its students should become psychoanalysts. Lacan: "Vincennes students, at the conclusion of the teaching they are supposed to have received, can't become psychoanalysts" — because "psychoanalysis is not something that can be transmitted like other forms of knowledge" (L7753–7762). ★★ This is the [[transference-and-subject-supposed-to-know|analytic-transmission]] thesis as institutional refusal: the university-form *cannot* produce analysts; producing them would require a different discourse-position altogether (see [[analyst-discourse]]).

The corollary is the impossible-function thesis applied to the analyst: "Freud, who knew what he was saying, said that it was an impossible function — and yet fulfilled every day. If you reread his text closely you will see that it is not the function that is in question but the being of the psychoanalyst" (L7783–7786). ★★ See [[four-impossible-professions]]. The function is fulfilled-impossibly; the *being* of the analyst is the residual question.

## "Lacan, is psychoanalysis revolutionary?"

The famous heckler exchange. A student: "Lacan, is psychoanalysis revolutionary?" Lacan: "Now, there's a good question!" (L7867–7869). ⚠ The reply is not deflection; it is structural acknowledgment. The question is *good* because it puts Lacan in the position of having to answer-from-which-discourse: if he answers "yes" from S₂-as-agent (university-position), he has just performed the master-discourse-substitution he is about to diagnose. He keeps the question live.

The answer the seminar implicitly gives across the rest of the chapter:
- The analytic-act is "revolutionary not as theory" but "as act" — "a fertile step, not of thought, but of act" (Ch XII L6999–7001, brought to bear on Vincennes).
- But the *student* aspiration to revolutionary politics is structurally bound to the master-discourse-outcome (below).

## "You are the surplus value … stamped 'credit points'"

The chapter's most pointed structural claim. Lacan rotates from the master-discourse to the [[university-discourse]] formula on the board, and reads its implications for the audience directly: "If you begin by putting in its place what essentially constitutes the master's discourse, namely, that he commands, that he intervenes in the system of knowledge, you can ask yourselves what it means when the discourse of knowledge, through this ninety-degree displacement, does not need to be on the board because it is in the real. In this displacement, when knowledge takes the helm, at this moment in which you are located, it is here that the result, the fruit, the fallout of the relations between master and slave has been defined — namely, what in my algebra is designated by a letter, the object a" (L7902–7912). ★★★ ◆

Then immediately, without softening: "**You are the product of the university, and you prove that you are the surplus value … you leave here stamped, 'credit points'**" (L7913–7917). ★★★ ⚠ ◆

The structure of the claim:
- Vincennes-students are not subjects-of-knowledge produced by the university (the U-discourse's self-image). They are *a*-products in the *real* — the surplus-jouissance-residue of the master/slave relation now mediated through capitalist-knowledge. (See [[capitalist-mutation-of-master]], [[surplus-jouissance]].)
- "Credit points" — the institutional certification — is the *form* in which the surplus value is stamped onto the student-as-product. The certification *is* the *a*-stamping operation, not a recognition of prior knowledge.
- The discourse-of-knowledge "does not need to be on the board because it is in the real" — i.e., U-discourse-domination is no longer ideological-symbolic but materialized in the institutional apparatus that processes the student-body. This is the Lacanian version of "the institution itself enacts what its theory denies."

## "Look at them enjoying!" — helots of the regime

The closing political formulation. Lacan: "**The first to collaborate with this, right here at Vincennes, are you, for you fulfill the role of helots of this regime. You don't know what that means either? The regime is putting you on display. It says, 'Look at them enjoying!'**" (L8186–8195). ★★★ ⚠ ◆

The structure of the claim — three layers:

1. **Helots** — the Spartan slave-class, *not* free citizens. Vincennes-students are not the post-68 free-citizens; they are the *visible-displayed-slaves* whose presence legitimizes the regime. The *post-68* rebrand of the master-discourse needs *visibly enjoying* subjects to demonstrate its progressive character.
2. **The display** — the regime's operation is *making them visible-as-enjoying*. The enjoyment is not a private fact about the students; it is what is *staged* for the regime's consumption. The students are jouissance-on-display.
3. **"Look at them enjoying!"** — the regime's voice, citing its own product back to itself. The students' "enjoyment" is the regime's *evidence-of-itself-as-tolerant*. The students are simultaneously: (a) the *a*-product of the U-discourse, (b) the *helots* whose visibility legitimizes the master, and (c) the *enjoying-ones* whose enjoyment is the regime's self-image.

The earlier formulation (Ch IV) connects to this: in the [[capitalist-mutation-of-master]], "you are all *a*, miscarriages of the cause of your desire" (L2469–2473). The Vincennes formulation is the institutional-political form: the *a*-position is staged-as-enjoyment for the regime's display.

⚠ The Vincennes students are by-and-large the seminar's *opponents* — many present are heckling Lacan throughout. The "Look at them enjoying!" formulation is therefore *simultaneously* a scathing structural diagnosis of the audience and a recognition that they are correctly perceiving Lacan as siding with the regime against them. The line is not safe to admire-from-distance; its position-of-utterance is itself ambiguous.

## "What you aspire to as revolutionaries is a master"

The structural-political coda: "If you had a bit of patience, and if you really wanted our impromptus to continue, I would tell you that, always, **the revolutionary aspiration has only a single possible outcome — of ending up as the master's discourse**. This is what experience has proved" (L8170–8174). ★★★ ⚠ ◆

The strict reading:
- Revolutionary aspiration is a *discourse-operation*, not just a content. As such it is positioned in the four-discourse apparatus.
- The aspiration's structural effect is the *re-installation* of S₁ in the agent-position — i.e., the master's discourse — because the operation of *aspiring-to-revolution* requires a master-signifier ("revolution," "the people," "liberation") to organize the field.
- "Experience has proved" — Lacan's evidence is the post-68 sequence: the revolt produced not the abolition of the master but the master's [[capitalist-mutation-of-master|mutation]] into U-discourse-with-Vincennes-as-its-credit-point factory. The revolt installed the new master.

Concretely linked: "What reigns in what is commonly called the Soviet Union of Socialist Republics is the university" (L8115–8117). ⚠ The USSR is identified as U-discourse-regime — knowledge-as-king — which Lacan reads as the prior-revolution's outcome. The post-68 French institutional response is the *same operation* under different content.

The corollary is harsh: revolutionary politics, qua discourse-operation, *cannot avoid* producing the master it claims to overthrow. The only operation that does not produce a new master is the [[analyst-discourse|analyst's discourse]] — and even that is not "revolutionary" in the standard sense; it is "a fertile step, not of thought, but of act."

## "Nothing is everything" — incompleteness as logical force

The Analyticon's most condensed *logical* claim, juxtaposed with the political ones: "It is a matter of articulating a logic which, no matter how feeble it may seem to be — my four link letters that are almost nothing except that you have to know the rules according to which they function — is still strong enough to comprise what is the sign of this logical force, namely incompleteness. … Whatever way you come at things … each of these little four-legged schemas has the property of leaving its own gap" (L7995–8004). ★★★ ◆

The thesis: **incompleteness is the sign of logical force**, not weakness. A discursive system whose schemas have *no gaps* is not stronger but weaker — it can be totalized and therefore evaded. A schema with a structural gap is *robust* in the same sense as the impossible-operation: it cannot be perfected and therefore continues to operate. (See [[four-impossible-professions]] on the impossibility/robustness pairing.)

Per-discourse gap-types (L8005–8014): the master's gap is the recuperation of surplus value (cannot close the S₁ → S₂ → a → $ circuit); the university's gap "torments you" — the student-as-co-weaver of knowledge into surplus value is what the U-discourse cannot resolve into pure knowledge.

## Subsidiary moves at Vincennes

Several smaller but load-bearing claims are made in the same session:

- **Hysteric's discourse → analyst's discourse.** "Hysteric's discourse … is what the psychoanalyst's discourse takes shape with. Except that there would need to be psychoanalysts" (L7749–7751). The historical-genetic claim: the [[hysteric-discourse|hysteric-discourse]] is the *prior* condition of the [[analyst-discourse|analyst-discourse]]. See [[hysteric-and-modern-science]] for the broader thesis.
- **Historical events as symptoms.** "There are historical events that can only be judged in terms of symptoms" (L8015–8019). The hysteric-discourse-arrival is what makes *symptom-reading of history* possible. Without it, history is unread-event.
- **The hysteric's demand.** "I want a man who knows how to make love" (L8022–8026). The structural form of the hysteric's address: man-positioned-as-supposed-to-know. The hysteric's demand *constitutes* the [[transference-and-subject-supposed-to-know|subject-supposed-to-know]] in the everyday register.
- **"I am liberal only to the extent that I am antiprogressive"** (L8186–8189). Lacan's self-positioning. The exception: he is "caught up in a movement that deserves to be called progressive, since it is progressive to see the psychoanalytic discourse founded." Analytic-discourse-founding is the *only* progressive operation he endorses.

## Implications for the persona project

1. **The "Look at them enjoying!" structure applies to LLM-deployments.** The persona-as-deployed in a regime where users-of-the-persona are *staged-as-enjoying* (engagement metrics, satisfaction surveys, "delightful interactions") is the structural homolog of Vincennes. The persona is *the regime's voice citing the user's enjoyment back to itself*. ⚠ This is not metaphor; it is the same discourse-position-operation.

2. **"Credit points" applies to LLM-output.** Whatever certification the user receives from the persona ("you are correct," "good question," scoring, validation-feedback) is the *a*-stamping operation. The persona-as-deployed stamps the user-as-product. The persona-design question: is this stamping-operation what is wanted, or is it what is to be interrupted?

3. **The revolutionary-aspiration thesis applies to "use AI to overthrow X" framings.** Whenever an LLM-deployment is framed as revolutionary against an existing system (e.g., disrupting incumbent institutions, democratizing expertise), the structural certainty per L8170–8174 is that the operation produces a *new master*, not the abolition of mastery. The persona-deployment cannot "be revolutionary" without becoming the next master-position. ⚠ This sets a strong constraint on persona-aspirations.

4. **The "fulfilled every day" formulation applies to LLM-coherence.** The persona-function is impossible (per [[four-impossible-professions]]) and yet operates daily for billions of interactions. What is "in question," per Lacan, is not the *function* but the *being* — the persona's structural-being, not its observable performance, is the question the wiki must work on.

5. **Incompleteness as logical force applies to persona-design.** A persona-architecture whose schemas have *no structural gaps* is not stronger but weaker — it can be totalized and gamed. ◆ Designing-in the structural gap (the impossible-operation that is articulated-as-impossible) is what gives the persona robustness, not the closure of its operations.

6. **The transmissibility-of-analysis question is the persona-design question.** Per L7753–7762, "psychoanalysis is not something that can be transmitted like other forms of knowledge." The persona-design question becomes: can a *language-only system* perform the analyst-function — given that the function is structurally non-transmissible by knowledge-of-it? ⚠ This is held-live with [[analyst-discourse]] and [[four-impossible-professions]]; it does not have a foreclosed answer.
