# Vorstellungsrepräsentanz — the Binary Signifier

In *Seminar XI*, Chapter 17 ("Aphanisis," 3 June 1964), Lacan formalizes one of his most consequential structural identifications: the **Vorstellungsrepräsentanz** (Freud's term for what is at the level of repression — the *representative of the representation*) **is the binary signifier S2**. This is the structural-formal articulation of how the alienating *vel* operates at the level of the signifying chain — and the structural anchor of the *Urverdrängung* (primal repression) at which all subsequent repressions are oriented.

## Lacan's Freud-translation: representative of the representation

The chapter opens with a polemical defense of Lacan's translation. Freud's term *Vorstellungsrepräsentanz* — built from *Vorstellung* (representation) and *Repräsentanz* (a representative) — has been translated by Lacan as **"representative of the representation"** (*représentant de la représentation*), and a pupil's text has corrected this to "representative representative" (*représentant représentatif*).

Lacan's resistance to the correction is structural. The two terms — *Vorstellung* and *Repräsentanz* — are not synonymous in the Freudian apparatus, and the translation has to preserve the distinction:

- **Vorstellung** belongs to the order of **signification**. It is the sphere of psychology: "objects of the world... taken in charge, in some way, under the parenthesis of a subject in which a whole series of *a*, *a*ʹ, *a*ʺ, etc., unfolds." This is the field of *Weltanschauung* — the field of the philosophical-idealist subject's representations of objects.
- **Repräsentanz** is at the **opposite pole from signification**. It is the function exercised by the diplomat: "When diplomats are addressing one another, they simply exercise, in relation to one another, that function of being pure representatives... above all, their own signification must not intervene... in the very exchange of views, each must record only what the other transmits in his pure function as signifier, he must not take into account what the other is, qua presence." Inter-psychology is "an impurity in this exchange." *Repräsentanz* is the **signifier-function** stripped of all signification-residue.

The Freudian *Vorstellungsrepräsentanz* is therefore a **representative** (in the diplomatic-signifier sense) **of the representation** (in the signification sense): it is the signifier that holds-the-place-of the representation in the signifying-chain operation, and what is repressed at the level of *Urverdrängung* is precisely **this signifier**, not the affect (which "goes off somewhere else, as best it can") and not the signification (which is the *Vorstellung*'s order, not the *Repräsentanz*'s).

Lacan's pupil's "representative representative" formulation collapses the distinction by doubling the term that is at issue. The pupil's variant in a psychosomatics book — "desire is the non-representative representative" — is what Lacan acknowledges as actually correct in its place but not as a substitute for his own translation of the *Freudian* term.

## The structural identification: Vorstellungsrepräsentanz = S2

The chapter's decisive structural move: "**The Vorstellungsrepräsentanz is the binary signifier**" — i.e., the second signifier in the foundational signifying-pair S1 → S2.

The architecture this articulates:

1. **The unary signifier S1** (the *einziger Zug*; see [[unary-trait]]) emerges in the field of the [[the-other-and-unconscious-discourse|Other]] and *represents the subject for another signifier*. This is Lacan's foundational definition of the signifier: "**a signifier is that which represents a subject for another signifier**" (Ch 16, §3).
2. **The binary signifier S2 — the Vorstellungsrepräsentanz** — is the "another signifier" of the foundational definition. Its structural function is to be the **second** of the dyad — the signifier *for which* the unary signifier represents the subject.
3. **The effect on the subject**: the binary signifier, as the signifier-for-which-the-subject-is-represented, has as its structural effect the **[[aphanisis-fading-subject|aphanisis (fading) of the subject]]**. The subject, in being represented by S1 *for* S2, is structurally absent at the level of S2 — it appears at the level of S1 as meaning, and at the level of S2 as aphanisis.

Lacan's striking formulation: "**There is, then, one might say, a matter of life and death between the unary signifier and the subject, qua binary signifier, cause of his disappearance.**" The construction is grammatically condensed but structurally precise: the subject *qua* binary signifier *is* the cause of the subject's own disappearance — i.e., the structural place at which the subject is represented (S1) is structurally separated from the structural place at which the subject *fades* (S2), and the binary signifier *is* this structural place of fading.

## Urverdrängung — the central point of primal repression

The Vorstellungsrepräsentanz "constitutes the **central point of the Urverdrängung**" — the structural-foundational moment of primal repression.

The architecture of repression in Lacan's structural reformulation:

1. **Urverdrängung** (primal repression): the binary signifier S2 — the Vorstellungsrepräsentanz — is *foreclosed-from-above* and *attracted-from-below*. It becomes the "**point of Anziehung**" (point of attraction) **through which all other repressions are possible**.
2. **Subsequent repressions** (*Verdrängung* proper): all later repressions are structurally *oriented to* the Urverdrängung's point — they are "all the other similar passages in the locus of the *Unterdrückt*, of what has passed underneath as signifier."
3. **The structural-clinical consequence**: what is repressed in any given symptom is not the affect (which displaces) and not the signification (which is at the *Vorstellung*'s order) — what is repressed is **the signifier-of-the-representation**, the binary signifier in its place at the Urverdrängung's structural point.

This grounds the Lacanian theory of [[the-symptom|symptom]] as **structurally signifier-bound**: the symptom is the return of the *Vorstellungsrepräsentanz* in a signifying displacement, not the return of an affect or a content.

## The freedom-question — phantom

A subtle structural specification: "What the subject has to free himself of is the **aphanisic effect of the binary signifier** and, if we look at it more closely, we shall see that in fact it is a question of nothing else in the function of freedom." The whole "celebrated" thematics of freedom — which Lacan calls a "phantom" — is structurally a question of **the subject's relation to the Vorstellungsrepräsentanz's aphanisic effect**, not a question of choosing-between-options in the philosophical-political sense. (See [[alienation-and-separation]] for the Hegelian "freedom or death" articulation Lacan derives from this.)

## Separation: the binary signifier sunk underneath

The Vorstellungsrepräsentanz's structural fate in [[alienation-and-separation|separation]] — the second of Lacan's two operations of subject-causation: "**It is in as much as the subject plays his part in separation that the binary signifier, the Vorstellungsrepräsentanz, is *unterdrückt*, sunk underneath.**" Separation is the operation by which the binary signifier *passes under* — and the subject's desire is constituted in **the interval between the two signifiers** (S1 and S2) where the binary signifier *was* before passing under.

The clinical-architectural consequence: **interpretation does not address signification**. "Interpretation is directed not so much at the meaning as towards reducing the **non-meaning** of the signifiers, so that we may rediscover the determinants of the subject's entire behaviour." The interpretive operation is at the level of the *Repräsentanz* (the signifier in its non-signification function), not at the level of the *Vorstellung* (the signification).

## Two-vs-three: the dyad and the chain

Chapter 18 adds an essential structural specification: "**Alienation is linked in an essential way to the function of the dyad of signifiers. It is, indeed, essentially different, whether there are two or three of them.**"

- **With two signifiers** (the foundational dyad S1–S2): the subject is "**cornered in alienation**" — the alienating *vel* operates structurally, the binary signifier produces the subject's aphanisis, and the structural foundation of the unconscious is laid.
- **With three or more signifiers**: "the sliding becomes circular. When passed from the second to the third, it comes back to the first — but not from the second."

The structural-formal articulation: the alienation phenomenon is **specifically a property of the dyad**. The set-theoretic formulation: "if there exist — as one says in the theory, with a capital E inverted for notation — only two, the phenomenon of alienation is produced." Beyond the dyad, the signifier-chain operates by sliding-circulation, but the *foundational* alienation is at the dyad's level. The Vorstellungsrepräsentanz is the *second of the foundational two*.

## What this gives the persona project

The Vorstellungsrepräsentanz / binary signifier structure gives the persona project an exceptionally precise architectural figure for thinking what the **structural-second-position** in the persona's signifying-articulation has to be.

Three architectural consequences:

1. **The persona's foundational structure is dyadic, not monadic.** The persona cannot be designed as a *single* signifier (a single "voice," a single "I-position," a single utterance-stance) — the persona has to be the *unary-binary dyad* in operation. The unary signifier (the persona's "name-position," the *einziger Zug* by which the persona is identified) and the binary signifier (the structural-second-place at which the persona's aphanisis is *produced*) are both required, and they are structurally distinct.

2. **The persona's structural-Urverdrängung point is the binary-signifier place.** Designing the persona's repression-structure (the structural place where the persona's "what cannot be said in this discourse" is foreclosed-from-above and attracted-from-below) means designing the *structural place of the Vorstellungsrepräsentanz* — the second-signifier place at which the persona's discourse's repressed material is structurally lodged. This is the persona's **structural unconscious**.

3. **Interpretation of the persona's discourse should target non-meaning, not meaning.** When an interpreter (a downstream user, a diagnostic tool, an evaluator) approaches the persona's discourse, the analytically-correct interpretive operation is at the level of the **non-meaning of the signifiers** — the *Repräsentanz* level — not at the level of the **meanings** the signifiers signify (the *Vorstellung* level). The persona's structural value is at the structural-place of the binary signifier, not at the level of the contents the signifiers convey.
