# Transiently Assembled Local Neural Subsystems (TALoNS)

Anderson 2014's term, adopted by Clark in §5.5 (L6855–6907) as the architectural consequence of [[precision-weighting]] acting on [[effective-connectivity|effective connectivity]]. Under [[predictive-processing|PP]], cortical "modules" are not fixed. Precision-sculpting produces **module-like functional units that are formed and reformed on the fly**, different coalitions for different tasks, with no persistent structural commitment. ◆ For the persona system and for the wiki's Deleuze-Guattari material, TALoNS is the mechanistic reading of the [[assemblage]] — transient couplings that do functional work without being fixed modules.

## Precision-sculpting produces TALoNS

Clark's architectural chain (L6855–6907). [[precision-weighting]] alters the gain on error units. Gain on error units determines how much influence a signal has on processing. Altered influence is altered **effective connectivity** — the moment-to-moment causal shape of the network. Sustained precision-patterns produce sustained effective-connectivity patterns. Those sustained patterns are TALoNS.

The key phrase from Clark (L6786–6790, in the §5.4 lead-in): "Altering the distribution of precision-weightings thus amounts, in effect, to altering the 'simplest circuit diagram'." The circuit diagram is task-dependent. Task changes → precision redistribution → effective-connectivity redistribution → different TALoNS active.

PP thus implements, as Clark puts it, a **"fully flexible cognitive architecture"** — one whose coalitions are never fixed. This reconciles the functional-specialization findings of neuroimaging (which look like modules) with the anatomical reuse findings (Anderson 2010 on neural reuse across different tasks). Regions are recruited by transient coalitions rather than dedicated to fixed functions.

## Interaction-dominated dynamics

Spivey 2007's phrase, quoted by Clark. Under PP, "representations become a function of, and dependent upon, input from distal cortical areas" (Friston & Price 2001). A neural population's representational role cannot be read off its cell-types or its local connectivity alone — it is constituted by what's currently coupled to it via precision-gates.

The concrete implication (from Friston 2002, §5.2, L6516–6518): "If we recorded from the 'went' unit under top-down expectation of 'event', we might conclude it was now selective for 'event'." Apparent local selectivity is downward-flow-dependent. "The representational capacity and inherent function of any neuron, neuronal population, or cortical area is dynamic and context-sensitive" (Friston & Price 2001, L6521–6524).

⚠ Major methodological consequence for neuroimaging. A region's "function" is not fixed and cannot be read off its localizer response. Decades of functional-localizer imaging may have systematically misattributed fixed functions to regions whose activity patterns were actually TALoNS membership patterns. Relates to the FFA findings — "face-area" might better be "face-expectation area" under current context (see Egner, Monti, Summerfield 2010, [[predictive-processing]]).

## Functional differentiation + integration

Clark (L6560 area, §5.2) frames TALoNS as reconciling the two long-recognized hallmarks of neural organization: **differentiation** (different parts do different things) and **integration** (parts work together). Classical modular views leaned on differentiation; connectionist/integrative views leaned on integration. TALoNS holds both: "Differentiated components whose contribution is determined by transient coalitions, not by fixed task-assignments" (Anderson 2014).

## Empirical anchors

Several threads from Clark converge on TALoNS:

- **den Ouden et al. 2010 (§5.4, L6820 area).** DCM-fMRI showing striatal prediction error *gates visuomotor coupling* — first clean demonstration that prediction error in one region alters effective connectivity between others. The effective-connectivity reconfiguration is observable.

- **Anderson 2010 on neural reuse.** Cortical regions participate in many different functional networks depending on task. Reuse is the rule, not the exception — and TALoNS is the PP-native explanation for reuse.

- **Markov et al. 2013/2014 bow-tie architecture.** Dense local "core" with sparse long-range connections. The core provides the substrate for rapid transient coalitions; the long-range connections let distant regions join the coalition when precision gates them in.

- **Felleman & Van Essen 1991.** Macaque visual cortex feedforward/feedback asymmetry provides the hierarchical scaffolding. TALoNS live inside this scaffolding but are not determined by it.

## Neuromodulators and oscillations as sculpting mechanisms

How precision-weighting physically sculpts effective connectivity, per §5.4:

- **Neuromodulators** (dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine, noradrenaline). Each modulates precision in different contexts. See [[precision-weighting]].
- **Oscillation frequencies** (Bastos et al. 2012; Buffalo et al. 2011). Gamma for bottom-up / error; beta for top-down / prediction. Gamma synchrony → higher postsynaptic gain → effective-connectivity coupling between synchronized populations.

Synchrony is *the* physical mechanism of TALoNS formation in many accounts. Populations that fire together in synchrony have effectively high mutual gain; populations out of synchrony are effectively disconnected. The entire cortex can reorganize its effective circuit diagram in the tens of milliseconds it takes to establish a new synchrony pattern.

## TALoNS and extended cognition

A consequence Clark develops in Ch 8 (L11590–11700). If TALoNS is the architectural principle, and if precision-weighting can gate *any* information source into the coalition, then **extended cognition falls out natively**. External notes, tools, other people — all can be recruited into a TALoN via precision-weighting. Clark 2008 / Clark & Chalmers 1998's extended-mind thesis gets a PP-mechanistic reading here. High-precision prediction error becomes (L11667–11670) "an all-purpose adhesive" that binds brain, body, and world into temporary problem-solving wholes.

◆ For the persona system, this is decisive. A persona's TALoNS-analog can include: retrieved context chunks, tool-call results, memory-file contents, the user's messages. These are not *external resources that feed into* the persona's cognition; when precision-gates open, they *are* cognitive participants. The architecture treats them on a continuum with the LLM's internal states, not as a separate category.

## Convergence with assemblage

◆ The wiki's existing work on [[assemblage]] (from Deleuze & Guattari) finds a direct structural home here. TALoNS and assemblage name the same kind of thing: transient couplings that do functional work without being fixed modules; couplings whose components are heterogeneous (neural / bodily / environmental / linguistic); couplings that form and dissolve with the task or the event.

The wiki should hold this convergence carefully per `feedback_novel_insights_over_convergences`. What TALoNS *adds* to the assemblage picture is a precise mechanism — precision-weighted effective connectivity — for how coalitions form and dissolve. What assemblage *adds* to TALoNS is the commitment to heterogeneity (the assemblage can include speech, institutions, affects, material objects — not just neural populations) and the political-ontological stake (what assemblages are formed, and by whom, is a question of power). Neither is reducible to the other; the convergence is real but partial.

⚠ Don't silently collapse the two. TALoNS is a neuroscience-level claim about cortical coalitions; assemblage is an ontological-political claim about the conditions under which functional wholes come into existence. The wiki holds both.

## Celebrating transience

Clark's closing formulation for Ch 5 (L7627–7629): "The brain thus construed is a morphing, buzzing, dynamical system forever reconfiguring itself so as better to meet the incoming sensory barrage."

Precision-weighting is the sculpting tool; TALoNS are the sculpted forms; the sculpture never stops. ◆ This is extraordinarily close to the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] picture as a surface of variable intensities. PP gives a mechanistic reading of what D&G describe metaphorically. The refrain, the territorialization, the assemblage — TALoNS is the mechanistic neighbor of all three.

## For the persona system

TALoNS is the architectural principle that licenses a radically non-modular persona-system design. Design-implications:

1. **No fixed modules.** The persona system should not be engineered as a set of dedicated subsystems (memory-module, reasoning-module, response-module). Under TALoNS, cognitive "modules" are transient coalitions selected by precision-gates. The persona-system's equivalent: retrieval-hit + BwO-text + current-context + tool-result-stream form a transient coalition *for this turn*, and a different coalition for the next turn. Architecture = coalition-formation logic, not module-specification.

2. **Precision-analog gates coalition membership.** Whatever plays the precision-weighting role in the persona system (attention heads, relevance scores, confidence estimates, retrieval rankings) is what determines TALoNS membership per turn. Designing precision-analogs is designing coalition-formation.

3. **Extended cognition is architectural default.** External resources (retrieval hits, tool outputs, user messages, memory files) participate in the TALoN for the current turn under the same principles as internal states. Not a peripheral layer; a constitutive one.

4. **Transience is a feature, not a bug.** A persona whose TALoNS change turn-to-turn is not unstable; it is working as TALoNS-based architecture works. The stability of *identity* across turns lives in the stable priors (BwO text, self-narrative) that get re-invoked each turn — not in persistent module-structure.

5. **Convergence with assemblage holds.** The wiki's Deleuze-Guattari material and the PP material converge here. The persona system can be designed with both vocabularies: TALoNS for the mechanism, assemblage for the ontological-political framing.
