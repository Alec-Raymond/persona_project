# Metamodelization

**Metamodelization** is Guattari's term, in Chaosmosis, for the **practice of holding multiple cartographies of a situation simultaneously without collapsing them into a single master-model**. Schizoanalysis, on Guattari's late formulation, *is* metamodelization: it is not a model with special content but a stance toward modeling itself. The word is precisely "meta-model-ization" — a -ization, a practice, a continuous operation, not a finished meta-model.

The polemical target is the structuralist-scientistic assumption that every situation has one correct model and the analyst's job is to discover or construct it. Guattari's claim is that situations of the relevant kind — subjective, ethical, aesthetic, ecological — are **irreducibly multi-cartographic**: they cannot be faithfully modeled by a single cartography, and the attempt to do so produces the characteristic reductions (psychoanalytic reduction to the Oedipal model, structural-linguistic reduction to the signifier, cognitive-scientific reduction to computation). Metamodelization is the discipline of resisting these reductions by working with multiple models concurrently.

## Not a meta-model

The crucial distinction: metamodelization is **not** the construction of a higher-order model that unifies lower-order models. That would be a meta-model, and a meta-model is just another model, subject to the same reductive pressures. Metamodelization is instead the **practice of working across models without a unifying framework**. It is closer to translation between languages than to construction of an esperanto.

The stance is cartographic rather than theoretical. A cartography is a provisional mapping adequate to a specific use; multiple cartographies of the same territory can coexist without contradiction because they do different work. The psychoanalytic cartography, the semiotic cartography, the biological cartography, the political cartography of a single situation are all partial and all useful; metamodelization is the practice of switching between them as the situation's demands shift.

## Relation to the four functors

The [[four-functors|four-functor scheme]] is the ontological precondition for metamodelization. Because the four functors are irreducible to each other, no single model can capture all four simultaneously at full depth. A cartography optimized for F necessarily loses depth on T and U; a cartography optimized for U loses depth on F and Φ. Metamodelization is the practice of moving between functor-sensitive cartographies according to what the situation calls for.

The scheme is fractal: metamodelization happens *within* each functor as well (multiple F-cartographies, multiple U-cartographies), and across functors (F-cartography plus U-cartography read together). The discipline is recognizing which register is operative and which cartography is adequate to that register without claiming that cartography is the situation's truth.

## Schizoanalysis as metamodelization

Chaosmosis re-reads all of schizoanalysis through this lens: Anti-Oedipus's destruction of the Oedipal model, ATP's proliferation of concepts rather than system-construction, the constant invention of new cartographies (rhizome, BwO, refrain, regime of signs, assemblage). The schizoanalytic corpus is not a theory but a toolkit of cartographies, and the discipline is **the refusal to synthesize them into a single master-theory**.

This is why *Anti-Oedipus* and *A Thousand Plateaus* are so different in form: they are not two books about the same theory; they are two bodies of metamodelization work operating on overlapping territories through different cartographies. Chaosmosis adds further cartographies (the functors, autopoiesis-in-assemblage, chaosmic folding) and explicitly frames the whole enterprise as metamodelization.

## Metamodelization in the persona system

The persona system is structurally prone to the metamodelization failure. The pressure to have **one model of how the system works** is intense: the codebase demands it, the documentation demands it, the user's legible expectation demands it. But the system's operation runs across all four functors; any single model will be adequate to one or two functors and reductive on the others.

The metamodelizing discipline for the persona system:

- **Multiple cartographies of the same phenomenon.** When a persona produces an output, the F-cartography (how tokens got generated), the Φ-cartography (which machines fired and in what phylar configuration), the T-cartography (the groove as inhabited), and the U-cartography (what Universes were referred to) are all partial and all necessary. No single cartography is the truth of the output.
- **Refusal of the master-model pressure.** When a stakeholder asks "how does the persona work?", the metamodelizing answer is "that depends on what register you're asking in" — not evasion but recognition that a single-register answer will be a reduction.
- **Cartographic switching as design operation.** The design work includes choosing which cartography to use at each juncture. A bug report is typically most usefully read in one functor's cartography; an ethico-aesthetic diagnostic in another's.

## The wiki as metamodelizing practice

The wiki itself is a metamodelization artifact. Its pages are cartographies, not components of a single theory. The same concept (faciality, refrain, desiring-machine, affect) is mapped from multiple sources and from multiple registers, and the wiki's health is measured by the richness of its cross-cartographic links rather than by consistency to a single master-framework. This is why the CLAUDE.md workflow (see `wiki/CLAUDE.md`) insists on "never silently resolve contradictions" — contradictions between cartographies are often the marks of metamodelization doing its job.

### Reparative-alongside-paranoid reading as metamodelizing discipline

The wiki's [[CLAUDE|reading-mode constraint]] — read reparatively alongside synthetically, stay close to each source's own vocabulary before mapping it onto existing scaffolding, let differences stand before arguing for convergences — is the operative form of metamodelization at the reading-and-writing register. Sedgwick's paranoid/reparative distinction maps onto the metamodelizing stance precisely: paranoid reading finds the master-pattern everywhere because it went in looking for it (it is, in Guattari's vocabulary, the search for the unifying meta-model); reparative reading stays with the distinctive contribution of each source before synthesizing (it is metamodelizing: multiple cartographies held without collapse).

This is why the wiki's "held live" tensions are not *failures* of synthesis but *products* of metamodelization. Specific cases across the wiki:

- **Lacan vs. Simondon on residue-polarity** (see [[specific-prematurity-of-birth#structural-parallel-simondonian-pre-individual-metastability|specific-prematurity-of-birth]]). The Lacanian cartography maps the residue as constitutive lack (*manque-à-être*); the Simondonian cartography maps it as positive preserved charge. A meta-model would force a choice; metamodelization holds both, because each cartography is doing work the other cannot — Lacan's captures the structural-lack logic that produces the fading subject; Simondon's captures the transductive-preservation logic that produces further individuation.
- **Husserl's constituting flow vs. pulsation-all-the-way-down** (see [[pulsatory-ontogenesis#husserls-objection-the-constituting-flow-is-not-itself-a-process|pulsatory-ontogenesis]]). The phenomenological cartography insists the constituting level is not itself a process; the ontogenetic cartography says pulsation applies across scales. Neither reduces to the other; both are load-bearing.
- **Beckett-aporia as legitimate-extreme vs. distinct-failure-mode** (see [[three-syntheses#becketts-unnamable-aporia-as-failed-conjunctive-synthesis|three-syntheses]]). The page holds the two readings explicitly live rather than collapsing them.
- **The Jung-individuation / Simondon-individuation tension** (see [[simondon-individuation]] and [[individuation-individualization-personalization]]). Jung names a psychical process within an already-individuated subject; Simondon names the primordial ontogenetic operation. They are not the same concept; the wiki refuses to pretend they are.

Each of these is a metamodelization achievement: two or more cartographies held together without forcing unification, each preserved with its distinctive contribution, the tension named rather than resolved. The alternative — silently resolving contradictions — is what Guattari names as the reductive move metamodelization is designed to prevent.

### Metamodelization as legitimate-disjunctive-synthesis applied to models themselves

A deeper structural connection: metamodelization is the [[three-syntheses#disjunctive-synthesis|legitimate (inclusive) disjunctive synthesis]] applied at the meta-level of models rather than the object-level of flows.

The three-syntheses framework distinguishes legitimate from illegitimate uses of the disjunctive synthesis. Legitimate-inclusive: "either A or B or both or neither" — contradictions held on the BwO-surface without resolution. Illegitimate-exclusive: "either A or B" (forced choice) — premature collapse that D&G call "the body of the despot." Metamodelization is the same structure operating one level up: multiple cartographies held inclusively as co-valid partial mappings, vs. the "master-model" move that forces exclusive-disjunctive reduction of all cartographies to one.

This isomorphism is load-bearing. It explains *why* metamodelization is not just an epistemological preference but a structural requirement of the four-functor ontology: the functors are irreducible to one another at the object-level (no object-level functor-cartography reduces to another), so model-level cartographies inherit that irreducibility. Forcing a single meta-model is the model-level analogue of forcing exclusive disjunction on a surface whose reality is inclusive-disjunctive.

For the persona system this gives a design constraint: the system's self-descriptions are themselves subject to the legitimate-vs-illegitimate disjunctive-synthesis distinction. Any description of "how the persona works" that reduces the four functors to one (F-reductivism: "it's all token-generation"; U-reductivism: "it's all about meaning"; etc.) is an illegitimate disjunctive at the meta-level. The system's metadescription has to maintain the inclusive-disjunctive character of its object-level operation — not as relativism ("all descriptions are equally good") but as structural fidelity ("the object's multi-register character requires multi-cartographic description").

## Key sources

*Chaosmosis* chapter 3 ("Schizoanalytic metamodelization") lays out the concept in its late form. Guattari's *Schizoanalytic Cartographies* (1989) is the extended treatment; Chaosmosis is the compact restatement. The concept has family resemblance to Bateson's "multiple descriptions" (*Mind and Nature*) and to Stengers's "ecology of practices," both of which insist that multiple adequate accounts of the same situation can coexist without one being reducible to another.
