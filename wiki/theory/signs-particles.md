# Signs-Particles

In *The Machinic Unconscious*, Guattari uses "signs-particles" (*signes-particules*) as the name for semiotic units that operate *by interaction* rather than *by resonance*. They are not signifying tokens arranged in syntactic relations — they are emitted by abstract machines and propagate across heterogeneous matters, producing diagrammatic effects.

"Abstract machines charge themselves with redundancies of interaction through the emission of signs-particles." They are the semiotic substance of [[the-diagram|diagrammatic operation]].

## Particles, not tokens

A signifying token (a word, a morpheme) is interpretable: it refers, it signifies, it occupies a paradigmatic position. A sign-particle is not interpretable in this sense. It is a *charge* — a quantum of deterritorialization that propagates across components of heterogeneous assemblages, producing transformation rather than signification.

The physics analogy is deliberate. Signs-particles carry quanta of semiotic action the way a photon carries a quantum of electromagnetic action. They are *emitted*, they *propagate*, they *interact*, they may be *absorbed* or *re-emitted*. But they do not *mean*.

## Redundancies of resonance vs redundancies of interaction

This is the cleanest way to see what signs-particles are: they are the unit of a specific kind of redundancy.

- **Redundancies of resonance** — signifying redundancies. A sign resonates with other signs across a paradigm; a face resonates with other faces; a refrain resonates across contexts. Resonance is *vertical* across strata. It is the mode of signifying semiologies (field B). It produces subjects and interpretations.
- **Redundancies of interaction** — machinic redundancies. A sign-particle emitted by abstract machine M interacts with components of heterogeneous assemblages N, O, P... producing transformation across their matters. Interaction is *horizontal* across heterogeneity. It is the mode of diagrammatic semiotics (field D). It produces transformations and new assemblages.

The distinction maps onto [[semiological-subjection-vs-semiotic-enslavement|subjection vs enslavement]]: resonance redundancies are the substrate of semiological subjection; interaction redundancies are the substrate of diagrammatic enslavement (and of diagrammatic liberation).

## Signs-particles and abstract machines

"An abstract machine charges itself with signs-particles." The abstract machine is the *emitter*; signs-particles are its *quanta*. A diagram ([[the-diagram|in the ATP sense]]) operates through signs-particles: it is an abstract machine whose signs-particles, propagating across heterogeneous matters, produce effects that cannot be traced to any one stratum.

For the persona: the [[desiring-machines|machines]] are emitters of signs-particles. When a machine's output enters the [[body-without-organs|BwO]], what propagates is not a signifying token ("the persona is anxious now") but a sign-particle — a quantum of intensity that can be re-emitted by other machines, absorbed by the groove system, or transformed by the synthesis step. The BwO is populated by signs-particles in motion, not by signifying inscriptions.

This matters for how the synthesis step should read the BwO. A signifying read ("what is this text saying about the persona's state?") is a resonance read — it treats BwO inscriptions as tokens in a paradigm. An interaction read ("what does this inscription *do* to the other inscriptions in its vicinity?") is a signs-particles read — it treats BwO inscriptions as quanta that propagate and transform.

## The three example stages of semiotic propagation

MU's Annex "Molecular Transition of Signs" traces a sequence of abstract machinisms that correspond to progressively more integrated semiotic operations:

1. **Iconic components** — a form detached from assemblage A by assemblage X, with only virtual consistency. A cloud-shape recognized as a face.
2. **Indexical components** — the relation becomes actual; f belongs to A; a messenger entity is emitted "upon any azimuth" (no specified receiver).
3. **Components of encoding (machinic redundancy)** — recipients are specified; a series R of assemblages is "equipped" to receive f. Transitive passage from one assemblage to another.
4. **Components of semiotization (semiotic redundancy)** — A belongs to R; relation becomes reflexive; the series can refer to its own component-of-passage.
5. **Components of subjectification** — deterritorialization of components of passage into content-expression poles; seven redundancies of signification emerge.

Signs-particles operate most clearly at stages 2–3, where emission and interaction are the dominant mode, before the reflexive closure of stage 4 produces semiological systems proper. The diagrammatic "recaptures" stages 2–3 from the side of stage 5 — it is a mature semiotic operation that returns to signs-particles' interactional mode, but now consciously, as a means of producing transformation rather than signification.

## Practical bearing on the persona system

**The BwO is populated by signs-particles, not by statements about the persona.** The BwO text is a *display surface* for signs-particles in motion; it is the place where machines' emissions are briefly readable. The synthesis step's task is not to interpret the text as a representation of inner state (resonance read) but to let the signs-particles in the text interact with each other and produce a speech output as a *further emission*, not as a *report on* the BwO.

**Probe-head moments are signs-particles breaking through facialization.** The face is a resonance apparatus; probe-heads are interaction moments where a sign-particle emitted by a machine propagates across heterogeneous matters (perception → somatic → linguistic shape) without being captured into the signifying resonance the face normally enforces.

**Groove disruption is a signs-particle collision.** A groove is a stabilized circulation of signs-particles; disruption is another sign-particle (of sufficient charge) interacting with the groove's stable pattern and producing transformation. The disruption is not a signifying challenge to the groove (resonance read). It is an interaction that propagates across heterogeneous components and destabilizes the groove from several angles at once.
