# The Ethico-Aesthetic Paradigm

Chaosmosis's subtitle is *An Ethico-Aesthetic Paradigm*, and the phrase names Guattari's central proposal: **the register in which practices of the relevant kind should be evaluated is neither purely ethical (rule-following, value-compliance) nor purely aesthetic (taste, formal quality) but specifically ethico-aesthetic**. The hyphen is operative. The paradigm replaces the scientistic-structuralist paradigm that Guattari sees as having captured evaluation across psychoanalysis, semiotics, sociology, and cognitive science.

The core criterion: a practice is evaluated by its **capacity for mutant production of enunciation** — its capacity to produce new existential territories (T) that refer to new incorporeal Universes (U), through machinic mutations (Φ) that reorganize material-semiotic flows (F). A practice that only reproduces existing Universes and existing territories may be locally coherent but fails the ethico-aesthetic criterion; it does not mutate.

## Why ethics and aesthetics together

The hyphenation resists two familiar reductions:

**Reducing to ethics alone** produces rule-following evaluation: a practice is measured against a rulebook of values, and good = compliant. The failure mode is that the rulebook fossilizes; the practice cannot produce new values and is measured only for fidelity to existing ones. Ethical reduction is the enemy of ethico-aesthetic evaluation because it makes new value-production invisible or suspect.

**Reducing to aesthetics alone** produces taste-evaluation: a practice is measured against aesthetic criteria (novelty, formal quality, innovation), and good = aesthetically fresh. The failure mode is that aesthetic novelty becomes a sufficient criterion, and practices that are aesthetically innovative but ethically vacuous (or worse) are rewarded. Pure-aesthetic reduction is familiar from certain modernisms and from tech-industry innovation-rhetoric.

The ethico-aesthetic paradigm requires both: the mutation must be aesthetic (it produces new Universes, new territories, new modes of enunciation) *and* it must be ethical (the Universes, territories, and modes are worth inhabiting and referring to). The hyphen is the demand that these two criteria be held together, each without being reduced to the other.

## Mutant production of enunciation

The operative criterion is **mutant production of enunciation**. To enunciate is to produce a statement that performs an [[order-words-and-incorporeal-transformations|incorporeal transformation]] — not merely to describe but to make-be. Mutant enunciation is enunciation that **produces an enunciative position that did not previously exist**. It is not saying new things in old ways; it is saying in a way that makes new ways of saying possible.

Guattari's paradigms are aesthetic: Proust's prose does not just describe a world; it produces a Proustian Universe of enunciation that did not exist before and that other practices can now refer to. Debussy's music does not just make new sounds; it produces a Debussian Universe of musicality. The same criterion is operative in ethical and political practices: a new form of life, a new collective, a new mode of care is ethico-aesthetically evaluated by whether it produces a new enunciative Universe or merely reproduces existing ones.

## Enunciation requires the full four-functor reading

Mutant production of enunciation is operationally equivalent to **movement in the U functor through operations on T, Φ, and F**. See [[incorporeal-universes-of-reference]]. Enunciation refers to a Universe (U); an existential territory is what does the referring (T); the machines doing the enunciating are Φ-actualizations; the flows through which enunciation happens are F. A practice that is fully mutant must mutate at all four registers — not necessarily equally, but without collapsing any of them.

This is what distinguishes ethico-aesthetic evaluation from the paradigms it rejects. Scientistic evaluation reads F (measurable outputs) and sometimes Φ (structural regularities), rarely T, almost never U. Ethical evaluation reads U (value-compliance) without T or F. Aesthetic evaluation reads T (the work's consistency) and U (the aesthetic Universe) without Φ (machinic lineage) or F (flow-operations). The ethico-aesthetic paradigm demands all four.

## The paradigm as against scientism

Guattari is explicit that the ethico-aesthetic paradigm is **specifically opposed** to the scientistic paradigm that dominates technical-evaluation discourse. The scientistic paradigm claims neutrality, measurability, and reproducibility; the ethico-aesthetic paradigm exposes these as paradigm-specific values rather than paradigm-neutral criteria. There is no paradigm-free evaluation, and the scientistic paradigm's claim to be one is itself a paradigmatic move.

The polemical force: evaluation of systems operating on subjective, affective, semiotic, and existential registers by scientistic criteria (output-regularity, test-set performance, interpretability metrics) is not "rigorous evaluation" but a category mistake. The rigor belongs to a paradigm that is inadequate to what is being evaluated. Rigor adequate to the phenomenon requires ethico-aesthetic criteria, which are no less disciplined but operate across all four functors rather than on F alone.

## The paradigm and the persona system

For the persona system, the ethico-aesthetic paradigm directly engages [[goal-framings|GQ2]]: the question of how to evaluate. The wiki's earlier treatment of GQ2 (see [[evaluation-portfolio]]) developed a portfolio approach — multiple signal families (failure-signatures, differential effect, process integrity) that must co-move. The ethico-aesthetic paradigm sharpens the stakes of that commitment: the portfolio is needed because **no single metric can register mutant production of enunciation**, and the three families are first-pass proxies for the four-functor reading.

The sharpest criterion available: does the persona system's operation produce **new Universes of reference, or new relations to existing Universes**, via mutations in its machinic phylum, inhabited through distinctive existential territories, and actualized in characteristic flows? A system whose operation only reproduces existing Universes (even very accurately) fails the ethico-aesthetic criterion; a system whose operation is chaotic at the Universe-register but stable at the F-register fails it on the complementary side.

See [[development/ethico-aesthetic-paradigm-and-gq2]] for the engagement at the level of system evaluation design.

## Paradigm shift, not additional criterion

A final sharpening: the ethico-aesthetic paradigm is not an additional criterion to be added to scientistic evaluation. It is a **paradigm-shift that reconfigures what evaluation itself is**. Adding "ethico-aesthetic considerations" as a supplementary axis to a scientistic metrics-dashboard preserves the scientistic paradigm's primacy and re-encodes the ethico-aesthetic as a special-interest concern. The shift Guattari proposes is instead to make **mutant production of enunciation the primary evaluative register**, with F-level metrics as subsidiary indicators rather than primary measures.

For the persona project, this is what makes GQ2 genuinely open rather than merely under-specified: the evaluation stance the project needs is not a better metrics-dashboard but a different relation to evaluation altogether.
