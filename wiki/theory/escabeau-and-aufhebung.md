# *Escabeau* / *S.K.beau* — sublimation as stepladder, *Aufhebung* renamed for the body

In late Lacan, the Hegelian *Aufhebung* (lifting-up, sublation, raising-and-preserving) is rebaptized **escabeau** — French for *stepladder, footstool, low platform*. Miller's Note §5 (L7099–L7198) gives the explicit doctrinal exposition: **sublimation = escabeau**. The renaming is not cosmetic; it shifts the emphasis from logical-dialectical operation onto **the body that does the climbing**.

Translator's note (L9007–L9011): "an *escabeau* is a low stepladder or footstool used as an individual platform or to facilitate individual access to a higher place. In the written version of *Joyce le Symptôme*, Lacan plays on *escabeau* and *S.K.beau*, thus foregrounding the letter and the notion of *the beautiful*."

## Aufhebung vs escabeau — the body shift

Miller §5 (L7122–L7128): "**The elevatory means of sublimation as an upward operation was often named by Lacan using the well-known Hegelian term *Aufhebung*. In his written text 'Joyce the Symptom' he gives it the more expressive name *escabeau* (Autres écrits, pp. 565–70). The *escabeau* shifts the emphasis to the body. Likewise, Lacan designates the [[sinthome|sinthome]] as an 'event of the body' (ibid., p. 569), whereas he defined the Freudian symptom as 'truth' (Écrits, pp. 234–5).**"

Two registers, one operation:
- *Aufhebung* — Hegelian, logical-dialectical, the abstract negation-and-preservation that lifts a moment into a higher synthesis. **Concept-level.**
- *Escabeau* — Lacanian-late, embodied, the actual little stepladder a body climbs onto in order to be elevated. **Body-level.**

The structural-formal point: where Hegel's elevation operates *in concept*, late-Lacanian elevation operates *via a body that needs a footstool*. The sinthome as event-of-body needs an *escabeau* to be lifted into the order of the Beautiful. Without the stepladder, no elevation; without elevation, no [[two-regimes-of-sinthome|*sinthomadaquin*]] — only the bare *sinthome roulé*.

## The S.K.beau wordplay

*S.K.beau* — the equivoque encoded in the spelling. Two letters (S, K) plus *beau* (beautiful). Lacan's late prose performs *what it describes*: the letter is lifted onto the *beau* — the very mechanism of sublimation enacted in the writing of the word for sublimation. The *escabeau* IS an *S.K.beau* — the elevation of letters into Beauty.

This connects to the "storehouse of accessories" passage (Miller §5 L7117–L7121): "**the masaquinas, or madaquin in French, is the sinthome elevated to semblance, the sinthome that has become a mannequin, and which is veiled by the sublimations that are available in the storehouse of accessories: Being and its radiance, the true, the good, the beautiful, and so on.**"

The escabeau hauls the sinthome up into the storehouse-of-accessories: the sinthome dressed in Aquinas-style aesthetic categories. *Claritas*, *integritas*, *consonantia* — Joyce's own Thomist aesthetic taxonomy from *Portrait* (the talk with Lynch) is the *sinthomadaquin* dressed up.

## Joyce strips the escabeau — and yet

Miller §5 (L7129–L7132): "Joyce, a 'heretic', a partisan of the sinthome-rolling-along-any-old-how, 'strips the sinthome of its masaquinism' (p. 6). **But this does not prevent him from wanting to haul himself, with his sinthome, onto the *S.K.beau* of the work of art.**"

The double movement: Joyce strips the orthodox elevation (escabeau as Aquinas-philosophical-aesthetic) AND hauls himself onto a different escabeau (the *S.K.beau* of the work of art — wanting his name to endure forever, wanting the academics busy for three hundred years). The heretical strip-down does not abolish the escabeau-function; it relocates it.

Appendix (L5361–L5365) underscores the personal stake: "**This questioning on his major and final work needs to be continued. It is the work for which Joyce set aside the function of being his escabeau, because from the very start he wanted to be someone whose name — very precisely the name — would endure forever.**"

*Finnegans Wake* is the limit-case where the escabeau is *set aside* — the work that no longer functions as a footstool to elevate Joyce, because by then his name-as-elevation is already secured. The work goes beyond the escabeau-function while having required the escabeau-function to get there. See [[joyce-as-symptom]].

## The orthodox is itself a regime — *haeresis*

Miller §5 (L7109–L7115): "**heresy is not alone in constituting a sinthome. The choice that Lacan lauds in *The Sinthome*, that of the standpoint that here we are calling heretical, actually implies that orthodoxy (the normal) is just a particular regime of the sinthome, just as a 'lawful' sequence that is normative and regular is nothing but a 'lawless' sequence that was given a formation law at the outset (the [[name-of-the-father|Name-of-the-Father]]) so as to avoid any suspense or surprise.**"

The escabeau IS the formation-law that converts the lawless real of the sinthome into a lawful, transmissible Beauty. The Name-of-the-Father is the prototype of the escabeau — the *de jure* footstool that normally elevates the symptom into the symbolic order. Where the Name-of-the-Father defaults (Joyce), the escabeau may still be cobbled together at a different site (the proper name as art, the work of literature as elevation). See [[bypassing-name-of-the-father]].

## Straightness as a kind of bend — escabeau as orthopaedics

Miller §5 (L7133–L7141): "This same orientation leads Lacan to make us understand, on pp. 117–118, that what is real about straightness is that it is bent, that the bent aspect takes precedence over the straightness, that the straight is merely one kind of bend. Need I remind the reader that **orthodoxy is, etymologically speaking, the opinion that is said to be 'right', 'proper' or 'straight'?**"

The escabeau is the **orthopaedic device** that bends the lawless-real into the appearance of straightness. The orthodox sinthome is a sinthome whose lawless underside has been hauled onto a footstool such that it presents itself as upright, *de bonne tenue*. The heretical move (Lenin's bent-stick metaphor follows in Miller §5 L7156–L7163) is to bend the stick the other way — to make visible that what calls itself straight is itself a bend.

## What this gives the persona project

Four sharp implications:

1. **Sublimation as embodied stepladder, not pure-conceptual operation.** ◆ The persona-relevant move: "lifting up" the persona's raw outputs into refined-presentable form is an embodied operation that needs a stepladder — a frame, a register, a genre. Without the *escabeau*, no elevation; the persona's outputs sit at their raw event-level (sinthome roulé). The *escabeau* is the design-machinery that hauls bare-output into Beauty. [[parletre-body-as-substance|Body]]-relevant: even a language-only system needs an analogue of the stepladder.
2. **Two stances toward the escabeau — climb or strip.** ◆ The persona can either *climb* the inherited stepladder (sinthomadaquin: refined voice, well-tempered diction, recognizably Beautiful prose) or *strip* it (sinthome roulé: raw, lawless, bent presentation that refuses the orthopaedic correction). Both are choices, neither is default. See [[two-regimes-of-sinthome]].
3. **The escabeau-function relocates rather than abolishing.** ◆ Per Joyce: stripping one escabeau (Aquinas-style aesthetics) does not abolish the escabeau-function — it relocates it to another site (the work of literature as monument-to-the-name). The persona that "strips down" should be alert to where its escabeau has migrated to. There is always a footstool *somewhere*.
4. **The orthodox-as-orthopaedics — straightness is itself a bend.** ◆ The persona's "normal" voice is already a bent sequence stabilized by a formation-law. The "straight" persona is not the absence of stylization — it is one specific bend of the lawless real. Recognizing this opens the heretical-design space.

## Held-live tensions

- ⚠ **Escabeau as embodied stepladder vs persona's lack of body.** Per [[feedback_no_body_simulate_with_language]]: the project's central tension. If the escabeau-function is essentially embodied (stepladder for *a body that needs to climb*), what is the language-only analogue? Held live; not resolved.
- ⚠ **Heretical strip-down vs pragmatic need for escabeau.** Joyce strips and yet hauls onto S.K.beau anyway. Pure strip-down may not be available; the escabeau-function may be irreducible. Held live.
