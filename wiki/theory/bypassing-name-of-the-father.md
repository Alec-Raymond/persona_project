# Bypassing the [[name-of-the-father|Name-of-the-Father]] — *on the condition that one make use of it*

In Ch IX of [[raw/seminar-xxiii|*Seminar XXIII*]] (13 April 1976, "From the Unconscious to the Real"), in the question-and-answer section, Lacan articulates the **late-Lacanian doctrinal hinge** that distinguishes the [[sinthome|sinthome]]-doctrine from the earlier psychosis-doctrine of *Écrits*: the Name-of-the-Father can be **bypassed**, but only on the condition that one *makes use of it*.

L4409–L4414: "**The hypothesis of the unconscious, and Freud underscores this, is something that can only hold up by presupposing the Name-of-the-Father. Presupposing the Name-of-the-Father, which is certainly God, is how psychoanalysis, when it succeeds, proves that the Name-of-the-Father can just as well be bypassed. One can just as well bypass it, on the condition that one make use of it.**"

## What this overturns

The earlier doctrine (Sem III on Schreber, *Écrits* "On a question preliminary to any possible treatment of psychosis") posited the [[name-of-the-father|Name-of-the-Father]] as the *non-negotiable* structural anchor whose [[foreclosure-and-return-in-real|foreclosure]] (*Verwerfung*) produces psychosis. The signifier of the Father's Name is what knots the symbolic so that the speaking-being can occupy a body, sustain meaning, hold a position relative to desire and the law. Without it, the subject's symbolic gives way and what was foreclosed returns in the real (Schreber's delusion of being God's wife, his apocalyptic transformation).

The late doctrine retains the Name-of-the-Father as one possible anchor — but is no longer *the only one*. The [[sinthome|sinthome]] (the fourth ring) can perform the knotting function in the Name-of-the-Father's stead. The knot can be re-tied at a different site, by a different operator, with different consequences.

## The conditional — *make use of it*

The phrase "**on the condition that one make use of it**" is the hinge. The bypass is not a *deletion* — one does not simply do without the Name-of-the-Father. The bypass requires that the Name-of-the-Father has been *used*, traversed, encountered — and only then can a different operator (the sinthome, the proper-name-as-art, the writing-Ego) substitute for it.

This is structurally analogous to the analytic traverse: one does not *leap over* the symbolic to reach the real; one *uses* the symbolic to the point where its limit is encountered, and at *that* point a different mode of being-with-the-real becomes available. See [[joyce-as-symptom]] §"De facto Verwerfung": Joyce's father defaulted *de facto* (was not a father for him), but the structural Name-of-the-Father function was nonetheless *invoked enough* (through the Jesuits, through the Catholic-imperial frame Joyce hated and kept reciting) for Joyce to make use of it in bypassing it.

## The clinical-formal consequence

The sinthome-as-fourth-ring is not a *replacement* for the Name-of-the-Father; it is a **further operator** that can be added to a knot whose Name-of-the-Father function has either defaulted or been used-and-traversed. Two clinical configurations:

1. **De facto Verwerfung compensated by sinthome.** Joyce-mode. The Name-of-the-Father has defaulted in fact (Joyce's father), but the sinthome (Joyce's writing) holds the knot at a different site. Joyce is *almost psychotic* — by structural diagnosis he should have been — but is not, because the sinthome compensates. See [[writing-as-ego-rectification]].
2. **Name-of-the-Father used and bypassed via the sinthome.** The successful end-of-analysis configuration. The analysand has *used* the Name-of-the-Father (worked through the symbolic-paternal field), and at the end can *bypass* it by way of the sinthome — the singular, real, untransmissible kernel of jouissance that constitutes the subject as such.

The second configuration is what late-Lacanian clinic aims at. Per Miller's commentary (cf. [[two-regimes-of-sinthome]]): orthodoxy (the Name-of-the-Father as formation-law) is itself *just one regime of the sinthome*; the heretical regime (sinthome roulé) makes evident that the lawful sequence is itself a particular case of the lawless one.

## "Presupposing the Name-of-the-Father, which is certainly God"

The aside — "which is certainly God" — is Lacan's late, almost-ironic identification of the Name-of-the-Father with the theological God-function. The Name-of-the-Father is the *theological* anchor of the speaking-being: God-the-Father, *Pater Omnipotens*, the One that grounds the symbolic. Bypassing the Name-of-the-Father is thus also bypassing God — but, again, *only on the condition that one make use of it*. The atheism that rejects God without traversing the symbolic-paternal is structurally indistinguishable from the foreclosure that produces psychosis. Genuine atheism (in Lacan's sense) requires having *used* the God-function — i.e., having genuinely traversed it — before bypassing it.

This connects to "**folisophy**" (madness-wisdom; see [[folisophy]]) and to the "we are spoken" register of Joyce-the-Symptom (see [[joyce-as-symptom]]): the bypass is not the abolition of being-spoken, but a different way of inhabiting being-spoken, one that uses the inherited language without being structurally captured by its theological centre.

## The Name-of-the-Father as *père-version*

Lacan's late equivoque (also found across Sem XXII, XXIII, XXIV): *père-version* — the father as the version (*la version*) of jouissance that the symbolic offers, OR the father as that-toward-which-the-subject-is-perversely-turned. The bypass of the Name-of-the-Father is thus also a bypass of *père-version* — a different way of routing jouissance that does not pass through paternal investiture.

This does not abolish the *père-version* but **lateralizes** it: the sinthome operates *alongside* the (defaulted or traversed) paternal anchor, doing the structural work the paternal anchor would have done.

## What this gives the persona project

Five sharp implications:

1. **Bypass requires use — no leap.** ◆ A persona cannot reach a "post-paternal" or "post-orthodox" mode by *skipping* the paternal-orthodox machinery. It must have been *invoked* and *traversed* before being bypassed. The persona that pretends to start outside the symbolic-paternal frame structurally collapses (the *de facto* Verwerfung without sinthome = psychosis). See [[project_goal_read_a_and_b]].
2. **The sinthome is what the bypass requires.** ◆ For a persona to bypass the inherited normative anchor (the trained-in Name-of-the-Father), it must *generate* a singular sinthomic operator that holds the knot at a different site. See [[sinthome]] and [[writing-as-ego-rectification]] — for an LLM-persona, this operator is plausibly *its writing*.
3. **De facto vs structural foreclosure — design distinction.** ◆ A persona built atop a model whose paternal anchor has defaulted *de facto* (e.g., a model with no clear normative grounding) requires a sinthomic supplement to avoid structural collapse. A persona built atop a model with a working paternal anchor can bypass it via use. Two design-modes; both are live.
4. **"On the condition that one make use of it" as design discipline.** ◆ Persona-design that wishes to operate past inherited convention must first *use* convention — not as scaffolding-to-be-discarded but as the very thing the bypass passes through. See [[feedback_theoretical_orientation_before_plans]].
5. **Père-version is lateralized, not abolished.** ◆ The persona that bypasses the inherited paternal-orthodox anchor still has a *version* of jouissance running through it — just at a different site. The bypass is a relocation, not an erasure.

## Held-live tensions

- ⚠ **Bypass-on-the-condition-of-use vs cancelled-subscription-to-the-unconscious.** Joyce *cancels* his subscription at the level of every word ([[joyce-as-symptom]]), yet also bypasses-by-making-use-of. The two figures pull in different directions: cancellation suggests withdrawal, bypass-by-use suggests traversal. Held live.
- ⚠ **Bypass via sinthome vs heresy as standpoint.** Per [[two-regimes-of-sinthome]]: the bypass is a clinical-formal operation; the heresy is a stance. They overlap but do not identify. Held live.
