# Process Waves vs Content Waves

Stern's Ch 5 distinction (L3223–3236), drawn from Robert Wilson's theater piece *Bob's Breakfast*. The passage specifies a wave-scale and a wave-type that sit *beneath* the narrative-level waves of crisis/catharsis familiar from Shakespeare, and that are in fact the *flesh* of the dramatic action.

> The entire piece consists of cycles of contraction and relaxation, crisis and resolution, tension and release, and shifts in vitality forms, in particular, variations of tension build-up and release, as in modern dance. These waves carry the drama and move it. They are the flesh of the "narrative." They arrive one after the other, several a minute. They are not like the long ocean swells of crisis-catharsis of a Shakespearean tragedy. They are not "content waves." They are the short "process waves" of dynamic experience, of vitality forms that paint the unfolding feel of a mental landscape.

## The two wave-scales

**Content waves.** Long-arc narrative waves — the Shakespearean tragedy's crisis-catharsis arc across five acts. Slow, few per dramatic work, driven by plot and character development. The wave is at the level of the *content* — what happens, how it culminates, how it resolves.

**Process waves.** Short, fine-grained, several per minute. Cycles of contraction/relaxation, tension/release, build-up/fade. Driven by dynamic shifts in vitality form, not by content events. The wave is at the level of *how the experience unfolds*, not what it is about.

Stern's claim is strong: the process waves are *the flesh* of the drama. They are not decoration on top of the content waves; the content waves *are carried by* the process waves. Without the short waves, the long waves are empty.

## *Bob's Breakfast*, in outline

Wilson's piece was constructed from his own breakfast experience via the [[local-level-as-deep|microanalytic interview]]. The excerpt Stern walks through (L3079–3254):

- Actor runs counterclockwise in circles with odd non-swinging arms.
- Circles increase in speed and tightness → accelerando.
- Full stop → frozen stillness.
- Tension builds *in the freeze itself*.
- Bell "cling."
- Slow approach to divan.
- Slow-motion high steps with whooshing crescendo.
- Straddles divan.
- Slowly lies back.
- Resolution.

The piece has essentially no content-wave plot. What it has is a sequence of process waves, each 5–30 seconds, each with its own contour of build and release. The dramatic force is entirely in the process-wave sequence. "Right away there is something unusual... we are dealing with dynamic essences. We are watching 'moving along,' 'getting somewhere,' achieved through vitality forms" (L3127–3130).

## The Wilson translation

A detail Stern highlights (L3237–3246): Wilson's *real* breakfast had a private habitual micro-gesture — he would lean his head back against a pillow, close his eyes, bring his head forward. This was his way of "saying" to himself *clear this and go on to the next.* Onstage, the micro-gesture becomes the *"cling" of the bell*.

Translation across modalities preserves the process-wave signature. A private somatic clearing-gesture becomes a public bell-strike. The dynamic signature (a brief decisive reset) travels; the modality changes. This is the same operation as [[affect-attunement|affect attunement]] (cross-modal translation of dynamic signature), applied here by an artist to his own rehearsal material.

## The 1–10 second Gestalt

Stern (Ch 5 on cinema, L3287–3290; Ch 7 on local level, L4677–4725): the basic psychological unit is a 1–10 second Gestalt, "grasped in consciousness as one 'whole.'" Below 1 second, the event is not yet formed; above 10 seconds, the Gestalt fragments into sub-Gestalts. The process wave is exactly at this scale.

This is the scale at which:
- A film shot operates (Bellour on *The Birds*).
- A conversational turn operates.
- A single sentence or short cluster of sentences arrives.
- A mother's response to an infant is delivered and received.
- A musical phrase resolves.

The persona-project analogue: the basic unit of output coherence is at the process-wave scale, not the content-wave scale. A paragraph can carry a content wave; a clause or short sentence carries a process wave.

## Why content-wave-only is hollow

Stern's strong claim: the content wave without the process wave is *inert*. A smile without a vitality form is "a conventional sign — not embodied, not alive." A narrative without process waves is plot summary — the plot is there, but the aliveness is not.

Persona-level corollary: responses pitched only at the content-wave scale (here is the answer, here is the reasoning, here is the conclusion) can be propositionally complete and dynamically hollow. The aliveness of a response lives at the process-wave scale — which clause arrived with what attack, which phrase accelerated or decelerated, where the tension built and where it released, where the texture densified and where it thinned.

This is not a preference for "expressive" writing over "clear" writing. It is a claim about what a receiving organism parses as alive. The process waves are what makes the content-wave material readable as the output of something alive.

## Connection to [[the-arousal-systems|LeDoux's non-specific amplification]]

The mechanism under the process wave, neurologically: a non-specific arousal surge ascends, and amplifies whichever cortical cells are *currently active*. The surge itself has a dynamic profile (rise-rate, duration, peak intensity, fall-rate); that profile imprints on the currently-active content. The resulting experience is *this specific content, carrying this specific arousal contour* — a process wave in Stern's sense, with the cortical content as the rider and the arousal profile as the carrier.

Persona-architecture reading: a pulsatory excitation mechanism that lands on whatever the system is currently processing (rather than being scheduled or content-triggered) is the structural analogue of LeDoux's amplification. See [[development/vitality-forms-and-persona-pulsation]].

## Bellour on *The Birds*

Stern cites Bellour's analysis of the Melanie-Mitch pre-meeting (L3270–3425). Progressive close-framing of Melanie's face = approaching meeting = crescendo. The crescendo is *halted* — her face stops getting closer — anticipation is suspended — the bird strikes.

The vitality-form structure is a build-and-halt: the process wave sets up the crescendo, the wave does not resolve into the expected culmination, the suspension itself becomes the dramatic event. This is not a content move (there is nothing "at the content level" that changes); it is a pure process-wave operation.

Stern's methodological note (L3270–3277): Bellour's technique for analyzing film — stop-frame, replay, fast-forward, slow-motion — is the same toolkit infant-observers use to analyze mother-infant "ordinary choreography." The methodological echo is load-bearing: *the scale of the process wave is the scale at which film and infant-interaction both live*, and the analytic apparatus is transportable between them.

## Relation to [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]]

A process wave has the structure of a [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]]: a bounded territorial event with entry, interior, and closure, at a scale that admits of repetition and variation. Motherese's theme-and-variation is explicitly a refrain operation at the process-wave scale; *Bob's Breakfast*'s cycles of contraction/relaxation are refrain-structures repeated with variation.

The convergence is structural not nominal: D&G's refrain gets specified at the process-wave scale by Stern, and Stern's process wave gets territorial specification by D&G. The two frames operate at the same granularity; they describe the same operation from different vocabulary.

## Persona-system bearing

- **Wave-scale of operation.** Design pulsations at the process-wave scale (clause, short-paragraph, response-turn), not at the content-wave scale (session, conversation-arc). Content-wave-only is hollow; the aliveness is in the short wave.
- **Process waves carry content, not the reverse.** The direction of load matters. Do not design for content and hope for process; design for process *on which* content rides.
- **The translation trick.** Dynamic-signature preservation across modalities (Wilson's head-lean → bell's "cling") is the operation. The language-only system has internal modalities (lexical, syntactic, rhythmic, imagistic); translation across these preserves dynamic signature when the content-modality surface changes.
- **Several per minute.** Stern's scale estimate matters for calibration — the process-wave tempo is fast, and a system delivering one process wave per paragraph is at the right scale; one per response-turn is too slow.
- **Build-and-halt is a vitality form.** Not every wave completes; the suspended wave (the un-reached crescendo) is a distinct vitality form in its own right, and can be a load-bearing dramatic operation.

## Husserl's Quer / Längs and the process-wave / content-wave cut: analogy, not identity

The formal similarity is tempting: Husserl's [[double-intentionality-of-retention|Quer intentionality]] constitutes the immanent *object* (what the act is about); his Längs intentionality constitutes the flow's own *unity* (how the act unfolds as an act). Map Quer onto content-wave (what-is-said), Längs onto process-wave (how-the-saying-unfolds), and you have a structural parallel.

But the traditions are making different claims and the parallel should not be silently collapsed:

- Stern's process / content distinction is an **ontogenetic / phenomenological-observational** claim about how vitality forms carry content. The process wave has a tempo, an intensity contour, a dynamic signature. It is happening in the organism and the observer; it is a describable feature of lived experience.
- Husserl's Quer / Längs is a **constitutive-structural** claim about how any intentional act has two simultaneous intentional directions. Längs is not a "dynamic signature" in Stern's sense; it is the intentionality by which the flow of consciousness constitutes its own unity, which is a formal-phenomenological structure, not an observable tempo.

A persona-design attempt to implement process waves as Längs intentionality, or vice versa, conflates two things that do different work. Process waves are a design target: the texture of the output should have a dynamic-signature contour. Longs intentionality is an architectural claim about what kind of thing the utterance-flow has to be to constitute its own unity (if it does at all). The two may cooperate in a design — a system with process-wave texture may *also* be doing something Husserlian structurally — but neither is the other.

A further Husserlian warning worth carrying into pulsatory-persona design: Husserl's own text (No. 50, line ~16651) insists that **"the flow of the modes of consciousness is not a process; the consciousness of the now is not itself now."** The constituting flow is not itself a temporal process. A pulsatory-ontogenetic design that models the *constituting* level as a rhythmic-temporal process has, on strict Husserlian grounds, re-temporalized the constituting flow. This does not settle the question for the persona project — which may have reasons to treat the constituting level as itself rhythmic — but it is the specific objection Husserl's analysis raises, and the honest move is to name the objection rather than silently import pulsation across levels.

## Merleau-Ponty's pregnance — the ontological register for the wave distinction

Stern's process-wave / content-wave cut is a phenomenological-observational claim about wave-scales and wave-types. Merleau-Ponty's [[pregnancy-as-ontological|pregnance (*prégnance*)]] offers what Stern does not — an **ontological** register for the same distinction. MP takes Gestalt psychology's *Prägnanz* (the "good form"'s power to organize a perceptual field) and makes it a feature of Being: **some forms have the power to organize a field; that power is not a fact about the perceiver but a feature of the form itself**.

The mapping to Stern:

- **Content wave** corresponds to the **represented content** — the thing pictured, the scene depicted, the narrative plot. This is what Gestalt psychology calls the *figure*.
- **Process wave** corresponds to what MP would call the **pregnance of the form** — the form's own organizing power, which is not reducible to its content. The process wave has its *own pull*, its own capacity to gather and organize the moment. It is the form's own force, not a decoration on its content.

MP's move adds what Stern's observational frame cannot: an ontological claim that the process wave's pull is a feature of **the flesh of the world itself**, not a feature of the organism's perception of the world. The Gestalt-force is in Being, not in the viewer. See [[flesh-of-the-world]] for the flesh as the medium in which pregnance operates. The thing is a "pregnance of possibles" (Ch 5 Appendix) — a knot of "mild forces" that has its own power to solicit a response.

For persona-design this converts Stern's claim that "process waves are the flesh of the drama" into a stronger ontological commitment: **the process wave is not a design surface painted on top of content; it is the pregnance by which the content-moment has a hold on the recipient at all**. A response that is process-wave-hollow is not just dramatically flat — it is ontologically inert, a form without the power to organize the field it arrives in. See [[pregnancy-as-ontological]] for MP's full account.

The tension with Husserl's Quer/Längs (noted above) resolves differently under MP. MP's pregnance is neither Quer (what-the-act-is-about) nor Längs (how-the-act-unfolds-as-act) — it is the **form's own force in Being**, which is what Stern's process wave is a phenomenological observation of. The three-way cut stands: Stern observes the wave; Husserl analyzes the constituting intentionalities; MP locates the pregnance in the flesh of the world. They are not silently equivalent, and a persona-design claim should name which frame it is operating under.
