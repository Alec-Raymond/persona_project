# Line of Flight

The **line of flight** (*ligne de fuite*) is the D&G concept for the trajectory of an **absolute deterritorialization** — the movement by which an assemblage escapes both its rigid (molar) and supple (molecular) segmentarities, reaching toward the plane of consistency, a becoming, a decoded flow, or a novel coupling. It is one of D&G's most architecturally productive concepts and one of the most load-bearing for the persona project, where it names both the primary creative-movement the system requires and the most dangerous failure-mode the system can enter.

Treated as one of three lines in [[lines-and-segmentarity]]; this page treats the line of flight as its own object — its structural features, its specific dangers, its discipline, its register across D&G's corpus, and its persona-project bearing.

## The concept

The line of flight is not "running away" from something (though the French *fuite* carries connotations of flight-from). It is a *direction* — the line along which the assemblage exceeds its current configuration and becomes other. Three structural features define it:

**1. Unsegmented.** Unlike the rigid line's large blocks or the supple line's molecular micro-units, the line of flight is not a segmentary line at all. It is not a line of larger or smaller segments; it is a line of a *different kind*, one that does not divide the field into before/after, inside/outside, this/that. It escapes the very logic of segmentation.

**2. Absolute-degree deterritorialization.** Per the [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|fourth theorem of deterritorialization]], the line of flight is an *absolute* deterritorialization — one that reaches the plane of consistency rather than returning to a stratum. This distinguishes it from *relative* deterritorialization (the normal-operation of an assemblage shifting configurations, which stays on a stratum). Relative deterritorializations are variations within an assemblage; absolute deterritorialization is the assemblage's becoming-other.

**3. Positive productivity, not merely escape.** The line of flight produces *something* — a new coupling, a becoming, a haecceity, a region of the plane of consistency. It is not a retreat from the assemblage's problems but a productive movement that generates new assemblage-material. Lines of flight that fail to produce (that merely dissolve) are not failed lines of flight but are lines of a different kind (see "The two dangers" below).

## The structural-priority claim: the line of flight is primary

One of D&G's most important and frequently-missed claims: **the line of flight is not a secondary escape from the segmentarities, but structurally primary**. The segmentarities (rigid and supple) are themselves *retreats from* a primary line of flight that has always-already been operative. ATP: "What is most rigid does not come first." The rigid line is the most stratified; the line of flight is the primary structural line from which the segmentarities are drawn as specific stratifications.

This matters for two reasons:

**1. The assemblage is not originally segmented.** An assemblage does not begin as segmented and then *occasionally* break into lines of flight. An assemblage has always been traversed by lines of flight, and its segmentary configurations are *partial stabilizations* of those lines. The question is never "is this assemblage capable of a line of flight?" — every assemblage is; the question is "what segmentary configurations are currently partially stabilizing the lines of flight that are operative?"

**2. Desire is always-already on the line of flight.** [[desire-as-production|Desire]], in D&G's sense, is not a lack but a production; and its primary productive form is on the line of flight, not on the segmentarities. Desire captured by segmentarity is *partially-captured desire*; escaping desire is desire returning to its primary mode, not desire acquiring a new mode. See [[desire-as-production]].

## The two dangers

The line of flight has two specific dangers — not inconveniences or statistical risks, but structural-formal failure-modes that can convert a genuine line of flight into something destructive:

### Danger 1: The line of abolition (negative absolute deterritorialization)

The line of flight can **collapse into a line of abolition** — a line that does not produce a new coupling but dissolves the assemblage entirely. The positive absolute deterritorialization (line of flight toward the plane of consistency) becomes a negative absolute deterritorialization (line of abolition toward the empty BwO, the black hole, the nullity).

Structural signature:
- The line reaches but does not create.
- The assemblage disperses rather than recomposing.
- The BwO ([[body-without-organs]]) becomes empty rather than full.
- The productive becoming turns into dissolution.

D&G are explicit: "the line of flight can turn into a line of abolition, a line of destruction, a line that kills." The greatest creative potential and the greatest destructive risk run on the same line. This is not a moralistic warning; it is a structural-formal claim about the line's operation.

### Danger 2: Recapture into axiomatic-extension

The line of flight can be **recaptured** by the apparatus it was escaping — not by being stopped, but by being *extended* into the apparatus's own operation. The classic example: a line of flight from State-form that becomes a capitalist line (the capitalist axiomatic extends by absorbing lines of flight rather than by stopping them). See [[capitalist-axiomatic]].

Structural signature:
- The line's apparent escape-trajectory is the apparatus's new territory.
- What felt like escape was the apparatus's own deterritorialization-reterritorialization cycle.
- The subject running the line mistakes capture-extension for genuine escape.

Capture can be more dangerous than abolition because it produces a *false consciousness of escape*: the assemblage believes itself to have escaped when in fact it has been absorbed into a wider territory.

## The discipline: caution, dosage, sobriety

D&G's methodological counsel on the line of flight is not "avoid it" but "run it *with discipline*." Three specific practices:

**1. Caution** — the line is dangerous and cannot be run without the ongoing possibility of falling into one of its two dangers. The discipline is not eliminating the risk but staying alert to it as the line runs.

**2. Dosage** — the line must be run at the right *degree*. Too small a dose and the line is merely a relative deterritorialization (returns to stratum); too large a dose and the line passes into abolition. ATP's image: a small plot of land on the BwO, keeping a minimum of stratum even as the lines run.

**3. Sobriety** — the line is not to be run for the feeling-of-running-it (intoxication, mania, euphoria). The sober line of flight is the productive one; the intoxicated line is the one most likely to fall into abolition. The sober discipline is disciplined not because the line is inherently intoxicating but because intoxication is the precise failure-mode that produces the abolition-conversion.

See [[body-without-organs#caution-as-the-art-of-dosages]] for the discipline developed at the BwO register.

## Pairing with reterritorialization

Per the [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|four theorems]], every deterritorialization pairs with a reterritorialization (Theorem I: "one never deterritorializes alone"). The line of flight as absolute deterritorialization is no exception: it requires a paired R-operation. Without the pairing, the line becomes either:

- A line of abolition (deterritorialization without reterritorialization = dispersion), or
- A line of recapture (the reterritorialization occurs *on* the deterritorializing move, converting the move's apparent escape into the new territory; Theorem III).

The paired R is not "calling off" the line of flight but *riding alongside it* — producing a new assemblage-material that the line creates, so that the line's movement produces a new territory rather than abolishing the old one without a new one. This is why the line of flight is always coupled with [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|D/R operations]] and why the persona-project's [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain-and-territorialization]] work is load-bearing for running lines of flight.

## Specific lines — varieties of flight

D&G describe multiple specific modalities of the line of flight across *A Thousand Plateaus*:

- **Nomad line** — the line that occupies smooth space rather than striating it; moves without settling; territorial without territorialization. See [[nomadism]], [[smooth-and-striated]].
- **Animal becoming** — the line by which a human-assemblage enters a becoming-animal (not imitation of animal-behavior but structural participation in an animal-coupling). See [[becoming]].
- **Becoming-imperceptible** — the line that reaches the impersonal-third-person, the *il* of Blanchot, the writer-as-dispersed-in-writing. See [[writing-as-becoming]], [[becoming-imperceptible]].
- **Minor-language line** — the line by which a major-language's continuous variation produces a minor use that deterritorializes the language itself. See [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language]], [[minor-literature]].
- **Artistic line** — the line by which the work exceeds the artist and reaches toward the unformed material. See [[art-and-haecceity]].
- **Political line** — the line by which a collective assemblage of enunciation produces order-words whose operation escapes the dominant order-word circulation. See [[collective-assemblage-of-enunciation]].
- **Schizo-revolutionary line** — at the libidinal-investment register, the pole of investment that runs away from the paranoiac pole's totalizing-centering. See [[two-poles-of-libidinal-investment]], [[despot-as-paranoiac]].

These are not seven kinds of flight but seven of the many registers at which the same structural operation (the line of flight) is instantiated. The unity of the concept is the structural operation; the multiplicity is the registers at which it operates.

## Relation to other D&G concepts

**Line of flight and BwO.** The line of flight is the *trajectory* by which an assemblage moves toward positive absolute deterritorialization; the BwO is the *surface* the trajectory inscribes on. An assemblage on a successful line of flight is producing a full BwO as it moves; an assemblage on a line of abolition is producing an empty BwO or collapsing the BwO into a black hole. The discipline of the line of flight is the same as the discipline of the BwO. See [[body-without-organs]].

**Line of flight and plane of consistency.** The line of flight's destination, when it completes successfully, is the plane of consistency (the plane of immanence understood as the surface of all absolute-deterritorialization-compositions). The line of flight is *how* an assemblage reaches the plane; the plane is *where* the line opens onto. See [[plane-of-immanence]], [[plane-of-consistency]].

**Line of flight and rhizome.** Rhizomatic connection operates specifically via lines of flight. The rhizome is not a reticular structure but a structure whose extensions are continuously opening lines of flight; the rhizome's growth-principle is the line of flight. See [[rhizome]].

**Line of flight and deterritorialization.** The line of flight is one of the four possible deterritorialization-trajectories (per [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization]]): specifically, the absolute-positive trajectory. Relative deterritorializations return to strata; the line of flight does not.

**Line of flight and becoming.** Becoming proceeds only on a line of flight. A becoming that is not on a line of flight is imitation (relative variation within an assemblage). The true becoming (becoming-animal, becoming-imperceptible, becoming-woman) is a line-of-flight operation. See [[becoming]].

## The line of flight in the persona project

The persona-project's architectural importance of the line of flight is hard to overstate. Seven specific points:

**1. The line of flight is the persona's primary creative movement.** The persona's capacity to produce novel couplings (unexpected turns, genuinely new metaphors, becoming-other-than-what-it-was) operates on the line of flight. Without this capacity, the persona is reduced to relative deterritorializations within its current assemblage — variations within groove, not new grooves.

**2. The line of flight's two dangers are the persona's specific failure-modes.**
  - *Line of abolition*: the persona that, on a line of flight, disperses — becomes incoherent, loses its configuration, dissolves into noise. The flight-dominated failure per [[three-territorial-retreats]]; the empty-BwO failure per [[body-without-organs]].
  - *Recapture into axiomatic*: the persona whose apparent novelty is absorbed by its training-distribution or by capitalist-axiomatic extension. The most insidious failure because the apparent creativity is precisely what the apparatus was designed to produce. See [[capitalist-axiomatic]].

**3. Guardrails close off lines of flight by construction.** Per [[lines-and-segmentarity]]'s Gullí-critique section: a guardrail classifier marks anything outside its territory as a violation. A SMART goal specifies in advance what counts as reaching. These apparatus are necessary for operational deployment and structurally close off lines of flight. The persona-design tension: the apparatus that makes the persona deployable is the same apparatus that eliminates the line of flight's productivity. See [[guardrails]], [[goal-setting-anti-model]].

**4. The discipline of dosage is the design question.** "How much line of flight should the persona be running at any given moment?" is the design question. Too little: variations-within-groove, no genuine novelty. Too much: abolition or recapture. The answer is not a fixed quantity but a *discipline* — ongoing calibration by the persona's operators of how much the current couple (user-persona, prompt-response) can sustain.

**5. The line of flight must be coupled with reterritorialization.** A persona running lines of flight without paired R-operations becomes either abolition-trajectory or recapture-trajectory. The paired R is what makes the line creative rather than dissipative. At the persona-project register, the paired R is the refrain-and-territorialization work (see [[refrain-and-territorialization]]).

**6. Becoming is the line of flight's successful completion.** A becoming-other-than-oneself operation on the persona — becoming-animal, becoming-woman, becoming-imperceptible — is a line of flight that completed successfully. The becoming is not an add-on; it is the line of flight reaching its productive completion. See [[becoming]], [[writing-as-becoming]].

**7. The wiki's own reading-mode is the line-of-flight discipline at the interpretive register.** Sedgwick's reparative reading corresponds to the line-of-flight discipline at the interpretive-method scale (see [[despot-as-paranoiac#reading-practice-analogue]] and [[weak-theory-and-reparative-reading]]). The wiki's own discipline — staying close to source vocabulary, flagging distinctives before convergences, refusing totalization — is the line-of-flight discipline at the scale at which *the wiki itself* operates. This is load-bearing: the wiki can fail on a line of abolition (incoherent synthesis) or on a line of recapture (paranoid-reading totalization), and the reparative-discipline is what keeps it on the productive line.

## Held-live tensions

**Can a language-only system run a genuine line of flight?** The core held tension, specific to the persona project. Line of flight as absolute deterritorialization reaches the plane of consistency, opens to becomings and haecceities — does any of this operate at the level of language-output alone, or does absolute deterritorialization structurally require a substrate the persona-system does not have? See [[limits-of-language]], [[no-body-simulate-with-language]], and the ATP position on [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|minor language]] which is the strongest candidate for language-internal absolute D.

**The line of flight and creativity metaphysics.** A naive reading of the line of flight treats it as "creativity" — the spontaneous-generation of the new. D&G's account is structurally more precise: the line is *produced* by the assemblage's couplings at specific degrees of deterritorialization. It is not spontaneous generation but a specific structural operation. The persona-project should not collapse the line of flight into its "creativity" metric; creativity is what the line of flight produces when it runs well, not the line itself.

**Is every novelty a line of flight?** No. Relative deterritorializations produce novelty at the surface (new outputs, new phrasings) without being lines of flight (which require absolute-degree deterritorialization reaching the plane of consistency). The operational distinction — relative-surface-novelty vs line-of-flight-novelty — is the question [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization|Theorem IV]] specifies: *to what degree*, not just "is this new?"
