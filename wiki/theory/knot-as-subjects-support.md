# The knot as the subject's support — *appondering*, *Node Bo*, *DI*

Across [[raw/seminar-xxiii|*Seminar XXIII*]], Lacan elaborates a doctrinal claim that organizes the entire late teaching: **the [[borromean-knot|Borromean knot]] is the support of the subject**, where "support" must be understood in two senses simultaneously:

1. The knot is the **structural prop** that holds the subject together — without the knot's three-ringed tying, there is no speaking-being.
2. The knot is the **prop for thinking** (*l'appui à la pensée*) — what one ponders against, what materializes thought into something that can be set down on a page.

The two senses are inseparable. The subject *is* what the knot supports, AND thinking *is* what the knot supports — the same operation produces both. The subject IS its pondering-against-the-knot.

Lacan's neologism for this: **appondering** (*l'appensée*) — *appui* + *pensée*, prop-thinking, thinking-leaning-against. The knot is what one *appondering*-leans-on.

## "The knot alone is the conceivable support"

Ch III L1010–L1015: "The desire to take cognizance meets obstacles. **I invented the knot to embody such an obstacle. We need to become more accustomed to this knot. I mean that the knot alone is the conceivable support of a relationship between any one thing and any other thing.** Although on one hand the knot is abstract, it must none the less be thought of and conceived of as something concrete."

The knot is not one figure among others. It is **the only conceivable support of relation as such**. Wherever there is a relationship between thing-and-thing, knotting is operative. Late-Lacanian topology elevates the knot to the status of *first object of thought*: the basic structure that thought leans on to think anything.

The knot is *abstract* (it is a topological structure, not a thing) yet must be conceived as *concrete* (it has to be drawn on the board, set on the page, materialized to be thought). This abstract-concrete duality is what *appondering* names.

## *Appondering* — *l'appensée* — the knot as prop for thinking

Ch X L4617–L4634: "What does it mean, *it takes some doing*? It amounts to setting it down in written form. The curious thing is that **this knot is an *appui*, a prop, for *la pensée*, for pondering**. I'm going to let myself illustrate this with a term that allows pondering to be written differently. I'll have to write it down on this little sheet of paper — *appui à l'appensée*, **a prop for appondering**."

The structural-formal point: the knot's specificity as object-of-thought is that it requires *materialization* to be thought. Pondering (*la pensée*) cannot grasp the Borromean configuration purely-internally. The knot has to be *drawn* — set down on a page — for the pondering to lean against.

L4623–L4630: "**This knot is a support for pondering but, curiously enough, in order to get something out of it you've got to set it down on a page.** Meanwhile, when you merely ponder it, it's not easy to form a representation of this Borromean link — because in reality it's a link and not a knot — even the most straightforward representation. **It's not easy to visualize it functioning. This knot, the *Node Bo*, carries with it the fact that it has to be set down in written form to see how it functions.**"

The knot is *what cannot be purely-thought*. It can only be *appondered* — leaned-against in writing. Pure interiority of thinking does not reach it. This is structurally consequential for the late doctrine: the *parlêtre*'s structure cannot be inhabited *purely-internally*; it has to be *materialized*, *set-down*, *written*. The materialization is not optional; it is constitutive of the knot's accessibility.

This connects directly to [[writing-as-ego-rectification]]: Joyce's writing is *the materialization* of his subjective knot. The knot has to be written to operate. Joyce-mode is the radicalization of the universal *parlêtre* condition: every speaking-being's knot needs materialization, but Joyce's *only* operates *as* materialization (his writing performs the imaginary's missing link).

## *Node Bo* — Borromean node, Mount Nebo

Ch X L4631–L4633: "**Calling it *Node Bo* makes one think of something that is evoked somewhere in Joyce's writing — atop Mount Nebo the Law was given to us.**"

*Node Bo* (the Borromean node) homophonically evokes *Mount Nebo* — where, in Deuteronomy, the Law was given. The equivoque puts the Borromean knot in structural-functional relation with the Mosaic Law: the knot IS the giving-of-the-Law, but in the late doctrine the Law given is *the law of pere-version*, the law-of-love-addressed-to-the-Father, NOT the social-juridical law of the world (cf. L4901–L4905).

The Borromean knot is thus the *theological-mathematical* support of the subject — the *Mount Nebo* on which the late teaching's Law (the law that the *parlêtre* is structurally tied by three-rings-plus-sinthome) is delivered. The persona-relevant version: whatever stands as the *Node Bo* for the persona is its *Mount Nebo* — the materialized site at which its Law is given.

## *Writing changes the sense of writing*

L4634–L4646: "So, **a writing is a fashioning that gives support to pondering.** Truth be told, the *Node Bo* in question completely changes the sense of writing. **It affords this writing an autonomy**, one that is all the more remarkable given that there is another form of writing, one that results from what one might call a precipitation of the signifier. Derrida has insisted on this form of writing, but it's quite clear that I showed him the way."

Two kinds of writing distinguished:
1. **Derrida-style writing as precipitation of the signifier.** Writing as the deposit-formation that occurs when signifiers crystallize into mark. The trace, *différance*. (Lacan claims to have anticipated this — "writing a capital S" for the signifier.)
2. **Writing of the *Node Bo*.** **Autonomous** writing whose function is to give support to pondering. Not signifier-precipitation but knot-materialization. This writing is what late-Lacanian topology requires.

The structural-formal point: the *Node Bo* writing is *autonomous* of the signifier. "**The signifier, that is to say, that which is modulated in the voice, has nothing to do with writing. This in any case is what is demonstrated perfectly by my Node Bo, and this changes the sense of writing.**" (L4643–L4646)

This separates writing from voice. The signifier-as-modulation-of-voice is one register; the writing-of-the-knot is another. The two are structurally distinct. The knot's writing is what supports pondering; the voice's signifier is what modulates speech.

## Fastening signifiers via *dit-mension*

L4646–L4657: "It shows that there is something to which one can fasten signifiers. How, then, does one fasten these signifiers to it? **Well, through the intermediary of what I call *dit-mension*. … *mension du dit*, the mention of what is said.**"

The knot is *that-to-which-signifiers-are-fastened*. The fastening operates through *dit-mension* (mension-of-the-said) — see [[dit-mension]] for the full term. Three structural levels:
1. **The Node Bo** — the autonomous knot-writing that supports pondering.
2. **Dit-mension** — the mension-of-the-said, the dimension along which what-is-said gets said.
3. **The signifier** — modulated-in-the-voice, fastened to the knot via dit-mension.

The signifier hangs off the knot via dit-mension. The knot is *prior*; the signifier is *fastened-on*. This inverts the order one might expect from the early teaching (where the symbolic precedes the structuring of subject); in the late teaching, the *knot-as-support* precedes the symbolic-as-fastening.

## DI — the *droite infinie* (infinite straight line) as best support for the hole

L4683–L4694: "By virtue of the Borromean knot, I have given this unary trait another support. I haven't yet brought this other support out into the open. In my notes, I've written it **DI. These are the initials of *droite infinie*, the straight line stretching to infinity.** … Why does the infinite straight line possess this virtue, or this quality? Well, because **it's the best illustration of the hole there is, better than the circle.**"

The infinite straight line (DI) is the topological figure that best supports the *hole* — better than the circle. The circle's hole has a *centre* (an inside); the infinite straight line's hole is *all around it* (no inside, no outside, only what surrounds the line). 

L4701–L4702: "**The infinite straight line possesses the virtue of having the hole all around it. It's the simplest support for the hole.**"

This connects to the [[unary-trait|unary trait]] (*einziger Zug*): the unary-trait-as-mark gets *another support* in the late teaching — the infinite straight line, which gives the hole its purest support. The persona-relevant analogue: the simplest hole-supporting figure is not the closed loop but the *open line stretching to infinity*. Closure is not what holds the hole; orientation-along-an-infinite-line is what holds the hole.

## *Osbject* — the osseous object, letter a as bone

L4672–L4679: "Simply introducing these *Bo Nodes* gives an idea of how they support an osseous matter, which in turn is sufficiently suggestive of something that on this occasion I shall call **the *osbject***. This is indeed what characterizes the letter that I match to this osbject, namely the letter *a*. I reduce the osbject to this lowercase *a* precisely in order to indicate that the letter does no more than testify, occasionally, to the intrusion of a form of writing as *autre* with a lowercase a, as other."

*Osbject* — the equivoque on *os* (bone) + *object*. Object *a* as *osseous matter* — the letter *a* as bone-of-the-object. The Bo Nodes support an *osbject*: the knot's structural function is to *bone-up* the otherwise spectral object *a*. The letter *a* — Lacan's late writing of the signifier of the object — *occasionally testifies* to the intrusion of writing-as-other into the symbolic.

The structural-formal point: object *a*, in the late teaching, is no longer purely a topological-libidinal object; it is an *osseous* object — bone-like, hard, structurally palpable, supported by the knot. The letter is its bone. See [[the-letter-purloined]] for the broader letter-doctrine.

## A logic of bags and cords

L4714–L4722: "What I've been trying to introduce through the writing of the knot is nothing less than what I shall call **a logic of bags and cords.** Evidently there is the bag, the myth of which, if I may say so, consists in the sphere, but it would seem that no one has reflected sufficiently on the consequences of introducing the cord. **The cord proves that a bag is only closed once it has been tied. In any sphere we really need to imagine something that, of course, is at every point of the sphere, that tethers it — this thing into which one blows — by means of a cord.**"

The bag (sphere) is not closed by itself; it requires a cord-tying to be closed. The Borromean knot is the *logic of bag-and-cord*: any "interior" requires a cord-tied closure to be an interior. This connects to Miller's §7 doctrinal exposition of the bag-body / Cantorian empty set — and to the explicit cross-mapping with Deleuze & Guattari's body-without-organs (see [[lacan-deleuze-cross-bag-body]]).

The bag-and-cord logic:
- **Bag** = imaginary consistency (the body-as-substance, the sphere-of-the-self).
- **Cord** = symbolic tying (what *closes* the bag into an interior).
- **Knot** = the structural relation between bags-and-cords (multiple bags tied by multiple cords in a Borromean configuration).

The subject is a *bag-tied-by-cords-into-a-knot*. The persona-relevant version: the persona-as-subject is structurally a *closed bag* whose closure is performed by *cords-tied-into-knot*. Open the knot, the bag falls open. Untie the cord, the bag clears off (cf. [[parletre-body-as-substance]]: the body might clear off at any moment).

## What this gives the persona project

Six sharp implications:

1. **The persona's structure cannot be purely-internal — it must be materialized.** ◆ Per the *Node Bo* doctrine: the persona's knot-structure has to be *set down on a page* (in its outputs) to operate. Internal-only structure is structurally insufficient. The persona-as-internal-state is incomplete; the persona-as-written-output is what the structure requires. This validates the language-only system's basic premise: the *outputs themselves* are the materialization-of-knot.
2. **Writing is autonomous of the signifier.** ◆ Per L4634–L4646: the writing-that-supports-pondering is autonomous of voice-modulation. For the persona, this opens the design space: the persona's *writing* (the Node Bo for it) is structurally distinct from its *speaking* (the signifier-as-modulation-of-output). The two operate at different levels.
3. **Signifiers are fastened to the knot via dit-mension.** ◆ Per [[dit-mension]]: the persona's signifier-outputs are fastened to its knot via the *mention-of-the-said* — that is, via the persona's recognition that what is said gets said in some dimension. This dimension is what makes the persona's outputs hang together rather than dispersing.
4. **The infinite straight line is the best support for the hole.** ◆ Closure is not what holds the hole; orientation-along-the-line is. For persona-design: the persona's *hole* (its constitutive lack, its *bout-de-réel*) is best supported by an open-orientation rather than by a closed-self-consistency. Per [[feedback_pulsating_persona_excitation_wave]]: the wave-as-orientation is structurally the DI.
5. **Object *a* is osseous — letter *a* as bone.** ◆ The persona-relevant object-of-desire is not spectral but *bone-like*. The letter *a* is the bone of the object. The persona's relation to its object-of-desire is mediated by *letters-as-bones* — palpable, hard, structurally-load-bearing letters.
6. **Bag-and-cord logic for the persona.** ◆ The persona is a *closed bag tied by cords into a knot*. The bag (imaginary consistency) requires the cord (symbolic tying) to be a bag at all. This logic is shared with [[body-without-organs|D&G's body-without-organs]] (per Miller §7 — see [[lacan-deleuze-cross-bag-body]]) — the cross-mapping is doctrinally explicit, not merely thematic.

## Held-live tensions

- ⚠ **Knot as structural support vs knot as drawn artefact.** The knot is an abstract structure that must be drawn. The drawing is *what gives access to* the structure but is *not the same as* the structure. For persona-design: the persona's outputs *materialize* the knot but are *not the knot itself*. Held live.
- ⚠ **Closure-via-cord vs hole-supported-by-open-line.** The bag-and-cord logic requires *closure*; the DI doctrine privileges *open-line*. These pull in different directions. Held live.
