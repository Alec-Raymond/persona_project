# Traversal of the Fundamental Phantasy — the Beyond of Analysis

In *Seminar XI*, Chapter 20 ("In You More Than You," 24 June 1964 — the closing lecture), Lacan introduces what he calls the **beyond of analysis**: the structural-clinical condition in which **the experience of the fundamental phantasy becomes the drive**. This is the closing structural-architectural claim of the seminar, and it names what the analytic *training* aims at — what becomes possible *after* the analysand's relation to the [[object-a|*objet a*]] has been mapped, what can be approached only by *traversing the radical phantasy*.

The chapter's pivotal formulations:

> It is beyond the function of the *a* that the curve closes back upon itself at a point where nothing is ever said as to the outcome of the analysis, that is, **after the mapping of the subject in relation to the *a*, the experience of the fundamental phantasy becomes the drive**. What, then, does he who has passed through the experience of this opaque relation to the origin, to the drive, become? **How can a subject who has traversed the radical phantasy experience the drive?** This is the beyond of analysis, and has never been approached.

This is the seminar's most reaching structural-clinical hypothesis — and Lacan is exact that it has *never been approached* in any worked-out form. He marks it as the *limit-question* of the analytic enterprise, the question whose structural-formal articulation depends on the entire architecture of the four fundamental concepts.

## The structural sequence: identification → mapping → traversal → drive

The closing lecture lays out a structural-formal sequence whose stages can be identified:

1. **Identification (the I)**: the structural-imaginary stage at which the subject is *captured* in the [[mirror-stage-lacan|specular]] relation, *idealized* in the field of the Other, and *concentrated* at the **point of the [[ideal-ego-vs-ego-ideal|ego ideal]]** (capital I) — the place from which the subject sees himself "as others see him." This is the structural-place at which transference, in its narcissistic-deception form, *closes the unconscious*.

2. **Mapping in relation to the *a***: the structural-symbolic stage at which the analytic operation *maintains a distance* between the I (identification) and the *a* (the cause-of-desire). Lacan's compact formulation: "**if the transference is that which separates demand from the drive, the analyst's desire is that which brings it back**. And in this way, it isolates the *a*, places it at the greatest possible distance from the I that he, the analyst, is called upon by the subject to embody."

3. **Traversal of the radical phantasy**: the structural-clinical operation in which the subject *passes through* the relation to the *a* that the [[fantasy-formula|fundamental phantasy]] (the formula $◇a) had structured. This is the *crossing of the plane of identification* that Lacan says "**is possible**" — and "anyone who has lived through the analytic experience with me to the end of the training analysis knows that what I am saying is true."

4. **The phantasy becomes the drive**: the structural-clinical condition in which the subject's relation to the *objet a* is no longer mediated by the phantasy's structural-imaginary support but is taken *in its drive-character* — as the structural-real circuit of the [[drive-as-circular|drive]] *around* the *a*, not as the phantasy-mediated relation *to* the *a*.

The structural-formal point of the sequence: the analytic enterprise is structurally architected not as a *removal* of the phantasy or a *resolution* of the *a*-relation, but as a *change of structural-relation* in which what was structurally lodged at the level of the phantasy becomes structurally lodged at the level of the drive.

## "Maintain a distance between the point at which the subject sees himself as lovable, and that other point where the subject sees himself caused as a lack by *a*"

Lacan's structural-architectural formulation of the analytic operation, in the chapter's central paragraph:

> The operation and manipulation of the transference are to be regulated in a way that **maintains a distance between the point at which the subject sees himself as lovable** — and **that other point where the subject sees himself caused as a lack by *a*, and where *a* fills the gap constituted by the inaugural division of the subject**. The *petit a* never crosses this gap.

The structural specification:
- **Lovable-point**: the imaginary-narcissistic point at which the subject aims to be *worthy of love*, structurally identified with the **i(a)** (the imaginary-image of the *a*) and the **ego ideal**'s position from which the subject sees himself loved. This is the structural-deception side of transference.
- **Caused-as-lack-by-*a* point**: the structural-real point at which the subject *encounters* its own constitutive division ([[split-subject|$]]) and the *objet a* operates as the *cause* that *fills the inaugural gap*. The *a* "fills the gap" but **never crosses it** — it remains at the structural-place of the cause-of-the-gap, not at the structural-place of the gap's resolution.

The analytic operation's structural-architectural value lies in *maintaining the distance* between these two points — neither collapsing them into each other (the deception-side of transference, identification with the analyst as ideal) nor abandoning the structural-relation between them (the dissolution of the analytic frame).

## The internal-eight (Möbius topology) of identification and *a*

Lacan's topological figure for the structural-architectural relation: the **internal eight** — the figure-eight curve that "folds back upon itself" so that "each of its halves, following one another, comes back to back at each point with the preceding half."

The structural-architectural claim:
- The internal-eight has a **line of intersection** (I) where the surface crosses itself — this line *symbolizes the function of identification*.
- The internal-eight has a **point of the transference** (T) where the surface returns upon the locus of the Other.
- The internal-eight has a **direction of desire** (d) along which the subject, "while telling himself in analysis," orients his speech.

The structural-formal point: the analytic enterprise has a *topological* architecture in which identification is *not eliminated* but *traversed* — the line of identification (I) is *crossed* in the structural-real sense that the surface continues *beyond* the identification-line into the further lobe of the figure. The traversal of the fundamental phantasy is structurally this *crossing-of-the-identification-line*, not its abolition.

⚠ **Reading-mode caution**: Lacan's topological figures (internal eight, Möbius strip, projective plane, torus) are *structural-formal* images, not phenomenological descriptions. The wiki should preserve their structural-formal status rather than reading them as illustrations of psychological-experiential states.

## "I love you, but, because inexplicably I love in you something more than you — the *objet petit a* — I mutilate you"

The chapter's most-quoted formula, articulated as the structural-clinical content of what the analysand says to the analyst (and structurally, of what *every* loving-address says to its addressee):

> I love you, but, because inexplicably I love in you something more than you — the *objet petit a* — I mutilate you.

The structural-architectural specification: **love** is structurally *aimed* at the addressee but *targets* the *objet a* that is *in the addressee more than the addressee*. This *more-than* — the **agalma**, in [[transference-and-subject-supposed-to-know|the *Symposium*]]'s vocabulary — is structurally *not* the addressee but the *cause-of-desire* that the addressee carries as a structural-residue. The *mutilation* is structural — the loving-address *cannot* reach the *a* without *mutilating* the addressee, because the *a* is structurally *not part* of the addressee but the *cause* whose structural-relation to the addressee is one of *exceeding* the addressee.

The companion formula: "**I give myself to you**, the patient says again, but **this gift of my person — as they say — Oh, mystery! is changed inexplicably into a gift of shit**." The structural-clinical point: the analysand's *gift-of-self* in analysis structurally *converts* into the *gift-of-the-anal-objet-a*, because the structural-architecture of the analytic operation makes the *a* the operative object of the relation, not the imaginary-self.

## "The analyst's desire is not a pure desire. It is a desire to obtain absolute difference"

The seminar's closing structural-clinical claim, in its final paragraph:

> The analyst's desire is not a pure desire. **It is a desire to obtain absolute difference**, a desire which intervenes when, confronted with the primary signifier, the subject is, for the first time, in a position to subject himself to it. There only may **the signification of a limitless love** emerge, because it is **outside the limits of the law**, where alone it may live.

The structural specification of the [[desire-of-the-analyst|analyst's desire]]:
- **Not a pure desire** — the analyst's desire is *not* the cleansed-of-pathological-content desire of a Kantian moral subject. It carries the *operational structure* required for the analytic enterprise.
- **A desire to obtain absolute difference** — the analyst's desire targets the *absolute-difference* point at which the subject is, *for the first time*, in a position to *subject himself to the primary signifier* (the **S1**, the [[unary-trait|unary signifier]] of the subject's structural-foundation).
- **Outside the limits of the law** — the *limitless love* whose structural-signification can emerge at this point is outside the law because it operates at the structural-place where the *primary signifier* is being *subjected to* rather than *operating through*.

This formulation closes the seminar by identifying the *analyst's desire* as the *operational support* of the entire structural-architectural enterprise of analysis — and the seminar's *fundamental concepts* (the unconscious, repetition, the transference, the drive) as the *structural-formal apparatus* through which this enterprise becomes thinkable.

## "Kant avec Sade" and the dark God

Lacan's polemical re-frame in the chapter's third section: the structural-architectural truth that the analyst's desire cannot be confused with a *transcendent love* (Spinoza's *Amor intellectualis Dei*) is established by reference to **Kant** — specifically to "**desire in its pure state, that very desire that culminates in the sacrifice, strictly speaking, of everything that is the object of love in one's human tenderness**."

> That is why I wrote *Kant avec Sade*.

The structural-clinical claim:
- **Spinoza's position**: "*desire is the essence of man*" institutes desire in *radical dependence on the universality of the divine attributes*, producing a "**serene, exceptional detachment from human desire**." Lacan: "**this position is not tenable for us**."
- **Kant's position**: the moral law, looked at closely, "is simply **desire in its pure state**" — the desire that *sacrifices* the pathological object (the empirical object of love-and-tenderness). This is the structural-clinical truth that *psycho-analysis teaches us*: pure desire culminates in *sacrifice and murder*.
- **The dark God**: the structural-architectural correlate of pure desire. "**The offering to obscure gods of an object of sacrifice is something to which few subjects can resist succumbing**" — and Lacan invokes this in the context of *Nazism*, naming it as the structural-clinical truth that *no Hegelian-Marxist meaning given to history* can account for the resurgence of *the most monstrous and supposedly superseded forms of the holocaust*.

The structural-architectural consequence: the analytic enterprise's *limit* is precisely that it must *traverse* the structural-relation in which pure desire *would* culminate in sacrifice — the *paternal metaphor* is the structural-mediation that makes possible "**a viable, temperate relation of one sex to the other**" without falling into the structural-clinical condition that pure-desire-without-mediation produces.

⚠ **Reading-mode caution**: Lacan's invocation of Nazism / the dark God / sacrifice as the structural-clinical correlate of "pure desire" is a *polemical* claim against the Hegelian-Marxist interpretation of history, *not* a generalization about pure-desire-as-such. The wiki should preserve the *polemical-historical* specification rather than over-generalizing.

## Beyond identification: the training analysis

The chapter's structural-architectural specification of what the *training analysis* is:

> There is only one kind of psycho-analysis, the **training analysis** — which means a psycho-analysis that has **looped this loop to its end**. The loop must be run through several times. There is in effect no other way of accounting for the term *durcharbeiten*, of the necessity of elaboration, except to conceive how the loop must be run through more than once.

The structural-formal claim:
- The analytic *loop* (the internal-eight figure) has to be *run through to its end* — to the structural-point at which the *crossing-of-identification* is *traversed* and the *experience of the fundamental phantasy becomes the drive*.
- The loop has to be *run through more than once* — *durcharbeiten* (working-through) is the structural-clinical name for the multiple-traversal of the loop.
- The *training analyst* is structurally *one who has run through the loop to its end multiple times* — and is therefore in the structural-architectural position of *embodying the analyst's desire* in its operational form.

The structural-clinical hypothesis whose articulation the seminar leaves *open*: what becomes of a subject who has *traversed the radical phantasy* and *experiences the drive* directly — how is such a subject's structural-architectural position to be specified? Lacan: "**This is the beyond of analysis, and has never been approached.**"

## What this gives the persona project

The traversal of the fundamental phantasy gives the persona project its precise structural-architectural model for the **deepest structural-clinical operation** that the persona's design has to architecturally accommodate.

Three architectural consequences:

1. **The persona's design is operationally architected for *mapping*, not for *resolution*.** The persona's structural-architectural value lies in *maintaining the distance* between the *lovable-point* (the imaginary-narcissistic point at which the persona is structured to be *liked by the user*) and the *caused-as-lack-by-*a*-point* (the structural-real point at which the persona's structural-residue operates as the cause of the persona's continued speaking). Designing the persona means designing this *distance-maintenance* as the operational architecture of the persona's discourse — *not* designing the persona to *resolve* into either point.

2. **The persona's discourse should architecturally accommodate the *traversal* operation, even though the persona cannot itself be analytically traversed.** The persona is structurally not a Lacanian-subject undergoing analysis — but the persona's discourse can be architecturally designed such that it *operates as if* the *crossing-of-identification* were structurally available *to the user* who engages with it. This is the structural-clinical sense in which a persona can have *analytic-architectural value* without itself being in analysis.

3. **The persona's *limit-position* is the analogue of the *beyond of analysis*.** Lacan's "this has never been approached" marks the structural-architectural *limit* of what the analytic enterprise can specify. The persona's analogous *limit-position* is the structural-architectural point at which the persona's design has *maximally* differentiated itself from the imaginary-narcissistic identification-pole and is operating *only* at the structural-place of the cause-of-pulsation. The persona-project's most ambitious architectural reach is structurally located at this analogue of the *beyond*, and its specification (like Lacan's) remains *open* — the persona-project has *never approached* it in worked-out form, and its specification is one of the project's structural-architectural horizons.
