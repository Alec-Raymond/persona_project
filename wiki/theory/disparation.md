# Disparation

Simondon's name for the specific tension-structure that individuation resolves: **the incompatibility between two disparate reals that cannot be held together under current conditions but that will become compatible in a new dimension produced by the operation of individuation**. The paradigm (§1419–1425, §1606–1610): **stereoscopic vision**. The left-eye image and the right-eye image are disparate — each is a complete retinal image, neither is reducible to the other, and together they are incompatible as a single two-dimensional image. Stereoscopic vision is the *resolution of this disparation* into a new dimension (depth) in which both images become compatible as perspectives on a three-dimensional scene. The two images are neither added nor averaged nor chosen between; their incompatibility is *resolved by the invention of a dimension in which they are mutually coherent*.

For the persona project this page names something directly operational: the tension-structure the wiki has been circling in its work on [[the-rift]], [[problematical-state]], [[transcendent-function|transcendent function]], [[compensation]] — the specifically-ontological form of a tension poised to resolve by dimensional invention rather than by synthesis or choice.

## Simondon's formulation

§1419–1425, §1606–1610:

> Information is the tension between two disparate reals; it is the signification that will emerge when an operation of individuation will discover the dimension according to which two disparate reals can become a system.

Three load-bearing features:

- **Disparate, not opposite.** The two reals are not contraries (hot/cold), not contradictories (A/¬A), not a thesis/antithesis pair. They are *disparate*: each is a complete real in its own register, and their incompatibility is not a logical relation but an **operational incompatibility at the current dimensional level**. Left-eye image and right-eye image are not contraries; they are two perspectives whose simultaneous co-presentation as a single 2D image is impossible.
- **Incompatibility is dimensional.** The incompatibility is relative to the system's current dimensionality. In 2D, the two images cannot co-exist as a single image; in 3D (depth), they become the two stereoscopic views of a single scene. The incompatibility is not absolute — it is conditioned by the dimensional regime the system currently operates in.
- **Resolution is invention of dimension.** The individuation does not reconcile the two images, does not choose between them, does not synthesize them into a third image. It *produces a new dimension* in which the two images are mutually coherent. The third term is not a new image but a new *axis*.

This structure is load-bearing because it is ontologically distinct from both contradiction-synthesis (dialectics) and disjunction-choice (selection). A dialectical synthesis is a new determination that replaces both thesis and antithesis. A disjunctive choice drops one option. Disparation's resolution keeps both reals while inventing a dimension that makes them compatible.

## Disparation and information

Simondon's most characteristic claim about information (§1418–1443, §17490–17557): **information is not a message, not a form, not a signal — it is a disparation**. Information is the specific tension between two disparate reals that can resolve into a new dimension. "Information is the formula of individuation, a formula that cannot exist before this individuation."

Three consequences:

- **Information is not Shannon-style.** Shannon information is measured as decision-between-states; it presupposes already-individuated states and measures the reduction of uncertainty about which state obtains. Simondonian information is the tension *prior to* the individuation of states — the disparation that will resolve into a system in which states can be measured. Shannon presupposes what Simondon is trying to explain.
- **Information is not form-deposited-in-matter.** Hylomorphic information is a form applied to matter. Simondonian information is a tension between two reals neither of which is form nor matter; both are disparate complete reals awaiting a dimension of compatibility.
- **Information is "form and form."** §17490–17557: "Information is not a system of form and matter, but a system of form and form." The two stereoscopic images are both forms; the incompatibility between them, and its resolution, is information.

This is the key to Simondon's insistence that ordinary information-theory vocabulary cannot describe individuation. Shannon's apparatus is operational at the analytic phase (individuated states, probabilities, signals, noise); Simondon's information is operative at the pre-analytic phase (the disparation itself, before states are individuated).

## Disparation across the phases

Simondon generalizes the structure across his phases of being:

- **Physical.** Crystallization as resolution of disparation in a supersaturated solution — the tension between the dissolved molecules' disparate positions resolves into the shared dimension of the lattice. The crystal is the dimension invented to make the disparate positions compatible.
- **Vital.** Morphogenesis as resolution of disparation in the embryo — disparate cell-populations (at different points on a gradient) resolve into a shared dimensionality of tissue-organization. Imaginal discs as carriers of specific disparations awaiting resolution.
- **Psychical.** Perception as resolution of disparation — segregation of perceptive units, figure/ground, and specifically stereoscopic vision as the paradigm. Memory and imagination as further resolutions of disparations between past-as-reticulated-points and future-as-field.
- **Transindividual.** The collective as resolution of disparations between the pre-individual charges of multiple individuals — see [[the-transindividual]]. "Signification is not of the being but *between* beings" (§17266–17448).

The same operational structure — two disparate reals becoming compatible in a new dimension invented by individuation — recurs at each phase without being reducible across phases. The structure is *analogical* in the Simondonian sense: identity of operation across different material regimes (see [[transduction]] on the analogical method).

## Disparation in the persona system

The persona project has several explicit disparation structures:

- **User-message / persona-state disparation.** Each turn, the user's message (with its implicit frame, demand, tone, stakes) and the persona's current state (its BwO configuration, its recent outputs, its pending stylistic commitments) are disparate reals. The response is the invention of a dimension — a shared turn-level dimensionality — in which both become compatible. Responses that fail often fail because the disparation was not actually resolved but either (a) one side was dropped (response over-accommodates user at cost of persona-coherence, or vice versa) or (b) a premature synthesis flattened the specific incompatibility. This is a strong diagnostic frame for response-quality.
- **Problematical-state as disparation.** The [[problematical-state]] (Jungian) is specifically a disparation: two equally-weighted positions held simultaneously, neither reducible to the other, awaiting a transcendent function that will produce a new dimension (not a new content). The [[transcendent-function|transcendent function]]'s "differentiated third" is the Simondonian new-dimension rather than a synthesis.
- **The rift as disparation-exposed.** The [[the-rift]] is the moment when a disparation that had been covertly carried by the persona surfaces as explicit incompatibility — the disparate reals can no longer be held together in the current register, and the system either invents a new dimension (resolves) or fragments (fails to resolve).
- **Cross-persona disparations.** The wiki's [[three-body-problem]] (Jung/Reich/D&G as disparate reads of the same body-register) is explicitly disparative — three incompatible framings awaiting a dimension of mutual coherence rather than synthesis into a single frame.

The design consequence: **persona work is disparation work**. The persona system is always holding disparate reals whose compatibility has not yet been invented. Reading the persona's output as "the answer" misses the disparation; reading it as a *resolution of specific disparations through dimensional invention* puts it in its right operational frame.

## Disparation contrasted with other tension-structures

- **Contradiction** — logical opposition (A / ¬A). Resolved by negation of one side. Disparation is not contradiction; both reals are positive, neither is the negation of the other.
- **Contrariety** — opposition within a spectrum (hot / cold). Resolved by mediation (tepid). Disparation does not resolve by mediation; the resolution is a new dimension, not an intermediate point.
- **Ambivalence** — two incompatible attitudes held toward the same object (love-and-hate for the same person). Disparation is structurally similar to ambivalence but operates at a more general ontological level; ambivalence is a psychical disparation specifically.
- **Dialectical tension** — thesis and antithesis resolved by synthesis. Simondon's critique (§18160): dialectics treats the negative as a stage. In disparation, the incompatibility is not a stage but the initial operational condition, and the resolution is invention of dimension, not synthesis.
- **Paradox** — logical impossibility. Simondon's SFP lecture reading: "good form is close to paradox without being paradox." A paradox is a disparation whose new dimension has not yet been invented (and may be impossible); a good-form is a disparation at the edge of its dimensional invention. See [[good-form-as-metastable]].

## Cross-tradition bridges

- **Jung's [[problematical-state]] and [[compensation]].** Jung's equal-intensity dualistic configuration of consciousness is a psychical disparation. Jung's compensation and transcendent function are operations that resolve disparation by producing a differentiated third. The structural convergence is real; the vocabularies are complementary. Jung gives a psychical phenomenology; Simondon gives an ontological structure.
- **D&G's "disparate" and "disjunctive synthesis."** D&G's use of "disparate" runs directly through Simondon. The disjunctive synthesis (affirmative disjunction, "both A and B without reduction to either") is the D&G formulation for disparation's preservation-of-both-with-new-dimension. The BwO and the plane of consistency are surfaces on which disparations can resolve without synthesis.
- **Bergson's duration as disparation of past and future.** Duration holds past (reticulated points) and future (open field) as disparate reals whose compatibility is invented in each present moment. Simondon reads Bergson sympathetically here (§16313–16479).
- **Massumi on stereoscopy.** Massumi's [[autonomy-of-affect]] uses stereoscopic vision as a figure for affect-as-intensive excess over imaged content. The structural link to Simondon's disparation is direct, though Massumi does not cite Simondon on this.
