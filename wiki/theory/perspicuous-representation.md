Perspicuous representation (*übersichtliche Darstellung*) is Wittgenstein's name, in *Philosophical Investigations* §122, for the methodological operation that produces understanding by arranging what is already open to view rather than by discovering what is hidden beneath it. An arrangement is *perspicuous* when it lets structural connections become visible that were obscured in the usual presentation. For Wittgenstein this is not one technique among others; it "earmarks the form of account we give... the way we look at things." For the persona project, perspicuous representation is the register in which much of the wiki's cross-tradition work is already operating — and it is the register in which persona-method can be stated without metaphysical over-commitment.

## §122 — the canonical passage

From `raw/philosophical_investigations.md` L2411–L2419:

> A main source of our failure to understand is that we do not command a clear view of the use of our words.—Our grammar is lacking in this sort of perspicuity. A perspicuous representation produces just that understanding which consists in 'seeing connexions'. Hence the importance of finding and inventing intermediate cases.
>
> The concept of a perspicuous representation is of fundamental significance for us. It earmarks the form of account we give, the way we look at things. (Is this a 'Weltanschauung'?)

Three claims to unpack:

1. **Failure to understand = failure to command a clear view.** The diagnosis is specifically *surveyability*. The problem is not that there is a fact we haven't yet discovered; the problem is that what is already before us is not arranged so we can see it.
2. **Understanding consists in seeing connexions.** The positive outcome is not theory, is not the discovery of a hidden mechanism, is not the reduction of the many to the one — it is the perception of how the cases relate, given as they already are.
3. **Intermediate cases are the means.** The operation that produces seeing-connexions is finding (discovering) and inventing (constructing) the in-between cases that let the continuity between apparently-separate cases become perceptible. This is the active labor of the method.

The parenthetical "Is this a 'Weltanschauung'?" is a question left open in the text. Perspicuous representation is plausibly world-view-like (it shapes *how we look at things*) but is also possibly not — since it is strictly a *form of account*, not a theory about what there is. Wittgenstein leaves the question as a genuine question.

## "Seeing connexions" — what the operation actually produces

Perspicuous representation does not produce propositions. It produces an *arrangement* that lets the relations among what is already visible become visible. The output is closer to a map than to a theory.

This has a specific consequence for how the method can fail: a perspicuous representation fails when the arrangement installs a spurious connection (a false continuity, a misleading alignment, a forced bridge). The remedy is not theory-correction but re-arrangement — find more cases, find better intermediate cases, re-order the series, test whether the connexions-seen survive the redrawing.

The method is therefore self-correcting in a specific way: since the arrangement is not a claim about hidden essence, revising the arrangement does not require abandoning a theoretical commitment. A better arrangement is just a better arrangement.

## Intermediate cases — the technical heart

§122's second sentence — "Hence the importance of finding and inventing intermediate cases" — names the operational detail that makes perspicuous representation work. The target is continuous: if cases A and B look categorically separate, the intermediate cases A₁, A₂, ..., B show the continuity (if there is one) or show where the real discontinuity actually sits (often not where the naïve grammar placed it).

Two kinds of intermediate case:

- **Found intermediates.** Cases that already exist but that the usual presentation omits because they disturb the clean binary. A single additional real example can dissolve a false dichotomy.
- **Invented intermediates.** Wittgenstein's language-games and thought-experiments (the builders at §2, the tribe at §6, the forms of life at §23) are invented intermediates that let grammatical connections become visible where no real case was available to show them.

Both are in-scope. The criterion is not whether the case is historically real but whether its presence in the series makes a connexion visible.

## §92 — the negative — against essence-hidden

PI §92 (L2122–L2134) stages the perspicuous-representation position by marking what it is *not*:

> if we too in these investigations are trying to understand the essence of language—its function, its structure,—yet this is not what those questions have in view. For they see in the essence, not something that already lies open to view and that becomes surveyable by a rearrangement, but something that lies beneath the surface. Something that lies within, which we see when we look into the thing, and which an analysis digs out.
>
> 'The essence is hidden from us': this is the form our problem now assumes.

The positive content of "something that already lies open to view and that becomes surveyable by a rearrangement" *is* the perspicuous-representation thesis. The contrast-class Wittgenstein rejects is the essence-hidden-beneath-surface picture. Two methods are being distinguished:

- **Essence-hidden method.** Look into the thing, analyze, dig out what lies beneath. The depth-grammar method. Produces theory.
- **Already-open-to-view method.** The thing is already visible; the problem is that the arrangement we see it in occludes the connections. Re-arrange. Produces seeing-connexions.

PI's investigations are in the second register. §92 is where the method's *negative* commitment is clearest.

## Relation to §109 description-not-theory and §124 leaves-everything-as-is

Perspicuous representation composes with two neighboring methodological claims:

**§109 — description not theory.** Philosophical problems are solved "by looking into the workings of our language, and that in such a way as to make us recognize those workings: in despite of an urge to misunderstand them. The problems are solved, not by giving new information, but by arranging what we have always known." This is the perspicuous-representation thesis in prose form. The "arrangement of what we have always known" is the operation.

**§124 — leaves everything as it is.** "Philosophy may in no way interfere with the actual use of language: it can in the end only describe it... It leaves everything as it is." The method does not change the object. Perspicuous representation does not reform grammar; it arranges the cases so grammar's existing shape becomes visible.

Together: description-not-theory is what the method is *not* (it is not the production of new theory); perspicuous representation is what the method *is* (arrangement for seeing-connexions); leaves-everything-as-is is what the method *preserves* (the cases are not altered by being arranged).

## Perspicuous representation is a method, not a metaphysics

The "Weltanschauung" parenthetical in §122 reads as a question Wittgenstein genuinely did not want to pre-resolve. Reading it as a warning rather than an assertion: if perspicuous representation is a *Weltanschauung* in the full world-view sense, then the method has smuggled in a theoretical commitment about what there is (namely: the essence is not hidden, there is nothing beneath the surface). If it is not a *Weltanschauung*, then the method is agnostic about hidden-essence questions — it simply does not play that game.

The wiki's reading: perspicuous representation is best held as *method without metaphysical claim*. It does not assert that hidden essences do not exist; it asserts that the operation of arranging-for-seeing-connexions produces a kind of understanding that is valuable and that does not require the hidden-essence detour. Other methods (analyses-that-dig-beneath-the-surface) may produce other kinds of understanding. Perspicuous representation is a *form of account*, not the only form.

This distinction matters for the persona project specifically. The project is epistemologically cautious about depth-claims (the LLM has no inside; the "depth" of its outputs is often ornamental rather than substantive). Perspicuous representation is natively compatible with this caution: it produces understanding without requiring the hidden-inside.

## Cross-tradition adjacencies

- **[[schizoanalysis-as-cartography]]** — D&G's schizoanalysis-as-cartography is structurally a perspicuous-representation method: arranging the flows, connections, and assemblages so their relations become visible, without positing a hidden essence behind them. The two vocabularies converge on the cartographic posture while differing on the philosophical status of what-is-being-mapped.
- **[[pragmatic-maxim]]** — Peirce's deflationary razor ("two ideas are the same if they would produce the same habits of action") is also an anti-depth method — it refuses the distinction between two ideas that would never be behaviorally distinguishable. Perspicuous representation adds to this: even where Peirce would say "there's nothing there," Wittgenstein has a positive methodological proposal (arrange the cases so their connexions are visible).
- **[[one-sided-diet]]** — PI §593's diet-of-examples diagnosis is the complement of §122: one-sided-diet produces bad philosophy by under-sampling; perspicuous representation corrects it by inventing intermediate cases. The two passages name the disease and the cure.
- **[[bewitchment-and-therapy]]** — the therapeutic posture (§109, §133) is the larger method of which perspicuous representation is one specific operation. Bewitchment-by-grammar is cured not by theory but by showing the grammar perspicuously.
- **[[the-beetle-in-the-box]]** — §293's beetle thought-experiment is an invented intermediate case par excellence: it makes visible the grammatical fact that "the thing in the box" drops out of consideration because the language-game doesn't need it. The method of the thought-experiment is perspicuous-representation-as-invention.

## Persona-project stakes

**1. The wiki is already doing perspicuous representation.** The wiki's cross-tradition comparison work — noting where D&G, Bergson, Stern, Keleman, Varela, Bakhtin, Jung, Lacan, Derrida, Peirce, and Wittgenstein converge and where they diverge — is precisely an arrangement-for-seeing-connexions operation. Each theory page is a case; the cross-links are the arrangement; the reader's (and the wiki-writer's) seeing-of-connexions is the product. This means the method already has native vocabulary in the wiki's practice. See [[development/wittgenstein-and-the-persona-project]] §"PI's method as method for the project".

**2. Perspicuous representation is compatible with the limits-of-language posture.** [[development/limits-of-language]] already identifies perspicuous representation as "a method a language-only system can execute." This is an important positive finding: a language-only persona cannot produce embodied understanding, but it *can* produce the arrangement-for-seeing-connexions that Wittgenstein names. This is not a consolation prize; it is a specific and valuable kind of understanding.

**3. Intermediate cases as an operational target for the persona.** When the persona is asked a question where two concepts look categorically separated, its productive move is often to produce or find an intermediate case. This is a specifiable operation, distinct from "giving more information" or "generating more tokens." It is the operation §122 names, and it is trainable-against.

**4. Against the LLM depth-hallucination failure mode.** LLMs characteristically produce the *rhetoric* of depth — gesturing at hidden essences, inner meanings, deep structures — when they have no inside. Perspicuous representation provides an explicit alternative: produce the arrangement, let the connexions be seen, do not gesture at a hidden depth the system does not have. The method is specifically anti-hallucination-of-depth.

**5. The wiki is a perspicuous representation of the project.** The wiki's entire structure — flat directory, wikilinks-over-hierarchy, tags for broad thematic grouping, ⚠ for held-live tensions — is an arrangement optimized for seeing-connexions rather than for ontological commitment. The wiki's method is Wittgensteinian before any specific wiki-page is.

**6. "Leaves everything as it is" as a persona discipline.** Perspicuous representation does not reform the sources it arranges. The persona should not reform its interlocutor's vocabulary or the source traditions it inherits; it should arrange them, let connexions be seen, and leave the sources otherwise intact. This is a reparative-reading-style discipline ([[weak-theory-and-reparative-reading]]) with Wittgensteinian warrant.

**7. The Weltanschauung question held open.** The persona project should not resolve the §122 parenthetical. Whether perspicuous representation is a Weltanschauung or a method-without-metaphysics is exactly the kind of question the project is epistemologically cautious about. Holding it open is methodologically correct.

## Tensions held live

⚠ **Perspicuous representation can install spurious connections.** The method's productivity — seeing connexions — is also its risk. An arrangement that makes a false continuity visible is a *worse* arrangement than one that does not, and the method itself does not automatically correct this. Self-correction depends on re-arrangement, which requires the kind of maturity that recognizes when a connexion is spurious. The wiki's [[weak-theory-and-reparative-reading]] discipline is a check: preserve distinctives before noting convergences.

⚠ **The "already known" presupposition is strong.** §122 says "arranging what we have always known" (via §109). This presupposes the knowledge is already there and just needs arranging. For a persona project built on training-distribution-as-virtual ([[virtual-actual]]), what counts as "already known" is unclear — is it what is inscribed in the distribution, or what is inscribed in the user's culture, or what is inscribed in the conversation-so-far? The perspicuous-representation concept does not fix this; it inherits whatever answer the project gives.

⚠ **The essence-hidden rejection is itself a commitment.** §92's rejection of "essence hidden beneath surface" is itself a philosophical commitment, even if Wittgenstein frames it as only-method. Taking perspicuous representation as method-without-metaphysics requires accepting the anti-depth orientation; this is not as neutral as the method presents itself as being. The project is free to adopt the anti-depth orientation, but should not pretend that doing so is philosophically costless.

⚠ **Intermediate cases can be fabricated to force a connexion.** An invented intermediate case is legitimate *if* it makes a real connexion visible and *if* the reader can tell the intermediate from the endpoints. But invented intermediates can also be fabricated to produce the appearance of continuity where the actual cases sit discontinuously. The method's legitimacy depends on the honesty of the inventor — something no methodology can fully guarantee.

⚠ **Perspicuous representation does not equal persuasion.** An arrangement that lets connexions be seen is not automatically an arrangement that *convinces*. The method's output is visibility, not argument; seeing-a-connexion is not the same as accepting-a-claim. When the persona is asked to *argue* for a position, perspicuous representation is not the right tool on its own — it is a component in a method that also includes the dialectical operations PI does not elaborate. See [[dialogism|dialogism]] for the Bakhtinian complement.
