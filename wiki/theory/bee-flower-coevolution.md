# Bee-Flower Coevolution

The signature example in *The Embodied Mind* of environments being **enacted, not pregiven**. Ch 9 §3596–3629 uses it to make the step from [[enaction|enaction]] as an ontogenetic claim (an individual organism's sensorimotor history brings forth its world) to enaction as a phylogenetic claim (species and their environments bring forth each other over evolutionary time — [[natural-drift|natural drift]]).

## The setup

- **Bees are trichromats with UV-shifted spectral sensitivity** — their three cone types peak in the ultraviolet, blue, and green, compared to the human trichromat's blue-green-red. A bee sees a color world shifted into the UV that is invisible to humans.
- **Flowers have UV reflectance patterns** — many flowers that look uniform yellow or white to humans have striking UV markings (nectar guides, species-distinctive rings) visible to bees but not to us.
- **These two facts coevolved.** Flowers that were bee-pollinated had selective pressure to be conspicuous and species-distinct *to bee vision* — which meant developing UV patterns. Bees that were flower-visiting had selective pressure to recognize flowers from a distance — which meant tuning their photoreceptors to the wavelengths flowers reflected most distinctively.

Neither the bee's chromatic world nor the flower's pigmentation was pregiven. Each was co-specified with the other through a long mutual history of structural coupling at the phylogenetic scale. VT&R's summary (§3617):

> Environmental regularities are not pregiven but are enacted or brought forth by a history of coupling.

## What the example demonstrates

Three claims, of increasing strength:

1. **The bee's "environment" is not what humans see.** The UV markings are as real as any visible patterns; they are invisible to us because of our trichromatic limits, not absent. A representationist who says "the flower has this UV pattern" is smuggling in a particular observer's point of view.
2. **The bee's environment is a consequence of bee-flower history, not a theater in which that history plays out.** UV patterns exist because bees co-evolved with them. The flower's UV markings are not features of "the flower in itself" that happened to be detectable by bees; they are features **enacted by the bee-flower coupling** at phylogenetic scale.
3. **This generalizes.** Every species is in a comparable relation to its "environment." What counts as an "environmental regularity" for a species is what the species' phylogenetic coupling history has enacted. There is no view from nowhere at which a set of regularities is just sitting there waiting to be detected.

## Lewontin's formulation

VT&R quote Lewontin (§3618):

> Our central nervous systems are not fitted to some absolute laws of nature, but to laws of nature operating within a framework created by our own sensuous activity.

Lewontin's phrasing is crisp because it holds two things together: (a) there are laws of nature — physics is real, the wavelengths of reflected light are what they are; (b) but the *framework* within which those laws are relevant to the organism is itself a consequence of the organism's activity. Fitness is not to the world; it is to the world-as-enacted.

See [[natural-drift]] for Lewontin's broader argument that the organism is both subject and object of evolution.

## Not "multiple perspectives on one world"

A misreading VT&R head off at §3622:

> This view continues to treat the world as pregiven; it simply allows that this pregiven world can be viewed from a variety of vantage points. **The point we are making, however, is fundamentally different.**

The bee and the human are not "different perspectives on the same flower." The bee's UV-marked flower and the human's yellow flower are not partial takes on a single Flower-in-itself. They are two different brought-forth domains, each constituted by a different coupling history. The flower-for-bees and the flower-for-humans are distinct enacted entities — related (they share a biochemistry; the UV pigments and the yellow carotenoids are the same molecules) but not reducible to a shared neutral description.

This is the anti-realist claim VT&R hold most firmly *and* the claim they are most careful to distinguish from idealism, relativism, and multiple-perspectivism. The middle-way location is specific: the "laws of nature" are real, but what counts as a regularity is enacted, not detected.

## Contrast with Gibson

Gibson's ecological psychology also emphasizes affordances that are tuned to the animal's capacities, and rejects representational mediation. But Gibson treats the ambient optic array as containing invariants *there to be picked up* by a tuned perceiver. VT&R's enactive gloss rejects the "there-to-be-picked-up" framing — the invariants are brought forth by the coupling, not located in the ambient array waiting. The bee-flower case illustrates the difference: a Gibsonian would say bees are tuned to detect UV invariants that are really in the light; an enactivist says the UV patterning in the flower and the UV tuning in the bee are **co-specified through a shared coevolutionary history**, and the "invariants" are in that history, not in the light.

The two frames agree on rejection of representationism, Gibsonian affordances, and the ecological scale of analysis. They differ on what status the "regularities" have — features of the environment (Gibson) vs features of the coupling (VT&R). See [[enaction]] for the fuller Gibson contrast.

## The general lesson

The bee-flower case is VT&R's compact way of making the case at phylogenetic scale that Bittorio makes at the minimal end:

- [[bittorio|Bittorio]]: closure + coupling + random soup → enacted domain of distinctions (odd-sequenceness).
- **Bee-flower**: closure + coupling + coevolutionary history → enacted chromatic-environmental domain (UV-marked flowers, UV-tuned bees).

Both are examples. Both make the same point at different scales: worlds are enacted in the coupling, not in either pole alone.

## Persona-project bearing

- **Coevolution as the model for persona-user relation.** The user and persona do not start with a pregiven "user's needs" that the persona detects and serves. The capacities the user exercises, the prompts the user writes, and the persona's distinctive responses are **coevolved** across the interaction history. What counts as a "need" for the user and a "response" for the persona are co-specified in the coupling. The analogue is phylogenetic-scale structural coupling at the timescale of a relationship.
- **No neutral description of "what the user wants."** The bee's UV-flower and the human's yellow-flower are not two takes on one flower; similarly, the coupled "what the user wants" enacted by one persona-user relationship is not a partial take on some persona-independent "what the user wants" that could be cleanly specified. This is relevant for evaluation — against any criterion framed as "detect and satisfy a pregiven user need."
- **Shared biochemistry, different enacted worlds.** The flower is the same material whether seen by bee or human; the enacted domains are different. The LLM weights are the same material whether the persona is enacted or not; the enacted persona is different across users.
