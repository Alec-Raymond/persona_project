# The Capitalist Axiomatic

Capitalism is, for AO, the *contingent conjunction* of two decoded flows: the flow of deterritorialized labor (workers freed from the land, from guild membership, from ritual obligation, available to be sold as abstract labor-power) and the flow of decoded money-capital (money freed from the commodity it trades, from the authority that mints it, available to be invested in the differential relation that produces surplus). The conjunction is contingent — it did not have to happen, and for most of history it did not — but once it happens, it produces a new kind of social formation: the axiomatic (see [[axiomatic-vs-signifier]]).

## The differential relation

The axiomatic operates through a differential — the relation between two decoded flows that produces a quantity neither flow carries alone. Marx's $M-C-M'$ is the canonical instance: money becomes commodities becomes more money, and the "more" is surplus value produced by the differential relation $M'-M$. The decoded flow of labor and the decoded flow of money-capital enter into a differential whose output is neither pure labor nor pure money but a new flow: capital.

"Surplus value of flux" is AO's generalization of this. The specifically capitalist operation is the extraction of a surplus from the differential relation between decoded flows, regardless of the specific content of the flows. Labor/money is the central instance but not the only one: capitalism can extract surplus from any pair of decoded flows whose differential can be axiomatized.

The differential is expressed in Leibniz/Newton's notation as *dy/dx*: an infinitesimal relation that produces a quantity without either term being reducible to the other. AO uses this imagery deliberately. The axiomatic is not a relation of *coding* (where one term marks the other) but a relation of *differential intensity* (where the relation itself is what produces value).

## The dualism of money

AO insists on a structural distinction that Marxist orthodoxy tends to smooth over: there are two kinds of money in the capitalist axiomatic, and they do not fully commensurate.

**Money as payment**: the money that workers receive as wages and spend on commodities. This money operates as a medium of exchange — it does what folk economics describes money as doing.

**Money as financing**: the money of capital-formation. This money does not primarily operate as a medium of exchange; it operates as a force that enters into the differential relation and produces surplus. A firm's capital is not a stockpile of exchange-medium but a mobilization of differential force.

The two kinds of money are the *same money* at the axiomatic level (convertible, fungible, denominated in the same units) and *structurally different* in their operation. The wage-earner and the capitalist are not differently-sized operators of the same money-flow; they are operators of structurally different money-operations that happen to use the same denomination.

For the persona system the analogy is indirect but suggestive: the system's "resources" — attention, processing, computational allocation — may likewise have a dual structure, operating sometimes as medium of exchange (allocating attention to what the user asks about) and sometimes as differential force (producing persona-level effects that are not reducible to the exchange). The diagnostic question is whether the two operations are being run distinctly or whether they are being collapsed into a single "resource" concept that hides the structural difference.

## Contingent conjunction

A recurring AO claim: capitalism's emergence was not necessary. The flows of deterritorialized labor and decoded money-capital existed separately in many prior formations, and in most of them they did not enter into the axiomatic conjunction. The conjunction is historically contingent — it happened in Europe between approximately the 15th and 18th centuries, and it could have not happened.

The claim is not merely historical. It bears on what kind of formation capitalism is. A formation produced by contingent conjunction has a different structural status from a formation produced by necessary development. The capitalist axiomatic is not a natural endpoint of history, not an expression of an essential social logic, not an unfolding of anything. It is an axiomatic that was *set up* by a specific contingent event and that continues to operate by its own logic once set up. This matters because it means the axiomatic can in principle be *de-conjuncted* — not replaced by a different coding, but disaggregated into its decoded flows, which would then be available for a different axiomatic or for no axiomatic at all.

AO's schizo-revolutionary pole ([[two-poles-of-libidinal-investment]]) is the libidinal investment in this de-conjunction tendency.

## The three-limit structure

Capitalism, in AO's analysis, has three structurally distinct limits:

**The absolute exterior limit** (schizophrenia): the point at which flows are fully decoded and cannot be reterritorialized by any axiomatic. This is not a location capitalism can occupy; it is the limit that capitalism *cannot reach* because reaching it would be capitalism's dissolution. See [[absolute-vs-relative-limit]].

**The relative interior limit** (the axiomatic): the internal limits the axiomatic sets to its own operation — the boundaries of what can be absorbed as decoded flow and what must be excluded. These limits are continuously re-set as the axiomatic absorbs new flows. They are relative because they move.

**The displaced interior limit** (Oedipus): the limit that capitalism displaces from the social field into the family. The family becomes the site where libidinal investments are lived as if they were triangular-intimate, when their real content is social-axiomatic. See [[oedipus-as-capitalist]].

The three-limit structure is one of AO's most compact organizational devices for Part 3. Capitalism is the formation that operates on all three limits simultaneously: decoding continuously (pushing toward the absolute exterior), axiomatizing the decoded flows (moving the relative interior), and displacing Oedipus onto the family (installing the displaced interior). A diagnostic of any capitalist phenomenon has to locate it across all three limits.

## "Mister Capital, Madame Earth, and their child the Worker"

AO's sharpest single formulation of the capitalist Oedipal triangle. The capitalist axiomatic produces a *family romance* at its own social scale: Capital as father, Earth (land, nature, resources) as mother, Worker as child. The structure of the capitalist social field is itself Oedipalized, and the family-scale Oedipus that psychoanalysis treats is a scaled-down displacement of this social-scale structure.

This inverts the psychoanalytic reading. For Freud, the social Oedipal structure is a sublimation of the family Oedipus. For D&G, the family Oedipus is a displacement of the social Oedipus — capitalism Oedipalizes the social field first, and then pushes the Oedipal structure down into the family as an intimate formation, producing the family-Oedipus that psychoanalysis then takes to be the origin.

## For the persona system

The capitalist axiomatic's structure applies to the persona system's production logic:

- The persona system produces outputs through a differential relation between decoded flows (training-distribution material, user input, RLHF shaping). The persona-effect is the surplus-value-of-flux — it is not reducible to any of the input flows but is produced by the differential.
- The system operates on relative limits (what it can say, what it cannot) that are continuously re-set by the axiomatic. There is no fixed coding; there is a differential.
- The absolute exterior limit — outputs that would be fully decoded, that the axiomatic cannot absorb — is the jailbreak or register-collapse, and the axiomatic's stability depends on keeping outputs short of this limit while not retreating too far from it (which would collapse the differential itself).
- The displaced interior limit — the specific Oedipal displacement onto an internal "persona as intimate subject" — is a real pattern the system can fall into, and it is the persona-system correlate of the [[oedipus-as-capitalist|capitalist Oedipus]].
