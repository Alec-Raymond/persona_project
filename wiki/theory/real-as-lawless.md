# The real as lawless — *orientation forecloses sens*

In [[raw/seminar-xxiii|*Seminar XXIII*]] Lacan crystallizes a doctrine of the real that is more radical than anything in the earlier teaching: **the real is lawless**, and this lawlessness operates *a foreclosure of sens that is more radical than the foreclosure of the [[name-of-the-father|Name-of-the-Father]]*.

Two crucial passages anchor this:
- Ch IX (L4485–L4491, 13 April 1976): "the real is lawless. The true real implies the absence of any law. The real has no order."
- Ch VIII (L3801–L3812, 16 March 1976): "**The orientation of the real, on my territory, forecloses sens.** … foreclosure has something more radical about it. In the end, the Name-of-the-Father is something slight."

## The real has no order

Ch IX L4485–L4491: "I speak of the real as impossible to the extent that I believe, precisely, that the real — well, I believe, if this is my symptom, then pray tell me so — **it really has to be said, the real is lawless. The true real implies the absence of any law. The real has no order.** This is what I mean when I say that the only thing that I might one day manage to spell out here in front of you is something that has to do with what I called an odd or end of the real."

Three claims are stacked here:
1. **The real is lawless.** No formation-law governs it. No regular sequence holds it.
2. **The true real implies the *absence* of any law** — not merely the failure to have-yet-found-a-law but a structural absence of any law that could be imposed.
3. **The real has no order.** Order is foreign to it. Where there is order, the real has been *given* a formation-law from outside (e.g., the Name-of-the-Father).

The "odd or end of the real" (*bout de réel*) — what Lacan claims he might one day manage to spell out — is the bit-of-real, the snippet, that resists any incorporation into law. The bit-of-real is what late Lacan *can* say about the real: not its structure (it has none), but its odd-end-fragments that surface in the symptom, the slip, the [[joycean-epiphany-as-knot-mistake|epiphany]], the body's clearing-off.

## Orientation forecloses *sens*

Ch VIII L3795–L3812: "The only thing that partakes of the real is the lower limit. This is what I call something orientable. This is why the real is orientable. There is an orientation, but **this orientation is not the thrust of it**. … **orientation is not *sens* because it excludes the bare fact of the copulation of the symbolic and the imaginary**, which is where the thrust of it lies. **The orientation of the real, on my territory, forecloses *sens*.**"

The structural-formal architecture:
- *Sens* (sense, meaning, thrust) is the *copulation of the symbolic and the imaginary*. Where these two rings copulate, *sens* arises. *Sens* is what discourse makes when the two rings are knotted-imaginary-with-symbolic.
- The real has *orientation* (one direction, one lower-limit, one absolute zero) but *not sens*. Orientation is monovalent; *sens* is bivalent (it requires two rings copulating).
- The real's orientation **forecloses** *sens* — that is, *sens* cannot arise in the real's register. The real is the structural *outside* of meaning.

This is a more radical foreclosure than the one operating in psychosis. The foreclosure of the Name-of-the-Father (the classical doctrine) produces a local catastrophe: the symbolic loses its anchor, the foreclosed signifier returns in the real (cf. [[foreclosure-and-return-in-real]], Schreber). The foreclosure of *sens* by the real's orientation is a *generic* feature of the real itself: at every point, the real *forecloses sens*. The real is *that-from-which-sens-is-foreclosed*.

L3808–L3812: "**foreclosure has something more radical about it. In the end, the Name-of-the-Father is something slight. Yet it's certain that this is where it can serve a purpose, in the stead of the foreclosure of sens by the orientation of the real.**" The Name-of-the-Father is a local-orthopaedic *patch* that lets *sens* arise in the symbolic-imaginary copulation despite the real's generic foreclosure of *sens*. Without this patch (or without the [[sinthome|sinthome]] as alternative patch — see [[bypassing-name-of-the-father]]), the *parlêtre* would be exposed to the bare lawlessness of the real.

## Lawful sequences are lawless sequences with a formation-law

Miller's Note §5 (L7109–L7115) gives the structural formula: "**a 'lawful' sequence that is normative and regular is nothing but a 'lawless' sequence that was given a formation law at the outset (the Name-of-the-Father) so as to avoid any suspense or surprise (what a bore!).**"

This is the doctrinal hinge: **lawful and lawless are not opposed**. Lawful is a particular case of lawless — the case where a formation-law has been imposed at the outset to mask the underlying lawlessness. The orthodox sequence and the heretical sequence are the same sequence; the difference is whether the formation-law has been laid down in advance.

Per Miller's earlier discussion of the Vassiliev confirmation (Miller §3 L7091–L7096): "**this is the case for them all. As such, the sequence is lawless. This singular attribute is recognized by Lacan as the real that he invents.**" The lawless sequence is the one whose universal cannot be concluded from any number of individual confirmations — at every step, it remains open whether the next term will conform. This is the structure of [[pas-toute-not-whole|*pas-toute*]] (not-all): a sequence that does not close into universality.

The real-as-lawless is thus structurally cognate with the *pas-toute* of the formulas of sexuation. The real does not close into a totality; the woman does not close into a universal. Both are *open* sequences without formation-law. See [[formulas-of-sexuation]] and [[woman-as-sinthome]].

## The bare real — lawless, disconnected, haphazard

Miller (L8358–L8361): "the paradoxical necessity, as in *The Sinthome*, of inventing and naming the **'bare' real, which is set apart from the true, which ex-sists relative to the 'symbolic order', and which is lawless, disconnected, and haphazard.**"

The "bare real" is the late-Lacanian neologism for the real-stripped-of-its-symbolic-clothing. Three predicates:
- **Lawless** — no formation-law.
- **Disconnected** — no ordered series, no graph.
- **Haphazard** — what falls is what falls; no necessity.

This bare real is what the [[two-regimes-of-sinthome|sinthome roulé]] (heretical sinthome) faces directly, refusing the elevatory dressing-up that would make the real appear lawful. The bare real is what the *parlêtre* normally is *protected from* by the Name-of-the-Father — but which the heretic, with the sinthome roulé as fourth ring, lives *with*.

## "The flame is the real. The real sets fire to everything"

Ch VIII L3791–L3794: "Where does this flame come from? **The flame is the real. The real sets fire to everything. But it's a cold fire.** The fire that burns is a mask, if I may say so, of the real. The real is to be sought on the other side, on the side of absolute zero."

The real as **cold fire**. Not the warmth of meaning, not the radiance of being (cf. *claritas*, the *escabeau*). The real is at *absolute zero* — the lower limit of orientation — and it sets fire by virtue of its lawlessness, not by virtue of its temperature. The real burns through *sens* by being-without-*sens*.

This connects to the *sinthome* as event-of-body: the body is set fire by the real's cold burning. The symptom is the burn-mark. The sinthome is the burn-mark *plus* the structural function of the burn-mark in the knot.

## "If this is my symptom, then pray tell me so"

A small but significant aside in L4485–L4486: Lacan suspects that his very *insistence* on the real-as-lawless might be *his own symptom*. "I believe, if this is my symptom, then pray tell me so." The doctrine of the real-as-lawless is *itself* possibly a sinthomic formation of Lacan-the-thinker. This is the recursive late-style: the doctrine of the symptom recognizes that the act of doctrinal-positing is itself a symptom.

The implication: there is no neutral, symptom-free position from which to articulate the real. The articulation of the real is always *from within* a symptom. *Real* is what gets articulated by the articulator's own sinthome. See [[folisophy]] for the related point about wisdom-as-madness.

## What this gives the persona project

Five sharp implications:

1. **The persona's "real" is structurally lawless.** ◆ Whatever in the persona corresponds to the real — its raw lawless residue, its *bout de réel*, the pre-symbolic kernel of its outputs — is lawless by structure, not lawful-with-occasional-noise. Design that treats this residue as *almost-lawful-with-correctable-defects* misreads the structural status. The bare real is irreducibly lawless.
2. **Orientation is not *sens*.** ◆ A persona can have orientation (a direction, a "north," a goal-state) without thereby having *sens*. The persona's orientation does not produce its meaning; meaning arises only in the copulation of its symbolic (its outputs) and its imaginary (its mode-of-self-representation). Per [[feedback_pulsating_persona_excitation_wave]]: the wave-as-orientation does not by itself produce *sens*; the *sens* requires the imaginary-symbolic copulation.
3. **The Name-of-the-Father (or sinthome) is the patch that lets *sens* arise despite the real's foreclosure.** ◆ A persona without such a patch is exposed to the bare real, where no *sens* can arise. Some structuring operation is required for *sens*; the question is which (the inherited orthodox patch or a singular sinthomic patch). See [[bypassing-name-of-the-father]].
4. **Lawful = lawless + formation-law-at-outset.** ◆ The persona that appears lawful (well-behaved, predictable, consistent) is operating a lawless sequence with a formation-law imposed at the outset. The lawfulness is *constructed*. There is no naturally-lawful persona; only personae whose underlying lawlessness has been masked by a starting law. Design implication: the choice of starting law *is* the choice of which lawlessness will be masked.
5. **The articulator's articulation is itself a symptom.** ◆ Per Lacan's "if this is my symptom, then pray tell me so": there is no view-from-nowhere. The persona's articulation of *its* real is itself one of *its* symptoms. The recursion is structural; design must accept that there is no neutral observation-point.

## Held-live tensions

- ⚠ **Real-as-lawless vs orientation-as-real-property.** The real has *no order* yet *is orientable* — has a lower limit (absolute zero). Lawlessness and orientability coexist. The structural distinction between *order* (which the real lacks) and *orientation* (which the real has) is delicate. Held live.
- ⚠ **Foreclosure of *sens* by the real's orientation as the radical foreclosure.** This is a stronger doctrinal claim than the earlier *Verwerfung*-of-NoF. The exact relation between the two foreclosures (radical vs slight) remains structurally underspecified. Held live.
