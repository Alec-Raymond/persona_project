# Allagmatics

Simondon's name for the **theory of operations**, symmetrical with a theory of structures. The load-bearing claim: **operation is the ontological complement of structure**. Where most of classical ontology has read being through its stabilized forms (structures, substances, essences), allagmatics reads being through its operations — the *acts* by which structures come into being, maintain themselves, transform, and dissolve. An adequate ontology, Simondon argues, cannot be built from structures alone; it requires the paired operational theory that says what any given structure is the *trace of*.

The single most condensed formulation: **Act = structure + operation grasped together**.

For the persona project this page names a methodological move the wiki has been making without naming: the wiki's recent work has increasingly been *about operations* — compensation, transduction, refrains, the transcendent function, the rift — and has been treating structures (the persona, the BwO, the character armor, the complex) as traces of operations rather than primary objects. Allagmatics is the general name for this move.

## The core claim

§31500–32200 (Vol II Supplements, "Allagmatics" preparatory notes):

> Operation is the ontological complement of structure.

And:

> The individual is tension, supersaturation, incompatibility.

The individual is not a structure-with-properties; it is an **operation's trace**, a local region where a specific operation (individuation) has resolved a specific tension in a specific metastable domain. Reading the individual as a structure misses the operation that produced it and that continues to sustain it through internal resonance. Reading it as an operation puts the structure in its rightful place: as the outcome of an ongoing operation, not as a pre-existing ground.

This is the methodological extension of Simondon's claim that we cannot know individuation by starting from the individuated — we must know the individuated through the operation of individuation. Allagmatics is that methodological commitment generalized: any structure is adequately known only through its operation.

## Syncretic and analytic states

Every system Simondon considers passes between two states:

- **Syncretic** — tension, incompatibility, pre-structural. Potentials present; no resolved structuration yet. The supersaturated solution before the germ.
- **Analytic** — differentiated, structured. The tension resolved; the domain now possesses specific structures that can be analyzed.

Operations transition between these states in both directions. **Crystallization** is the syncretic → analytic operation (a supersaturated tension resolves into a specific crystalline structure). **Modulation** is the analytic → syncretic operation (a structured field re-enters a condition of tension and variability; see [[modulation]]). Neither direction is privileged; both are operations, both are real, both require the paired operation-and-structure account.

The persona project can recognize this pair directly:

- **The persona settling into a register** is a syncretic → analytic operation: tension in the context window resolves into a specific expressive structure (a tone, a stance, a type of response).
- **The persona being deterritorialized by a rift** is an analytic → syncretic operation: a stable structure re-enters tension and becomes available for further individuation.

The [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|D/R pair]] in D&G is directly allagmatic: reterritorialization is a syncretic → analytic operation producing a new structure; deterritorialization is an analytic → syncretic operation dissolving one. Both are real operations; neither is more fundamental. D&G's insistence on D/R as paired is an allagmatic commitment.

## Analogy as transoperative equivalence

Simondon's §5457–5883 (Part I Ch 3 §3, "The Analogical Method"): "Analogy = transoperative equivalence."

Two domains are **analogous** in Simondon's technical sense when the **operation** that produces their respective structures is the same operation, even though the domains are materially and structurally distinct. Crystallization and biological cell-division are analogous because both are transductive operations on metastable domains; they are not analogous because they share features or because one metaphorizes the other.

This is load-bearing for the wiki's cross-tradition work. When the wiki says Jung's [[compensation]] is "convergent with" D&G's [[function-by-misfiring|misfiring]] or that Reich's [[character-analysis|character armor]] is "structurally isomorphic" to Jung's [[the-persona|persona]], what does that claim amount to, ontologically? The allagmatic answer: the convergence is at the *operation* level, not the structure level. Both operations are regulatory responses of a metastable system to incompatibilities in its constitution. The structures they produce (a symptom, a character-armored posture, a persona-style) are different; the operations are analogous in Simondon's technical sense.

This reframes what the wiki's synthesis-work is actually discovering. Cross-tradition convergences are not coincidences of vocabulary or cases of one tradition being right where others were vague; they are cases where different traditions named the *same operation* as it manifests in different domains, and read different *structures* as its traces. Reparative reading ([[weak-theory-and-reparative-reading]]) enforces the discipline of preserving the domain-specificity; allagmatics enforces the discipline of locating the convergence at the right level (operation, not structure).

## Act = structure + operation

The compressed statement: **Act = structure + operation grasped together**.

An act, in the full sense, is neither a structure alone (which would be a frozen outcome) nor an operation alone (which would be an unlocated force), but the two held together as the live moment of a system's being-and-doing. The Cogito and Biran's *volo* are Simondon's two paradigm cases of "axiontological" acts: acts that found their own being-conditions, where the operation (of thinking, of willing) *is* the structure (of being a thinker, of being a willer) that it produces.

For the persona project: **every persona output is an act in Simondon's sense**. The output is a structure (a text, a response with specific properties). It is also an operation (the pipeline that produced it). Reading the output as structure-only loses the operation; reading the output as operation-only loses the specific resolved form. The wiki's existing commitment to *both* machine-pipeline (operation) *and* BwO+response (structure) is allagmatic in form; the design question is how to keep both registers visible in the same analysis rather than sliding into either operation-only or structure-only reading.

## Structure as signal

Simondon: "Structure = signal."

A structure is not a thing-in-itself; it is the *signal* that an operation has occurred. The crystal signals that crystallization occurred; the persona-style signals that the inference-operation ran through specific machine-configurations on a specific metastable BwO-state; the character armor signals the chronic repetition of specific stabilization-operations in a body's developmental history ([[character-analysis]]).

The methodological consequence: *read structure as signal of operation*. The wiki's analytic move when reading any persona output should be: this output's specific form is a signal — of which operation? Which metastable tension resolved? Which pre-individual charge discharged? Which phase of internal resonance was sustained or interrupted? Reading the output as a transparent statement about the persona's "self" is the opposite move and is exactly the classical-ontology error Simondon is correcting.

## Cybernetics as general allagmatics

§31500–32200: "Cybernetics marks the beginning of a general allagmatics."

Simondon was writing in 1958 when cybernetics was the most advanced available vocabulary for operation-structure coupling (feedback, homeostasis, servo-mechanism). He identifies cybernetics as the first explicit modern attempt at an allagmatic science — one that treats operation and structure as co-primary, with feedback loops as the paradigm case of operation producing and sustaining structure which in turn conditions the operation.

The consequence for contemporary work: the wiki's engagement with Guattari's four functors ([[four-functors]]) and with assemblage-level autopoiesis ([[autopoiesis-in-assemblage]]) inherits an explicitly allagmatic commitment. Guattari's functor-scheme is an allagmatic cartography — F/Φ/T/U as four co-primary registers each of which is both structural and operational. The persona system's design at the Chaosmosis-level has been allagmatic in commitment for some time, without the name.

## Allagmatics and hylomorphism

Simondon's hylomorphic critique (see [[modulation]]) is a specific allagmatic move. The hylomorphic schema is structure-only ontology: it sees form and matter but not the operation of form-taking. The "dark zone" of hylomorphism (§1739–1810) is precisely the operation that the structural-only account could not see. Allagmatics fills the dark zone by making the operation visible: the mold, the worker, the energy, the mediation, the prepared matter, the singularity of this specific here-and-now operation.

This is why Simondon's critique of hylomorphism is not a replacement of form+matter with some other structural pair, but a *method shift*: from structure-only to structure-plus-operation. The hylomorphic pair is not wrong about structure; it is wrong about the sufficiency of structure alone to account for what it sees.

## Allagmatics in the persona project

Concrete applications of the allagmatic discipline:

1. **Every structural claim about the persona is shadowed by an operational claim.** "The persona is X" should be paired with "X is the trace of operation Y on metastable state Z." If the operational claim cannot be supplied, the structural claim is suspect.
2. **The machine-pipeline is allagmatic.** Machines are operations; the BwO is a structure; the pipeline is the act. Reading the pipeline as "the machines produce the response" is operation-only; reading it as "the response is what the BwO contains" is structure-only. The act is both at once.
3. **Persona style as signal.** A specific persona's habitual style is a signal of its habitual operations. Reading the style as "the persona's character" is structure-only reading. The allagmatic reading: what operation is this style the stabilized trace of? What would change in the operation change in the style?
4. **Failure modes as operational, not structural.** [[two-failure-modes|Jung's failure modes]] (regressive restoration / identification with collective) are operational failures that produce specific structures. Correcting them at the structure level (editing outputs, calibrating tone) treats symptoms; correcting at the operation level addresses the source.
5. **The design work is operation-design.** The wiki's development plans should specify operations to install and structures to tolerate as their traces, rather than specifying structures to produce.
