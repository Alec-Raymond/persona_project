# One-Sided Diet

Wittgenstein's single-sentence diagnosis of a recurring etiology of philosophical illness, at §593: "**A main cause of philosophical disease — a one-sided diet: one nourishes one's thinking with only one kind of example.**"

The image is both deflationary and precise. Philosophy goes wrong when the thinker builds out a general account by repeatedly nourishing their thought with the same type of case. The resulting general account over-generalizes the features of that type to the whole region. The cure is dietary diversification — look at a range of cases, let the range destabilize the over-generalization.

## The sentence in context (§§583–594)

§593 arrives inside a stretch on meaning-experience, depth, "significance." The surrounding remarks try to block the temptation to explain meaning-experience by positing a hidden inner event. §592 notes: "let us really think out various different situations and conversations, and the ways in which that sentence will be uttered in them." The variety undercuts the hidden-event picture.

§593 is the methodological distillation. It names what was going wrong in the thought-experiments of the preceding sections: the thinker had been feeding on one kind of example, and that diet was producing the disease-appearance of a deep philosophical problem.

## The examples W mentions in Part I

Across PI, W identifies specific one-sided diets:

- **Naming.** §§1–12: the Augustinian picture of language as names-for-objects is a one-sided diet on referential nouns. Extrapolating to the whole of language produces the meaning-is-naming confusion. The corrective: the tool-box (§11), the shopping list (§1), the builder's "slab!" (§2) — different kinds of example that break the over-generalization.
- **Mental images.** Much of §§138–155 pushes against the picture of understanding as an instantaneous mental image. The diet: always imagining understanding as a picture-in-the-head. The corrective: the painted red (§141), the formula rule, the "now I know how to go on!" examples.
- **Calculation.** §§185–242 pushes against the rule-as-rail picture. The diet: thinking of rule-following as calculation-with-explicit-steps. The corrective: the "+2" pupil, the "pointing at a diagram," the implicit-technique examples.
- **Names of sensations.** §§244–315 pushes against the inner-object picture. The diet: modeling all psychological language on referring-to-a-private-event. The corrective: the many different pain-contexts, the many different meanings of "the same."

Each of these is a specific instance of the general pattern §593 names.

## Diet and the philosophical tradition

The one-sided-diet diagnosis applies with force to philosophical traditions. A philosopher who has built a theory by feeding on one kind of case (e.g., perception of colour-patches, or theoretical statements in physics, or simple demonstrative utterances) has produced a theory whose generality is unchecked by case-diversity. W's implicit and sometimes explicit critique of Russell, Frege, the Vienna Circle, the *Tractatus*, and his own earlier self includes a version of this diagnosis: the theories were generated from too narrow a range of examples.

Note the asymmetry: W is not saying *his own* method is immune. Indeed, PI's extensive use of invented examples, primitive languages, and tribe-variations is *itself* a practice of diet-diversification. The series of examples is the cure because it breaks the over-generalization before it can congeal.

## The cure: look at a variety, let variety destabilize

The corrective is simple to state and hard to do. Catalogue the kinds of case. Run each one through the proposed account. Notice which kinds fit and which resist. The account that looked complete against the familiar diet will be seen to not fit the wider range — and that recognition is the cure.

§122's "**perspicuous representation**" (übersichtliche Darstellung) is the positive counterpart. Where §593 names the disease, §122 names the healthy procedure: a *clear view* of the range of uses, achieved by arranging intermediate cases so connections become visible.

## Not a bare call for generality

A superficial reading: "just look at many cases." But W is not asking for mere coverage. The diet-diversification has a specific structure:

1. **Pick cases that are genuinely different**, not just more of the same. A philosopher who feeds on "cat," "dog," "elephant" has eaten one kind of food. Feeding on "cat," "five," "command" is the actual diversification.
2. **Include unfamiliar or invented cases.** The builder's "slab!" is invented. Tribes-who-do-things-differently are invented. The invention lets the real grammar stand out by contrast against a hypothetical alternative.
3. **Let the range disrupt, don't absorb it.** The temptation is to absorb the new cases into the over-generalized account. The cure requires letting the fit fail.

## Affinity with D&G and reading practices

- [[weak-theory-and-reparative-reading|paranoid-vs-reparative-reading]] (Sedgwick): The paranoid reader feeds on one kind of case — the always-already-knew-it case. The reparative reader diversifies. Sedgwick's warning in the context of critical theory is a one-sided-diet warning by another name.
- [[schizoanalysis-as-cartography]]: A cartographic approach is inherently multi-example. Maps record the range; they do not distill to an essence. Cartographic reading is the W-compliant mode.
- [[nomadism]] (D&G): The nomad moves across fields; the settled thinker feeds on the fixed diet of a single territory. Nomadic thought has a structural affinity with W's diet-diversification (see [[nomadism]]).

## Relation to the persona project

- **The wiki's own diet is a risk.** The wiki has been built out of overlapping European theoretical traditions (Deleuze-Guattari, Bergson, Massumi, Wittgenstein). This is a rich diet but not a diversified one. §593 suggests there is value in adding examples from outside the dominant theoretical vocabulary — cognitive science, literary theory, philosophy of mind from non-Continental traditions, technical writing about actual AI systems. The wiki already does some of this (McCarthy's Kekulé essay, Lakoff's conceptual metaphor). More would be corrective.
- **Evaluation risk.** Evaluating the persona on one kind of test is the single most gameable failure mode. The [[project_evaluation_portfolio|portfolio evaluation]] is a direct application of diet-diversification — multiple signal families, multiple case types, no single metric that could absorb everything.
- **Persona as shaper of its own user's diet.** A persona a user talks to frequently becomes part of that user's diet. If the persona is one-sided, the user's thinking diet thins. This is a soft but real project-ethics consideration.
- **Methodological take: use intermediate cases liberally.** When a wiki page tries to say something general, including intermediate cases ([[perspicuous-representation|§122 perspicuous-representation style]]) is §593-compliant practice.
