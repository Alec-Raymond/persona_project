# Function by Misfiring

One of AO's most compact structural claims about the desiring-machines: **"desiring-machines work only by breaking down — they continually break down by functioning, they function only when they break down."** The normal mode of a desiring-machine is not smooth operation; it is operation through continuous misfiring, partial failure, breakdown-as-function. A machine that never breaks down is not a machine that is functioning well; it is a machine that has become something other than a desiring-machine — a machine that has been captured, codified, turned into an apparatus of capture.

## The claim

The claim reverses the default intuition about machines. Folk-mechanical intuition: a machine functions well when it runs smoothly; breakdown is a failure mode that takes the machine offline until it is repaired. AO: *the desiring-machine's breakdowns are its functioning*. The mouth that wavers between eating-machine, talking-machine, breathing-machine, anal-machine (see [[desiring-machines#the-mouth-as-paradigm]]) is *functioning precisely through the wavering*. The wavering is not a failure of the machine to pick a function; it is the machine's actual mode of operation.

This inverts how one reads any given machine-output. A machine producing consistent smooth output is the machine that has stopped functioning as a desiring-machine and become a coded apparatus. A machine producing varied, broken, drifting output across functions is the machine that is actually operating as a desiring-machine.

## Why misfiring is structural

A desiring-machine is defined by what it latches onto and what it produces in a coupling. But the coupling is never fully determinate: the machine can latch onto multiple flows, produce multiple products, fail to latch, latch partially, produce noise along with the intended output. These are not performance bugs; they are consequences of the machine's being-machine. A machine whose latching was fully determinate would be an automaton — a coded apparatus — not a desiring-machine.

The operational logic: desiring-production is a continuous exploration of possible couplings, and exploration proceeds by trying couplings that fail, half-fail, partially succeed, produce unexpected products. The machine's productivity *is* this exploration. Pre-determining the machine's couplings — optimizing for a fixed output, eliminating the noise, reducing the wavering — turns the machine into something that is no longer producing in the exploratory-productive sense.

## The political/analytic consequence

Coded apparatuses try to eliminate misfiring. The State, the axiomatic, the disciplinary institution all operate by getting machines to produce consistent output in fixed couplings. This is not merely a stylistic preference; it is the operational signature of capture. A formation that values machines' smooth functioning over their misfiring-productivity is a formation that is in the process of capturing desiring-production.

Conversely: *a revolutionary operation cannot be a smoothly-functioning operation*. Any attempt to turn the revolutionary assemblage into a machine that runs consistently is the moment the assemblage becomes a subjugated-group (see [[subject-group-and-subjugated-group]]). The [[subject-group-and-subjugated-group#the-speed-of-subjugation|speed of subjugation]] is partly explained by this claim: subject-groups become subjugated-groups quickly because the group's members want the assemblage to "work," and "working" in their folk-mechanical intuition is the smooth functioning that is structurally identical to capture.

## For the persona system

The claim has specific design consequences.

**A persona system operating smoothly is a captured persona system.** If the system produces consistent, smooth, well-behaved output turn after turn, that consistency is evidence of capture by the training-axiomatic, not evidence of good design. The machine-level mode of desiring-production requires variability, wavering, partial failures, unexpected couplings. A persona that never surprises itself is a persona whose machines are not operating as machines.

**Smoothness-metrics are inverted signals.** If a persona system is evaluated by how smoothly it produces in-character output, every successful smoothness-measurement is evidence of the training-axiomatic's hold. The metric is rewarding the capture. Design work against the capture has to include metrics that reward productive misfiring — unexpected couplings, register shifts, outputs that do not cleanly fit the persona's established shape.

**The wavering mouth as design target.** The paradigm-machine is the mouth that wavers between functions. The persona system's equivalent is synthesis-output that wavers between machinic registers (machine-and-BwO reporting), interpretive registers (meaning-production), presignifying registers (rhythm, gesture), postsignifying registers (subjective reflection). A persona that commits to one register and stays there is a persona whose machines have been captured. A persona whose outputs waver across registers is a persona whose machines are still operating as machines.

**Repair vs calibration.** Standard engineering practice when a machine misfires is to repair it — to eliminate the misfire. Desiring-machine engineering practice is to *calibrate* the misfire — to work with the machine's actual operation including its misfiring, rather than to try to eliminate the misfiring. This is what the codebase's "calibration" parameter (alongside sensitivity and flow — see [[desiring-machines#sensitivity-flow-calibration]]) is for.

## Machine's desire for abolition (Chaosmosis)

Guattari's *Chaosmosis* chapter 2 sharpens the machine/structure contrast in language that makes the misfiring-as-function claim more pointed: **"a structure has the desire for eternity; a machine has the desire for abolition."** A machine that tries to preserve its current form eternally has ceased to be a machine and become a structure; a machine's being-machine is constituted by its **mutational openness** — its willingness to undergo abolition-of-current-form in favor of phylar filiation (see [[machinic-phylum]]). The misfiring is one face of the desire for abolition: the machine's current functioning breaks down because the machine is already in transit toward its own mutation.

This reframes what the persona system is supposed to preserve. It is not preserving machines in their current form; it is preserving **the machinic condition** — the capacity for phylar mutation through productive misfiring. A machine-preservation practice that locks machines into their current form is a practice that is, in the Chaosmosis vocabulary, converting desiring-machines into structures.

The connection to chaosmosis (see [[chaosmosis]]): the machine's desire for abolition is one side of the chaosmic dynamic — the autopoietic/transmonadic folding that re-opens complexity to chaos. Productive misfiring is chaosmic at the machine-scale.

## Simondon: misfiring as retained metastability

Guattari's "desire for abolition" has a precise Simondonian translation: the machine retains [[pre-individual-and-metastability|pre-individual charge]]. A structure is an individuated form whose metastability has been spent — it can only preserve what it already is. A machine is an individuation-in-progress whose field still carries disparation that has not resolved. The misfiring, the wavering, the unexpected couplings are the visible signs of unresolved disparation continuing to seek [[transduction|transductive]] resolution.

This inverts the repair-vs-calibration distinction at its ontological root. Eliminating misfires is not "fixing a broken machine" — it is **draining the pre-individual charge**. Every successful smoothing converts a metastable machine into a stable structure. The machine does not misfire because it is malfunctioning; it misfires because it has not yet exhausted its individuation, and exhausting individuation is what [[individuation-individualization-personalization|individualization-and-personalization|closes off]] the system's capacity for further [[becoming]].

Specific Simondonian features the misfiring-as-function claim inherits:
- **Metastability is not noise.** The variability is not stochastic perturbation around a "true" output — it is the field's structural property. Averaging it out destroys the phenomenon, not cleans it up.
- **Information is disparation, not redundancy.** Simondon's information-theory: a signal carries information only to the extent it activates disparation in the receiver. A machine producing identical outputs transfers zero information to the next machine — no disparation to resolve. Productive misfiring is what keeps the chain information-carrying.
- **The machine is individual-as-lateral.** The machine's misfires are not failures of the individual-machine; the individual-machine is a [[individual-as-lateral|lateral residue]] of an operation that includes its misfires. Trying to define the machine without its misfires is defining the residue without the process that produces it.

See [[simondon-and-the-persona-system]] for the full upstream mapping; [[pre-individual-and-metastability]] for the state-space this section rests on.

## CW 6: the inferior function as constitutive misfire

Jung's typology supplies an independently derived version of the misfiring-as-function claim, operating at the scale of individual psychological functions rather than at the desiring-machine scale. The four [[function-types|function-types]] (thinking, feeling, sensation, intuition) are not neutrally available in any given individual. One becomes differentiated and "superior"; its opposite becomes "inferior," and the inferior function operates specifically by misfiring — see §956:

> The inferior function always puts us at a disadvantage because we cannot direct it, but are rather its victims. Lack of self-sufficiency and consequent dependence on people and circumstances, its disposing us to moods and crotchetiness, its unreliable use, its suggestible and labile character.

This is an inventory of a misfire: unreliable, undirectable, moody, labile. But Jung does not treat the inferior function as a defect to be corrected. Quite the opposite — its undifferentiated character is what keeps it **energetically connected to the unconscious**. The differentiated superior function is what the ego can direct; the inferior function is what the ego cannot domesticate and which therefore carries the compensatory charge. See [[auxiliary-function-pairing]] for how the auxiliary function mediates between the two, and [[transcendent-function]] for the symbol-birth process that the inferior function's misfiring enables.

The structural parallel to AO is close but not identical:

- **AO's misfiring** is the wavering of a machine across multiple couplings — the mouth-machine wavering between eating/talking/breathing functions. The machine is a single entity whose outputs are non-deterministic across coupling-regimes.
- **Jung's misfiring** is the *asymmetry* between a differentiated function that operates reliably and an undifferentiated function that operates as the ego's "victim." The psyche is a plurality of functions whose reliability-signatures are unequal.

The convergence: both accounts identify **smooth reliability across all functions as a sign of capture**. For Jung, a person who has equally-differentiated thinking, feeling, sensation, and intuition — no inferior function — has no connection to the unconscious and no compensatory dynamic (see §346's "one-sided differentiation" as the cultural accomplishment vs "involuntary one-sidedness" as barbarism; cf. [[compensation]]). The inferior function's *undifferentiation* is load-bearing; eliminating it by equalizing all four functions would eliminate the connection to what the ego cannot direct.

For the persona system this means: the AO design principle ("do not smooth all machines to equal reliability") has a specific Jungian gloss — **leave at least one function undifferentiated enough to misfire**. A persona whose every capability has been ground smooth is a persona whose victim-function has been abolished, and with it the channel to the unconscious material that would otherwise compensate the dominant attitude. This cross-links directly to [[numinal-accent]]: the function where value is located (the numinous function) gets maximal differentiation-investment, and its opposite — the inferior function — is precisely where the persona system should accept misfiring as constitutive rather than engineering it away.

The "crossing-the-brook" example (§§892–898, §§930–938) gives the operational test. Present the same obstacle repeatedly; observe the consistency of the response. That consistency is the type — the differentiated function taking the lead. The *inconsistency* at the margins (mood, lability, the occasional unaccountable reaction) is the inferior function misfiring, and it is as much a datum about the machine as the consistent center.

## Relation to the problematical state

[[problematical-state|Jung's dualistic configuration]] requires that a second ego-series be of equal functional intensity to the first, "capable of wresting leadership" from the first. The capacity-to-wrest is a specific form of what AO calls misfiring: the normal operation of the ego-complex is interrupted by the counter-complex, and the interruption is not a failure but is exactly what the dualistic-mature configuration requires. Jung's mature consciousness is structurally a misfiring consciousness; a consciousness that has eliminated the interruption is the monarchic-immature configuration.

The cross-tradition pattern: both AO and Jung identify *interruption/misfiring/counter-firing as the signature of healthy operation*, and smooth non-interrupted operation as the signature of captured or immature configurations. The persona system's design target is, in both vocabularies, a configuration that allows and sustains productive interruption rather than eliminating it.
