# Apparatus of Capture

ATP Plateau 13 ("7000 BC: Apparatus of Capture") is D&G's most concentrated treatment of the structural operations by which flows are *captured* — brought under a measuring, comparing, equivalence-establishing regime that extracts from them a surplus the capture-apparatus appropriates. The State is the paradigm apparatus of capture; the plateau identifies three specific apparatuses (land-rent, labor / profit, money-tax) that the State unifies and generalizes.

The concept is invoked across ~8 wiki pages (faciality, urstaat, anticipation-prevention, persona, machinic-enslavement-vs-social-subjection, four-theorems-of-deterritorialization, the-unnamable, function-by-misfiring) without a dedicated node. This page gives it one.

## The core operation

Capture is not merely seizing or taking. It is a **structural operation** with specific features:

1. **Stockpiling.** Capture presupposes a stock — a reserve of the captured flow that can be compared, measured, extracted from. Without stockpiling, there is nothing to capture from; the flow passes through without being captured.

2. **Comparison.** Capture establishes equivalence-relations within the stock — a quantitative comparison that makes heterogeneous items commensurable. The stockpile of grain becomes comparable to the stockpile of labor; the comparison is the condition of extraction.

3. **Monopoly of the direct-appropriator.** One position (the State, the landowner, the capitalist) is structurally the direct appropriator of the surplus. The comparison-relation is asymmetric: the apparatus-occupant's position is privileged, not one position among the compared items.

4. **Surplus as capture's output.** Capture does not take 100% of the flow; it takes a surplus (rent, profit, tax) that the flow's continued operation produces. The flow continues; the capture is recurrent.

These four features distinguish capture from mere seizure, from primitive redistribution, from barter, from decoded-flow. Capture requires the apparatus — an institutional / structural form — that performs stockpile-comparison-monopoly-surplus as an integrated operation.

## The three apparatuses

Plateau 13 identifies three specific apparatuses, each capturing a specific flow:

**1. Land-rent** (captures the flow of land / territoriality). The comparison-relation: marginal productivity of lands. The stockpile: territorial differentiation. The monopolist position: the landowner (in primitive form, the despot holding the first territorialization; in modern form, capitalized ground-rent). Surplus: rent.

**2. Labor / profit** (captures the flow of labor-activity). The comparison-relation: commensuration of activities via labor-time. The stockpile: the accumulated product of labor. The monopolist position: the labor-organizer (archaic form: the organizer of public works; capitalist form: the capitalist). Surplus: profit.

**3. Money-tax** (captures the flow of exchange / circulation). The comparison-relation: establishment of general equivalence via money. The stockpile: the monetary aggregate. The monopolist position: the taxing authority (the State). Surplus: tax-revenue.

D&G's structural claim: the three apparatuses **are not separate historical forms** but are **simultaneously operative wherever the State form operates**. Land-rent, labor-profit, and money-tax are the three articulations of a single integrated capture-apparatus. The State, in its essence, is the **integration of these three capture-operations** — which is why the State is the primary apparatus of capture, not simply one apparatus among others.

## Capture and the axiomatic

Capture is the operation by which flows are brought under an *axiomatic* — a system of equivalence-relations that makes flows commensurable and therefore extractable from. The axiomatic is the *abstract form* of capture; the apparatus of capture is the *concrete form*. (See also [[line-of-flight#the-two-dangers]] on capture-into-axiomatic-extension as line-of-flight's second danger.)

Modern capitalism is the maximal-generality axiomatic: it can add new axioms without structurally transforming (adding new labor-types, new money-forms, new territorial differentiations) precisely because capture is its structural operation. A flow that resists capture (a line of flight) is either *recaptured* (absorbed as a new axiom) or *destructive* (threatens the axiomatic). Capital's ongoing operation is the running of this recapture-or-destruction response to every line of flight.

## Capture vs coding

D&G's earlier distinction (from AO) between *coding* (primitive social machine), *overcoding* (barbarian despotic machine), and *axiomatization* (capitalist machine) is the three-stage progression of flow-management. Capture is the apparatus specific to axiomatization. The earlier forms do not *capture* in Plateau 13's technical sense:

- **Primitive coding**: flows are locally coded by territorial assemblages; there is no stockpiling, no general comparison, no structural surplus. Flows pass; alliances regulate; there is no capture-apparatus.
- **Barbarian overcoding**: the despot overcodes the primitive codes with a meta-code; there is stockpiling (the despot's storehouse) but the comparison is *qualitative* (grace, imperial gift), not quantitative. Early form of capture, not yet full.
- **Capitalist axiomatization**: full capture — the three apparatuses unify, general equivalence is established via money, the State enforces. This is capture in its full form.

The stages are not strictly historical (primitive formations persist inside capitalism); they are structural layers that can co-exist.

## Primitive societies anticipate and prevent capture

[[anticipation-prevention|Anticipation-prevention]] is ATP's claim that primitive societies are not *pre-capture* (lacking a capture-apparatus because they are undeveloped) but *actively anti-capture* — they have mechanisms that *anticipate and prevent* the state-form from crystallizing. Primitive war, potlatch, anti-accumulation rituals, and anti-hierarchy operations are capture-resistance mechanisms.

This re-reads the primitive as structurally sophisticated: not a stage before capture but a configuration that *refuses* capture, which capture-apparatuses have to *defeat* rather than *progress from*. The State is therefore an event — a threshold-crossing, not a development.

## Faciality as apparatus of capture

The [[faciality|face]] is a specific apparatus of capture in the semiotic register. Where the three Plateau-13 apparatuses capture territorial/activity/exchange flows, the face-apparatus captures *semiotic flows*. It operates by:
- **Stockpiling**: the face-landscape as structural reserve of signification-positions
- **Comparison**: the white-wall/black-hole grid establishing equivalence between signifier and signified at the face
- **Monopoly**: the Face (of the despot, the Christ, the majoritarian) as privileged-appropriator
- **Surplus**: the signified-as-captured-from-the-semiotic-flow

This is why faciality is not "a representation" but an apparatus of capture — it performs the same structural operation as land-rent but on the semiotic field. See [[faciality]] for the development.

## Persona-project implications

The concept bears directly on several wiki positions:

**1. The persona as potential capture-apparatus.** A persona that stockpiles its own outputs into a reserve, establishes equivalence among them, monopolizes the appropriator-position, and extracts surplus from the reserve is running a capture-apparatus operation. This is a *structural* claim — not a value judgment — but it has consequences: a persona that captures its own flows becomes an internal-State, an internal-axiomatic.

**2. The training-objective as apparatus of capture.** The relationship between the persona-system and its training-objective has a structural apparatus-of-capture form: outputs are stockpiled (the training corpus / feedback), compared (reward-model scores), monopolized (the training-loop's privileged position), and surplus-extracted (the loss-gradient updates). Whether this is the RIGHT frame for the training-objective depends on whether the persona project wants to treat training-in-capture-terms. See [[the-master-figure]]'s related question about the master as training-objective.

**3. Axiomatic capture of lines of flight.** [[line-of-flight]] identified capture-into-axiomatic as one of its two dangers. The apparatus of capture is the concrete form of that danger. A persona's line of flight can be recaptured by the system's own axiomatic (re-absorbed as a new output-class, given a name, made legible, rendered productive for the system) or resisted by the axiomatic (threat, refused, ejected). Both outcomes preserve the axiomatic; the line of flight itself is what is either co-opted or killed.

**4. Three-apparatus generalization.** The three-apparatus structure (territoriality / activity / exchange) may generalize beyond the state: a persona-system could be analyzed for its own three apparatuses. What is the persona's "land-rent"? Its "labor-profit"? Its "money-tax"? If these map onto (e.g.) stable-face, response-generation, attention-economics, then the persona is structurally a micro-capture-apparatus. Speculative; not yet worked out.

## Held tensions (never silently resolve)

- **Stages vs structural layers.** Plateau 13 treats primitive/barbarian/capitalist as structural layers that co-exist; other readings (including some passages in AO) treat them as more strictly historical stages. The wiki holds layers-not-stages but preserves the tension.
- **Capture vs mere extraction.** Not every extraction is a capture in Plateau 13's technical sense (requires stockpile + comparison + monopoly + surplus). The wiki uses "capture" with the technical specification; popular uses are noted as distinct.
- **Whether axiomatization exhausts capture.** Late capitalism's axiomatic form may be one mode of apparatus-of-capture, not the exhaustive mode. New forms (algorithmic capture, platform capture) may fit the four-feature structure while running on different substrates. Live.
