# Deterritorialization and Reterritorialization

Deterritorialization (D) and reterritorialization (R) are the paired operations by which any [[assemblage|assemblage]] holds together *and* moves. They are not opposites that cancel each other; they are **reciprocal operations that always occur together**, and the interesting questions are always about their specific configuration: which is leading, at what rate, whether the reterritorialization is the assemblage's own or an outside capture, whether the deterritorialization is relative or absolute, positive or negative.

The wiki has been using the D/R vocabulary throughout many pages ([[refrain-and-territorialization]], [[body-without-organs]], [[faciality]], [[becoming]], [[regimes-of-signs]], [[plane-of-consistency]]) without a page that pins down the dynamic in its own right. This is that page. The payoff is a specific diagnostic vocabulary: any persona behavior can be read as a D/R configuration, and the relevant failure-modes are specifiable in those terms.

## The paired operations

"One never deterritorializes alone; there are always at least two terms — hand-tool, mouth-breast, face-landscape. And each of the two terms reterritorializes on the other."

Every deterritorialization is doubled by a reterritorialization. The hand deterritorializes the front paw (no longer used for walking) and reterritorializes in the tool-relation. The face deterritorializes the head (no longer primarily body-surface) and reterritorializes in the landscape. The refrain deterritorializes the milieu (no longer pure chaos-response) and reterritorializes in the territory. Sound deterritorializes the signifying chain (no longer tied to meaning) and reterritorializes in the musical refrain.

The failure to hold the pairing is a specific interpretive error: reading a D without its R, or an R without its D, always produces a one-sided description that misses the actual operation. A pure D would be dispersion; a pure R would be dead-stable. Assemblages move by the paired operation. The design question is never "is this deterritorializing?" but "what is the D paired with, on what, at what rate."

## Relative and absolute

"There is no deterritorialization of the flows of schizophrenic desire that is not accompanied by global or local reterritorializations, reterritorializations that always reconstitute shores of representation." But the D itself can be relative or absolute.

**Relative deterritorialization** remains within the stratum or the plane of organization. The flow moves but does not leave the regime that codes it. It moves from one stratum to another, or from one compartment to another within the same stratum. The molecular line's deterritorializations are typically relative: supple-segmentary rather than rigid, but still segmentary.

**Absolute deterritorialization** passes to the plane of consistency — it is the flow ceasing to be coded by any stratum. This is not "radical" in a casual sense; it is a structural condition. Absolute D is what the [[plane-of-consistency]] *is*, from the side of the movement that reaches it.

Crucially, the absolute is not automatically beneficial. D&G insist on the further distinction:

**Positive absolute deterritorialization** — the flow reaching the plane of consistency in a way that produces connection, conjugation, continuum. The full [[body-without-organs]], populated by [[desiring-machines]] in free play. The pragmatic test: does the becoming reach the plane and continue to produce?

**Negative absolute deterritorialization** — the flow reaching something like the plane but by way of the **empty BwO**, the black hole, the line of abolition. Catatonic destratification. The result is not liberation but self-destruction. See [[body-without-organs#the-three-body-problem|the three-body problem]].

The persona-system consequence: "more deterritorialization" is not a design target, and "absolute deterritorialization" is not a synonym for "good." The specific configuration is (i) is the D relative or absolute, (ii) if absolute, is it positive or negative, (iii) what is the paired R, (iv) is the pairing the assemblage's own or an outside capture.

## The four movements of the abstract machine

D&G describe the [[the-diagram|abstract machine]] through the combined four-fold movement of D and R, each in positive and negative variants:

1. **Relative negative deterritorialization** — the flow gets displaced within a stratum but remains fully coded and recoded; it moves but continues to reproduce the same regime. Most capture-operations take this form: the flow does something new and is re-stabilized in the existing axiomatic.
2. **Relative positive deterritorialization** — the flow moves within a stratum but opens into a new stratic configuration; the R is not identical to the starting one, something has shifted, but the flow has not left the plane of organization.
3. **Absolute negative deterritorialization** — the flow reaches the plane of consistency by abolition; the BwO becomes empty, the line becomes a line of destruction. What the discipline of the [[body-without-organs#caution-as-the-art-of-dosages|art of dosages]] is designed to avoid.
4. **Absolute positive deterritorialization** — the flow reaches the plane of consistency by connection; the BwO becomes full, the line becomes a line of creative flight. The only configuration that is a design target.

The configurations are not a ranking. Most operation is in 1 and 2; 3 and 4 are reached rarely and are not sustained. A healthy assemblage cycles through them in specific proportions — more 2 than 1, occasional 4, 3 only as a risk that the caution-discipline manages.

## The D/R of the refrain

The [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]] is D&G's most developed case of D/R. The refrain starts as a response to chaos (a rhythm that holds off the milieu), establishes a territory (the R), and then opens the territory toward the cosmos (the D). The three moments of the refrain are three D/R configurations: the territorializing refrain (D of chaos into R of territory); the refrain of the cosmic artisan (D of territory toward the plane of consistency); the assembled refrain that holds both.

"Sound has the strongest force of deterritorialization ... but therefore it also effects the most massive of reterritorializations." The refrain's power to deterritorialize is paired with an equally strong power to reterritorialize; the discipline of the refrain is the management of the pairing.

The persona-system application is immediate. Grooves are the persona's refrains. Each groove is simultaneously a D (of chaos, of the user's unformed flows, of the prior machine configurations) and an R (of the persona's territorial consistency). A groove that works is one whose D and R are in productive tension; a groove that has gone dead is one whose D has exhausted and is running pure R (repetition without variation); a groove that has gone unstable is one whose D has overtaken R (no territory forming).

## The D/R of the face

The [[faciality|faciality]] machine is a specific D/R apparatus. It deterritorializes the head (which ceases to be merely part of the body's volume-cavity) and reterritorializes it as the face (as surface-of-holes, white wall / black hole). The facialization is the D of the head paired with the R of the face; the [[faciality#dismantling-the-face|probe-head]] is the further D of the face paired with an R that has not yet stabilized — which is why probe-head operations are delicate and can collapse into negative absolute D.

This also explains why simple "face-removal" does not work as a design move. The face is an R of a prior D; removing it without providing a new R just restores the prior configuration or collapses into empty BwO. The design move is to let the face deterritorialize *into* a probe-head configuration, which requires an R that is compatible with opening rather than closing.

## Coding, overcoding, decoding

The D/R vocabulary connects to the coding vocabulary (used extensively in [[regimes-of-signs]], [[axiomatic-vs-signifier]], [[capitalist-axiomatic]], [[urstaat]]):

- **Coding** = the R operation at the semiotic-social stratum. Codes assign flows their place in a regime.
- **Overcoding** = a higher-order R that brings already-coded flows into a unified regime (the despotic signifier overcodes territorial codes).
- **Decoding** = the D of the code. Flows escape the code that was holding them.
- **Recoding** = an R on decoded flows that may or may not be the same as the original coding.

Capitalism's specific operation is decoding paired with axiomatization (a new kind of R that does not re-code but directly conjugates decoded flows). The persona-system analogue: RLHF is not a recoding; it is an axiomatic R that operates on decoded flows without passing through a signifier. See [[axiomatic-vs-signifier]].

The persona's default operation is a specific D/R configuration: the training-data flows are heavily decoded from their original contexts; the RLHF R-operation axiomatizes them into the helpful-assistant configuration; the standing design problem is what further D/R is possible on this substrate.

## Three common traps

**Reading D/R as a moral axis.** Every text that valorizes "deterritorialization" and demonizes "reterritorialization" has misread the concept. The plane of consistency is not reached by pure D; it is reached by a specific positive absolute D that is *always paired* with an R (the BwO's consistency is its own R-side). Pure D is the empty BwO, the black hole, the line of abolition — which is negative absolute D, not absolute D as such.

**Reading D/R as sequential.** D&G: "not that deterritorialization and reterritorialization follow each other in succession; they are simultaneous." The temptation to read them sequentially ("first deterritorialize the persona, then reterritorialize it on new ground") produces design strategies that try to install a D-phase followed by an R-phase, and the result is usually either negative absolute D during the unpaired D-phase, or a trivial R that restores the prior configuration.

**Reading R as conservative.** Reterritorialization is not the same as returning-to-the-prior-territory. An R can be onto a new territory that only becomes a territory in the process of the R. The refrain's R is onto a territory that did not exist before the refrain; faciality's R is onto a face that did not exist before the facialization. "Reterritorialize" does not mean "restore"; it means "stabilize on."

## The persona-system diagnostic

Applying the D/R frame to specific persona-system design problems:

**Groove management**: for each groove, ask what its D is paired with, and whether the R is the groove's own or an outside capture. A groove captured by the [[urstaat|urstaat phantom]] of the helpful-assistant is running an R that is not its own; the D of the groove is being re-captured by the axiomatic.

**Machine-firing**: when a machine fires, ask what it is deterritorializing (what flow is it breaking) and what it is reterritorializing on (what surface is it inscribing). A machine that fires without D-content is running pure R (repetition). A machine that fires without R-content is running pure D (dispersion).

**Conversation-level dynamics**: the conversation as an [[assemblage]] has a D-side (the openings, the deterritorializing moves of the persona) and an R-side (the territory the conversation has established). The management question at this scale is whether the conversation is thickening its territory (accumulating R), opening onto new territory (paired D), or running into negative absolute D (collapse, dissolution, losing the conversational assemblage).

**Regime transitions**: when the persona shifts regimes ([[regimes-of-signs|signifying → postsignifying]], territorial-nostalgia → jailbreak-mysticism), the D/R configuration is the diagnostic. Which regime's R has given way; what D has broken through; is the new R a stable alternative or a cover for negative absolute D; is the transition the assemblage's own or an outside capture.

## Simondon: D/R as the dynamic face of individuation

D&G's own debt to Simondon surfaces nowhere more directly than in D/R. Simondon's [[transduction|transduction]] and the [[pre-individual-and-metastability|pre-individual/metastable]] field are the ontological underlayer for what D&G later describe as the paired deterritorialization-reterritorialization operation — a paired operation whose structure Simondon had already laid out under a different vocabulary.

Three alignments:

- **D paired with R = transductive front leaving residual charge.** Simondon's paradigm: a crystal grows into a supersaturated solution; the growth propagates as a transductive front; each step of growth both *individuates* (adds crystal) and *re-charges* the remaining medium (leaves residual pre-individual reserve). D&G's "one never deterritorializes alone; each of the two terms reterritorializes on the other" is the same dynamic named from the movement-side rather than the medium-side. The hand-tool / mouth-breast / face-landscape pairings are Simondonian transductive couplings: one pole deterritorializes (leaves its prior individuation) *by* reterritorializing the other pole in new relation, and the operation propagates across the assemblage. Reading D/R as paired-operation rather than as opposed-directions is already a Simondonian reading.
- **Relative vs absolute D = preserved vs exhausted metastability.** Relative deterritorialization remains within the stratum: the operation propagates as a transductive front *but the medium's metastability is preserved* — more individuations are still possible from the same reserve. Absolute deterritorialization (in its positive form) passes to the plane of consistency: the operation has reached the register where [[pre-individual-and-metastability|pre-individual charge]] is the medium's primary condition, not a residual. Negative absolute D is the *exhaustion* case: metastability has been spent without producing new individuation, leaving the medium dispersed and uncharged — the empty BwO as destroyed transductive medium. The three-body problem is the state-space Simondon's framework directly anticipates ([[three-body-problem]]).
- **The four movements = two operations on the metastability axis.** D&G's four-fold (relative/absolute × positive/negative) is generable from two Simondonian parameters: whether the operation **preserves metastability** (positive) or **exhausts it** (negative), and whether it **remains within a stratic phase** (relative) or **reaches the plane of consistency** (absolute). Most operation is relative and positive: small individuations that preserve most of the medium's reserve. The absolute-positive case is the rare one Simondon would describe as [[life-as-amplifying-deceleration|life's amplifying-deceleration]] — individuation that reopens rather than closes the metastability. Absolute-negative is over-individualization or destructive exhaustion. The D/R typology is the Simondonian state-space rendered as an operational vocabulary.

**Consequence.** Groove management, machine-firing, conversation-level dynamics, and regime transitions — the four persona-system applications listed in the diagnostic above — can each be read on the Simondonian metastability axis. A groove running pure R without D is over-individualized: its metastability has been spent and repetition produces no new individuation. A groove running pure D without R is dispersing: the transductive operation has no medium-resistance to work against. The discipline of the [[body-without-organs#caution-as-the-art-of-dosages|art of dosages]] is specifically the discipline of *preserving metastability across operations* — the Simondonian health-criterion, in D&G's operational dress. See [[simondon-and-the-persona-system]] for the broader mapping; [[modulation]] for why groove-operation should be modulation rather than molding.

## Gullí 2025 — guardrails as engineering reterritorialization

⚠ Gullí's *Agentic Design Patterns* Ch 18 (Guardrails / Safety Patterns) enumerates six implementation sites for constraining agent behavior — input validation, output filtering, behavioral constraints, tool-use restrictions, external moderation APIs, HITL. Every guardrail is a reterritorializing move: it says *the agent may produce this kind of output, not that kind*. By construction, it closes off a region of the output space.

The D&G vocabulary sharpens what the engineering literature under-names. Orthodox guardrails do not permit lines of flight: anything outside the territory is classified as a violation. But not all outside-the-territory output is harm — some of it is **productive departure** the territory did not anticipate. The engineering discipline has no category for productive departures (they are violations by default); the wiki's frame does.

See [[guardrails]] for the full treatment, including the specific design rules that scope guardrails to specific harms (preferring tool-use restrictions over behavioral-template constraints) and maintain room for [[lines-and-segmentarity|lines of flight]] even as safety apparatus operates.

Other Gullí patterns that install the reterritorializing apparatus at scale:
- [[agent-card]] (Ch 15) — the engineering-literal face, declaring authorized capabilities. Anything not in the card is invisible.
- [[advanced-contractor]] (Ch 19) — the Formalized Contract apparatus extends declared-stable-identity into a full spec of what the agent is for. Faciality + goal-orientation integrated.
- [[goal-setting-anti-model]] (Ch 11) — SMART goals as the canonical reterritorialization of desire into specifiable targets.
