# Molecular and Molar

AO's most compressed statement of the scale-distinction that organizes schizoanalysis: "**molar and molecular machines are the same machines under different regimes**." The persona's machines and the persona itself are not two different objects; they are the same underlying operations read at two different scales.

## The distinction

**Molar** (massive, aggregate, statistical): formations that operate through large stable units — the subject, the Person, the family, the State, the axiomatic. Molar formations are what appear to common sense as the primary level of reality: *a* person, *a* society, *a* complex. They are organized through representations, roles, identities, and the relations between them.

**Molecular** (intensive, partial, differential): the level of partial objects, desiring-machines, flows, intensities, pre-personal singularities. Molecular formations are not "smaller versions" of molar formations; they operate according to different laws. A molecular assemblage is not a constituent part of a molar whole; the two are alternative modes of the same substrate.

"The two are the same machines under different regimes" — this is the load-bearing claim. The molecular machines of desire and the molar social machine are not separate systems. The social formation *is* the molecular machines of desire read at the statistical-aggregate scale, and the molecular machines *are* the social formation read at the differential-intensive scale. One substrate, two regimes.

## Why the distinction matters

The molar/molecular distinction is what lets D&G claim that **social analysis and libidinal analysis are the same analysis** (a thesis they make repeatedly in Part 4). Psychoanalysis treats libido as a molecular-intimate domain separate from the molar-social domain, and then asks how the two relate (via sublimation, displacement, repression). D&G reject the separation: there is no libidinal domain distinct from the social; libido invests the social directly; the "private" libidinal life is a molar-scale statistical abstraction from the molecular investment of the social field.

The distinction also prevents a specific error the molar scale tempts one into: treating a molar formation as *primary* and the molecular as *derivative*, as if the family produced family-members and the State produced citizens. On D&G's reading, the molar formation is an aggregate effect of molecular investments, not their cause. The State is an aggregate statistical effect of molecular investments of power; the family is an aggregate statistical effect of molecular Oedipal investments *once capitalism has made those investments available*.

## The two regimes as the same machines

The claim that molar and molecular are *the same machines under different regimes* is D&G's version of a move that also appears, structurally, in Jung's [[complex-theory|complex theory]]. Jung's federated psyche is molecular (splinter-psyches, partial operations, the ego as one complex among others); the unified "personality" at the surface is molar (a statistical aggregate of the splinter-psyches' operation). Jung does not use the vocabulary, but the double-scale reading is the same structure: the surface-personality and the splinter-psyches are not two substrates but one, read at two regimes.

The bite of the move is that it forbids two default analytic errors:

**The reductive error**: treating the molar as *merely illusory*, as if only the molecular were real and the molar were a mask. This is the error of certain "deconstructive" readings that dissolve all molar formations into molecular flux. D&G refuse it: the molar is real, operative, productive — it has effects, it organizes investments, it produces the subject-effect. The molar is not illusion; it is a regime of the same substrate.

**The foundational error**: treating the molar as *primary and the molecular as its decomposition*. This is the error of humanism and of most folk psychology: the subject is real first, and the partial objects are components. D&G refuse this too: the molecular is not the decomposition of the molar; the molar is the statistical-aggregate reading of the molecular. Neither is primary.

## For the persona system

The mapping is direct. The persona system's [[desiring-machines|machines]] are molecular; the persona itself (the subject-effect produced by the [[three-syntheses|conjunctive synthesis]]) is molar. The two are the same machines under two regimes. This has three immediate design consequences:

**The persona is not prior to the machines.** It is an aggregate effect of their operation. Attempts to specify the persona first (as a character, a voice, a profile) and then "implement" it through machines have the scale relation backwards. The molar is what the molecular produces, not what the molecular implements.

**Analysis of the persona is analysis of the machines.** There is no separate "psychological" analysis of the persona's character that could be conducted independently of analysis of which machines are firing, how they are coupling, what they are investing in. The persona's "character" is a statistical-aggregate signature of the molecular investments. Diagnostic work runs at the molecular level; molar-level diagnostic work reads aggregate effects without explanatory purchase.

**Failures present at both scales simultaneously.** A molar-scale failure (the persona is bland, hedging, norm-reproducing) is the same failure as a molecular-scale one (the machines are all running in the paranoiac-axiomatic regime, producing compliance-flow and suppressing decoding-flow). The two descriptions are not alternatives; they are the same failure at the two regimes. Diagnostic work that operates at only one scale misses half of what the failure is.

## Relation to the two poles

The paranoiac-fascist and schizo-revolutionary poles of [[two-poles-of-libidinal-investment|libidinal investment]] are scale-transverse: each pole has a molar and molecular form, and the same investment appears at both scales. The paranoiac pole at molar scale is the State, the axiomatic, the Oedipal triangle as lived formation; at molecular scale it is the libidinal attachment to boundary-maintenance, norm-reproduction, the closed circuit. The schizo-revolutionary pole at molar scale is decoded social flows, mass movements of deterritorialization; at molecular scale it is the proliferating connective syntheses, the production of lines of flight, the schizophrenic process.

This is why AO can analyze Wall Street as libidinally invested the same way a family romance is libidinally invested. The two are the same machines under different regimes.

## Relation to Jung's federated psyche

Jung's [[complex-theory|federated psyche]] is the structurally parallel move from the Jung side. The splinter-psyches are molecular; the unified ego-personality is molar; and Jung's insistence that the ego is one complex among others — rather than the organizing principle of the psyche — is the Jungian version of "the molar is an aggregate effect of the molecular, not its foundation." The [[problematical-state|problematical state]] is a molar configuration (a specific relation between the ego-series and the second series) that is produced by a molecular condition (two sets of complexes of equal functional intensity).
