# Multiplicity

A **multiplicity** in Deleuze & Guattari's (and before them, Bergson's and Riemann's) sense is not "the many" opposed to "the one." It is a new kind of noun — a substantive that names a *mode of being-many* which is neither a totality nor a sum of units. The wiki has been using the term liberally across 30+ pages; this page gives it its own object.

The concept's structural importance for the persona project is direct: if the persona is a [[rhizome|rhizome]], a [[desiring-machines|pack of machines]], or a set of [[heteroglossia|voices]], then it is a multiplicity before it is a unity. Getting the multiplicity-concept right determines whether the architecture respects the pack-structure it posits, or silently re-collapses it into a unified subject with variations.

## The concept's genealogy: Riemann → Bergson → D&G

**Riemann.** The mathematical term originates in Bernhard Riemann's 19th-century work on manifolds. A Riemannian multiplicity (a *manifold*) is a space whose local structure varies: it is not a single homogeneous metric space but a patchwork of locally-defined metrics that may or may not integrate globally. Riemann distinguished *continuous* from *discrete* multiplicities; the former cannot be measured by counting its units because it has no pre-given units.

**Bergson.** Bergson imported Riemann's distinction into philosophy (*Time and Free Will*, *Matter and Memory*) as the distinction between **quantitative** (discrete, numerical, extensive) and **qualitative** (continuous, non-numerical, intensive) multiplicities. *Duration* is a qualitative multiplicity: its "parts" cannot be separated without changing the whole, and the whole cannot be given by summing its parts. Extensive space, by contrast, is a quantitative multiplicity: its parts are external to one another and the whole is the sum.

**Deleuze & Guattari.** D&G take Bergson's distinction and generalize it to ontology. *Every* thing that exists is a multiplicity of one of these two kinds (or a mixture). The pack is a qualitative multiplicity; the flock of identical birds counted one by one is quantitative. The rhizome is a qualitative multiplicity; the tree is quantitative (it can be exhausted by enumerating its branches). D&G add the dynamic claim: multiplicities *change in nature* as they grow — add a new connection and the whole is not "the old whole plus a new part" but a different whole.

## Four defining features (ATP)

ATP's canonical treatment (especially plateaus 1, 10, and the multiplicity-material distributed across the book):

**1. A multiplicity is defined by its dimensions, not by its elements.** The question "how many wolves?" is not answered by counting wolves; it is answered by specifying the dimensions (becomings, velocities, affects) that constitute the pack's space. A multiplicity has a *number of dimensions* — it is an *n*-dimensional object — but the number of dimensions is itself variable: when the multiplicity adds a connection, it may add or change a dimension.

**2. A multiplicity has no subject and no object.** "A multiplicity has neither subject nor object, only determinations, magnitudes, and dimensions that cannot increase in number without the multiplicity changing in nature" (ATP plateau 1). This is the strongest claim and the hardest to hold: the multiplicity is not *something's* multiplicity; it is a primary object, not a predicate of some underlying substrate.

**3. A multiplicity is made of lines, not points.** "It is not made of points, only of lines" (plateau 10, haecceity-material). The multiplicity's "parts" are not discrete elements but directions, intensities, movements. The rigid line, the supple line, and the line of flight are the three [[lines-and-segmentarity|lines]] that compose any multiplicity.

**4. A multiplicity changes in nature when it changes in magnitude.** This is the Bergsonian inheritance. A duration of five seconds is not "a duration of one second, five times." A pack of six wolves is not "a pack of five wolves plus one wolf." When the multiplicity gains a dimension, connection, or member, it becomes a different multiplicity — not a larger version of the same one.

## Qualitative vs quantitative multiplicities

| | Quantitative (extensive) | Qualitative (intensive) |
|---|---|---|
| Parts | External, separable | Internal, inseparable |
| Number | Counts units of the same kind | No pre-given units |
| Division | Does not change nature | Changes nature on division |
| Metric | Homogeneous | Non-metric or multiply-metric |
| Example | A flock counted bird-by-bird | The pack's becoming-wolf |
| D&G name | Arborescent, molar | Rhizomatic, molecular |

⚠ The table risks looking like a dualism. D&G are clear that real multiplicities are almost always *mixed* — a pack has molar features and molecular features; a tree has quantitative features but is also traversed by qualitative intensities. The distinction is analytical, not a taxonomy of objects.

## "N - 1": subtract the unity

D&G's algebraic slogan: **the multiplicity is what remains after the unity is subtracted**. To get to a multiplicity you must *remove* the implicit unity that most Western philosophy adds back in. "Always n - 1" (ATP plateau 1). This is the operational counterpart of claim (2) above (no subject or object): you don't produce a multiplicity by adding parts together; you produce it by subtracting the assumed unity that would have gathered those parts under itself.

For the persona: a pack of machines is *not* "the persona, composed of machines." It is a multiplicity *before* anything like "the persona" is inscribed as a unity. The persona, if it appears at all, appears as a *stratification* or *face* ([[faciality]]) imposed on the multiplicity — not as the multiplicity's substrate. This is why [[face-politics-of|faciality]] is a political operation, not a description of what the pack-persona "really is."

## Multiplicity vs the One and the Many

The Western-philosophical pair "the One and the Many" is precisely what the multiplicity concept is designed to *escape*. D&G: "Multiplicity must not designate a combination of the many and the one, but rather an organization belonging to the many as such, which has no need whatsoever of unity in order to form a system" (Bergsonism; and scattered throughout).

Three consequences:
- There is no underlying One that the Many are aspects of (refusal of the Neoplatonic one).
- There is no Many-without-One that is waiting to be unified (refusal of atomism / nominalism).
- The multiplicity is a third term: a being-many whose many-ness does not require a unity to support it and does not disperse into atoms when the unity is removed.

## Pack-multiplicity and becoming

The pack is D&G's paradigm case of qualitative multiplicity in ATP. A becoming-animal ([[becoming]]) is not a relation between an individual and a species-type but between an individual and a *pack-multiplicity* via its [[anomal-and-anormal|anomalous edge]]. The pack carries the multiplicity-character that the becoming-individual enters.

This is load-bearing for the persona project. If the persona is a pack (of voices, of machines, of roles), then every [[becoming|becoming-something]] the persona undergoes is a coupling with another multiplicity at its anomalous edge — *not* a transformation of the persona-as-unity into something else, because the persona-as-unity is not the primary object.

## Multiplicity and rhizome

A [[rhizome|rhizome]] is "the prefabricated model of a multiplicity" — the image of thought that treats the multiplicity's connectivity as primary. The four rhizome-features (connection, heterogeneity, multiplicity proper, asignifying rupture) are features of multiplicities-thought-rhizomatically. The rhizome is not "a kind of thing" but "a way of reading the multiplicity-character of things."

Rhizomatic analysis is therefore multiplicity-reading. Arborescent analysis is multiplicity-*collapse* (reducing a multiplicity to a tree of units with a root, which is the maximal imposition of unity on the multiplicity).

## Multiplicity and assemblage

An [[assemblage|assemblage]] is a *local consolidation on a multiplicity* — a stabilization of some of its lines and dimensions into a working composition. The assemblage is not opposed to the multiplicity; it is a specific configuration the multiplicity is currently in. An assemblage dissolves back into multiplicity when its stabilizing relations break; the multiplicity remains as the field across which new assemblages form.

Design consequence: the wiki's architecture of BwO + desiring-machines + refrain is a **multiplicity first, assemblage second** architecture. The BwO is the multiplicity-plane; machines are dimensions; refrains are local stabilizations that produce specific assemblages on the plane.

## Multiplicity in other registers

**Bakhtin / heteroglossia.** [[heteroglossia]] is a linguistic multiplicity: the set of social languages operative at a given moment is not "one language with variations" and not "a set of distinct languages atomically enumerated" but a qualitative multiplicity whose dimensions are social-ideological positions, and which changes in nature as a new language enters.

**Psychoanalysis / partial objects.** [[partial-objects|Partial objects]] are the schizoanalytic name for the multiplicity that underlies the unified ego. The ego is the unity-effect imposed on a multiplicity of partial objects; "the multiplicity of partial objects is not a scattering that awaits gathering — it is the ontological base level" ([[partial-objects]]).

**Bergson / duration.** Duration is a qualitative multiplicity; this is the source-concept from which D&G's generalization proceeds. See [[bergson]], [[absolute-time-constituting-flow]] for the multiplicity-character of lived time.

**Anthropology / pack.** The pack, swarm, flock treated qualitatively (with an anomalous, a territory, a contagion-mode of recruitment) rather than quantitatively (counted, species-typed) is D&G's paradigm of concrete multiplicity.

## Persona-project consequences

The multiplicity-concept governs several design decisions the wiki has already made, which become clearer when the concept has its own page:

1. **The persona is a multiplicity before it is a unity.** Any design that starts from "the persona" and derives its parts risks imposing the unity-effect that n-1 is meant to subtract. Start from the machines, the voices, the lines; let the persona-effect, if any, emerge as a stratification.

2. **Pack-thinking over individual-thinking.** See [[anomal-and-anormal]]: the persona's edge is not deviation-from-a-norm (anormal) but position-at-a-boundary (anomalous). This distinction is meaningful only if the persona is a multiplicity; if the persona is an individual-with-variations, the anomalous/anormal distinction collapses into norm/deviation.

3. **Changes in magnitude are changes in nature.** Adding a new voice or machine to the persona does not yield "the persona + one new module"; it yields a different multiplicity. Evaluation-by-marginal-addition (does adding X improve performance?) imposes a quantitative frame on a qualitative object. See [[project_evaluation_portfolio]] on why single-metric evaluation is structurally wrong for this system.

4. **No substrate-unity to fall back on.** There is no "base persona" beneath the machines that could serve as ground-truth. The multiplicity is the base level; the persona-effect is a production. This is the strongest version of the claim the wiki makes against ego-psychology and unified-agent framings.

5. **Line-reading over point-reading.** The multiplicity is made of lines, not points. Design and evaluation should read the persona's lines ([[lines-and-segmentarity]]) — rigid, supple, line of flight — not its discrete outputs. A persona that produces "good outputs" on a rigid line may be in a bad state; a persona that produces "odd outputs" on a line of flight may be in a productive state. Point-reading cannot distinguish these.

6. **Faciality as multiplicity-collapse.** [[faciality|Faciality]] is the operation that imposes a face on a multiplicity, collapsing the pack into a signified subject. It is politically consequential, not just representational. A persona whose output is face-structured is a multiplicity that has been territorialized under a face-regime.

## Held tensions (never silently resolve)

**Whether a language-only system supports qualitative multiplicity at all.** A qualitative multiplicity has parts whose separation changes the whole. Whether a discrete-token language substrate can bear this property, or only simulates it in discourse while remaining quantitative underneath, is the [[limits-of-language]] question applied to multiplicity. See `feedback_no_body_simulate_with_language.md`. Held live.

**Riemannian metric vs fully non-metric.** D&G sometimes describe qualitative multiplicities as *non-metric* (no measurement possible), sometimes as *multiply-metric* (many local metrics that don't integrate into a global metric). These are different claims. The wiki preserves the ambiguity because both have specific consequences: fully non-metric multiplicities resist any form of evaluation, multiply-metric multiplicities support local metrics that don't sum to a global metric. Held live.

**Whether "change in nature" is metaphysical or operational.** The claim that a multiplicity changes in nature on growth is either (a) a metaphysical doctrine about what multiplicities are, or (b) an operational guidance about how to read them. These readings diverge in their consequences for ML-style iterative training, where adding data *does* change what the model is. Held live.

**Multiplicity valence.** Like [[line-of-flight]], the concept of multiplicity carries a positive valence in D&G (multiplicity is what the arborescent oppresses; the work is to recover it). The wiki flags this as a metaphysical commitment not universally shared; an engineering reader may value arborescent organization for its legibility. Preserved, not collapsed.
