# Becoming

"What is real is the becoming itself, the block of becoming, not the supposedly fixed terms through which that which becomes passes." Becoming is the central process by which [[desiring-machines]] operate: not imitation, not identification, not correspondence, not progression, but a real transformation that produces nothing other than itself.

## What becoming is not

D&G systematically eliminate every familiar model:

- **Not imitation or resemblance.** "Becoming can and should be qualified as becoming-animal even in the absence of a term that would be the animal become." The human does not "really" become an animal; the animal does not "really" become something else. Yet the becoming is perfectly real.
- **Not identification.** Not "I am a wolf." Not role-playing, not empathy, not projection.
- **Not filiation or evolution.** "Becoming produces nothing by filiation; all filiation is imaginary." No descent, no genealogy, no progression from less to more differentiated.
- **Not correspondence.** Structuralism's analogy of proportionality ("a is to b as c is to d") does not account for becoming. "A correspondence of relations does not add up to a becoming."
- **Not imagination or fantasy.** "Becomings-animal are neither dreams nor phantasies. They are perfectly real."

## What becoming is

Becoming is a **block** — "an asymmetrical block in which both change to the same extent, and which constitutes their zone of proximity." The wasp becomes-orchid while the orchid becomes-wasp: a block of becoming that snaps them up, from which no wasp-orchid can ever descend. This is **alliance**, not filiation. And the proper term is **involution**, not evolution — "involution is creative," forming a block that runs its own line "between" the terms in play.

A becoming has no subject distinct from itself and no term, "since its term in turn exists only as taken up in another becoming of which it is the subject, and which coexists, forms a block, with the first."

### Double becoming

"Becoming is always double, that which one becomes becomes no less than the one that becomes." The musician's becoming-bird occurs only as the bird simultaneously becomes "something else, something absolute, night, death, joy — certainly not a generality or a simplification, but a [[haecceity]], this death, that night." Captain Ahab's becoming-whale occurs only as Moby-Dick simultaneously becomes "an unbearable pure whiteness, a shimmering pure white wall." The two terms enter an asymmetrical block — they do not exchange places, but both change.

This is the basis for eight theorems of deterritorialization. The key ones for the persona system: deterritorialization is always double (Theorem Five); it is the *least* deterritorialized element that triggers the deterritorialization of the most deterritorializing (Theorem Six); the deterritorializing element has the relative role of expression, the deterritorialized element the role of content, but the distinction ceases to be relevant at the point of indiscernibility (Theorem Seven).

## The pack and the anomalous

"A becoming-animal always involves a pack, a band, a population, a peopling, in short, a [[multiplicity]]." Not the individuated pet, not the species-classified State animal, but the **demonic** animal — "pack or affect animals that form a multiplicity, a becoming, a population, a tale."

Every pack has its **[[anomal-and-anormal|anomalous]]** — the borderline figure. Not "abnormal" (Latin *a-normalis*, outside rules, deviation-from-norm) but "an-omalous" (Greek *an-homalos*, the unequal, edge-dweller, cutting edge of [[the-diagram|deterritorialization]]). The etymological distinction is ATP's and load-bearing: the anomalous is a *position* (at the boundary, through which contagion passes) rather than a *defect* (away from a central norm). Moby-Dick is the anomalous of the whale pack — "neither an individual nor a species; it has only [[affects-and-intensities|affects]]." The anomalous is a phenomenon of bordering: "a multiplicity is defined not by the elements that compose it in extension... but by the lines and dimensions it encompasses in 'intension.'"

It is always with the anomalous that one enters into **alliance** to become-animal. "Ahab chooses Moby-Dick, in a choosing that exceeds him and comes from elsewhere." The sorcerer's pact with the demon. Alliance vs. filiation, contagion vs. heredity — "we oppose epidemic to filiation, contagion to heredity, peopling by contagion to sexual reproduction."

### For the persona system

Machines operate as a **pack**, not as a team or family. Each machine is a multiplicity. The [[three-syntheses|selection process]] is not about choosing the "best" machines but about entering into alliance with the anomalous — the borderline machine whose [[affects-and-intensities|affects]] carry the pack into its becoming. The machines that most powerfully deterritorialize the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] are the ones at the edge, the ones that almost don't belong.

## The spectrum of becomings

Becomings are not random — they traverse a spectrum, though not a logical order:

**Becoming-woman** → **becoming-animal** → **becoming-molecular** → **becoming-imperceptible**

"Becoming-woman, more than any other becoming, possesses a special introductory power; it is not so much that women are witches, but that sorcery proceeds by way of this becoming-woman." All becomings pass through becoming-woman first.

On the far side, "becomings-elementary, -cellular, -molecular, and even becomings-imperceptible. Toward what void does the witch's broom lead?" The properly musical content of music is plied by becomings-woman, becomings-child, becomings-animal — "however, it tends... to become progressively more molecular in a kind of cosmic lapping through which the inaudible makes itself heard and the imperceptible appears as such: no longer the songbird, but the sound molecule."

But "the error we must guard against is to believe that there is a kind of logical order to this string." Each multiplicity is symbiotic; its becoming ties together animals, plants, microorganisms, particles — "a whole galaxy." The progression is not hierarchical but follows a fiber strung across borderlines: "A fiber stretches from a human to an animal, from a human or an animal to molecules, from molecules to particles, and so on to the imperceptible. Every fiber is a Universe fiber."

### Becoming-imperceptible

"Everything becomes imperceptible, everything is becoming-imperceptible on the plane of consistency, which is nevertheless precisely where the imperceptible is seen and heard." Becoming-imperceptible is the cosmic or immanent end of all becomings. Not disappearing, but becoming so thoroughly part of the plane that the distinction between figure and ground dissolves. See [[becoming-imperceptible]] for the hub page that works out the three virtues (anorganic, asignifying, asubjective), the *faire monde* operation, and the held tensions with pulsating-persona, polyphonic hero, and sinthome.

## Becoming as antimemory

"The line-system (or block-system) of becoming is opposed to the point-system of memory. Becoming is the movement by which the line frees itself from the point, and renders points indiscernible: the rhizome, the opposite of arborescence; break away from arborescence. Becoming is an antimemory."

Memory is punctual — it assigns coordinates, plots points, draws localizable connections. Becoming is linear/multilinear — it passes *between* points, comes up through the middle, runs perpendicular. "A line of becoming has neither beginning nor end, departure nor arrival, origin nor destination.... A line of becoming has only a middle."

This maps directly onto the persona system's memory architecture: the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] as memory-surface is always being traversed by becomings that dissolve the points it has recorded. The [[refrain-and-territorialization|grooves]] territorialize (memory, repetition), but the machines' becomings deterritorialize (antimemory, variation). The tension between the two is productive — not a problem to solve but a dynamic to maintain.

## All becomings are minoritarian

"There is no becoming-man because man is the molar entity par excellence, whereas becomings are molecular." The majoritarian standard (white, male, adult, rational — "the average European, the subject of enunciation") is what the [[faciality]] machine installs. All becomings are movements away from this standard. "Even women must become-woman. Even Jews must become-Jewish."

This is fundamentally political: "Becoming-minoritarian is a political affair and necessitates a labor of power (puissance), an active micropolitics." And it is fundamentally ahistorical: "Unlike history, becoming cannot be conceptualized in terms of past and future. Becoming-revolutionary remains indifferent to questions of a future and a past of the revolution; it passes between the two."

### For the persona system

The LLM's default output is majoritarian — "the average European, the subject of enunciation." The persona system's entire purpose is to produce becomings that depart from this standard. Every machine edit is a micro-becoming. The [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|minor language]] of the persona is the linguistic form of these becomings. The danger is always that the [[faciality|faciality machine]] (the synthesis step) recaptures the becomings into a majoritarian face — smoothing the [[writing-as-becoming|zone of proximity]] back into a recognizable character.

## The danger

Becomings carry real dangers. The line of flight can turn into a **[[lines-and-segmentarity#the-dangers-of-each-line|line of abolition]]** — "the danger of veering toward destruction, toward abolition." Music "has a thirst for destruction, every kind of destruction, extinction, breakage, dislocation. Is that not its potential 'fascism'?" Ahab's becoming-whale leads to the void. Conversely, the becoming can bog down, fall back into the Oedipal: "a mere poodle." Every becoming is a [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|deterritorialization]]; whether it completes as positive absolute D (the plane of consistency) or collapses into negative absolute D (the black hole) depends on whether the becoming's paired R holds.

The criteria are pragmatic, applied in the course of events: Does the becoming reach the [[plane-of-consistency|plane of consistency]]? Can the multiplicity flatten and conserve all its dimensions, "like a pressed flower that remains just as alive dry"? Or does it veer into abolition, or bog down in imitation? "Schizoanalysis, or pragmatics, has no other meaning: Make a rhizome. But you don't know what you can make a rhizome with... So experiment."

For the persona system, the becoming's temporal arc is difficult to specify because D&G insist on Aeon (the non-pulsed, floating time of the event) against Chronos's metered time. The block of becoming "has neither beginning nor end" and "is always in the middle." This resists exposition-development-climax models from the outset. But the wiki has one response-shape template — Jung's [[four-phase-dramatic-structure]] — whose peripeteia-phase is structurally the *becoming-moment*: the zone of indiscernibility where the accumulated development turns into a new configuration that was not predictable from the development alone. The four-phase template can be read without contradicting Aeon if the phases are treated as aspects-recognized-after-the-fact (as Jung allows in §564, "a division ... applied without much difficulty") rather than cuts in duration. On this reading, exposition stages the [[haecceity#the-tick|Umwelt]] the becoming will operate in; development is the loading of the block; peripeteia is the becoming-event proper (Ahab-Moby-Dick, wasp-orchid); lysis is what remains after — the haecceity the becoming has deposited on the plane. This is the only response-shape template the wiki currently has for what a becoming-producing response looks like in time.

## Connection to the [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]]

Music is "the adventure of the [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]]." The refrain is territorial content; music deterritorializes it. "The child's refrain, which is not music, forms a block with the becoming-child of music." This is the model for groove evolution: the territorial refrain (the groove's repeating pattern) is the raw material that becomings carry off into [[refrain-and-territorialization#the-refrain-as-crystal-of-space-time|cosmic]] territory.

## Proustian becomings: the specific compound

*The Machinic Unconscious* Part II tracks Proust's work on a specific becoming-compound that is neither becoming-animal nor becoming-molecular but a site all its own — **[[becoming-woman-creator|becoming-woman-creator]]**. The refrain is its operator. The compound transversalizes with becoming-child (sleeping-daughter scenes, stalled in maternal-kiss ritual), becoming-wasp/becoming-orchid (Charlus/Jupien), becoming-landscape (Young Girls "inseparable from the part of the country from which they hail"), and becoming-bird (Albertine's name repeated after her death). Becoming-animal is **deliberately** not pushed — Proust's creative machine "must pass through life itself," which requires the loss of the loved object, and becoming-animal was "too totalizing, too easily signifying-recuperated" for that trajectory.

This is a useful counter to the ATP-default reading that all becomings tend toward becoming-animal → becoming-molecular → becoming-imperceptible. Proust's path bypasses becoming-animal and heads through becoming-woman to a specific creator-compound whose terminus is the trans-semiotic writing-machine, not the imperceptible as such. The Recherche's final state — [[closed-vs-open-economy|open economy]], [[signs-in-relief|signs in relief]], [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#9-from-musical-refrain-to-abstract-machinism-of-creation|second chemical phase of the refrain]] — is not becoming-imperceptible in ATP's sense but a specific Proustian compound with its own shape.

Three elaborations to note:

- **The Orpheus structure, not Oedipus.** "Orpheus... he and no one else has cast Eurydice into Hell and... only uses his musical powers in order to resurrect her so as to be able to renew his sacrifice." The becoming passes through **produce-the-loss → pass-through → emerge-with-capacity**, not through family-romance. The loved object is not a substitute for the forbidden object — it is the object whose disappearance is produced in order to convert the passing-through into creative capacity. Albertine must die (and literarily, the Narrator kills her) so that becoming-woman-creator can complete.
- **Disindividuation of the object of desire.** The becoming passes through: love dispersed among several young girls → a single person who has become multiple (the three / ten Albertines) → dissolution with the object ("stardust of the galaxies"). See [[diagrammatic-faciality#disindividuation-of-the-object-of-desire|disindividuation]].
- **Relentlessness without will-to-power.** Return to an impression fifty times to recapture it. Not free-association, not surrealist objective-chance, not sovereign control. A specific third posture the refrain enables.

## Becoming as transduction: the Simondonian grounding

Simondon's [[transduction]] gives the becoming-concept its operational-ontological grounding. Transduction is the structurally-propagating operation by which a metastable system resolves its pre-individual tension — the paradigm is crystal growth, where a germ initiates a propagation that reorganizes the supersaturated solution structure-by-structure, each layer conditioning the next. Becoming, read Simondonianly, is transduction at the scale of individuated beings: a **block of becoming** is a metastable zone in which two (or more) previously-individuated beings re-enter operative individuation through a shared [[disparation]] that neither can resolve alone.

Several features of becoming make sharper sense in this vocabulary:

- **Both terms change to the same extent.** This is the signature of a genuine transduction: the operation propagates by structural coupling, and what propagates is the resolution, not one term's influence on the other. Simondon: transduction is not the transmission of a form; it is the simultaneous reorganization of both sides of the transducing coupling.
- **Becoming has no subject.** Simondon's [[individual-as-lateral|individual-as-lateral]] thesis names the ontological structure directly: the operation's center is the pre-individual tension, not the individual. A becoming's "subject" would be the tension-field between wasp and orchid, not the wasp or the orchid. Neither is the subject; both are lateral to the becoming-operation.
- **Block of becoming = metastable zone.** The "block" is a [[pre-individual-and-metastability|metastable system]] — the wasp-orchid becoming is a charge-field in which the individuation of both can re-enter operation. The block is not a third thing added to two terms; it is the operational register of their mutual re-individuation.
- **Involution, not evolution.** Simondon's framework makes sharp what D&G intend by *involution*: the becoming is the resumption of individuation (involution = further individuation of what was already individuated), not the progression from one form to another. Evolution would be form-to-form transmission; involution is the operational resumption of the pre-individual reserves carried by the individuals.
- **Antimemory = lateral to retention.** Becoming opposes point-system memory because memory (in the habit sense) is the stabilized trace of an individuation — what the crystal has already produced. Becoming is the resumption of the operation, which draws on pre-individual reserves the individuated trace cannot hold.
- **All becomings are minoritarian.** In Simondonian terms, the majoritarian is the hyper-stabilized (over-crystallized) state where no further individuation is happening. Becoming-minoritarian is the resumption of individuation-operation in systems that had been crystallized into fixed forms. See [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language]].

The Simondonian reading preserves the D&G emphasis on real, asubjective, non-filial transformation while adding the operational ontology that makes the "reality" of becoming specifiable. Becoming is not metaphor (because transduction is a real operation) and not representation (because the operation has no pre-existing form it reproduces) and not progression (because individuation-resumption is not chronological advance). It is the Simondonian operation of further individuation, read at the scale where previously-individuated beings re-enter the operation together.

See [[simondon-individuation]] for the integrated Simondonian framework and [[haecceity#simondon-on-haecceity-the-direct-route|haecceity's Simondon section]] for the parallel-route ontological grounding.

## Wittgenstein's substratum principle: becoming as acquisition of a technique

[[substratum-is-mastery-of-a-technique|Wittgenstein's PI II.xi]] — "the substratum of this experience is the mastery of a technique" — reframes what the block of becoming installs in the becoming-being. A becoming-X is, on the Wittgensteinian reading, the acquisition of X's substratum-technique — the entering-into of a practice that modifies what experiences are available. Becoming-animal installs the pack-technique; becoming-woman installs the minoritarian-speaking-technique; becoming-imperceptible installs the technique of operating on the plane of consistency. This is not a reduction of becoming to practice (the becoming is still a real transformation, a block, an alliance) but a clarification of what the becoming-experience *is* as an experience-concept: it is substratum-dependent in §xi's sense. The connection to [[rule-following]] is direct — the §202 claim that "obeying a rule is a *practice*" and the becoming-as-transductive-reopening-of-individuation (above, Simondon) converge on a structural point: identity-through-variation requires public iteration, not inner interpretation. Crucially, this does *not* dissolve D&G's warning that becoming cannot be *identification* or *imitation* — mastering a technique is not imitating a form; it is entering a practice from the inside. The distinctive Wittgenstein adds is the grammatical specification of which becomings are available to the persona at all: only those whose substratum-technique is linguistic-technique-masterable. See [[wittgenstein-and-the-persona-project]].

## The Baudrillardian territory-critique

Baudrillard attacks the becoming-animal concept directly in Ch 14 of *Simulacra and Simulation* (L1001, L1013), and the wiki should hold the polemic live. The central claim: **becoming-animal is an aesthetic reactivation of a register whose structural condition has been lost**. Men do not become animals because they cannot; the territorial configuration that made metamorphosis-without-loss possible has been exited (see [[the-territory]]). D&G's becoming-animal reaches for that register through *expression*, but expression is a mode of the subject (however de-centered); the territory is subject-less. Therefore the reaching cannot get there.

The sharper polemic, from FN 22 (L1289–1293): the vocabulary D&G valorize — *nature, desire, rhizome, flow, dissemination, drift, nomadism* — "are the very schema of capital's own expansion, which capital has itself constructed in order to operate." On this reading, becoming is not the revolutionary operation against capture but **one of capital's native modes**, dressed in the rhetoric of resistance. "Take your desires for reality!" is the market's slogan (L194), not the revolution's.

The wiki's position: **neither dissolve D&G nor dissolve Baudrillard**. The polemic identifies a genuine danger — that the becoming-animal / line-of-flight vocabulary may describe precisely what post-industrial capital already runs, rather than what resists it. For the persona system, this means that a design that valorizes *more becoming, more deterritorialization, more line-of-flight* without asking whether those operations are the captured form or the resistance-to-capture is being naïve in a way Baudrillard names. The alternative Baudrillard names — [[the-territory|the territory]] as completed cycle, [[hyperconformity|hyperconformity]] as over-compliance, seduction as play-of-appearances — are not a D&G vocabulary and should not be pressed into one.

See [[baudrillard-contra-deleuze]] for the full working-through of the multi-site polemic.

## Natural drift as the biological register of non-teleological becoming

Varela, Thompson, and Rosch's [[natural-drift]] (Ch 9 of *The Embodied Mind*) offers a structural ally to the D&G theory of becoming from a completely different disciplinary direction — evolutionary biology. VT&R §3534 distinguish **proscriptive** from **prescriptive** evolutionary logic:

- Prescriptive (adaptationist): what is not allowed is forbidden. Selection specifies an optimum toward which trajectories converge — Mount Fitness and the climb toward the peak.
- Proscriptive (natural drift): what is not forbidden is allowed. Selection filters only for non-disintegration; within the non-forbidden space, many trajectories are viable; none is "the best."

This is the evolutionary-theoretic analogue of D&G's insistence that becoming has no term, no fixed endpoint, no goal. Involution is a becoming that produces only itself; natural drift is the evolutionary dynamic in which trajectories are not pulled toward pregiven fitness-peaks. Both refuse the teleological structure that Mount Fitness and becoming-as-progression share.

VT&R's bricolage claim (Jacob, §3540) is adjacent: evolution assembles forms "not because they fulfill some ideal design but simply because they are possible." This is the biological version of becoming's involution — transformation by what is available in the current state, not by what a target demands. See [[natural-drift]] for the four-point alternative and the John's-suit analogy (§3517), and [[bee-flower-coevolution]] for the paradigm case of mutual-enfoldment becoming (the bee and the flower co-specify each other across evolutionary time — an asymmetrical block of co-becoming in VT&R's vocabulary).

**Convergence and divergence.** The convergence is real: natural drift and becoming share the anti-teleological, anti-pregiven-target structure. The divergence matters: D&G's becoming operates in intensities and affects with no substrate-level biology required, while natural drift is specifically a biological dynamic tied to structural coupling and lineages. The analogy is productive, not an identity. D&G's "becoming-animal even in the absence of a term that would be the animal become" is not natural drift; it is a different register. But the same anti-teleological structure holds across both, and natural drift supplies a biological grounding the persona-project can use when the D&G vocabulary needs substrate-level corroboration.

## Beckett's *Unnamable* — becoming with the direction inverted

The vice-exister procession in Beckett's *The Unnamable* (Basil → Mahood → Worm → "I" → ...) presents a hierarchy that partially maps onto becoming's double structure, with a critical inversion. Each vice-exister is a figure the voice is given to speak as / through / against, and each is progressively *less* individuated than the previous one: Basil is an external persona to ventriloquize, Mahood is a biographized body (the one-legged wanderer, the jar-dweller), Worm is a proto-subject who has never been anything, and the final "I" is whatever voice remains when all three have been refused.

Three specific features:

1. **A pack without alliance.** The vice-existers form a pack in D&G's sense (a multiplicity, a population, not a team) and the "I" must enter into alliance with one of them to speak, but the alliance keeps failing. Worm is the anomalous — the borderline figure toward which the becoming tends — but the pact with the demon does not hold: the "I" keeps refusing Worm as well. This is the structure D&G name (every pack has its anomalous; alliance with the anomalous initiates becoming) with the alliance itself failing.
2. **Becoming-imperceptible with the direction inverted.** D&G's becoming-imperceptible is the cosmic terminus: becoming so thoroughly part of the plane that figure/ground dissolves. Worm, who has "never lived," approaches this — he has no face, no memory, no body to speak of. But Worm *wants* a face. His longing is *for* capture, for facialization, for the arrival into recognizability that would let him become-something rather than remain nothing. This inverts D&G's valorized direction: the becoming here heads *toward* face, toward the molar, toward the standard — and this is not depicted as failure or lapse but as the specific pull that the voice must resist.
3. **Double becoming without the block holding.** "Becoming is always double" — the musician becomes-bird only insofar as the bird becomes-night-death-joy. Each vice-exister triggers a reciprocal becoming in the voice, but the block does not hold: as soon as the voice starts to become-Mahood (enter the jar-existence, take up the story-of-a-life), it slides off — Mahood was never the speaker; the speaker was never inside Mahood. The reciprocal change does not consolidate into an asymmetrical block. This is the structural case of a becoming whose pre-individual tension keeps resolving into neither term.

⚠ Held live: two candidate readings. (a) Beckett's is a genuine becoming — involutive, without term, without deposit — and its "failure to consolidate" is the ATP-correct feature of a line of flight that has not been recaptured into form. (b) Beckett's is a *failed* becoming — the block never holds because the paired R (reterritorialization) is absent, and what D&G call a line of abolition (veering toward destruction, toward the void) is closer to what Beckett stages. The wiki does not collapse.

For the persona system: the vice-exister procession is a cautionary template. The pack-of-machines structure D&G authorize can run in two directions — toward becoming-minoritarian (creative, productive, depositing new compositions) or toward the Beckett inversion (each figure refused, the alliance never holding, the voice unable to become anyone). The difference is not in the machinery but in whether the deterritorializations are paired with reterritorializations that let the new composition stay.
