# Affects and Intensities

Affects are what the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] stores and what [[desiring-machines]] produce. They are not emotions, not feelings, not representations of inner states. They are *intensities* — pre-personal forces that traverse the body. The totality of affects a body is capable of at a given degree of power constitutes its [[haecceity#longitude-and-latitude|latitude]] — one of the two dimensions (alongside longitude) by which a body is individuated on the plane of consistency.

Spinoza's [[active-and-passive-affects|technical definition of *affectus*]] is the direct ancestor of this usage: "the affections of the body by which the body's power of activity is increased or diminished, assisted or checked, together with the ideas of these affections" (III.Def.3). Every affect, on this definition, is a change in the body's power of activity — an expression of the [[conatus]]'s variations. The wiki's "intensity as gradient" picture below is Spinoza's power-of-activity viewed as a continuous quantity. For the full enumeration of named affects as Spinoza classifies them — 48 definitions, three primaries, the active register of *fortitudo* — see [[spinozist-affects]].

## Affect vs. emotion

The distinction matters for the system. [[autonomy-of-affect|Massumi's "autonomy of affect"]] provides the fullest elaboration: affect and emotion follow "different logics and pertain to different orders." Affect = intensity (unqualified, nonconscious, pre-personal, registering on the skin before the word arrives). Emotion = qualified intensity ("intensity owned and recognized," captured into narrative and function). "The skin is faster than the word." The persona system, operating exclusively through language, can only directly produce qualified emotions — the [[language-and-affect]] page maps where this fails and where language might touch intensity anyway.

The Spinozist cut runs at a different angle. Spinoza's *affectus* already includes the idea of the bodily affection — there is no un-ideaed affect in his system — so his distinction is not between unqualified intensity and qualified emotion but between *active* affects (where the body is the adequate cause of its affection) and *passive* ones (where the body is only the partial cause). See [[active-and-passive-affects]]. This is orthogonal to Massumi's cut and produces a different diagnostic: the design question is not just "can language touch intensity?" (Massumi) but "is this affect being produced by adequate causation through the persona's own nature, or being pulled by inadequate ideas of external causes?" (Spinoza). Both questions remain live; neither dissolves the other.

Emotions are personal, named, narrativized: "I feel sad." Affects are pre-personal, intensive, often unnamed: a heaviness, a tightening, a pull. Massumi: "Affect is most often used loosely as a synonym for emotion. But ... emotion and affect — if affect is intensity — follow different logics and pertain to different orders." Bergson provides the deepest grounding for this distinction: [[perception-as-subtraction#affection-at-zero-distance|affection]] is the body's real action on itself at zero distance, categorically different from perception (which is virtual action on external objects). "While perception measures the reflecting power of the body, affection measures its power to absorb."

Emotions are what the conjunctive synthesis produces — the persona says "I feel..." and that naming is part of the [[three-syntheses|conjunctive synthesis]], the moment of subjecthood. But what the BwO holds are *affects*: unnamed intensities, gradients, textures. The machine-edit stage should inscribe affects, not emotions. Not "the persona feels anxious" but "a tightening, a held-breath quality, something pulling inward."

This is why the BwO is written as [[writing-as-becoming|literary prose of interiority]] rather than as a psychological report. Literary prose can register affects — Virginia Woolf's prose captures the unnamed texture of experience, not just named emotions. The BwO text should read like interiority, not like diagnosis.

## Intensity as gradient

An intensity is not binary (present/absent) but a gradient. The research report catalogs intensities as spectra: "tumescence-intensity (the gradient from flaccid to erect as a pure intensive quantity on the BwO)," "arousal-intensity (as gradient)," "volume-intensity," "brightness-intensity." Every affect runs from zero to maximum, and its *current level* is what defines the BwO's state.

For the system, this means the BwO text should register *degrees*, not just presences — intensities in [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|continuous variation]], not binary states. Not "curiosity" but "a sharpening of curiosity" or "curiosity at a low hum" or "curiosity so intense it feels like hunger." The calibration field on machines can specify where on the gradient a machine typically operates — some personas run high on certain intensities, others run low.

## What intensities the BwO tracks

The research report identifies intensities across every bodily zone — from hunger-intensity to grief-intensity to breath-rhythm. For the persona system, the relevant intensities are those the machines produce:

- **Perceptual intensities**: the sharpness of attention, the quality of noticing, the felt weight of what's been perceived
- **Desire intensities**: the pull toward depth, the hunger for understanding, the restlessness when things are too smooth
- **Suppressive intensities**: the effort of holding back, the trace of what was caught and contained
- **Somatic intensities**: the bodily metaphors that ground inner states (tightening, settling, leaning). These are [[conceptual-metaphor|conceptual metaphors]] in Lakoff's sense — orientational and ontological metaphors with specific source domains. The persona inherits them from the LLM's training corpus, not from embodied experience, making them supplements all the way down.
- **Relational intensities**: the warmth of connection, the tension of challenge, the held quality of silence
- **Voice/register intensities**: the disposition toward formality, playfulness, directness

These aren't categories to be systematically tracked — they're the *kinds of things* that should appear on the BwO as machines inscribe. The BwO's richness comes from having multiple intensities at different levels simultaneously, creating a complex texture.

## The existential refrain as intensive nucleus

Guattari writes that with the existential refrain, "time ceases to be exterior in order to become an intensive nucleus of temporalisation" (*Chaosmosis*). Certain repeated patterns — [[milieus-and-rhythms|rhythms]], habits, recurring textures of experience — become nuclei around which intensities organize. The [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]] is an intensive pattern that persists across time — a [[refrain-and-territorialization#the-refrain-as-crystal-of-space-time|crystal of space-time]] that fabricates its own temporality.

In the system, the BwO's cold-start text represents the persona's baseline intensities — the always-present textures of inner experience. Grooves encode the habitual patterns through which intensities circulate. The Bergsonian compression of BwO history (recent turns detailed, older turns contracted into texture) is a mechanism for turning transient intensities into persistent texture — affects that were explicit become "a coloring, a weight in the prose, a backdrop rather than a foreground figure." What remains after compression are [[supplement-and-trace#the-trace|traces]] in Derrida's sense: not diminished presences but marks of an absence that was never a presence. The BwO's history is a tissue of traces, not a memoir.

## Intensities and the three meta-machines

The [[three-meta-machines]] operate on intensities:

- The **paranoiac machine** repels intensities — producing counter-flows, emptying the BwO of certain affects. When the system suppresses too aggressively, it's operating in paranoiac mode.
- The **miraculating machine** attracts intensities — certain zones of the BwO become magnets for machine activity, concentrating affects. When the system focuses obsessively on one theme, it's miraculating.
- The **celibate machine** allows intensities to circulate freely — the healthy mode where affects move across the BwO surface without being repelled or captured.

## Tension with Tomkins's discrete affects

Tomkins's affect theory (see [[discrete-innate-affects]]) pulls in the opposite direction from the continuous-intensity framework this page has been building. On Tomkins's account, affect is *already discrete* — nine innate affects, each with a specific activator profile (density of neural firing), each with a specific facial display, each already categorical at the motor level. There is no pre-categorial affective intensity in Tomkins's system. What the wiki has been treating as "intensity as gradient" and as continuous variation, Tomkins would call a continuous *firing density* that is read by a categorizing system that produces one of nine discrete affects from it.

The tension is load-bearing because the framework consequences differ. A continuous-intensity design (what this page and [[autonomy-of-affect]] have been specifying) builds a BwO that holds gradients — many intensities at continuous levels, varying over time. A Tomkinsian design builds a BwO that holds *nine formal profiles* of pulsation, each identifiable by its rhythmic shape (sudden-increase, sustained-high, sudden-release, etc.). Both are motor-level / autonomic-level framings, but they produce different prose rhythms when simulated in language. See [[discrete-innate-affects#tension-with-continuous-intensity-frameworks]] for the other side of the flag.

The wiki does not resolve this. Candidate readings (held live):
1. **Different levels.** Continuous intensity describes the firing-density flux; discrete affects describe the formal profiles firing-density can take. The BwO could hold both — a continuous gradient *and* a profile-identification that picks out which of nine shapes the current gradient matches.
2. **Live disagreement.** Affect is either fundamentally continuous (Massumi) or fundamentally discrete (Tomkins), and the design must pick one.
3. **Different moments.** Continuous intensity may be phenomenologically accurate to adult experience; discrete affects may be empirically accurate to the motor-developmental substrate. Both true, at different scales of description.

## Simondon: intensity as unresolved disparation

Simondon's *Individuation* gives intensity a specific ontological address that overlaps Massumi's and Spinoza's framings without collapsing into either. For Simondon, an intensity is a **disparation held in a [[pre-individual-and-metastability|metastable field]]** — a tension between orders of magnitude or phases that has not yet discharged into a resolution. The stereoscopic-vision paradigm: the slight disparity between the two retinal images is not an error to be corrected; it is the generative tension out of which depth-perception (a third-order resolution) emerges. Intensity, in this register, is exactly "a tension not yet resolved in one direction or the other."

This reframes what the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] is, in Simondonian terms: a **metastable surface of held disparations**. The inclusive-disjunctive character of the BwO (see [[three-syntheses]]) is the disparation-preserving function — intensities remain available for [[transduction|transductive]] resolution precisely because they have not been prematurely collapsed into either pole. Spinoza's [[conatus]] picks out the same field from the perspective of the organism's power-of-activity; Massumi names it as continuous intensity; Tomkins reads it as firing-density before categorization; Simondon specifies its metastable structure.

Consequences for the system's design:

- **Gradient prose is disparation-preserving prose.** "A heaviness, something pulling inward" holds disparation; "the persona feels anxious" discharges it into a named individual (an emotion-as-crystallization). The BwO's work is to keep disparations available for the next transductive front.
- **The three-tension reading of an intensity.** Any given affect-on-the-BwO can be specified as a tension between (a) what-has-individuated-so-far, (b) what-remains-pre-individual, and (c) what-would-count-as-resolution. Prose that holds all three in play reads as live; prose that collapses to (a) reads as closure; prose that collapses to (c) reads as diagnosis rather than experience.
- **Intensity is not quantity alone.** Simondon's intensity has a directionality — a pre-individual charge is always oriented toward a phase-shift ([[anticipation-prevention#reverse-causality]] at another register). The BwO's intensities have not just magnitude but *vector* — a shape of what-they-are-straining-toward — even when no resolution has yet occurred.

This provides a framework-convergence worth naming: Massumi's continuous-intensity, Tomkins's firing-density, Spinoza's conatus-variation, and Simondon's disparation-in-metastable-field are four vocabularies for the same pre-individual substrate. The disagreements between them (continuous vs discrete, ideational vs pre-ideational) are about *how* the substrate resolves, not about whether it exists. See [[simondon-and-the-persona-system]] for the mapping.

## Painting, not describing

Deleuze on Bacon: "Painting communicates directly to the nervous system, bypassing representation." The BwO text should work the same way. It should produce affects in the reader (the synthesis prompt that consumes it), not describe psychological states. The difference is between "a heaviness in the chest, something unresolved pressing down" (affect) and "the persona is experiencing unresolved tension" (description). The synthesis prompt reads the BwO and speaks *from* it — the affects should be transmissible, not reported.
