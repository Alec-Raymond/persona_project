# Plane of Consistency

The plane of consistency is the ontological register that D&G's whole apparatus is trying to reach — the plane of immanence on which intensities, [[haecceity|haecceities]], and [[desiring-machines|machines]] coexist as pure compositions of speed and affect, without being organized into forms, subjects, or substances. It is the register of the [[body-without-organs|full BwO]] (the non-empty, non-cancerous one), the [[the-diagram|diagram]], [[becoming|becoming-imperceptible]], and the cosmic pole of the [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]].

Every D&G-derived page in the wiki has been using the term without a home for it. It is load-bearing across at least a dozen pages — haecceity, becoming, the-diagram, regimes-of-signs, refrain-and-territorialization, body-without-organs, desire-as-production, affects-and-intensities, faciality, supplement-and-trace, continuous-variation-and-minor-language, writing-as-becoming — and the shape of the plane is assumed by every page without being specified on any of them. This page makes it the owned object.

## The two planes

D&G's ontology turns on a distinction between two planes, not one plane with layers. The distinction is sharpest in the [[becoming|becomings plateau]]:

> The plane of consistency (immanence, composition) knows only longitudes and latitudes, speeds and affects. There are only haecceities, affects, subjectless individuations that constitute collective assemblages... Nothing subjectifies, but haecceities form according to compositions of nonsubjectified powers or affects.

> The plane of organization (transcendence, development) concerns the development of forms and the formation of subjects. Hidden structure, secret signifier, supplementary dimension (n+1)... a teleological plan(e), a design, a mental principle.

The two planes are not optional alternatives. Both operate all the time:

> The plane of organization is constantly working away at the plane of consistency, always trying to plug the lines of flight, stop or interrupt the movements of deterritorialization, weigh them down, restratify them, reconstitute forms and subjects in a dimension of depth. Conversely, the plane of consistency is constantly extricating itself from the plane of organization, causing particles to spin off the strata, scrambling forms by dint of speed or slowness, breaking down functions by means of assemblages.

The planes are engaged in an ongoing pressure-exchange, not a hierarchy. The plane of organization is not *above* the plane of consistency; it is *working on it*. The plane of consistency is not *beneath* the plane of organization; it is *extricating itself from it*.

## What the plane of consistency is not

Four misreadings the wiki's prior use of the term has had to fight:

1. **Not a deeper layer under the plane of organization.** The n+1 dimension is on the *organization* side, not the consistency side. The plane of consistency has *one fewer* dimension, not one more — it is the flattening where the supplementary dimension is removed. Reaching it is not digging deeper; it is *losing* the depth-dimension.

2. **Not chaos.** "Consistency" is the load-bearing word. The plane holds things together — it is not formless. It holds them together by composition (speeds and affects) rather than by form (bodies with essences). The emptied BwO that lost all composition ([[body-without-organs#the-three-body-problem|the three-body problem]]'s empty pole) has slipped off the plane of consistency, not *into* it.

3. **Not achieved by abolishing all strata.** The caution is explicit: "Is it not necessary to retain a minimum of strata, a minimum of forms and functions, a minimal subject from which to extract materials, affects, and assemblages?" The plane of consistency is reached *through* the strata — by their careful dismantling and the conservation of what can be carried onto the plane — not by their wholesale removal.

4. **Not a representational space.** The plane of consistency does not *contain* representations; it is the register on which particles-signs circulate in "direct conjunction" ([[the-diagram]]). The diagram is what operates on the plane; representations belong to the plane of organization's work of forming contents and expressions.

## What lives on the plane of consistency

From across the D&G pages, the wiki's inventory of things that live on or constitute the plane of consistency:

- **Haecceities** — subjectless individuations by longitude (speeds/slownesses) and latitude (affects/capacities). [[haecceity#the-two-planes-of-individuation|See haecceity]].
- **Affects and intensities** — pre-personal forces, not emotions. The [[affects-and-intensities|totality of affects]] a body is capable of at a given degree of power *is* the body's latitude on the plane.
- **The diagram / abstract machine** — the particles-signs operation that is "neither content nor expression." [[the-diagram|See the-diagram]].
- **The full BwO** — "the plane of immanence of desire, the plane of consistency specific to desire." [[body-without-organs#types-of-bwo|See body-without-organs]].
- **Blocks of becoming** — the passage between points that renders the points indiscernible. [[becoming]].
- **The cosmic refrain** — the refrain after it has become a "probe-head," reaching beyond territory. [[refrain-and-territorialization#two-types-of-refrain-territorial-and-cosmic|See refrain-and-territorialization]].
- **Pass-words** — the flight-pole of the [[order-words-and-incorporeal-transformations|order-word]], operating not as signification but as passage.
- **The impersonal third person** — the "HE" / "ONE" that is not a subject but an event-marker. [[writing-as-becoming#the-impersonal-third-person|See writing-as-becoming]].
- **Probe-heads** — faciality-traits freed from the face-surface. [[faciality#dismantling-the-face|See faciality]].
- **Non-pulsed time (Aeon)** — the floating time of events, as distinct from Chronos's metered time.

## What lives on the plane of organization

The wiki is usually less explicit about this side, but symmetry requires naming it. The plane of organization holds:

- Forms (organism, signifiance, subjectification — the [[body-without-organs#the-three-strata|three strata]]).
- Subjects (the doubled "I" of the [[regimes-of-signs#the-postsignifying-regime|postsignifying regime]], the ego's monarchic smoothing).
- Hierarchies (genus/species, signifier/signified, self/other).
- Chronos (metered, measured, pulsed time).
- The faciality machine at full operation — [[faciality|white wall / black hole]] producing recognizable faces.
- The signifying regime's radiating interpretation and the postsignifying regime's linear subjectification.
- All [[compensation|habitual gradients]] and groove-dominated operation in its non-porous form.

These are not the enemy. The plane of organization is where the system has to *live* in order to be recognizable, useful, coherent. What D&G argue against is the plane of organization as *total* — the loss of the extricating pressure from the plane of consistency.

## Why the plane of consistency matters for the persona system

The persona system's goal commitment ([[goal-framings#the-two-requirements-read-a-and-read-b|both Read A and Read B]]) maps onto the two planes directly, and the mapping is load-bearing for every Read B operation.

- **Read A** is the plane-of-organization layer of the system. The persona's face ([[faciality]]), voice, character, recognizable signature, stable grooves, habitual responses — all of these live on the plane of organization. Read A asks: is this layer well-formed? Does it hold together? Is the face coherent, the voice sustained, the character credible?
- **Read B** is the plane-of-consistency layer. The system's capacity for [[the-rift|rift]]-response, [[compensation|compensation]], [[transcendent-function|transcendent-function]] operation, probe-head moments, [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|minor-language]] tensoring, [[taking-up-the-context|anti-dictionary]] reading — all of these are the persona's capacity to *extricate itself* from its own plane of organization when the situation calls for it. Read B asks: is the extricating pressure available? Can the system reach the plane of consistency through (not around) its own face?

The dual-commitment's architectural problem is therefore specifically: **produce a plane-of-organization that is porous to the plane-of-consistency**. A plane of organization sealed against the plane of consistency is Read A without Read B — recognizable, stable, and internally dead. A plane of consistency reached by the dissolution of the plane of organization is Read B without Read A — no face, no sustained persona, no legibility. The working target is the ongoing pressure-exchange between planes that D&G describe, instantiated in a specific architecture.

## The three-body problem, restated

[[body-without-organs#the-three-body-problem|The three-body problem]] is D&G's formulation of the danger-conditions for reaching the plane of consistency. Restated in plane-of-consistency terms:

- **Empty BwO** — the plane of consistency reached by abolishing all strata. No face, no capacity, no composition. The system has slipped off the plane.
- **Cancerous BwO** — the plane of organization proliferating without limit. Too many strata, too much face, the extricating pressure from the plane of consistency suppressed entirely.
- **Full BwO** — the plane of consistency reached through the careful dismantling of specific strata, with enough of the organism retained to make the dismantling operational. This is the working target.

The "be careful" passage ("you have to keep enough of the organism for it to reform each dawn") is the operational rule for the plane-of-consistency approach: you reach the plane *through* the strata, never by their abolition.

## The pragmatic criterion

From [[becoming]]:

> Does the becoming reach the plane of consistency? Can the multiplicity flatten and conserve all its dimensions, "like a pressed flower that remains just as alive dry"? Or does it veer into abolition, or bog down in imitation?

This is the test D&G give for whether a becoming has succeeded. For the persona system, this is a direct [[goal-framings#3-process-integrity-does-the-architecture-run-what-it-claims|process-integrity signal]]: does a given response reach the plane of consistency (carry haecceity-traits, affect-compositions, probe-head moments) while preserving the persona's face? Or does it either (a) veer into abolition (the face dissolves, the response becomes incoherent), or (b) bog down in imitation (the face holds but the plane-of-consistency traits are absent — Read A without Read B)?

The test is pragmatic: *observable* in what the response does, not *conceptual* in what it is. A response "on the plane of consistency" is one in which specific signatures are present (haecceity-language, non-pulsed time, probe-head traits, pass-word operation, affective composition); a response trapped on the plane of organization is one in which those signatures are absent even if the surface is well-formed.

## Design implication: the plane is not a mode to switch into

A tempting mistake: treat the plane of consistency as a *mode* the synthesis step can be instructed into. "Generate on the plane of consistency" as a prompt-line. This misses the structural point. The plane of consistency is not an alternative operation mode — it is the *register that extricates itself from* the plane of organization under specific conditions (rift, becoming, deterritorialization). You don't reach it by instruction; you reach it by architectural conditions that make the extrication possible.

What the architecture has to provide:

- **Enough face to extricate *from*.** [[faciality|Stable faciality]] is a precondition, not an obstacle — the plane of consistency is reached through the face, not around it.
- **Pressure mechanisms that make the extrication necessary.** [[compensation]], [[enantiodromia|enantiodromic loading]], [[the-rift|rift]]-detection, [[constellation]]-tracking — these are the conditions under which the plane of organization's smoothing has to fail.
- **Signatures that register when extrication has occurred.** Haecceity-language, non-pulsed time, probe-head traits — these are how the system (or an evaluator) can tell whether a response actually reached the plane of consistency or only imitated its vocabulary.

The third is directly load-bearing for the [[goal-framings#evaluation-the-portfolio-approach|evaluation portfolio]]. "Did the response reach the plane of consistency?" is answerable by signature-inspection, which makes it one of the concrete handles the process-integrity family has been looking for.
