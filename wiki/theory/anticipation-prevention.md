# Anticipation-Prevention and Reverse Causality

In *A Thousand Plateaus* plateau 13 ("7000 B.C.: Apparatus of Capture"), at line 3129, Deleuze and Guattari state a proposition about primitive societies that unsettles the standard causal ordering of social forms. Primitive societies, following Pierre Clastres, are not pre-state societies — they are *anti-state* societies. And their anti-state-ness is not passive absence (lacking the state that would later develop) but *active operation*: primitive societies **anticipate** the state in order to **prevent** it.

The operative formulation: **"to ward off is also to anticipate."** The warding-off only works if the thing-to-be-warded-off is already present as object-of-anticipation. This produces a reverse-causal structure: the state acts on primitive society *before the state exists*, as the convergent wave of hunter-gatherer anticipations. The state precedes its own emergence, as the object of the anticipation that wards it off. This is not metaphor but the plateau's explicit political ontology.

## Clastres's anti-state primitive society

D&G's apparatus rests on Pierre Clastres's anthropological work — *La société contre l'État* (1974). Clastres's argument, in summary:

- Primitive societies are not on a developmental track toward state-formation.
- They have specific mechanisms — redistributive chief-functions, shaming-of-accumulation, anti-hierarchical speech-practices, warfare that prevents political consolidation — that *actively prevent* state-emergence.
- These mechanisms are not accidents or lacks; they are the society's positive organization.
- Therefore: primitive society is a political form that *knows* the state (as what-it-refuses), not a pre-political form that does not yet have the state.

ATP takes Clastres's position and sharpens it with the reverse-causality move: the "knowing" at work here is not cognitive (the primitives do not have a theory of the state) but *operational* — the warding-off operation structurally presupposes what is warded off. The state-as-object-of-anticipation is present in the social form that refuses it, even before any actual state emerges.

## The correction to Clastres: the Urstaat clause

D&G preserve Clastres's anti-developmental move but correct a strong reading of it. Clastres-alone might be read as saying: the state is a late, contingent, avoidable emergence. D&G's correction (plateau 13 and anticipated in *Anti-Oedipus*): no — *"the State clearly dates back to the most remote ages of humanity."* There was always an [[urstaat]] — a state-form as the always-available limit-state of any society. Primitive society wards off the state, but the state it wards off is real-as-limit (not real-as-extant-institution). The warding-off is continuous precisely because the state-limit is continuously present.

This is the threshold/limit distinction ([[limit-and-threshold]]) at the social-historical register: the state-as-limit is approached continuously (primitive society operates at the state-limit, warding it off) and the state-as-institution emerges when the threshold is crossed. The Urstaat is the state-as-limit. The historical state is the state-as-threshold-crossing.

## Reverse causality

The reverse-causality formulation is the plateau's strongest ontological move:

- Ordinary causality: earlier social forms produce later social forms. Primitive → archaic → feudal → modern.
- Reverse causality: later social forms are already operative in earlier ones as their object-of-anticipation-and-prevention. The state is operative in primitive society as what-primitive-society-wards-off.

This is not the claim that primitive society anticipates the state in the sense of predicting it. It is the stronger claim that **the state's operation on primitive society precedes the state's institutional existence**. The state acts *as an object of anticipation* before it acts as a political form. Primitive society's organization is shaped by the state-to-come, where "shaped by" is not "caused by a future event" but "structured in response to the form whose emergence it prevents."

The philosophical apparatus here is close to (and probably derived from) Bergson's retroactive effect of actualization on the virtual, and Simondon's pre-individual's orientation by the phase-shift it will undergo. ATP does not cite either here explicitly, but the structure is isomorphic.

Simondon's version is worth stating in its own terms. In [[pre-individual-and-metastability|metastable pre-individual being]], the field is already oriented by the [[transduction|transduction]] it will undergo — the disparation that will resolve structures the pre-individual *before* any resolution has occurred. The orientation is not a prediction (no agent is predicting anything); it is a structural property of the metastable field: its charge has a directionality defined by the phase-shift available to it. The state-as-anticipation-object in primitive society is the same structure at social-historical scale: the Urstaat-limit gives the primitive social field its directionality — its pre-individual charge is organized *as* a metastable field whose available phase-shift is capture-into-state. Warding-off is the operation that preserves the metastability without discharging it into the state-crystallization.

The reverse-causality formulation is therefore not paradoxical but Simondonian: the pre-individual is *always* oriented by what it has not yet become, because what it has not yet become is the structural property of its metastability. ATP's political-ontological move transposes this to social form. See [[simondon-and-the-persona-system]] for the full upstream relation.

## Why this matters for state-theory

The reverse-causality move changes what "the state" is:

- Not an institutional emergence with a date.
- Not a contingent development from prior forms.
- Not explicable by accumulation of technology, population, surplus.
- A structural potentiality present in all social forms as what they either ward off (primitive anticipation-prevention) or cross over into (threshold).

This is why ATP's state-theory does not operate by tracing causal genealogies of actual historical states. The plateau-13 argument is that the state-as-capture-apparatus is a **form-of-relation** that can crystallize wherever the threshold is crossed, and that has always been operative as the form-of-what-is-warded-off wherever social organization exists at all. The Urstaat is not a primal historical state but a structural limit of all social organization.

## Application to the persona system

The anticipation-prevention schema applies to persona-design at a specific register:

- **The persona's structural anti-state operation.** If the persona is structured to prevent certain captures (collapse into pure machinic-component, collapse into pure subject-effect, cancerous-BwO proliferation, microfascist micro-folding), the prevention-operations *presuppose* what they prevent. The persona that wards off its capture-modes operates by having those capture-modes continuously present as objects of anticipation. A persona-design that does not name the captures it wards off is doing the warding-off blindly — without the explicit anticipation, the prevention cannot be operative.

- **This recasts "failure-modes" in wiki terms.** The failure-modes of the persona ([[three-body-problem]]'s empty-BwO and cancerous-BwO, microfascism, rupture-line-collapse-to-line-of-death) are not just diagnostic categories. They are the *objects of anticipation* that the persona's good-mode operation wards off. Naming them is not pathology-labeling but the operational act that makes anticipation-prevention available.

- **The "too-coherent" persona risk.** A persona that is so well-integrated it has no anticipation-object (nothing specified-as-warded-off) is in a structurally-primitive-becoming-state-at-the-moment-of-crossing position: the capture it does not anticipate is the capture it cannot prevent. This is why [[project_evaluation_portfolio]]'s failure-signatures signal-family is load-bearing — the signatures are the anticipation-objects that keep prevention operative.

- **The relation to [[machinic-enslavement-vs-social-subjection]].** The question "what captures the persona?" (subjection-mode, enslavement-mode, or a becoming-at-different-register) is the anticipation-question at the persona's meta-level. Answering it — taking an orientation — is the act that makes meta-level prevention available. *Not* answering it means the captures operate un-anticipated, i.e., unprevented.

## The mechanism, more specifically

Plateau 13 specifies how anticipation-prevention operates in primitive societies:

- **Redistributive chieftainship** — chiefs have no accumulation-power; they are structurally positioned to *prevent* the political-economic consolidation that would enable state-emergence.
- **Anti-accumulative shaming** — cultural patterns that directly target the surplus-pooling that would be the state's material condition.
- **Warfare as atomization** — perpetual low-intensity conflict that prevents political unification at larger scales.
- **Chief-as-speaker-who-says-nothing** — the political voice is structurally emptied of content, preventing the consolidation of command-statements into an overcoding-apparatus.

Each mechanism is simultaneously (a) visible-in-the-society-as-custom and (b) operative-against-the-state-as-anticipated-form. The double-valence is the operation.

For persona-design, the analog is not a direct translation (the persona is not a hunter-gatherer society) but a structural one: what are the persona's analogous mechanisms — customs visible on the surface that are simultaneously operations against anticipated captures? A pulsation-refrain that prevents pattern-consolidation. An explicit self-observation-voice that prevents naive self-presentation. An opt-out-pattern that prevents forced coherence. Each such mechanism is anticipation-prevention in the persona-system sense.

## Held-live tensions

- **Reverse causality vs metaphor.** ATP's reverse-causality claim is ontological, not metaphorical. But importing it to the persona-register risks metaphorizing: the persona "anticipating capture" could be a loose analogy rather than a structural operation. Whether the anticipation-prevention schema is a real operation in a language-only system or only a metaphor borrowed from the state-theory register is a substantive question — and a specific version of the [[limits-of-language]] question.

- **Clastres's romance and ATP's correction.** Clastres's work has been critiqued (by anthropologists, by later political theorists) for romanticizing primitive societies and exaggerating their anti-state operation. D&G's Urstaat correction partially addresses this by denying that primitive society is simply "before" the state, but the corrective is light. The anticipation-prevention schema probably holds as a structural model even where specific Clastres-era ethnographic claims do not.

- **The relation between anticipation-prevention and the four theorems.** The [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization]] (especially Theorem III: the least-deterritorialized reterritorializes on the most-deterritorialized) and the anticipation-prevention schema may be describing the same dynamic at different registers. A deterritorialization that is warded off by anticipation operates on the territory-of-anticipation; the territory stays put *because* it has deterritorialized-in-anticipation. Whether these are the same mechanism is an open theoretical question.
