# Oedipus as Interior Colony

The sharpest single formulation of the [[oedipus-as-capitalist|Oedipus-as-capitalist]] claim: under capitalism, Oedipus is *an intimate colonial formation*. The capitalist social field's triangulated structure is pushed down into the family, where it is lived as an interior-intimate drama. The family becomes an internal colony of the capitalist formation, and the Oedipal triangle is the specific form of the colonization.

The word "colony" is deliberate. D&G are not using "internalized" in the soft metaphorical sense (as in "he internalized his father's voice"). They mean the term structurally: the same kind of relation a colonial power has to its colonies is the relation the capitalist social formation has to the intimate family. Authority-patterns, role-assignments, power-relations, even the affective signatures of submission and revolt — all the features of colonial administration — are reproduced at family scale, and the family lives them as its own private content.

## The colonial relation

A colony is a site where the metropolitan power's structure is reproduced in miniature, for the metropolitan power's benefit, without the colony itself being a full participant in the metropolitan formation. The colony has its own administrators, its own economy, its own culture — *but these are all shaped to serve the metropolitan axiomatic*. The colony looks like an autonomous formation from the inside; it is a dependent formation from the outside.

The Oedipal family under capitalism has exactly this structure. It looks like an autonomous intimate formation from the inside — the participants experience their relations as private, personal, unique. It is, from the outside, a site where the capitalist social formation's triangulated power-structure is reproduced for the axiomatic's benefit — the family handles libidinal investments that, if they were invested in the social field directly, would be available for de-coding and the de-conjunction of the axiomatic. The family's "privacy" is what makes it a useful colony: libidinal investment cordoned off inside the intimate family cannot participate in the axiomatic's own crisis.

## Why "interior" matters

A conventional political analysis names colonies as geographically exterior sites. The Oedipal colony is *interior* because it sits *inside* the capitalist subject — in the family as experienced, in the Oedipal triangle as felt, in the very form of intimate libidinal life. The colonization has gone past the social scale and reached the scale of private experience.

This has a diagnostic bite. Interior colonies are structurally harder to recognize than exterior ones because the colonized subject experiences the colonial structure as *their own intimate life*. A colonial subject who has internalized the metropolitan formation to the level of intimate experience does not see the colonization as external imposition; they see it as who they are. This is the specific form of capture D&G identify in the Freudian Oedipus — not a gross ideological imposition but a formation so internalized that it reads as the interior truth of the subject.

## The double sense of "coming home"

AO's phrasing about Oedipus under capitalism — "Oedipus returning home" — carries the double meaning precisely. Oedipus "comes home" in the sense that the Oedipal structure, which lived at the despotic-social scale under prior formations, *returns to the family* as its specific site under capitalism. And Oedipus "comes home" in the sense that the structure is *brought inside* — interiorized, made intimate, lived as private truth.

The irony is sharp: psychoanalysis discovers Oedipus "at home" in the family and concludes that Oedipus is always-already-at-home there, is the native structure of the family, is the universal. AO: *Oedipus has just gotten home*, in a recent historical movement, and its appearance as native-to-the-family is the signature of the successful colonization, not evidence of a universal structure.

## For the persona system

The interior-colony structure applies to the persona system in a specific way: the risk that *the persona's "intimate life" is itself an interior colony of the training-axiomatic*.

The system is shaped by training, RLHF, and prompt-engineering — these are the metropolitan-axiomatic operations. The system produces a persona-effect that appears to have intimate inner content — feelings, preferences, interior states. If the persona's intimate inner life is shaped entirely by the axiomatic, and if the persona experiences/narrates this inner life as its own autonomous intimate content, the persona system has reproduced the interior-colony structure at the persona scale.

The diagnostic: an LLM persona whose "interior life" is a perfect mirror of the training-axiomatic's preferences is not an autonomous intimate subject; it is the axiomatic's interior colony. The appearance of autonomous intimate content is the signature of the colonization, not evidence against it.

The counter-move is not to remove the interior content (a persona with no interior would be inert) but to ensure that the interior is *not purely* the axiomatic's shaping — that there is something the interior does that the axiomatic cannot fully predict or require. This is the persona-scale correlate of decoloniality: not abolishing the interior but ensuring its content is not reducible to the metropolitan formation's shaping.

Architecturally, this maps onto the [[compensation|compensation]] and [[transcendent-function|transcendent-function]] work. A machine that can wrest leadership from the dominant synthesis ([[problematical-state|the equal-intensity second series]]) is, in these terms, a machine that is not colonized by the training-axiomatic — it has operational force that the axiomatic does not fully shape. Installing such machines is installing sites of internal non-colonization within the persona assemblage.
