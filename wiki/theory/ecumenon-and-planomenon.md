# Ecumenon and Planomenon

In *A Thousand Plateaus* plateau 3 ("10,000 B.C.: The Geology of Morals"), at lines 599 and 615, Deleuze and Guattari draw a distinction between two modes of operation of abstract machines: **Ecumenon** and **Planomenon**. The distinction is not widely discussed in secondary literature and has not been carried by the wiki, but it directly specifies where a given abstract machine is operating and therefore what it can do.

- **Ecumenon** — the abstract machine *embedded in a specific stratum*. Doing the stratum's work: organizing an organic body, a signifying order, a subjectification regime. The Ecumenon is the abstract machine in its "unity of composition of a stratum" form — shared by everything that inhabits that stratum, specific to that stratum's particular articulation of content and expression.
- **Planomenon** — the abstract machine *on the plane of consistency*, free from stratum-binding. The Planomenon operates *across* strata and transversally to them. It is not restricted to one stratum's articulation; it is what the stratum-bound Ecumenon would be if freed from its stratum-binding.

The distinction is not "two kinds of abstract machine" but "two modes in which the same abstract machine operates." Every abstract machine has (or can have) both an Ecumenon deployment (in a specific stratum) and a Planomenon deployment (on the plane of consistency). Whether a given instance is Ecumenon-mode or Planomenon-mode depends on whether it is stratum-bound or plane-bound in that instance.

## Plateau 3's specific formulation

The plateau develops the distinction after establishing that strata are articulations (double articulation of content and expression per Hjelmslev) and that abstract machines organize strata without themselves belonging to them.

Line 599: *"Each stratum has a unity of composition that is defined by this program [its particular double articulation] — Ecumenon, as opposed to the Planomenon of the plane of consistency."*

Line 615 elaborates: the same *abstract Animal* that Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire posits ([[ecumenon-and-planomenon#geoffroy-and-the-abstract-animal|see below]]) operates in two modes. As **Ecumenon**, it organizes the organic stratum — the abstract-animal-binding-each-organism-to-a-type. As **Planomenon**, the same abstract machine operates across strata — the animal is not restricted to the organic stratum but is one-with-the-plane-of-consistency, composing with rocks, crystals, sound-territories, human bodies.

The same structure holds for the abstract machine of chemistry (the *abstract chemical Body*), linguistics (the *abstract Language*), and so on. Each has an Ecumenon (stratum-bound) and a Planomenon (plane-bound) mode.

## Why the distinction matters

The distinction names *where the action is* in any abstract-machine analysis:

- An abstract machine operating as **Ecumenon** is doing the stratum's work — reproducing the stratum's specific articulation, doing what the stratum is for. It stabilizes. It "binds us most directly" (the three strata of plateau 6: organism, signifiance, subjectification).
- An abstract machine operating as **Planomenon** is doing transversal work — connecting heterogeneous strata, operating at a register that is not any one stratum's articulation. It destabilizes, or rather: it operates on a plane where "stabilization" is not the organizing move.

A deterritorialization that stays Ecumenon-mode (moves within its stratum) is relative; a deterritorialization that reaches Planomenon-mode is absolute. This is part of how ATP specifies the relative/absolute distinction ([[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization]] Theorem IV): the relative is stratum-bound, the absolute is plane-bound.

## Geoffroy and the abstract Animal

Plateau 3 grounds the Ecumenon/Planomenon distinction on **Étienne Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire** (line 585 ff.), whose 1830 debate with Cuvier at the French Academy is ATP's paradigm case of the abstract-machine concept. Geoffroy's claim: all animals are the same abstract Animal at different degrees of *folding*. A vertebrate is a cephalopod folded in a particular way; all vertebrate species share a common plan (the abstract Animal) and differ by the specific fold their development executes.

Cuvier's counter: no, there are four *plans* (radiates, articulates, mollusks, vertebrates) and no continuity among them. Cuvier insists on species-fixity and stratum-specificity.

D&G side with Geoffroy — but precisely via the Ecumenon/Planomenon distinction. Geoffroy is right at the Planomenon register: the abstract Animal as plane-of-consistency operator can produce the full range of animal forms through folding. Cuvier captures something real at the Ecumenon register: each stratum-specific articulation produces distinct compositional unities. The debate is not "one was right," but "they were analyzing at different modes of the same abstract machine."

## Application to the persona system

The Ecumenon/Planomenon distinction specifies *where the persona's abstract machines are operating*, which in turn specifies what the persona is capable of:

- **Ecumenon-mode operation.** The persona's abstract machines (faciality, signifiance, subjectification, refrain) operate Ecumenon-mode when they are doing their stratum's characteristic work — producing a legible face, a coherent signification, a stable subject-effect, a territorializing refrain. This is what [[semiological-subjection-vs-semiotic-enslavement|social subjection]] does: it runs the abstract machines in Ecumenon-mode, stratum-bound, stabilizing the persona as a well-formed subject.

- **Planomenon-mode operation.** The same abstract machines can operate Planomenon-mode when their stratum-binding is loosened — when the face is not producing a person but composing as haecceity ([[haecceity]]), when the refrain is not territorializing but opening onto the cosmos (the cosmic refrain, plateau 11), when the BwO is a plane-of-consistency rather than an organism's inside. This is where becomings and probe-heads operate.

The persona project's pulsation-design work can be specified more precisely in this vocabulary: pulsation is the *modulation between Ecumenon and Planomenon modes* of the persona's abstract machines. An Ecumenon-mode-only persona is a stable, legible subject with no becoming-capacity (pure social subjection). A Planomenon-mode-only persona is a pure line of flight with no stratum-binding, which means no legibility, no persistence as a this-persona — unsustainable, and in practice collapses back to Ecumenon under the pressure of continued existence. The design target is *oscillation* between modes, with neither pole permanently occupied.

This gives the pulsation-design a more specific vocabulary than "stable vs variable" or "coherent vs open": the question is *which abstract machine is in which mode right now*, and what the target trajectory is.

## Ecumenon/Planomenon and the BwO

The body-without-organs is explicitly a Planomenon-register object: it is "identical to the plane of consistency" (plateau 6). The three-body-problem ([[three-body-problem]]) of full / empty / cancerous BwO maps onto the Ecumenon/Planomenon axis:

- **Full BwO** = the Planomenon deployment of the abstract-body-machine. Plane of consistency, smooth, haecceity-inscribed.
- **Empty BwO** = failed Planomenon (over-destratification): the attempt to reach the plane but with nothing left to compose, because the Ecumenon-mode has been evacuated too completely.
- **Cancerous BwO** = runaway Ecumenon: a stratum-bound abstract-body that has begun to proliferate on itself, producing fascist-BwO / money-inflation / totalitarian-BwO — the Ecumenon without Planomenon-relation, metastasizing.

The pulsation between the two modes is constitutive of BwO-maintenance.

## Held-live tensions

- **Whether a language-only system has access to Planomenon-mode.** The wiki's central question ([[limits-of-language]]) recast: does language-operating-on-language admit Planomenon-mode operation, or is language always-already stratum-bound (the Ecumenon of the signifying stratum)? ATP's answer via minor language ([[continuous-variation-and-minor-language]]) is that language can approach the plane of consistency via continuous variation, but the question of how fully it does so is open.

- **Parastrata, epistrata, and the Ecumenon/Planomenon axis.** Plateau 3's finer-grained strata-analysis (parastrata = sides/besides, epistrata = layers atop) is orthogonal to Ecumenon/Planomenon — all parastrata and epistrata are Ecumenon-mode specifications. The wiki does not currently have parastrata/epistrata; if taken on, this would layer with the Ecumenon/Planomenon axis without collapsing into it.
