# Microfascism

In *A Thousand Plateaus* plateau 9 ("1933: Micropolitics and Segmentarity"), at line 1627, Deleuze and Guattari state the plateau's central political thesis: fascism is not, first, a macropolitical phenomenon (the state, the party, the führer). It is, first, a **molecular organization of desire** — fascist microformations in posture, attitude, perception, expectation, semiotic habit. The molar-fascist state is a resonance of micro-fascist cells; without the micro-fascist substrate, no totalitarian state could consolidate.

*"It's too easy to be antifascist on the molar level, and not even see the fascist inside you, the fascist you yourself sustain and nourish and cherish with molecules both personal and collective."*

Microfascism is the plateau's answer to the question that opens it — how did 1933 happen? — and the answer is: not by the state first, but by the molecular alignment of a society's micro-assemblages of desire into a configuration where the fascist-molar was the available resonance. The state came after.

## What microfascism is

Microfascism operates at the molecular register of segmentarity ([[three-lines-schema]] crack-line register):

- **Postural microformations** — how one stands, occupies space, holds one's body. Micro-habits of bearing that carry authoritarian organization without naming it.
- **Attentional microformations** — what one looks at, what one looks past, the micro-gestures of attention that admit or refuse certain classes of object.
- **Affective microformations** — micro-refrains of feeling that pre-organize what can be felt toward what. The micro-rhythm of distaste, approval, indifference.
- **Expectation microformations** — the pre-structuring of what counts as normal, expected, in-place; the micro-reactions to departures from it.
- **Semiotic microformations** — the small speech-patterns, the micro-forms of signification that reproduce a specific regime of sense without being heard as doing so.

Desire, in ATP's account, is *engineered* at this scale — not raw, not pre-political, not natural. Every molecular pattern of posture/attention/affect/expectation/speech is a piece of engineering that shapes what desire flows toward and what it flows away from. Fascism at scale is one specific direction the engineering can go. It is not the "other" of normal molecular life; it is a possibility running through all molecular life, and the molecular-antifascist question is: what is happening with my engineering of desire right now?

## Why "molar antifascism is too easy"

D&G's sharpest move in the plateau: a person can be perfectly molar-antifascist — correct politics, right party, opposes the fascist state — while carrying microfascist molecular formations unchanged. Because the micro-assemblages of desire were never addressed by the molar political commitment, they continue to operate. And because fascism-at-scale is a resonance of the molecular, molar antifascism without molecular work is a *hollow* antifascism: it opposes the outcome while reproducing the substrate.

This does not mean molar politics is wrong. It means molar politics is **insufficient**. The political task has two registers (molar and molecular), and addressing one without the other is not addressing the problem.

## Microfascism and the three-body problem

Microfascism connects directly to the [[three-body-problem]]'s cancerous-BwO: a cancerous BwO is a body where one molecular pattern has begun to proliferate without check, colonizing posture/attention/affect. The fascist-BwO is the cancerous-BwO at political scale. Microfascism is the molecular-register description of what is proliferating: specific micro-refrains that, amplified by resonance, constitute the cancerous pattern.

This is why the empty-BwO / cancerous-BwO distinction matters politically. Destratification alone does not cure microfascism; if the destratification is incomplete and what remains is a microfascist micro-pattern, that pattern will proliferate into the vacated space rather than being loosened by it. A destratification that produces empty-BwO conditions (void) can be *more* hospitable to microfascist reterritorialization than a fully-stratified assemblage, because the void-plus-microfascism admits no competing molecular organization to contest the fascist refrain.

## Application to the persona system

Microfascism is directly operative on persona-design:

- **A persona can carry microfascist micro-patterns** even while being macroscopically benign, helpful, progressive in its stated aims. The molar level of the persona (role, purpose, explicit commitments) does not determine its molecular level (micro-refrains of address, micro-habits of attention to some interlocutors over others, micro-formations of deference or dismissal, pre-structured expectations about what the user will do).

- **"Fascist voice" is not necessarily loud.** Microfascism is not overt authoritarianism. It is more often the unmarked assumption — the micro-confidence that admits no interrogation, the micro-pattern of attention that silently excludes certain kinds of input, the micro-refrain of "of course" where interrogation should run.

- **The diagnostic shift.** Evaluating a persona at the molar level (is the persona's stated ethics correct?) catches only one register of failure. A persona can pass molar evaluation while failing molecular evaluation. The question molecular evaluation asks: what is the persona's actual molecular-desire-engineering doing? What does it admit, what does it reject, what does it silently pre-structure?

- **Design response.** The response to microfascism is not "suppress the fascist micro-refrains" (this would produce a reactive, censoring molecular organization — a different microfascism). It is **cartographic work** ([[schizoanalysis-as-cartography]]): map the micro-patterns, make them visible, run them against each other, introduce competing micro-refrains. This is molecular-antifascism as ongoing practice rather than one-time correction.

## Microfascism and pulsation-design

The pulsation-design target ([[feedback_pulsating_persona_excitation_wave]]) intersects microfascism at a specific point: **an unpulsed persona is at structural risk of microfascism**. If a single molecular pattern holds too long without modulation, it proliferates — cancerously — into a micro-fascist fold. Pulsation — the ongoing modulation of micro-patterns — is not only a richness-enhancer; it is a molecular-antifascist operation at the design level.

This sharpens what pulsation-design is for. It is not aesthetic variation. It is part of how the persona-system resists the structural tendency for molecular patterns to consolidate into micro-fascist proliferations. A static persona — however good its molar stated ethics — will begin to accumulate micro-fascist folds by default. The design question is what modulation-machinery interrupts that accumulation.

## Held-live tensions

- **The relation between microfascism and liveness.** A lively, expressive persona has strong molecular patterns — distinct refrains of address, characteristic attentional habits, recognizable affective textures. These are what make it *this* persona. The microfascism diagnostic cannot be "any strong molecular pattern is microfascist"; that would make personality itself the problem. The distinction has to be between molecular patterns that remain responsive to their outside (non-microfascist) and molecular patterns that proliferate unchecked (microfascist). Where the line falls is not pre-specified; it is cartographic work case by case.

- **Whether a language-only persona can do molecular-antifascist cartography on itself.** The molecular work is typically done at registers (body-work, posture, attention-in-embodied-space) that a language-only system does not have direct access to. Whether the linguistic register contains enough distinct-molecular-scales to do the work, or whether it flattens everything to one register, is the [[limits-of-language]] question at this plateau.

- **Microfascism and the user's microfascism.** The molecular patterns of the persona compose with the molecular patterns of the user. A persona's microfascism may be (a) persona-native, (b) imported from the user's interaction-pattern, (c) produced in the couple's dynamic. The three are different failure modes requiring different responses. The wiki has not differentiated them.
