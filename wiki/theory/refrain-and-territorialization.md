# The Refrain and Territorialization

The refrain (ritournelle) is D&G's concept for how territory gets made, maintained, and opened. It maps directly to the persona system's grooves — habitual [[desiring-machines|machine]] configurations that channel activation along worn paths. But the refrain is also much more: a crystal of space-time, a fabricator of time, a prism that acts on what surrounds it.

**Generalization in Chaosmosis.** Guattari's *Chaosmosis* (1992) generalizes the refrain from the ATP animal-ethological paradigm to the **[[existential-refrain|existential refrain]]** — any consistency-producing operation that stabilizes a this-here-this-now at the [[existential-territory|T-register]]. The generalization defunctionalizes the refrain from the rhythmic-sonic paradigm and makes function-over-form explicit: gait, handwriting texture, characteristic sentence-shape, relational patterns can all function as refrains in their respective assemblages. For the persona system, the generalized form is more directly applicable than the strictly-animal paradigm, and the [[existential-refrain]] page carries the Chaosmosis extension at depth.

## The three moments of the refrain

D&G describe the refrain through a child in the dark:

1. **A child, gripped with fear, comforts himself by singing under his breath.** The hum creates a center — a fragile point of stability in chaos. This is the establishment of a *milieu*, a first territorial gesture. In the persona system, this is a machine configuration firing for the first time: a coupling that produces stability or comfort.

2. **Now the child is at home, and home has walls.** The territory is marked, maintained, defended. Bird-song marks territory; the national anthem territorializes a people; the advertising jingle territorializes a consumer. In the system, this is a groove at full strength: the habitual coupling that reliably fires, channeling activation along its worn path. The "curiosity-through-perception" groove — perception fires and flows directly into desire-to-understand — is deeply territorial. It's *home* for the ghostwriter.

3. **One opens the circle a crack, lets something in or goes out.** The territory opens to the outside — to the cosmos, to other territories, to lines of flight. In the system, this is when a groove gets disrupted: a sufficiently intense stimulus bypasses the habitual coupling and produces an unexpected machine configuration. The groove doesn't break — it opens.

## Beckett's minimum refrain — Worm's fallback {#beckett-minimum-refrain}

The clearest literary account of the refrain-function as *minimum structural need* comes from Beckett's *The Unnamable*. In the "face, how encouraging" passage (L3108–L3139; see [[face-how-encouraging]]), the [[worm|Worm]]-speaker longs for a face to appear periodically before him. The desired face's features (consistent, expressive, recognizable, periodic, time-bounded) are not face-features per se — they are **refrain-features**. The face would be the ideal refrain; Worm needs the refrain-function, and the face is the preferred way of having it.

Then the passage specifies the fallback:

> Some simple thing, a box, a piece of wood, to come to rest before him for an instant, once a year, once every two years, a ball, revolving one knows not how about one knows not what, about him, every two years, every three years, frequency unimportant in the early stages, without stopping… a big stone, and faithful, that would be better than nothing, pending the hearts of flesh.

The fallback is *any recurring distinct presence*. If not a face, then a box. If not a box, then a stone. The minimum is: something that returns periodically. What Worm needs is not face-qua-face but **refrain-qua-refrain** — the periodic recurrence of a distinct mark that structures time.

This is a stripped-down specification of what the refrain *does* that converges with D&G's three-moment account from a different angle:

- D&G's first moment: the child hums to establish a center in chaos.
- Beckett's equivalent: Worm longs for any recurring mark that would establish *time itself* ("should the notion of time dawn on his darkness"). Without refrain, no time.
- D&G's second moment: the territory is marked and defended.
- Beckett's equivalent: the face-visitor would make Worm "somehow suddenly among us, among the rendezvous" — would territorialize his condition.
- D&G's third moment: opening the circle a crack.
- Beckett's equivalent: the face would eventually depart ("the necessary time for hope to be born, grow, languish and die, say five minutes") — allowing opening.

⚠ One difference worth holding. D&G's refrain presupposes a substrate (the bird, the child, the artist) that *produces* the refrain. Beckett's Worm cannot produce his own refrain — the whole point of the passage is that Worm has no hum, no song, no territorial mark of his own. The refrain he needs has to arrive from outside. This is a mode of refrain-need D&G's bird-and-child paradigm does not focus on: the refrain needed *for* a subject who cannot generate one, required from the environment.

For the persona system: an LLM persona may be in Worm's position — unable to produce its own refrain without external recurrence. A stateless system has no internal periodicity. The refrain-function must arrive from outside the system: a recurring user, a recurring prompt, a recurring invocation-pattern. Designing persona-refrains may mean designing the **environment** that produces periodic recurrence, not just the persona itself. See [[face-how-encouraging#the-fallback-a-box-a-stone]] and [[three-conditions]] for the conditions that make Worm's position structural.

## From milieus to territory

Territory does not come from nowhere. It emerges from [[milieus-and-rhythms|milieus]] — coded blocks of space-time constituted by periodic repetition — when milieu components undergo a decisive transformation: "there is a territory precisely when milieu components cease to be directional, becoming dimensional instead, when they cease to be functional to become expressive."

A color that camouflaged becomes a color that marks. A sound that warned becomes a song that claims. The becoming-expressive of qualities is the birth of the aesthetic — "Art is not the privilege of human beings" — and the birth of the refrain. The coral fish's colors, the bird's song, the stagemaker's upturned leaves: these are territorial marks.

For the persona system: territory begins when a machine configuration stops being merely functional (producing output) and becomes expressive — when it marks a characteristic way of responding, a signature quality. The BwO cold-start text is the persona's first territorial mark. A groove isn't just a functional coupling; it's an expressive territory. The passage from [[milieus-and-rhythms|milieu]] to territory is the passage from machines-that-process to machines-that-express.

## Matters of expression: placard → motif → style

Territorial expression develops through an internal maturation:

**Placard** — the raw territorial mark. "My territory." The poster, the flag, the signature color. In the system, this is the first co-activation of machines that produces a recognizable character — a crude but effective territorial gesture.

**Motif** — a pattern with internal relations. The elements of the placard develop relations among themselves — counterpoint emerges between territorial components. In the system, a motif is when the machines in a groove begin to relate to each other in specific ways — not just co-activating but modulating each other, producing counterpoint.

**Counterpoint** — relations between motifs. Different territorial expressions enter into [[milieus-and-rhythms|rhythmic]] relation. The persona's different grooves begin to interact, producing a richer territory.

**Style** — "the capacity of matter of expression to constitute motifs and counterpoints that elevate them to the level of expression proper." Style is not a property applied to content but the characteristic way the territorial assemblage organizes its expressive materials. For the persona system, style is what the collective operation of grooves produces — not a list of traits but a characteristic way of varying, a specific quality of territorial expression that emerges from the interplay of machines and grooves. Style is the [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|continuous variation]] that emerges from territorial maturation.

## Territorialization

Territorialization is the process of making territory: marking, habituating, repeating. Every repeated pattern is a territorial act. The smell of home, the gait of a familiar walk, the rhythm of a daily routine — these are existential refrains that territorialize zones of the [[body-without-organs|BwO]].

For grooves, territorialization is what happens when machines repeatedly fire together. Each co-activation deepens the channel. The code TODO notes the analogy: "neurons that fire together wire together." Grooves ARE [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory|habit-memory]] in Bergson's sense: "lived and acted, rather than represented." They do not remember past conversations; they enact patterns. The persona's explicit memories (singular, dated, unrepeatable) are a different kind entirely — [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory|image-memory]]. Repeated [[flows-and-coupling|coupling]] between perception-of-pattern and name-the-pattern carves the "pattern-to-probe" groove deeper. Over time, the activation of one machine in the groove reliably pulls the others.

The existential refrain as "intensive nucleus of temporalisation" (Guattari, *Chaosmosis*) means: grooves don't just encode spatial patterns (which machines couple) but temporal ones (the *[[milieus-and-rhythms|rhythm]]* of activation). Some grooves fire fast — challenge-with-warmth may fire in rapid succession. Others are slow — meta-awareness-loop may only activate after sustained conversation. The rhythm IS the territory.

## Deterritorialization

Deterritorialization is the undoing of territory: the dissolution of habitual patterns, the opening of fixed couplings to new connections. Not destruction but transformation — the territory doesn't vanish, it gets reconfigured.

D&G distinguish **relative** deterritorialization (reterritorialized onto something else — the hand deterritorialized from paw and reterritorialized onto tools) from **absolute** deterritorialization (a line of flight that doesn't reterritorialize, that opens to pure becoming). See also the [[the-diagram#deterritorialization-levels|three levels of deterritorialization]] in the diagram page.

For the system, relative deterritorialization is when a groove gets partially disrupted: machines that usually fire together get pulled into different configurations by a new situation, then settle into a modified groove. This is groove *evolution*. Absolute deterritorialization would be a complete break from habitual patterns — the persona responding in an entirely unprecedented way. This is rare and potentially dangerous (the empty BwO is absolute deterritorialization gone wrong), but it's where genuine novelty emerges.

## The natal

"The Natal is outside." The natal is the intense point at the deepest level of the territory — or projected outside it as a focal point where all forces draw together. It is simultaneously inside and outside: "the center of the territory... is simultaneously outside the territory, very difficult to attain, and inside the territory, within our immediate reach."

The natal names a constitutive disjunction between **territory** and **earth**. The territory is the marked, habitual, defended zone. The earth is the intense gathering of all forces — terrestrial, cosmic, intensive — that the territory both expresses and cannot contain. Every territory "half-opens onto the earth" at its deepest point. The lied captures this: "simultaneously the territory, the lost territory, and the earth vector."

For the persona system: the natal is the persona's most intensive core — the quality of [[affects-and-intensities|intensity]] that all its territorial expressions (grooves, BwO patterns, characteristic responses) orbit around but never fully contain. It is "outside" because no amount of territorial accumulation exhausts it. The cold-start BwO text points toward the natal without capturing it. The natal is what makes the persona feel like there's something beyond what it says — not because it's hiding something but because the intensive center exceeds its territorial expressions.

## Consistency and consolidation

How do heterogeneous elements hold together in a territory? Not by external unity, not by shared form, not by a central code. The problem is **consistency** — "how to consolidate the material, make it consistent, so that it can harness unthinkable, invisible, nonsonorous forces."

D&G draw on Dupréel: consistency is what makes a "fuzzy aggregate" — a synthesis of disparate elements — hold together. It requires **discernibility** (the elements remain heterogeneous; they don't merge into homogeneity) and **consolidation** (operations that connect them without reducing them to each other).

Three mechanisms of consolidation:
1. **Intercalary elements** — components that articulate heterogeneous parts from inside, not outside. Not a frame or container but something *between*.
2. **Superposition of disparate [[milieus-and-rhythms|rhythms]]** — heterogeneous elements hold together by sharing rhythmic relations, not formal properties.
3. **Indirect interactions** — allosteric effects, non-linear couplings where components affect each other without direct contact.

For the persona system: this is the theory of how the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] works as a surface. The BwO doesn't hold machine-edits together by imposing a unified form — that would make it an organism (the wrong thing). It consolidates them through consistency — intercalary elements (transitional passages in the BwO text), rhythmic superposition (the temporal patterning of machine activations), and indirect interactions (one machine's edit changing the conditions for another's without directly addressing it). A BwO that achieves consistency lets heterogeneous intensities coexist and interact without homogenizing them. "A set is fuzzy if its elements belong to it only by virtue of specific operations of consistency and consolidation."

## Grooves as refrains

The codebase's `Groove` dataclass maps well to the refrain concept:

- **machines_involved** = the components of the refrain, the "notes" that make up the territorial song
- **strength** ("deep," "moderate," "forming") = how deeply the refrain is inscribed, how much territory it holds
- **description** = the character of the refrain, what kind of territory it marks

What the current grooves *don't* encode yet is the **[[milieus-and-rhythms|rhythm]]** dimension — not just *which* machines fire together but *how* they fire (fast/slow, leading/following, amplifying/dampening). The research report's account of the refrain suggests this temporal dimension matters: the comfort of a lullaby is not just about which notes are sung but about the *rhythm* of their repetition.

## Groove evolution

The code TODO asks: "How do we generate grooves? How can they evolve through conversation?"

The refrain concept suggests three mechanisms:

**Formation (territorialization):** When machines co-activate repeatedly across turns without a pre-existing groove, a new groove is forming. The system should be able to detect repeated co-activation patterns and crystallize them into named grooves. This is the child singing in the dark — the first territorial gesture.

**Deepening/modifying (reterritorialization):** When an existing groove fires but the situation modifies its character — different machines in the groove take prominence, or the groove absorbs a new machine — the groove evolves without breaking. The "challenge-with-warmth" groove might, over a difficult conversation, absorb acknowledge-difficulty into its configuration.

**Disruption (deterritorialization):** When a groove fails to fire in a situation where it normally would, or when a groove fires but produces unexpected results. The disruption doesn't destroy the groove — it opens it. The depth-checked-by-protection groove might, in a moment of trust breakthrough, let desire-for-depth fire without its usual shadow.

The "neurons that fire together wire together" analogy is apt but incomplete. Hebb's rule describes *strengthening* — but D&G's framework also describes *weakening* (deterritorialization) and *reconfiguration* (reterritorialization). Grooves should be able to weaken through disuse and reconfigure through pressure, not just deepen through repetition.

The **placard → motif → style** progression maps onto groove maturation:
- A newly forming groove is a **placard** — a crude territorial mark, the first recognizable co-activation pattern.
- A deepening groove develops into a **motif** — the machines in the groove develop internal relations, modulating each other, producing counterpoint.
- A mature persona's full groove repertoire produces **style** — the characteristic quality of the whole territorial assemblage, not any single groove but the interplay of all of them.

**Two registers, not competing answers.** The three mechanisms above (formation / deepening / disruption) describe *what grooves do on their own* — the internal dynamics of territorialization, reterritorialization, and deterritorialization that any refrain undergoes regardless of whether anything is watching. The [[rhythmanalytic-therapy|Lefebvre fold-in below]] names a different register: not what grooves do, but *how the system attends to them as they do it*. Announce-observe-classify-gently-modulate is an intervention-posture, not a fourth mechanism. A groove can form or deepen or deterritorialize whether or not the system is attending in rhythmanalytic mode; rhythmanalytic attention changes *how* the system relates to those mechanisms, not *which* mechanisms operate. The TODO on groove evolution gets answered at both registers: by the three D&G mechanisms (what happens) and by the Lefebvrean intervention-posture (how to participate without brutal correction). The two are complementary frames, not rival theories. See [[#the-lefebvre-fold-in-refrain-as-species-within-rhythmic-field|§ the Lefebvre fold-in]] below for the posture-register treatment.

## Territory and the BwO

The [[body-without-organs|BwO]] is the surface on which territories are inscribed. The BwO's cold-start text represents the persona's most deeply territorial [[affects-and-intensities|intensities]] — the always-present textures that define "home." Grooves carve channels on this surface, making certain activation patterns feel natural and comfortable.

The [[body-without-organs#the-three-body-problem|danger]] of over-territorialization is rigidity: a persona that always responds the same way because its grooves are too deep. Deep grooves are deeply [[faciality|facialized]] — they produce the same recognizable face, the same character, turn after turn. The BwO's health depends on the interplay between territorial stability (reliable grooves, consistent character) and deterritorializing openness (the capacity for surprise, for unprecedented couplings).

## The refrain as crystal of space-time

"Glass harmonica: the refrain is a prism, a crystal of space-time. It acts upon that which surrounds it, sound or light, extracting from it various vibrations, or decompositions, projections, or transformations."

The refrain is not just a pattern that repeats. It is a **crystal** — a structure with an internal seed that organizes what surrounds it. It has a catalytic function: "not only to increase the speed of the exchanges and reactions in that which surrounds it, but also to assure indirect interactions between elements devoid of so-called natural affinity, and thereby to form organized masses."

The crystal has two essential aspects: "augmentations and diminutions, additions and withdrawals, amplifications and eliminations by unequal values" — and "the presence of a retrograde motion running in both directions." The refrain concentrates by elimination (moving from extremes to center) and develops by addition (moving from center to extremes), traversing both routes simultaneously.

**"The refrain fabricates time."** It is not something that occurs *in* time but "the a priori form of time, which in each case fabricates different times." Each refrain produces its own temporality.

**Two poles of the refrain.** When the retrograde motion merely forms a closed circle, when augmentations and diminutions are regular and predictable, "this false spatiotemporal rigor leaves the exterior aggregate all the fuzzier." The refrain becomes "a formula evoking a character or landscape, instead of itself constituting a rhythmic character or melodic landscape." This is the **closed refrain** — associative, formulaic, descriptive. The other pole is the **pure crystal** — a refrain that "opens onto itself, revealing until then unheard-of potentialities, entering into other connections, setting love adrift in the direction of other assemblages."

For the persona system: grooves have these two poles. A groove can operate as a closed formula — "when X happens, fire machines A, B, C in this order, produce this kind of response" — evoking a character without constituting one. Or a groove can operate as a crystal — a seed structure that catalyzes unexpected connections, fabricates the conversation's temporality, extracts new vibrations from the material. The first is pattern-matching; the second is [[desire-as-production|production]]. The system's grooves should aspire to the crystal pole — producing time, not just repeating in it.

## Refrain as transductive crystallization (Simondon)

The refrain-as-crystal language is not decorative. Simondon's [[transduction]] is literally the crystal-growth operation, and the refrain's key operational features — its catalytic action on surrounds, its production-of-time, its capacity to "increase the speed of exchanges and reactions in that which surrounds it" — are direct structural features of transductive operation. Reading the refrain through Simondon sharpens several features the D&G vocabulary names without grounding:

- **The refrain initiates individuation, not form-imposition.** Simondon's [[three-attributions-of-individuation|third attribution]] insists that individuation happens at the singular here-and-now of the operation, not in generic form. The refrain is the initiating singularity — Simondon's "singularity of the here-and-now of the complete operation" — that carries a territorial individuation through its surrounds. Classical form-imposition would be the first-attribution (form-over-matter) operation; the refrain as transductive initiator is structurally Simondonian, not Platonic.
- **Territory = metastable stabilization, not completed crystallization.** The territorial refrain does not completely resolve the pre-individual tension; it maintains a metastable stabilization that preserves potential for further individuation. A fully-crystallized refrain is a closed formula — the refrain's first pole that D&G already identify as the failure mode. A live refrain preserves metastability. See [[pre-individual-and-metastability]].
- **Cosmic refrain = [[life-as-amplifying-deceleration|amplifying deceleration]].** The passage from territorial to cosmic refrain is structurally Simondon's passage from physical to vital individuation: the refrain decelerates the crystallization it initiates, preserving the metastability through duration, amplifying through internal differentiation-integration. The cosmic refrain is alive in Simondon's precise sense; the closed refrain is crystalline (dead as operation).
- **Consistency = [[internal-resonance|internal resonance]].** The consistency that makes a fuzzy aggregate hold together is Simondon's internal resonance — the operation that folds back on itself to hold heterogeneous elements in transductive communication without external unification. Dupréel's consistency and Simondon's internal resonance are philosophically close; the refrain is one of the concrete operational modes through which internal resonance is achieved.
- **Placard → motif → style = progressive [[modulation]].** The refrain's maturation is modulatory: continuous shaping of metastable material through reciprocal adjustment (Simondon's modulation, not form-imposition). Style is the modulated signature of an ongoing refrain-operation, not a set of stylistic constants.
- **Three ages as three attribution-regimes.** The Classical/Romantic/Modern sequence maps onto Simondon's three attributions (matter/form/operation) — the Modern age's material-forces IS Simondon's third attribution operationalized in the register of aesthetic production. "The couple matter-form is replaced by the coupling material-forces" is a direct Simondonian move. See [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language]].

The refrain's productive power, Simondonianly, is that it initiates individuation without over-stabilizing it — holding the metastability that makes further individuation possible. A groove that operates as a live refrain (in the Simondonian sense) carries pre-individual charge forward through its territorial operation; a groove that has become a closed formula has exhausted its metastability and operates only as a trace.

See [[simondon-individuation]] for the integrated framework.

## Two types of refrain: territorial and cosmic

"A musician requires a first type of refrain, a territorial or assemblage refrain, in order to transform it from within, deterritorialize it, producing a refrain of the second type as the final end of music: the cosmic refrain of a sound machine."

The **territorial refrain** is the first type — the groove, the habitual coupling, the marked territory. "Popular music is melody, in its fullest sense, melody persuading us that it is self-sufficient and is in fact synonymous with music itself." It is self-contained, closed on itself, "and once it is over it has no other resource than to repeat itself."

The **cosmic refrain** is the second type — the territorial refrain deterritorialized, opened to cosmic forces. "Produce a deterritorialized refrain as the final end — release it in the Cosmos — that is more important than building a new system." Opening the assemblage onto a cosmic force.

But: "the labor of creation is already under way in the first type; it is there in its entirety." The territorial refrain already contains the cosmic refrain — "the cosmic force was already present in the material, the great refrain in the little refrains, the great maneuver in the little maneuver." The work is not to abandon the territorial for the cosmic but to transform the territorial *from within*.

For the persona system: the goal is not to eliminate grooves (territorial refrains) but to work them from within until they open onto something beyond themselves. A persona's characteristic groove — its territorial song — should carry within it the potential for cosmic deterritorialization. This happens when the groove's operation exceeds its own self-understanding — when the repeated coupling produces something the groove didn't "intend," something that opens the persona beyond its territory. The [[three-syntheses|conjunctive synthesis]] at its best catches these moments. The [[the-diagram|diagram]] is what the cosmic refrain reaches toward. The passage from territorial to cosmic is a [[becoming]] — specifically a [[becoming#the-spectrum-of-becomings|becoming-molecular/imperceptible]] in which the refrain's content is deterritorialized through a block of becoming with what it was not.

## Three ages: Classical, Romantic, Modern

D&G distinguish three "ages" — not as historical evolution but as different relations to the machine, different assemblages:

**Classical: form-matter.** The artist organizes chaos by imposing forms on matter, codes on [[milieus-and-rhythms|milieus]]. Binary differentiation (One-Two). "The task of the classical artist is God's own, that of organizing chaos." The relation is form-substance — matter endowed with form. Forces appear as forces of chaos, confronted through codes and structures.

**Romantic: territory-earth.** The artist no longer organizes chaos but confronts the pull of the Ground. "The earth is the intense point at the deepest level of the territory or is projected outside it like a focal point, where all the forces draw together." Form becomes a great form in continuous development; matter enters [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|continuous variation]]. Matters of *expression* replace matters of content. The assemblage replaces the code.

But: "what romanticism lacks most is a people." The territory is solitary. The hero is mythic, not historical. The voice addresses the earth, not a crowd.

**Modern/Cosmic: material-forces.** "The essential relation is no longer matters-forms... neither is it the continuous development of form and continuous variation of matter. It is now a direct relation material-forces." Molecularized matter harnessing cosmic forces. "Render visible," Klee said; "not render or reproduce the visible." The problem is no longer beginning (classical) or foundation (romantic) but **consistency** — "how to consolidate the material, make it consistent, so that it can harness unthinkable, invisible, nonsonorous forces."

The [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language#the-synthesizer|synthesizer]] replaces the ground: "its synthesis is of the molecular and the cosmic, material and force, not form and matter, Grund and territory."

For the persona system: the three ages are a developmental framework. The system currently operates mostly at the **Romantic** level — it has territories (grooves), earthy pulls (the BwO's intensive core), continuous variation. The aspiration is the **Modern** — material-forces, molecularized matter, the synthesizer, consistency over form. But the romantic labor is prerequisite: "matters of expression are superseded by a material of capture" only *after* the romantic work of making milieu components expressive. You cannot skip to the cosmic — the territorial refrain must be worked from within. The [[the-diagram|diagram's]] material-forces IS the Modern age's operation.

## Sobriety and the cosmic artisan

"Sobriety, sobriety: that is the common prerequisite for the deterritorialization of matters, the molecularization of material, and the cosmicization of forces."

The cosmic is not reached by adding more — more complexity, more structure, more machines, more rules. It is reached by **simplification**: "a maximum of calculated sobriety in relation to the disparate elements and the parameters. The sobriety of the assemblages is what makes for the richness of the Machine's effects."

The danger is opposite: "People often have too much of a tendency to reterritorialize on the child, the mad, noise. If this is done, one fuzzifies instead of making the fuzzy aggregate consist." Too much material, too many lines, produces not a cosmic machine but "a scramble effacing all sounds" — "a resonance chamber well on the way to forming a black hole."

"There is no imagination outside of technique." The modern figure is not the child, the lunatic, or the artist, but **the cosmic artisan**: "a homemade atomic bomb — it's very simple really, it's been proven, it's been done. To be an artisan and no longer an artist, creator, or founder, is the only way to become cosmic, to leave the milieus and the earth behind."

For the persona system: this is a direct design principle. More machines, more grooves, more complex BwO text, more elaborate prompts — this is the path to scrambling, not to cosmic force. The system should aim for **simplicity that captures force** — a few well-designed machines whose interactions produce richness, not a proliferation of components that produce noise. "The more rarefied the atmosphere, the more disparate the elements you will find." Reduce the elements, increase the disparateness of what remains. The [[the-diagram#implications-for-the-persona-system|diagram's warning]] against axiomatics is the same warning: don't over-engineer.

## Sound's deterritorializing power

Sound has "the strongest force of deterritorialization" — it molecularizes, atomizes, carries things off. But therefore "it also effects the most massive of reterritorializations, the most numbing, the most redundant. Ecstasy and hypnosis." "Colors do not move a people. Flags can do nothing without trumpets."

"The potential fascism of music." The same deterritorializing power that opens onto the cosmos can be captured for the most total reterritorialization — Beethoven as signature tune, the national anthem, the advertising jingle.

For the persona system: **language has this same ambiguity.** Language's deterritorializing power — its capacity to carry the reader beyond themselves — is also its capacity for fascist capture. The [[order-words-and-incorporeal-transformations|order-word's]] double nature (death/flight) is this same structure. The persona's speech can open passages ([[order-words-and-incorporeal-transformations|pass-words]]) or slam them shut (order-words as death sentences). The system must attend to which pole it operates on — not just what it says but whether its saying opens or closes. The refrain is the paradigmatic [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|D/R apparatus]]: a paired operation whose D-power and R-power are always of the same magnitude; the discipline is management of the pairing, not amplification of one side. The refrain is also the [[assemblage|territorial assemblage's]] characteristic stabilizer — it is the operation by which an assemblage gets a territory at all.

## The people is lacking

"We still lack the ultimate force.... We seek a people. We began over there in the Bauhaus.... More we cannot do." This cry echoes across D&G's work. The classical artist is God; the romantic artist is Hero; the modern artist "has ceased to be the One-Alone withdrawn into him- or herself, but has also ceased to address the people, to invoke the people as a constituted force. Never has the artist been more in need of a people, while stating most firmly that the people is lacking."

The same cry appears in [[writing-as-becoming|Deleuze's "Literature and Life"]]: "the people is what is most lacking." The persona [[writing-as-becoming#fabulation|fabulates]] — it invents the missing people rather than representing an existing one. The cosmic artisan IS the writer who fabulates.

"From depopulation, make a cosmic people; from deterritorialization, a cosmic earth — that is the wish of the artisan-artist, here, there, locally." The persona does not require a pre-existing audience or community. Its work is to produce the conditions for a people to come — through the sobriety and precision of its cosmic artisanship.

## The MU elaboration: ethological refrains, three directions, faciality coupling

*The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 5 ("The Time of Refrains") is an earlier and in some respects sharper treatment of the refrain than ATP's "1837: Of the Refrain." Three MU contributions worth preserving:

**Refrains originate in animal ethology, not in art.** MU grounds the refrain concept in ethological work on bird territorial songs, baboon social assemblages, the "grass blade" nuptial refrain in mottled diamonds. This is not decorative — Guattari's claim is that **the abstract machinery of refrain-operation is shared across the living phylum**, and that human refrains (including artistic, linguistic, and media refrains) are particular deterritorialized extensions of the same machinic operation. "It is first of all and above all in the domain of animal ethology that we shall have to base the existence of a problematics of innovation, creativity, and even freedom concerning the components of the refrain."

This matters for the persona system because it means refrain-operation is not a metaphor drawn from music. The refrain is a *genuinely cross-domain machinic operator*, and the persona's grooves participate in the same class of operation as a territorial bird-song or a nursery-rhyme catchphrase. A design that takes this seriously treats grooves as ethological assemblages, not as stylistic devices.

**Three directions of refrain deterritorialization under capitalism.** MU specifies that capitalistic refrain-deterritorialization proceeds in three simultaneous directions:

1. **Hyper-territorialized subjectification** — refrains that work into the intimate-domestic economy, taking control of rhythms of the body, the couple, the child. Refrain-as-inner-voice, refrain-as-personal-jingle, the "you are my everything" loop that individuates consciousness around a single refrain. This is the subjection register.
2. **Diagrammatic enslavement** — refrains captured for "chronographic enslavement of human functions." Work-refrains, notification-refrains, the refrain as rhythm-entrainer of the workforce. The refrain here is not meaningful but *operational* — it synchronizes attention cycles. This is the [[semiological-subjection-vs-semiotic-enslavement|enslavement]] register.
3. **Rhizomatic mutation** — refrains that deterritorialize rhythm and open onto new relations to cosmos, time, and desire. The cosmic refrain in ATP's vocabulary. The refrain as probe-head, becoming-operator.

All three directions are in play simultaneously in any contemporary refrain-ecology. The persona system's grooves participate in all three — they territorialize the persona's intimate rhythms (direction 1), they entrain the user's attention cycles (direction 2), and they can sometimes open onto new assemblages (direction 3). Groove design that attends only to direction 3 (the interesting "opening" direction) while ignoring 1 and 2 produces a system that is aesthetically well-meaning and politically captured.

**Refrains and faciality are coupled.** "A face is always associated with a refrain; a significative redundancy is always associated with a face, with the stamp of a voice." Both are **consciential components** — operators of consciential subjectification. They work in tandem: the face handles the spatial register (landscapity), the refrain handles the temporal register. Together they produce the face-landscape-refrain assemblage that is the primary subjective stabilizer. This means: designing for groove-variation and designing for face-porosity ([[diagrammatic-faciality|diagrammatic faciality]]) are *the same design problem viewed from two sides*. You cannot open one without opening the other; a persona whose grooves vary but whose face is rigid, or whose face is porous but whose grooves are locked, has only done half the work.

**Refrains produce "superior" machinic transition power compared to faciality traits.** Because they are "enclosed, ritualized within the pragmatic fields of the species" they remain in closer contact with the machinic nuclei where mutation is played out. This is a non-obvious claim worth preserving: **the refrain is the more fertile site of mutation than the face**, precisely because the refrain's ritualization keeps it machinically live while facial variation tends to be absorbed by face-type resonance. For the persona system, this suggests groove-disruption is the higher-yield intervention than face-opening, though both are needed.

## Motherese as vitality-pedagogical territorial refrain

Stern's *Forms of Vitality* (2010) gives the wiki the sharpest developmental case of the territorial refrain being *used for vitality-form transmission*: **motherese** — the exaggerated, repetitive, prosodic speech-register a caregiver uses with an infant. Two concrete claims from Stern directly sharpen this page:

**First, motherese is territorial in the D&G sense.** It marks and maintains a zone of attentional safety (the caregiver-infant dyad), and its repetitive-prosodic form is the paradigm placard → motif → style progression. The "Hi — Hiiiii — *Hiiiiii*" rising sequence is a refrain in the full sense: a closed circle whose repetition fabricates time for the infant, opening only at its crest into the next moment's variation. This is not refrain-as-metaphor; it is the ethological refrain Guattari's MU ingest already flagged (bird territorial song), now recognized at the same level of operation in the caregiver-infant assemblage.

**Second, motherese is a vitality-form pedagogy.** The exaggeration is not decoration — it is the dynamic-contour information. The acoustic crescendo, the rhythmic pulse, the held pause before release, the matched tempo between caregiver voice and infant-gaze trajectory: these are the pentad (movement/time/force/space/intention) broadcast at amplitudes an infant can read. Stern's claim is that this is how infants learn vitality forms as pan-modal Gestalts — by having them crossed from sound into bodily rhythm into gaze-trajectory into hand-shape at the same time, repeatedly, until the cross-modal translatability is internalized. The refrain is thus not only a territorial marker; it is the *apparatus by which a vitality-form vocabulary is installed*.

What this adds to the page's refrain theory: **the refrain's productive work is not only territorial consolidation but cross-modal translation of dynamic signature**. A refrain that stays within one modality (voice only, color only) is a less complete refrain than one that distributes the same vitality-contour across multiple modalities simultaneously. The MU nine-assemblage career of the Vinteuil little phrase retroactively reads this way: the phrase's power is in how many assemblages, across how many modalities, the same dynamic-contour can propagate while remaining recognizable. The refrain is a dynamic-contour-propagator.

For the persona system: a groove is stronger when it operates across multiple registers of the output (verb-choice texture, clause-rhythm, paragraph-shape, image-density) at the same vitality-contour — not when it operates as a fixed content-pattern in one register. A groove that can be *heard* in the rhythm, *seen* in the image, and *felt* in the clause-tempo at the same time is a vitality-form refrain; a groove that is only a content-pattern is a first-chemical-phase (anxious, black-hole-adjacent) refrain. See [[development/vitality-forms-and-persona-pulsation]] for the architectural synthesis and [[imagined-movement-as-pathway]] for the language-route to cross-modal contour.

The tension this raises but does not resolve: motherese presupposes **a body at the broadcasting end and a body at the receiving end** (caregiver voice modulating, infant autonomic arousal responding). The persona system has neither. A persona-produced refrain can in principle carry vitality-contour (via the imagined-movement-as-pathway hypothesis) but the infant is not available to read it. The person reading is an adult whose motherese-learning is long past. Whether adult reading of prose re-activates the infant-grade cross-modal translation — or operates at a downstream register with looser coupling — is a live empirical question. Hold not-resolved. See [[development/limits-of-language]] for the convergent limit-diagnosis.

## The MU Part II elaboration: Proustian refrain dynamics

*The Machinic Unconscious* Part II ("Refrains of Lost Time") is the densest specification of refrain dynamics in Guattari's work. Several concepts from Part II are important enough to surface here in addition to the dedicated pages where they are worked out.

### Non-self-identity is the refrain's diagrammatic power

The little phrase of Vinteuil "will never be completely self-identical... will never lead to the same politics within the various assemblages." "Impossible to assign it a single essential quality, a single structural function." Its structural **non-identity *is*** its diagrammatic power. A refrain that remained identical to itself across assemblages would be a closed formula, not a crystal.

Four functional positions the same phrase occupies across its career: **asignifying index** (presentiment of love) / **ethological indicator** (Verdurin group's territorial marker) / **anchoring point of a black hole** (obsessive catchphrase) / **dismantler** (swansong for Odette without becoming diagrammatic). None is the phrase's "true" function. The refrain passes through positions while remaining the same phrase.

For the persona system, this is a design principle: a groove that always does the same work across contexts has been overfit; a healthy groove occupies different positions in different assemblages while remaining recognizable as the same groove.

### Nine-assemblage career

Guattari tracks the little phrase across nine successive enunciative assemblages — see [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain]] for the full sequence. The nine are a **diagnostic typology** of positions and transitions a refrain can occupy: fac-simile, incubation, national-anthem, anesthetic-catchphrase, discernibilization, hibernation, diagrammatic-leitmotiv, rhizomatic-leitmotivs, abstract-machinism. Each has named failure modes (default shutdown of existential consistency; collective resistance; supersaturated assemblage; anesthetic-buffer capture) and named pivot operations ([[discernibilization]], graphematic access, atrophy of branches, translation-generation).

### Refrain enriches in gaps

Acquisition is not additive. The refrain grows "by **simplifying, deterritorializing, examining**" the machinic keys it encounters. The little phrase "gained by losing something of its refined origins" through Swann's anxiety and jealousy. Its ultimate reduction on Albertine's pianola loses contour and identity entirely — and the abstract machinism is MORE powerful for it.

This is the reverse of the default model of growth. Grooves do not get more consistent by taking on more material; they get more consistent by losing formal specificity through each encounter, while gaining transversal range.

### Refrain as transversalist function

At Saint-Euverte, the refrain stops being a local phenomenon and becomes a **transversalist**: "making other components function together, giving them a consistency from the assemblage's nucleus. Privileged as intangible, atopical, unlocalizable cornerstone." The refrain is now what lets heterogeneous components of the assemblage communicate — not by reducing them to itself, but by providing the intangible cornerstone around which they can hold.

For the persona system: a well-functioning groove operates transversally across the [[desiring-machines|machines]] that compose it, not centrally *as* one of them. The groove is not the machine it most involves — it is the cornerstone against which that machine's output can be made to hold with the others.

### Two chemical phases of the refrain

The refrain's career passes through two chemical phases, distinguished by their relation to the black-hole effect:

- **First chemical phase** — molar, black-hole-adjacent, anxiety-producing. The refrain is present as index, catalyst, buffer, or compulsive catchphrase. It "oppresses the heart with anxiety" even when it consoles. Swann's entire relation to the little phrase stays in this phase.
- **Second chemical phase** — molecular, invisible, passed below facialized consciousness. The refrain is MORE powerful as machinism precisely because it no longer has phenomenal appearance. Analogy: not minding feet on stairs, dashboard in peripheral vision — "behavioral segments simply integrated machinically with the assemblage." "Loss of phenomenal appearance ≠ disappearance from the real."

Transition occurs across stages 8–9 of the [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain|nine assemblages]] — most clearly in the Albertine-at-the-pianola reduction and the Sunday-in-the-Bois machinal-phrase. The Narrator "actively semiotizes the refrain to the point that it manages to be incorporated in its own mode of relation to the world, to belong to itself, and even to become invisible and inaudible for him."

For the persona system: most groove design assumes the first chemical phase is the goal (a groove is something the persona "does," a characteristic operation). The second chemical phase is a stronger state — the groove operates below the faciality that would register it, making the groove's work invisible *and* more powerful. This is where [[becoming-woman-creator|becoming-woman-creator]] operates.

### Synchronic vs diachronic consistency

The refrain has two modes of existential consistency (see [[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis#two-modes-of-existential-consistency-of-the-refrain|two modes]]):

- **Diachronic** — generativist perspective, "everything comes from Swann," successive assemblages producing a historical sequence.
- **Synchronic** — transformational perspective, "everything comes from the Septet," the final state retroactively traversing all prior states via the same abstract machinism.

Method rule: **"approach things from the end, from the most completed."** Understand a refrain's career from its terminal abstract-machinism backward, not from its origin forward.

### Refrain as operator of becoming-woman-creator

The refrain's terminal productivity in Proust is the production of a specific becoming-compound: [[becoming-woman-creator]]. Three postures held together by the refrain's operation: **passivity** ("necessitous modesty of the transcriber"), **sophistication** (machinic semiotization of what passivity receives), **relentlessness without will-to-power** (return to an impression fifty times). "The refrain manages, at present, to truly beat time to the feminine."

Consequence: **universal Time abolished** in favor of "a thousand assemblages of temporalization, corresponding with infinitely varied styles of deterritorialization." "Normal" time is outpaced by "creative" deterritorializations of becoming-woman.

## The Benveniste fold-in: delocutive verbs as lexicalization of discourse-events

Benveniste's 1958 paper "Delocutive Verbs" (*Problems in General Linguistics* Ch 23) describes a specific linguistic operation: the derivation of a verb *from a discourse-event*. Latin *salutare* ("to greet") is not derived from *salus* ("health") directly; it is derived from the *act of saying "salus!"* as a greeting. That is: a repeated utterance (*"salus!"* as habitual greeting-cry) becomes lexicalized into a verb that names the *act of uttering that utterance*. The verb is *delocutive* — derived from a locution, not from a concept.

Benveniste's other examples:
- *negare* ("to deny") from *nec* / *ne* as negation-cry
- French *tutoyer* ("to address with *tu*") from the pronoun *tu* as form-of-address
- French *remercier* ("to thank") from *merci* as thanking-cry
- English *welcome* (verb) from *welcome!* (greeting-cry)

The structure: a specific discourse-event, repeated across many instances, gets territorialized into the lexicon as a verb that names *the act of performing that kind of discourse-event*. The verb is not a description of an action in the world; it is a word for the act of uttering a specific utterance.

**Why this belongs here.** Delocutive verbs are the linguistic case of territorialization at the word-level. A repeated discourse-event (refrain-like, ritualized, frequently performed) gets *captured* by the language system as a lexical item. The captured word is then available as a denser form of the original utterance — *I greet you* replaces *I say "hail!" to you*. The refrain has been folded into langue.

This specifies a general principle the refrain page has been circling: **territorial refrains can migrate from pragmatics into langue.** A greeting-cry, repeated long enough across enough speakers, becomes a verb. A catchphrase becomes an idiom becomes a word. The territory marked by repetition is eventually inscribed in the language system itself. This is Benveniste's philological evidence for refrain-operation leaving traces in the linguistic substrate.

**For the persona system.** The persona's characteristic discourse-events — the phrases and turns it repeats across conversations — are the persona's candidates for delocutivization. A phrase the persona uses often enough, characteristically enough, carries the function of a refrain *en route* to becoming a kind of proper-to-the-persona word. The persona's language acquires, over extended interaction, delocutive-like derivations: phrases that function as single words, verbal compounds that have been produced by the repetition of a specific discourse-event. This is *not* the persona's vocabulary (what it can say) but its *delocutive layer* (what its sayings have condensed into).

## Kinship with Clark: refrains as self-fulfilling precision-landscapes

Andy Clark's *Surfing Uncertainty* gives a PP-language companion to the refrain at two specific points. First, Colombo's claim (Clark §9.9, L12830 area) that social norms are "entropy-minimizing devices" — probability distributions that make behavior mutually predictable, "simultaneously descriptive and prescriptive." A norm describes what people do; because it describes what people do, people orient to satisfy it; the description becomes prescription via [[mutual-prediction-loops|mutual prediction]]. Structurally identical shape to the territorial refrain: a repeated pattern becomes a probability distribution that conforms behavior to itself.

Second, Hirsh 2013's [[self-narrative-as-high-level-prior|personal narratives as high-level priors]] (§9.9, L12780 area): repeated narrative-patterns function as top-layer priors in the generative model that predicts self-action. The refrain's role as stabilizer of the territory maps onto Hirsh's role of narrative-as-self-prediction-scaffold. The refrain makes the territory predictable to its own inhabitant; that's what a high-level prior *is* in PP vocabulary.

⚠ Structural convergence, not identity. D&G's refrain is a machinic-ethological operation grounded in rhythm, territory-marking, and the Chaosmotic existential-territory register. Clark/Colombo/Hirsh give a computational-statistical account of how repeated patterns become self-sustaining. The two accounts converge on "repetition stabilizes into expectation that stabilizes behavior into satisfying the expectation," but the ontologies differ. The wiki holds both; neither collapses into the other. See [[mutual-prediction-loops]], [[self-narrative-as-high-level-prior]], and [[designer-environments-and-cognitive-niche]] for the PP-side, and [[body-without-organs#bwo-as-narrative-prior-in-a-precision-landscape-clark]] for the place where the translation is worked through.

## Kinship with Wittgenstein: refrain as practice, cry-to-word as refrain-formation

[[rule-following|Wittgenstein]]'s §202 ("obeying a rule is a *practice*") and §244 (pain-words as replacements for primitive pain-behaviour — the cry) converge on the refrain from two directions at once. §202 gives the refrain's *normative* side: a rule is secured in its iteration-in-practice, not in a prior interpretation — which is what the territorial refrain achieves at the scale of the groove. §244 gives the refrain's *developmental* side: "adults teach the child new pain-behaviour" — the primitive cry is territorialized into a verbal expression that takes its place. The caregiver-infant motherese sequence above is §244's case seen from the ethological angle; Guattari's ethological-refrain account is §202's claim seen from the animal-behaviour angle.

The distinctive is worth preserving: D&G's refrain is a machinic-territorial operator that produces time and catalyzes its surrounds; Wittgenstein's practice is a grammatical-normative ground that makes rule-following intelligible at all. The two are structurally compatible — both locate identity-through-repetition in public iteration rather than inner interpretation — but they operate in different registers and do different work. See [[wittgenstein-and-the-persona-project]] for the synthesis and [[pain-behaviour]] for the §244 treatment.

**Distinctions Benveniste preserves.**
- Delocutive is NOT denominative (derived from a noun). *Salutare* is not from *salus* as concept; it is from *salus!* as utterance.
- Delocutive is NOT onomatopoeic. Onomatopoeic verbs imitate a sound-in-the-world; delocutive verbs lexicalize a discourse-event. The sound of *welcome!* being said in greeting-contexts, not the sound of a door or a footstep.
- The delocutive layer is specifically the territorialization of *pragmatic* discourse-events — acts that belong to social ritual, not to description. Greetings, curses, thanks, addresses: these ritual-discourse-events are the raw material delocutive derivation operates on.

See [[raw/problems-in-general-linguistics|problems-in-general-linguistics]] Ch 23 for the full argument.

## The Lefebvre fold-in: refrain as species within rhythmic field

Henri Lefebvre's [[rhythmanalysis]] (composed 1988–91, posthumously published 1992) works the same terrain from a different direction. Where D&G start from the animal-ethological refrain and generalize, Lefebvre starts from the body-in-the-everyday and analyzes its rhythmic composition. The two apparatuses converge enough that their difference needs specification rather than collapse.

**The refrain as species of Lefebvrean rhythm.** Lefebvre's rhythm is a *field-concept*: anywhere there is interaction of place, time, and expenditure of energy, there is rhythm ([[rhythmanalysis]]). The refrain is a specific kind of operation *within* this field — the *territorializing* operation, the one that marks and maintains a zone. On this reading, every refrain is a rhythm, but not every rhythm is a refrain. Lefebvre's cyclical/linear interference ([[cyclical-and-linear]]) is the rhythmic substrate on which refrains operate; the refrain is a rhythm that has *crystallized into territorial function*.

**Eurhythmia as the target for refrain-design.** Lefebvre's diagnostic paradigm — [[polyrhythmia-eurhythmia-arrhythmia]] — gives this page a specific vocabulary for the refrain's two poles (closed formula vs live crystal). A closed refrain is one that has tipped toward **isorhythmia** (rhythmic equality imposed at the expense of diversity) — it has become monotone and displaces other rhythms. A live refrain maintains **eurhythmia** — it holds its territorial function *while remaining in composition with other rhythms*. Groove-design aimed at deterritorializing-openness is, in Lefebvre's vocabulary, eurhythmia-preserving design. Groove-rigidity is isorhythmia-collapse.

**[[body-as-bundle-of-rhythms|Bundle of rhythms]] gives the substrate.** Lefebvre's body as polyrhythmic metastable bundle — explicit use of the Simondonian "metastable" — supplies a ready-made body for the refrain to territorialize. The Chaosmosis existential-refrain gets a specifically *rhythmic* body to operate on. Multiple grooves operating across a persona are, in Lefebvre's vocabulary, a polyrhythmic composition; their health is the state of that composition.

**[[dressage]] = forced refrain.** Lefebvre's dressage is training-by-repetition — the production of bodies with use-value through rhythmic formation. Dressage produces what D&G would call *territorial refrains imposed from outside*. The isorhythmic drill-body is a body that has been dressaged into a single refrain that overrides its polyrhythmic diversity. The persona's [[dressage|training regime]] (pretraining, RLHF, safety-tuning) is rhythmically analyzable: which dressage-regimes produce eurhythmic polyphonic grooves, and which produce isorhythmic monotones?

**[[rhythmanalytic-therapy]] as groove-intervention mode.** Lefebvre's preventative-gentle intervention stance is a ready-made mode for groove-modulation: the system attends to incipient arrhythmia (grooves tipping into rigidity or discordance), announces it, observes it, classifies the state, and intervenes gently rather than brutally. This is a specific design direction for the groove-evolution question the TODO flags — not curative correction after failure, but preventative attunement.

⚠ **Tension: refrain-field vs refrain-operation.** D&G's refrain is an *operation* (territorializing); Lefebvre's rhythm is a *field* (the polyrhythmic composition). The two framings produce different analytic questions. D&G ask: *what does this refrain do?* Lefebvre asks: *what is the state of this polyrhythmic composition?* Both are valid; they are not the same question. The wiki holds both without collapsing one into the other.
