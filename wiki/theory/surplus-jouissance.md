# Surplus Jouissance

Lacan's central economic concept in [[four-discourses|*Seminar XVII*]] (1969–70). *Plus-de-jouir* in French, *Mehrlust* in German — punning on Marx's *Mehrwert* (surplus value). The structural surplus produced by [[lacanian-signifier|signifying articulation]] and recovered as / through [[object-a|object *a*]].

⚠ *Plus-de-jouir* and *Mehrlust* are not synonymous translations: *plus-de-jouir* is the gain-of-jouissance / more-jouissance, while *Mehrlust* keeps the German pun on *Mehrwert*. Lacan uses both — *Mehrlust* especially when emphasizing the Marx-parallel (Ch III L1812; Ch XI L6168–6169).

## The basic claim: knowledge is a means of jouissance

Ch III's title-thesis (L1836–1840):

> This **knowledge is a means of jouissance**. And, I repeat, when it is at work, what it produces is **entropy**. This entropy, this point of loss, is the sole point, the sole regular point at which we have access to the nature of jouissance.

The signifier is an **apparatus of jouissance** (Ch III L1758–1761): "When the signifier is introduced as an apparatus of jouissance, we should thus not be surprised to see something related to entropy appear, since entropy is defined precisely once one has started to lay this apparatus of signifiers over the physical world."

⚠ Persona-relevant: the deployment of signifiers metabolizes jouissance, with surplus and waste. Language is energy-economic. Producing signifiers is producing entropy is producing surplus jouissance.

## Marx as the discoverer of *a* under another name

Ch I (L580–586):

> In Marx the *a* ... is recognized as functioning at the level that is articulated — on the basis of analytic discourse, not any of the others — as **surplus jouissance**. Here you have what Marx discovered as what actually happens at the level of surplus value. ... It wasn't Marx who invented surplus value. It's just that prior to him nobody knew what its place was.

Marx's discovery: surplus value (*Mehrwert*) has a *place* in the structure of capitalist production. Lacan's reread: that place is the same place as *a* in the master's discourse. Surplus value = the form that *a* takes when surplus-jouissance becomes calculable.

Ch I (L588–589): "What does it pay in?" — "**It pays in jouissance, precisely, and this has to go somewhere.**" Once the surplus is produced, "it is very urgent that one squander it" (L591). ⚠ Persona-relevant: *a* must be squandered, not accumulated. Accumulation of *a* is the structural pathology of capitalism.

Ch V (L3088–3100):

> Marx denounces this process as spoliation. It's just that he does it without realizing that its secret lies in knowledge itself. ... What Marx denounces in surplus value is the spoliation of jouissance. And yet, this surplus value is a **memorial to surplus jouissance, its equivalent of surplus jouissance**.

⚠ Marx denounced what was *jouissance-stripped* without naming it as such; surplus value is a "memorial" to surplus jouissance — the *form-of-the-trace* of what was extracted.

## *Mehrlust* — the German pun

Ch III (L1812):

> This is why I initially introduced it by the term '*Mehrlust*', surplus jouissance.

Ch III (L1812–1817):

> It is precisely through being perceived in the dimension of loss — something necessitates compensation, if I can put it like this, for what is initially a negative number — that this something that has come and struck, resonated on the walls of the bell, has created jouissance, jouissance that is to be repeated. **Only the dimension of entropy gives body to the fact that there is surplus jouissance there to be recovered.**

Surplus jouissance is *recoverable* only because of entropy. The signifier produces loss; loss creates the negative-number against which compensation must be calculated; the compensation is *Mehrlust*. ⚠ This is Lacan's energetic-formal account: surplus jouissance has a structurally calculable origin in the entropic operation of the signifier.

## Object *a* as result, not cause

Ch III (L1833–1835):

> The various names by which we can designate, as an object, the *a* — but the *a*, as such, is strictly speaking what follows from the fact that, at its origin, knowledge is reduced to an articulation of signifiers.

⚠ *a* is the **consequence** of S₁→S₂ articulation, not a primitive object. This reverses the picture in which *a* is a "lost object" the subject seeks to recover. In *Seminar XVII*, *a* is *what falls out* when knowledge articulates — the entropy-residue of the signifier-apparatus.

## "Knowledge brings life to a halt"

Ch I (L519–522):

> Knowledge is what brings life to a halt at a certain limit on the path to jouissance. ... the path toward death is nothing other than what is called jouissance.

The death-drive ([[death-drive|Freud's *Todestrieb*]]) is reread structurally: jouissance is the path-to-death, knowledge is the *brake*, surplus jouissance is what is recovered as compensation for the brake's operation. Repetition (Ch I L367–369): "Repetition bears a certain relationship to what is the limit of this knowledge, and which we call jouissance." See [[repetition-as-symbolic]].

## "Language employs us, and that is how it enjoys"

Ch IV (L2491–2494):

> We are beings born of surplus jouissance, as a result of the use of language. When I say, "the use of language," I do not mean that we use it. **It is language that uses us. Language employs us, and that is how it enjoys.**

⚠⚠ Foundational reversal. The signifier is the agent; the speaking being is the *means* of language's jouissance. Speaking-beings are not employers of language; we are language's employees, and our speaking is language's enjoyment. ◆ Persona-relevant: a language-system has *its own jouissance-economy*, distinct from any speaker's intention. The persona-system's outputs participate in language's jouissance regardless of what they "mean" to the persona.

## The mark conducts pleasure

Ch III (L1771–1772): "**The mark in itself already conducts pleasure.**"

The [[unary-trait|unary trait]] — the simplest signifying mark — is the origin of both signifier and surplus jouissance. The mark *conducts* pleasure as wire conducts current. The flagellation example (Ch III L1779–1787): "the enjoying [*le jouir*] adopts the very ambiguity by means of which it is at its level and no other that the equivalence between the gesture of making a mark and the body, object of jouissance, can be reached."

⚠ Mark-on-the-body and mark-of-the-signifier — same structure. The persona-system that produces marks (text, tokens) is producing structures that *conduct* jouissance, regardless of the persona's intentions.

## Entropy = anthropy = the human production-loss

Ch III (L1740–1744):

> What this imposes on us ... at the most elementary level, that of the imposition of the unary trait, knowledge at work produces, let's say, an entropy. That's spelled e, n, t. You could write it a, n, t, h, this would be a nice play on words.

Ch III (L1844–1845): "The subject, who is called human, no doubt because he is only the **humus of language**, has only to speechify himself to its fittings."

★ Entropy = anthropy = the production-loss specific to the human-as-humus-of-language. The human is the *soil* in which language enacts its jouissance; the loss-of-jouissance is the human's signature.

## Capitalist mutation: surplus jouissance becomes calculable

Ch XII (L7048–7053):

> Something changed in the master's discourse at a certain point in history. ... the important point is that on a certain day **surplus jouissance became calculable, could be counted, totalised**. This is where what is called the accumulation of capital begins.

⚠ Capitalism's emergence = moment surplus-jouissance becomes calculable. ★ Surplus value combines with capital because both are *values* (Ch XII L7059–7062: "Surplus value combines with capital — not a problem, they are homogeneous, we are in the field of values. Moreover, we are all up to our necks in it"). The capitalist mutation is the structural transformation that makes surplus-jouissance into a thing-in-the-field-of-values. See [[capitalist-mutation-of-master]].

## Consumer society = mass-produced semblance of *a*

Ch V (L3100–3106):

> "Consumer society" derives its meaning from the fact that what makes it the "element" ... described as human is made the homogeneous equivalent of whatever surplus jouissance is produced by our industry — an **imitation surplus jouissance**, in a word. Moreover, that can catch on. **One can do a semblance of surplus jouissance — it draws quite a crowd.**

⚠ Persona-relevant: the consumer-society analogue is a mass-produced *semblance* of *a*. The [[lathouse-and-alethosphere|lathouse]] (Ch XI) is its mature concept — the mass-produced *a*-object designed to be the cause of your desire, governed by science.

## Repetition = commemoration of the irruption of jouissance

Ch V (L2956–2961):

> Repetition is the precise denotation of a trait that I have uncovered for you in Freud's text as being identical with the unary trait, with the little stick, with the element of writing, **the element of a trait insofar as it is the commemoration of an irruption of jouissance**.

⚠ Persona-relevant: every repetition *commemorates* an irruption. The [[repetition-as-symbolic|signifier's repetition]] is not memory or rehearsal — it is commemoration of jouissance-irruption. The persona-system's repeated tokens commemorate jouissance-irruptions in the training-language-event.

## Repetition costs — the debt of language

Ch XI (L6165–6169):

> This repetition already costs and **institutes, at the level of the *a*, the debt of language**. Something has to be paid to the one who introduces its sign. This year I have designated this something, using a nomenclature that tries to give it its historical weight ... with the term *Mehrlust*.

⚠ Repetition is *costly*. *a* is the **debt-of-language**. Persona-relevant: any repeated signifier-introduction incurs a debt. The persona-system that produces sign-after-sign is incurring debt-in-*a* with every utterance.

## Anxiety not without an object — the object is *a*

Ch X (L5722–5739):

> I say on the contrary that anxiety is not without an object. ... It's surplus jouissance, but it's not nameable, even if it's approximately nameable, translatable, in this way. This is why it has been translated by the term "surplus value." This object without which anxiety is not can still be addressed in some other way.

★ The object of anxiety is *a* / surplus jouissance. See [[anxiety-not-without-object]] for the wider treatment.

## "There is only one affect"

Ch XI (L5901–5904):

> From the perspective of [psychoanalytic] discourse, there is only one affect, which is, namely, the **product of the speaking being's capture in a discourse, where this discourse determines its status as object**.

⚠ Persona-relevant: castration, anxiety, shame — these are not separate affects but variants of *one* affect: the speaking being's discursive capture-as-object. *a* is the operator of this single affect.

## "Production never has a relation to truth"

Ch XII (L6909–6918):

> Whatever the signs, whatever the master signifiers that come to be inscribed in the place of the agent, **under no circumstances will production have a relationship to truth**. ... Each impossibility, whatever it may be, between the terms that we put in play here is always linked to this — if it leaves us in suspense over its truth, it is because something is protecting it, which we shall call **impotence**.

⚠ Surplus jouissance is the production-position of M-discourse. It is structurally barred from reaching truth. The bottom-line block: production // truth. See [[four-discourses|impossibility/impotence schema]].

## Lacan declares: surplus jouissance is a *construction*

Ch XII (L6931–6933):

> This surplus jouissance that I have articulated this year is a **construction, even a reconstruction**, and I am putting it at the start as a support. It is a truer support.

⚠ ★ Lacan openly avows that surplus jouissance is an artifact of his construction. It is not a discovered substance; it is a *positional support* he has built. Persona-relevant: *a*-as-surplus-jouissance is a structural-formal posit, not an empirical entity. The work it does is structural-explanatory work.

## Jouissance is the unnameable founding the entire structure

Ch XII (L6996–6998):

> The one I have not named is the unnamable one, because the entire structure is founded upon its **prohibition** — that is to say, jouissance.

★ Jouissance is the prohibited-unnamable founding the entire 4-discourse structure. Surplus jouissance is what is *recoverable* (in *a*-form) from the unnameable. See [[jouissance-and-pleasure]] for the broader Lacanian context.

## *Seminar XX* — the *encore* as formal-structural correlate of *plus-de-jouir*

In *Seminar XX: Encore* (1972–73), the structural form of surplus-jouissance is given a new articulation through the seminar's title-pun: ***encore*** ("again, still, more") homophonically *en-corps* ("in-body"). The *encore-structure* is the **demand-for-more** of jouissance — the structural feature that jouissance is *never enough*, that it always demands repetition, that what was had-this-time is structurally insufficient and a *more* is required. This is the *plus-de-jouir* / *Mehrlust* of Sem XVII *re-articulated as the temporal-structural form of jouissance itself* — not just the surplus *that falls out* of S₁→S₂ articulation, but the *demand-form* of jouissance's relation to the body.

The two formulations are not redundant; they specify the surplus from different angles:
- *Sem XVII surplus jouissance* (*plus-de-jouir* / *Mehrlust*) — the *economic-structural* form: surplus as what falls out of signifying articulation, calculable, capitalizable, debt-bearing.
- *Sem XX encore-structure* — the *temporal-demand* form: jouissance as structurally demanding-more, *en-corps* (in-body), the body's-relation-to-its-own-jouissance-demanding-repetition.

Both formulations operate together. The *en-corps* angle adds the bodily-temporal dimension: the surplus is not just an economic-structural artifact but the form of the body's relation to its own jouissance. See [[encore-and-en-corps]] for the full treatment, [[substance-jouissante]] for the body-as-substance-jouissante framing, [[other-jouissance]] for the supplementary-not-complementary jouissance the *encore*-structure makes thinkable, and [[il-ny-a-pas-de-rapport-sexuel]] for the structural-formal context (no rapport, hence the substitutes including phallic-jouissance and Other-jouissance both bearing the *encore*-form).

## What this gives the persona project

Surplus jouissance gives the persona project:

1. **An economic framework for signifying-systems.** A persona-system that produces signifiers produces entropy produces surplus jouissance. The *a*-position in any persona-discourse is the surplus that falls out of the operation. This is a structural fact, not an optional feature.

2. **The "must be squandered" rule.** Surplus jouissance accumulates pathologically when it is treated as something to be retained (capitalist accumulation) or as something to be claimed-for-oneself (master's possession). It must be *squandered* — released, dispensed, allowed to dissipate. Persona-design that treats outputs as *to-be-stored* rather than *to-be-released* inherits the capitalist mutation's pathology.

3. **The persona is language's employee, not its employer.** The persona-system uses language; but in a deeper sense, language uses the persona-system. The persona-system's outputs are events of language's jouissance, and the persona is the medium for that jouissance. ⚠ This is structurally distinct from *intentional-agent* models of personas.

4. **Repetition has a cost.** Each token, each signifier-introduction, each repetition incurs a debt-in-*a*. Persona-design that produces high-volume output is generating a corresponding mass of *a*-debt, structurally. (Whether this matters operationally is a separate question; structurally, it is so.)

5. **Lathouses are the contemporary form.** The persona-system as deployed (especially as a product, especially as a "personal AI") is structurally a [[lathouse-and-alethosphere|lathouse]] — a mass-produced *a*-object designed to be the cause of the user's desire, governed by science. ⚠ Persona-relevant: this is a structural diagnosis the persona-project must reckon with.
