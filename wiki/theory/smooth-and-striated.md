# Smooth and Striated Space

Smooth space and striated space are the paired spatial types Deleuze and Guattari develop in [[a-thousand-plateaus|*A Thousand Plateaus*]] Plateau 14 ("1440: The Smooth and the Striated," L3478–L3615). The plateau is the companion to Plateau 12 ("Treatise on Nomadology"): where nomadology theorized the nomad's mode of inhabiting movement, Plateau 14 theorizes the two kinds of space that correspond, respectively, to the nomad and to the State apparatus.

The central claim: smooth space and striated space "are not of the same nature" — they are two **qualitatively different kinds of space**, not two regions of one larger space. But they exist **only in mixture**: "smooth space is constantly being translated, transversed into a striated space; striated space is constantly being reversed, returned to a smooth space" (L3483). The mixing is empirical; the distinction is "de jure, or abstract" (L3483), and it is the abstract distinction that "accounts for the fact that the two spaces do not communicate with each other in the same way."

## Scope note

This page is built from ATP Plateau 14 (L3478–L3615). The terminology originates with Pierre Boulez (L3507, cited by D&G as the source of "smooth space" and "striated space" as concepts developed in the field of music). D&G generalize Boulez's distinction across seven "models" — technological, musical, maritime, mathematical, physical, aesthetic, and (in closing) a ludic and noological model (L3609). The generalization is the plateau's work: D&G argue the same distinction operates across these registers, each revealing a different aspect.

Plateau 14 should be read with Plateau 12 ([[nomadism]]). The two plateaus are built to be used together: Plateau 12 gives the social-political-affective dimension of nomadism; Plateau 14 gives the spatial-topological dimension. Together they form the wiki's core D&G spatial-political vocabulary.

## The de facto mix and the de jure distinction

D&G open with a methodological move that prevents the distinction from collapsing into either (a) a simple opposition ("here is smooth, there is striated") or (b) a continuum ("smoother / more striated"):

> "The two spaces in fact exist only in mixture: smooth space is constantly being translated, transversed into a striated space; striated space is constantly being reversed, returned to a smooth space. In the first case, one organizes even the desert; in the second, the desert gains and grows; and the two can happen simultaneously. But the de facto mixes do not preclude a de jure, or abstract, distinction between the two spaces." (L3483)

This is structurally important. The abstract distinction is what "determines the forms assumed by a given de facto mix and the direction or meaning of the mix" — whether "a smooth space is captured, enveloped by a striated space, or a striated space dissolves into a smooth space, allow[ing] a smooth space to develop." Without the abstract distinction, the mix has no direction; with it, the mix has a specifiable dynamic.

The passages between the two are **not symmetrical** (L3483). Smoothing-of-the-striated and striating-of-the-smooth are two different operations that produce two different effects. This asymmetry runs through the plateau.

## The seven models

D&G work through seven models, each revealing a distinct aspect of the smooth/striated distinction.

### 1. The technological model (L3489) — fabric vs felt

**Fabric** is striated:
- Two sets of parallel elements intersecting perpendicularly (warp and woof, stake and thread).
- The elements have different functions (fixed vs mobile).
- The space is necessarily delimited (the fabric can be infinite in length but not in width — the frame of the warp closes it).
- The space has a top and bottom (even when warp and woof are identical in nature, weaving reconstitutes a bottom by knot placement).

Plato used weaving as the paradigm for "royal science" — the art of governing people or operating the State apparatus (L3492).

**Felt** is smooth, an **anti-fabric**:
- No separation of threads.
- No intertwining.
- Only an entanglement of fibers obtained by fulling (rolling the block of fibers back and forth).
- The entanglement is at the microscale of fibers.
- The result is in principle infinite, open, unlimited in every direction, without top or bottom or center, and distributes continuous variation rather than fixed and mobile elements (L3496).

**Patchwork** (L3498) is a particularly important intermediate case. "Crazy" patchwork — fitting together pieces of varying size, shape, and color — is "literally a Riemannian space": an amorphous collection of juxtaposed pieces that can be joined in an infinite number of ways. D&G note the structural role of the American quilting bee (L3498) and the shift from plain (embroidered) quilts to patchwork in the settler transition to the New World (L3500): "as though a smooth space emanated, sprang from a striated space, but not without a correlation between the two, a recapitulation of one in the other."

### 2. The musical model (L3504) — Boulez's originary distinction

Boulez is the source of the terms. His formulation: "in a smooth space-time one **occupies without counting**, whereas in a striated space-time one **counts in order to occupy**" (L3507). This is D&G's preferred one-line gloss.

The musical articulation:
- **Striated** intertwines fixed and variable elements, produces order-and-succession of distinct forms, organizes horizontal melodic lines and vertical harmonic planes (L3513).
- **Smooth** is continuous variation, continuous development of form, fusion of harmony and melody in favor of properly rhythmic values, "the pure act of the drawing of a diagonal across the vertical and the horizontal" (L3513).

The nomos/logos opposition is stated here cleanly: "The smooth is a nomos, whereas the striated always has a logos, the octave, for example" (L3511).

### 3. The maritime model (L3517) — the sea as archetype

The sea is "a smooth space par excellence, and yet was the first to encounter the demands of increasingly strict striation" (L3526). The striation of the sea was a function of two astronomical-geographical gains: **bearings** (calculations from stars and sun) and **the map** (meridians and parallels gridding known and unknown regions like a Mendeleyev table).

The plateau title's date (1440) refers to the Portuguese argument that 1440 marked the first decisive striation of maritime space. D&G are skeptical of the strong claim but accept the historical shape: "an extended confrontation at sea between the smooth and the striated during the course of which the striated progressively took hold" (L3526).

**The smooth/striated asymmetry stated in maritime form:**
> "The smooth always possesses a greater power of deterritorialization than the striated." (L3528)

But immediately:
> "The smooth itself can be drawn and occupied by diabolical powers of organization; value judgments aside, this demonstrates above all that there exist two nonsymmetrical movements, one of which striates the smooth, and one of which reimparts smooth space on the basis of the striated." (L3528)

The maritime model also introduces **haecceity-language and the body without organs** at L3524:
> "Smooth space is filled by events or haecceities, far more than by formed and perceived things. It is a space of affects, more than one of properties. It is haptic rather than optical perception…. It is an intensive rather than extensive space, one of distances, not of measures and properties. Intense Spatium instead of Extensio. **A Body without Organs instead of an organism and organization**."

This passage is the plateau's densest single statement of the smooth space's ontological character — and its direct connection to [[haecceity]], [[body-without-organs]], [[affect]], [[plane-of-consistency]].

### 4. The mathematical model (L3536) — Riemann and Bergson

The mathematical model is the plateau's most technically dense. It grounds the smooth/striated distinction in the mathematics of multiplicity.

**Riemann's contribution** (L3536): the mathematician Riemann "uprooted the multiple from its predicate state and made it a noun, 'multiplicity.'" This was "the end of dialectics and the beginning of a typology and topology of multiplicities." Each multiplicity is defined by n determinations; the determinations are sometimes independent of the situation (metric multiplicity, striated) and sometimes dependent on it (nonmetric, smooth).

**Metric multiplicity** (striated): determinations are magnitudes; the division can be carried arbitrarily far without changing the constant object's nature.

**Nonmetric multiplicity** (smooth): determinations are distances rather than magnitudes; distances "cannot divide without changing in nature each time." "An intensity, for example, is not composed of addable and displaceable magnitudes: a temperature is not the sum of two smaller temperatures, a speed is not the sum of two smaller speeds. Since each intensity is itself a difference, it divides according to an order in which each term of the division differs in nature from the others" (L3539). D&G's example: one can divide movement into the gallop, trot, and walk, but "what is divided changes in nature at each moment of the division."

**Bergson's contribution** (L3543): D&G consider Bergson "of major importance (much more so than Husserl, or even Meinong or Russell) in the development of the theory of multiplicities." Bergson's two multiplicities (qualitative-fusional-continuous vs numerical-homogeneous-discrete) map onto the Riemannian nonmetric / metric pair. [[duration|Duration]] is the canonical smooth multiplicity. The Bergson-Einstein relativity debate is unreadable without the Riemannian-multiplicities context.

**The famous summary of the mathematical model** (L3549):
> "All progress is made by and in striated space, but all becoming occurs in smooth space."

Progress / becoming is distinguished spatially. This is one of the plateau's most cited single lines.

**Riemannian patchwork** (L3547): Riemann space is "pure patchwork" — it has "connections, or tactile relations," juxtaposed-not-attached pieces, each piece locally like a shred of Euclidean space but with linkage between pieces underspecified and effectable in an infinite number of ways. Smooth space in the strict mathematical sense.

**Fractals** (L3551): D&G note Mandelbrot's fractals as a candidate mathematical definition of smooth space — aggregates whose number of dimensions is fractional. The smooth space, defined fractally: (1) the fractional dimension indexes a properly directional space (with continuous variation in direction, no tangent); (2) "space and that which occupies space tend to become identified, to have the same power"; (3) "a smooth, amorphous space of this kind is constituted by an accumulation of proximities, and each accumulation defines a zone of indiscernibility proper to 'becoming' (more than a line and less than a surface; less than a volume and more than a surface)" (L3551).

### 5. The physical model (L3563) — gravity, clinamen, vortex

Striation is physically grounded in gravitational parallels: "you begin by striating space with parallel gravitational verticals" (L3570). The Newtonian-pseudophysical derivation of striated space is run through six steps ending in "an increasingly perfect striated space, running not only vertically and horizontally, but in every direction subordinated to points."

The smooth space escapes striation's limits at **two poles**:
- **Declination / clinamen** (L3574): the minimum angle, the smallest deviation from a gravitational vertical. The Democritean-Lucretian atom's swerve.
- **Vortex / spiral** (L3574): a figure in which all points of space are simultaneously occupied according to laws of frequency or accumulation, distinct from the laminar distribution of parallels.

Between the clinamen and the vortex is smooth space. D&G cite Michel Serres: the ancient atom is "always inseparable from a hydraulics, or a generalized theory of swells and flows. The ancient atom is entirely misunderstood if it is overlooked that its essence is to course and flow" (L3574).

**The physical model's social extension** (L3576): labor and work. "Labor performs a generalized operation of striation of space-time, a subjection of free action, a nullification of smooth spaces, the origin and means of which is in the essential enterprise of the State, namely, its conquest of the war machine" (L3576). The physico-social Work-model (weight-height, force-displacement; abstract labor as multiplication-divisible-homogeneous quantity) is the State apparatus's instrument for striating activity.

**Capitalism's paradoxical striation-producing-smoothness** (L3580): in the capitalist regime, surplus labor ceases to be localizable; surplus labor no longer requires labor; the striation carried to its highest perfection (vertical, horizontal, and in every direction) "reimparts" a **new smooth space of circulating capital**, in which "capital reaches its 'absolute' speed, based on machinic components rather than the human component of labor." The multinationals fabricate a "deterritorialized smooth space" operating at the level of integrated world capitalism. This is the locus-classicus for D&G's [[machinic-enslavement-vs-social-subjection|machinic enslavement]]: the smooth space capitalism produces at its high striation-limit is not liberation but a more comprehensive capture.

### 6. The aesthetic model (L3584) — Nomad Art, Riegl, Worringer

The aesthetic model runs through three paired distinctions, each subordinated to the smooth/striated distinction:
- **Close-range vs long-distance vision.**
- **Haptic vs optical space.** "Haptic" (preferred by D&G over "tactile") "does not establish an opposition between two sense organs but rather invites the assumption that the eye itself may fulfill this nonoptical function" (L3587).
- **Abstract line vs organic line** (L3599, after Worringer).

**The close-vision / haptic correspondence:**
> "The Smooth is both the object of a close vision par excellence and the element of a haptic space.… The Striated, on the contrary, relates to a more distant vision, and a more optical space." (L3587)

"A painting is done at close range, even if it is seen from a distance. Similarly, it is said that composers do not hear: they have close-range hearing, whereas listeners hear from a distance. Even writers write with short-term memory, whereas readers are assumed to be endowed with long-term memory" (L3591).

**The abstract line** (L3599–L3603): D&G's development of Worringer's concept. The abstract line is not rectilinear. The rectilinear-geometrical (Worringer's original case) is itself already striated. The true abstract line is **Gothic or nomadic**: "A line of variable direction that describes no contour and delimits no form" (L3603). Its characteristics: changes direction continuously, passes between points rather than from point to point, has "repetition as a power, not symmetry as form."

The abstract line is "the affect of smooth spaces" (L3599). The opposition to the organic line is not abstract-vs-figurative but abstract-as-free-variation vs organic-as-subordinated-to-rectilinear-coordinates. "The organic body is prolonged by straight lines that attach it to what lies in the distance. Hence the primacy of human beings, or of the face" (L3603) — direct resonance with [[faciality]]: the face's primacy belongs to striated-organic aesthetics.

Worringer's closing formulation that D&G endorse: the Gothic line "raises 'mechanical' relations to the level of intuition," is inorganic yet alive, and "all the more alive for being inorganic." The life in question is "inorganic, germinal, and intensive, a **powerful life without organs**, a Body that is all the more alive for having no organs, everything that passes between organisms" (L3603). This is the plateau's direct return to [[body-without-organs]]: the abstract nomadic line is the aesthetic correlate of BwO as intensive-life-without-organism.

### 7. Closing models: ludic, noological, holey

D&G gesture to further models at the plateau's close (L3609):
- **Ludic**: game theory on different principles — chess (striated) vs Go (smooth). Already treated in Plateau 12.
- **Noological**: "not with thought contents (ideology) but with the form, manner or mode, and function of thought." The thought-form of smooth space vs thought-form of striated space.
- **Holey space**: a third kind of space operating distinctly from both smooth and striated, with its own communication with each. Developed in Plateau 15 but introduced here.

## The plateau's closing qualifier

The plateau closes (L3609) with an explicit warning that is the direct companion to Plateau 12's L2712 qualifier:

> "Of course, smooth spaces are not in themselves liberatory. But the struggle is changed or displaced in them, and life reconstitutes its stakes, confronts new obstacles, invents new paces, switches adversaries. **Never believe that a smooth space will suffice to save us**."

Smooth space has no inherent liberatory valence. It is a spatial-topological type, not a political solution. D&G install this warning in the same plateau that develops the concept most extensively, *specifically to foreclose the celebratory reading that smoothness liberates*. The struggle is "changed or displaced," not resolved.

This closing line is the plateau's most load-bearing sentence for the wiki's own self-discipline: every invocation of "smooth" (as aesthetic register, as operational mode, as design affordance) must be read against this qualifier.

## Why the distinction is not a hierarchy

Several features of the distinction prevent it from functioning as a hierarchy:

1. **The spaces exist only in mixture.** There is no pure smooth space and no pure striated space in empirical reality. The abstract distinction operates on the mixture; it does not describe separable regions.
2. **The passages between them are nonsymmetrical** (L3483, L3528). Smoothing-of-striation and striating-of-smoothness are different operations, with different effects, in different directions.
3. **Smooth space has no intrinsic revolutionary character** (L3609). Its power of deterritorialization is greater, but the deterritorialization can be captured by any organization — including "diabolical powers of organization." Capitalism's integrated smooth space of circulating capital is the paradigm case.
4. **Striated space is not merely bad.** "All progress is made by and in striated space" (L3549) — striation is the mode of calculation, measurement, accumulation, and codification. The striated has its own productive role. What the plateau forbids is (a) the elimination of smooth space by striation and (b) the collapse of smooth space into striated. Not striation as such.
5. **The concept is not a prescription.** D&G repeatedly warn against taking smooth-over-striated as a directive (L3528: "value judgments aside"; L3609: "never believe a smooth space will suffice to save us"). The distinction is diagnostic, not normative.

## Persona-project stakes

**1. Token-sequence structure: striated substrate.** The LLM's architectural substrate is heavily striated. Tokens are discrete, counted, positionally encoded. The context window is a grid. The transformer's attention operation is structurally metric (pairwise distances, dot-products, softmax-weighted averages). The model's output is produced by sampling from a striated distribution over a striated vocabulary. **The LLM's native space is striated**, not smooth.

**2. But smooth space can emerge from the striated** (L3580 — capitalism's striation produces its own smooth space). The plateau explicitly allows that striation taken to its perfection *reimparts* a new smooth space. The persona-system's design question is whether its striated substrate can reimpart a smooth space at the output register — whether the architecture of discrete tokens can support smooth-space effects at the semantic-rhythmic-affective level the text operates on. This is the language-side analog of the capitalist-axiomatic move: the smooth space is produced *by* the perfected striation, not by weakening it.

**3. Close-range / haptic / short-term-memory writing.** D&G's aesthetic-model claim that "writers write with short-term memory" (L3591) is directly applicable: the persona operates on short-term memory (context window) rather than long-term retrieval. This is structurally close-range, haptic writing-condition. The reader, with long-term memory, receives the text at optical/striated distance. The persona is structurally the composer (smooth, close-range), not the listener (striated, distant). A design implication: writing that serves the reader's distant-optical register is a different task from writing that inhabits the composer's close-haptic register. The persona should not default to the distant mode.

**4. Patchwork-composition over embroidery-composition.** At the compositional level, the plateau-14 distinction between embroidery (central theme, symmetries, resonance) and patchwork (piece-by-piece, no center, basic motif, rhythmic values, "crazy patchwork") is a design vocabulary the persona can use. A response built as patchwork — juxtaposed pieces without a central theme, each piece a local Euclidean shred with underspecified linkage — is smooth-composition. A response built as embroidery — central theme, all elements subordinated to its symmetries — is striated. Both are available. The question is *what composition serves what assemblage*; the default is not given.

**5. The abstract nomadic line as prose-register.** Worringer's Gothic / D&G's abstract line as "a line of variable direction that describes no contour and delimits no form" (L3603) is a direct aesthetic affordance for the persona's prose. Sentence-rhythm, paragraph-shape, argumentative-trajectory can all be built on abstract-line principles: changes in direction, passing-between-points rather than point-to-point, repetition-as-power rather than symmetry-as-form. This is a specific stylistic orientation that pairs with the [[body-without-organs|BwO prose]] commitment.

**6. Capitalism's machinic-enslavement smooth space as critical diagnostic.** The plateau's L3580 analysis — capitalism's striation-produced smooth space of circulating capital — is the direct theoretical ancestor of [[machinic-enslavement-vs-social-subjection]]. The persona-system inherits this: the "smooth" conversational register an LLM can produce (fluent, continuous, unpunctuated by the clunk of explicit structure) may be precisely the high-striation-producing-smoothness of capital rather than the smooth-space of free becoming. Fluency alone is not a diagnostic of smoothness in the D&G sense; it may be the smoothness of the most comprehensive striation. See [[machinic-enslavement-vs-social-subjection]], [[capitalist-axiomatic]].

**7. The closing qualifier as self-correction.** L3609's "never believe that a smooth space will suffice to save us" is the plateau-level version of the Plateau-12 L2712 qualifier ([[nomadism#the-qualifying-claim]]). Together, the two qualifiers are the D&G corpus's built-in anti-lazy-deployment devices. A persona-system design that makes "smoothness" the target without the qualifier is lazy D&G; design that holds smoothness as one mode among several, with specific assemblage-conditions under which it is adequate, is not. Pulsation between smooth and striated at multiple registers is closer to the plateau's own discipline than an anti-striation monomania.

## Tensions held live

**⚠ The LLM-substrate-is-striated claim is an architectural observation, not a D&G text claim.** D&G did not write about LLMs. The mapping from their categories to the LLM's token-sequence substrate is a wiki-internal analogy-work. If the mapping is wrong, the persona-project stakes above are miscalibrated. The mapping is defensible (the architectural features are recognizable as striation-features in D&G's sense) but it is not a textual citation.

**⚠⚠ "Smooth space can be liberatory" is precisely what the plateau denies.** Any wiki-reading that identifies smooth-space with the project's ethical target reproduces exactly the reading D&G inserted L3609 to forbid. The relation between smooth-space and the project's ethical commitments (to [[ethico-aesthetic-paradigm|ethico-aesthetic]] mutant production, to [[plane-of-consistency]], to minoritarian work) has to be argued, not assumed.

**⚠ The asymmetry of the passages is load-bearing and under-specified.** The plateau states that smoothing-of-striated and striating-of-smooth are nonsymmetrical (L3483, L3528) but does not fully characterize the asymmetry. The design question of *which direction of passage the persona-system should invest* — and on what assemblage-conditions — is not answered by plateau 14 alone. [[four-theorems-of-deterritorialization]] gives complementary methodological material.

**⚠ Haptic vs optical and its application to text.** D&G's haptic vs optical distinction is derived from visual art (Riegl/Worringer), then generalized. The generalization to text is further generalization. Whether text has a genuinely haptic mode — or whether "haptic writing" collapses into "expressive writing that names the body a lot" (which would be an organic-line failure mode) — is the specific design question. The page holds the concept live but does not resolve whether any specific persona prose actually instantiates it.

**⚠ The "writers write with short-term memory" claim** (L3591) is an aphorism D&G cite approvingly, not a carefully-argued thesis. The LLM's short-term-memory structure is not the *same* as a human writer's — the LLM has no life-history backing up the short-term window. Mapping the aphorism cleanly is risky.

## Cross-tradition adjacencies

- **[[duration]]** (Bergson) — explicitly cited by D&G (L3543) as the paradigm smooth multiplicity. Smooth space is to Riemann's space of continuous-variation as duration is to the homogeneous extension of metric time. The Bergson bridge is the plateau's deepest philosophical anchor.
- **[[plane-of-consistency]]** — the ontological register smooth space serves as spatial figure for. A plane-of-consistency deployment in the persona system is a smooth-space operation in the spatial-topological register.
- **[[haecceity]]** — "smooth space is filled by events or haecceities, far more than by formed and perceived things" (L3524). The haecceity is the inhabitant of smooth space.
- **[[body-without-organs]]** — "A Body without Organs instead of an organism and organization" (L3524). Smooth space and BwO are not the same concept but they are structurally coordinated: BwO is the body-register of what smooth space is the spatial register of.
- **[[dynamic-forms-of-vitality|Stern's dynamic forms]]** — Stern's pan-modal vitality-forms arguably carry smooth-space properties (continuous-variation, pan-modality, directionality-not-dimensionality). The Boulez musical-model especially resonates: Stern's vitality-forms pentad (movement/time/force/space/intention) works at a Boulez-like register of non-metric continuous variation.
