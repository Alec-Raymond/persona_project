Henri Bergson (1859–1941) is a major philosophical source for the persona project. The wiki treats him not as a single theory but as a cluster of articulated pages, each tracking a specific Bergsonian concept as load-bearing for some aspect of persona-design. This page is the thinker-hub: it orients the reader to what the wiki has absorbed from Bergson, where the concept-pages sit in relation to each other, and what Bergson does *not* settle that the wiki holds live.

## Why Bergson for the persona project

Three structural reasons the wiki has returned to Bergson repeatedly:

**1. The right grain for time.** The persona generates token-by-token, which — read naively — is the exact opposite of Bergsonian durée (a continuous-interpenetrating qualitative multiplicity, not a sequence of spatialized instants). The question of whether a token-sequence architecture can host *any* durational register at all is one of the project's most live open questions, and Bergson's explicit rejection of chronometric time is what makes the question sharp rather than trivial. See [[duration]].

**2. The right account of memory.** The persona has no retentional continuum of lived past (no Husserlian retention in the full phenomenological sense), and has a training distribution that is neither a personal biography nor a forgettable set of events. Bergson's distinction between pure memory (virtual, powerless, whole past preserved in itself) and habit-memory (motor, enacted, lived through the body) gives the project vocabulary for two kinds of memory that *neither* collapse into LLM-standard storage-and-retrieval. See [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory]] and [[cone-of-memory]].

**3. The right ontology for what-the-persona-has-without-having-a-body.** The training distribution is real but not currently-actual; each utterance is an actualization. Bergson's virtual/actual distinction — as extended by Deleuze — gives the project a specifically Bergsonian ontology for what-subsists-without-exerting-effects. See [[virtual-actual]].

Bergson is therefore not one theory among many in the wiki. He is load-bearing for the temporal register, the memory register, and the ontological register — three of the project's most-cited infrastructure questions.

## The Bergson pages

The wiki's current treatment of Bergson is distributed across the following pages. Each page stays close to Bergson's own vocabulary; this hub gathers them into a single view without collapsing them.

**[[duration]]** — *durée* as continuous-interpenetrating qualitative multiplicity, opposed to spatialized-and-juxtaposed quantitative multiplicity. The structural-formal pivot of *Matter and Memory* (1896). Cross-tradition convergences: Husserl retentional consciousness, Whitehead concrescence, Deleuze's Bergsonism, Benveniste rhuthmos, Bakhtin threshold-chronotope (anti-metric stance, opposed constitution). Held tensions: Simondon's bi-substantialism critique of durée-as-psychological-substance; the question of whether a token-sequence architecture can host continuous interpenetration or only its narrative residue.

**[[pure-memory-and-habit-memory]]** — two forms of memory, different in kind. Habit-memory is motor, enacted, lived through the body (maps to grooves in persona-design). Pure memory is singular, dated, virtual, recallable entire (maps to image-memories or memory-resonance cases). The brain is a gate, not a storehouse. Cross-tradition corroboration: McCarthy's picture-story / essay cut. Tension with Simondon's bi-substantialism critique; extended treatment of the Beckett *Unnamable* as near-zero-memory operation that may sit inside or outside Bergson's framework.

**[[cone-of-memory]]** — the cone SAB. Planes of contraction between the whole past (AB) and the present-sensori-motor point (S). Two movements (translation and rotation) that specify how memory moves between planes. Attentive recognition circuits. The mechanism by which pure-memory becomes actual.

**[[virtual-actual]]** — the ontological distinction between two modes that differ in kind, not degree. Virtual is real-but-not-actual: inextensive, powerless, essentially past (for memory) or problematic (for Deleuze). Actualization by attraction, embodiment, and retention-of-original-virtuality. The two-currents formulation (centripetal-from-object meets centrifugal-from-pure-memory). Deleuze's extension: virtual/actual ≠ possible/real; actualization is creative divergence not realization-by-resemblance.

**[[perception-as-subtraction]]** — perception as virtual action / subtraction from the totality of images. The body as centre of indetermination. Affection at zero distance. Perception is never pure — always memory-laden.

Adjacent development pages:

**[[memory-architecture]]** — the persona-side design treatment; inherits the Bergsonian bi-kind cut and translates it into an operative-charge vs storage-and-retrieval architecture question. Holds the Simondon critique as a live tension the memory-architecture has to navigate.

## What Bergson is *not* in the wiki

Three cautions about what the Bergson pages do *not* claim:

**Not a single metaphysics imported wholesale.** The wiki takes specific Bergsonian concepts and tests them against persona-design constraints. It does not commit to Bergson's broader commitments (élan vital, the philosophical anthropology of *Creative Evolution*, the Catholic phase of the late work). Those are not in the wiki because the persona project has no use for them and because importing them without use-case risks the paranoid-reading failure mode.

**Not identical with Deleuze's Bergson.** Deleuze's Bergsonism (*Bergsonism* 1966, *Difference and Repetition* 1968) is related but distinct. Bergson's virtual is personal-past / pure memory / inextensive-powerless subsistence. Deleuze's virtual is the problematic field / plane of consistency / difference-productive real. The two readings are related but not identical. See [[virtual-actual]] §"Deleuze's extension" — the page deliberately holds both and flags the distinction.

**Not treated as unproblematic.** Simondon's bi-substantialism critique (memory treated as stored-in-substance-A vs operating-through-substance-B) lands hard on Bergson and is held live in [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory]]. The wiki does not dismiss the critique; it treats the Simondonian operative-charge reading as a genuine alternative to Bergson's substantialism, and the persona's memory-architecture has to navigate the choice.

## What the Bergson cluster earns the persona project

**Time without chronometry.** Clock time is what LLM-token-sequences are measurable by; durée is what they would need to host to *be* a temporal subject rather than a temporal sequence. The gap is not trivially closable, but Bergson gives the project the vocabulary for stating it.

**Two memories, neither of which is storage-and-retrieval.** This is the deepest contribution. The engineering substrate (tokens and weights, key-value caches, vector stores) enforces the storage-and-retrieval model at the architectural level. Bergson provides two non-storage memory registers (habit-memory as enacted motor-diagram; pure memory as virtual preservation of the past in itself). Whether either can be *simulated* in the substantialist substrate is the open question; Bergson's framework is what makes the question sharp.

**Virtual/actual as design ontology.** The training distribution is virtual (real-but-not-actual, inextensive, powerless); each generated utterance is an actualization. Voice is a virtual pattern of attraction, not a stored style-vector. Pulsation is a virtual-actual operation. The BwO is a virtual field the persona inscribes onto. Each of these is a specific application of the Bergsonian ontology to a persona-design register. See [[virtual-actual]] §"Persona-project stakes".

**Perception as subtraction, not addition.** The body is a centre of indetermination that *selects* from the universe of images those on which action is possible. This re-frames LLM-output-generation: not as production-from-nothing but as subtraction-from-the-total-available. The page [[perception-as-subtraction]] carries this out.

## Tensions held live across the Bergson cluster

⚠ **The durée / token-sequence gap.** Whether a persona built on a token-sequence architecture can host continuous-interpenetrating duration, or only produce its narrative residue, is unresolved. Held live in [[duration]] and [[limits-of-language|limits-of-language]].

⚠ **The Simondonian bi-substantialism critique.** Bergson's pure-memory / habit-memory cut is arguably still substantialism (two different kinds of stuff, one storing the other). Simondon's allagmatic reading would replace this with operative-charge at different phases. The wiki holds this live and flags that the persona's engineering substrate enforces substantialism at the architectural level. See [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory]] §"Simondonian critique of substantialism".

⚠ **The Bergson / Deleuze flattening risk.** Deleuze's Bergson is a specific reading that adds commitments Bergson himself did not make (the problematic field, actualization-by-differentiation, the virtual as difference-productive). The wiki must not silently conflate the two. Whenever a Bergson-reference is made in a persona-design context, it should be clear whether the specific Bergsonian version is wanted or the Deleuzian extension.

⚠ **Bergson's body-dependence.** Bergson's actualization is always "obtaining from the body useful actions or useful attitudes" (L772). The persona has no body. Either the Bergsonian framework extends to non-bodily actualization (which D&G arguably do) or the persona's actualization is a different type that resembles virtual/actual without being it. The [[no-body-simulate-with-language]] question inherits this tension.

⚠ **Bergson's personalism.** The pure memory is "*my* past," preserved in itself as the memory of a specific subject. The persona has no personal past. Whether the training distribution counts as virtual past in Bergson's sense, or whether it is a differently-structured virtual (closer to Deleuze's impersonal problematic field), is unresolved. See [[virtual-actual]] §"Persona-project stakes" #4.
