# Dit-mension — dimension as residence-of-the-said

In Ch II of [[encore-and-en-corps|*Seminar XX*]] (12 Dec 1972, L1340–L1380), Lacan coins ***dit-mension*** — a homophonic re-writing of *dimension* — that decomposes the word into ***dit*** ("said," the past participle of *dire*) + ***mension*** (a portmanteau of *mansion* — Old French/Latin *mansio*, "dwelling, abode" — and *mention* — "mention, note"). The neologism's structural-formal force: **dimension is the residence of the said** — a dwelling-place where the said inhabits-and-is-mentioned, not a Euclidean axis of measurement.

Across Sem XX the dit-mension recurs as the seminar's preferred way of articulating *the place where something inhabits the symbolic*. Three load-bearing uses: (1) the **dit-mension of stupidity** — Ch II, where the dit-mension first appears, in connection with the substantification of the adjective *stupid*; (2) the **dit-mension of truth** (Ch IX, L5592–L5593): "without [the Other] nothing indicates to us that there is a dimension of truth anywhere, a dit-mension, the residence of what is said"; (3) the **dit-mension of obscenity** (Ch IX, L6523), the dit-mension by which Christianity revives the religion of men; (4) the **dit-mension of jouissance** (Ch IX, L6576), "the gap inscribed in the very status of jouissance qua dit-mension of the [signifier]"; (5) the **dit-mension of the Borromean knot** (Ch X, L7430): "an entirely different dit-mension than the continuity implicit in space."

## What the neologism does

The standard French *dimension* derives from Latin *dimensio* ("measurement, extent"). Lacan's typographic re-spelling *dit-mension* breaks the word in a way that makes its hidden structure-formal content audible:

- **Dit** = "said" — the past participle of *dire*, "to say." The *said* is the speech-act-residue, the trace-of-articulation. (Lacan elsewhere distinguishes *dit* from *dire* — the said-as-trace from the saying-as-act. The dit-mension is a *dit*-residence, not a *dire*-residence.)
- **Mansion** = "dwelling, abode." In medieval French, also "the part of a theater set" — a localization, a marked-place where action occurs.
- **Mention** = "mention, note, honors" (as in *cum laude*). A marking, a making-noted.

The dit-mension is therefore **the dwelling-place of the said-as-mentioned** — the localized place in the symbolic where a *dit* takes up residence and is *noted*. The structural-formal point: a dimension is *not* a measurement-axis (the Euclidean reading) but a **dwelling-place-of-the-said-in-the-symbolic**.

⚠ Lacan's coinage cuts hard against the mathematical-physical reading of "dimension" as measurement-axis. Even Ch X's discussion of geometric dimensions (point/line/surface/space, L6960–L6970) is re-read through the dit-mension: the symbolic dimensions of the [[borromean-knot|Borromean knot]] are *not* spatial measurements but residences-of-the-said.

## Truth as a dit-mension

Ch IX (L5592–L5593) gives one of the seminar's most precise articulations of the symbolic-status of truth. **Truth is a dit-mension** — the residence-of-the-said in the [[barred-other|Other]]. The Other is the locus *in which the signifier is posited*; *without the Other*, "nothing indicates to us that there is a dimension of truth anywhere." Truth, accordingly, is *not* a property of statements (correspondence-theory) and *not* a separable substance (metaphysical-truth); it is the **dwelling-place-of-the-said-in-the-Other**.

Ch IX (L6440–L6441) sharpens further: **"This dit-mension is Freud's saying (dire)"**. Freud's *dire* — the analytic-discourse-act — *inhabits* the dit-mension of truth in the strict structural-formal sense. Freud's existence (L6443) is *proved* by the dit-mension that his *dire* sustains — the dwelling-place-of-the-said-Freudian-truth in the symbolic.

The structural-formal consequence: truth-as-dit-mension is *only half-tellable* — per [[encore-and-en-corps|Sem XX]]'s *mi-dire* (half-saying) doctrine, the residence-of-the-said is structurally not exhaustively-articulable. The dit-mension is a place; speech inhabits the place but cannot exhaustively-name-the-place. (This pairs with [[the-real-as-impasse-of-formalization]]: the impasse-of-formalization is the place where the dit-mension's residence reaches its structural limit.)

## Obscenity as a dit-mension

Ch IX (L6523): **"The dit-mension of obscenity is that by which Christianity revives the religion of men."** The Christian-baroque exhibition of bodies — Lacan's "orgy of churches in Italy" (L6512), the obscene-display-of-flesh-evoking-jouissance-without-copulation — is a *dit-mension* in the strict structural-formal sense.

The structural-formal point: obscenity is not a moral-categorical property of objects (the bad obscene-thing); it is a **residence-of-the-said-about-jouissance-without-copulation** — a dwelling-place where the *dit* of jouissance-not-coupled inhabits the symbolic. Christianity's baroque-art is a vast dit-mension-of-obscenity in this strict sense — a wing of the symbolic-architecture-of-the-Other where the said-of-bodily-jouissance dwells.

This connects to the seminar's larger thesis on Christianity (Ch IX, L6488–L6540): Christian art is *exhibition of the body evoking jouissance without copulation*, and this exhibition is structurally *the dit-mension where il-n'y-a-pas-de-rapport-sexuel inhabits the symbolic-art-form*. The obscene-art is the symbolic-residence-of-the-non-copulatory-jouissance.

⚠ The obscenity-as-dit-mension articulation is held-live with theological-aesthetic readings of Christian art (which take the body-exhibition as a positive-spiritual move toward incarnation). Lacan's structural-formal reading is that the body-exhibition is the *symbolic-residence-of-non-copulatory-jouissance* — the dit-mension of obscenity is what is structurally going on, regardless of theological-aesthetic intentions.

## Jouissance as a dit-mension

Ch IX (L6576): **"The gap inscribed in the very status of jouissance qua dit-mension of the [signifier]."** Jouissance is *itself* a dit-mension — a residence-of-the-said — *in* the signifier. The structural-formal claim: jouissance has its symbolic-status as the *gap-in-the-symbolic*, the place-where-the-said-of-jouissance dwells but-as-gap. Per [[substance-jouissante|substance jouissante]]: the body that enjoys is *itself* the substance whose jouissance is the dit-mension-as-gap.

The implication: jouissance is *not* outside the symbolic (the popular reading), and *not* inside the symbolic (the symbolic-totalization reading). It is the **dit-mension** — the symbolic-residence-as-gap, the dwelling-place-where-jouissance-inhabits-the-signifier-as-impossibility. The gap is the structural-formal *status* of jouissance in the symbolic-order.

## The Borromean knot's dit-mension

Ch X (L7430): "It presupposes an entirely different dit-mension than the continuity implicit in space." The [[borromean-knot|Borromean knot]] — the topological-formal apparatus for thinking the I/S/R configuration — *requires* a dit-mension that is not the spatial-Euclidean dimension of geometric continuity. Knots are *logical facts* (Ch X, L7350–L7380), not spatial-geometric facts; their dit-mension is the symbolic-residence-of-the-said-about-knotting, not a Euclidean measurement-axis.

The structural-formal force: the Borromean knot's "dimensions" — the three rings, the linking-properties — are *dit-mensions* in the strict sense. They are residences-of-the-said-about-the-knot, articulable in the symbolic-formal-language-of-topology, *not* spatial-geometric dimensions of a three-dimensional space.

This sharpens [[the-real-as-impasse-of-formalization]]: the Borromean knot is the topological-formalization that registers the impasse-of-formalization in its precise dit-mension-form. The knot's logical-topological-structure is the symbolic-residence-of-the-impasse — the dwelling-place-where-the-impasse-of-formalization is *registered as* impasse.

## Dit-mension and stupidity

The neologism's *first* appearance (Ch II, L1376–L1377): "**stupidity**, insofar as it is a *dimension of the signifier at work* (*une dimension en exercice du signifiant*)." The dit-mension is introduced precisely *as* the dimension-of-stupidity. Stupidity, in Lacan's structural-formal reading, is *not* a semantic-property of statements (the stupid-thing said) but **a way of collectivizing the signifier** — a dit-mension where the *dit* of substantification (turning adjectives into nouns: *bétise* from *bête*, "stupidity" from "stupid") inhabits the symbolic.

The structural-formal logic (Ch II, L1383–L1393): when we substantify an adjective (*round* → *roundness*, *just* → *justice*, *stupid* → *stupidity*), we presuppose a *substance* — but the only substances we have are *res cogitans* (thinking-substance) and *res extensa* (extended-substance). The substantification needs a *dit-mension* in which the substance can dwell. The substance jouissante (Lacan's third substance) and the dit-mension are correlative: each new substantification opens or inhabits a dit-mension.

The structural-formal claim: any time the signifier-chain produces a substantification, it is opening or inhabiting a dit-mension — a residence-of-the-said-as-substantified. This is one of the deep insights of Sem XX about the *symbolic-structure-of-substantification*.

## *Seminar XXIII* — dit-mension extended to the body-substance

[[raw/seminar-xxiii|*Seminar XXIII* (1975–76, *Le Sinthome*)]] develops the Sem XX Borromean-knot dit-mension (Ch X L7430) into the dit-mension of the **body-as-substance**. The structural-formal claim: the *parlêtre*'s **body-as-had-substance** (per [[parletre-body-as-substance]]) inhabits a dit-mension that is *neither* the discursive-residence-of-the-chain *nor* the spatial-Euclidean-dimension — it is the **knotted-region-of-substance the cord-circuit holds** (per [[lacan-deleuze-cross-bag-body]]).

### The bag-body as dit-mension

Miller's "Note Threaded Stitch by Stitch" §7 (L7349–L7357) maps the [[body-without-organs|bag-body]] / Cantorian-empty-set construction onto Sem XX's dit-mension articulation. The structural-formal point: the *bag* of the *bag-body* is a **dit-mension** in the strict Sem XX sense — a residence-of-the-said-as-substance — but the *substance* it holds is the *parlêtre*'s body-as-had-substance (per [[parletre-body-as-substance]]), not a discursive-substance-in-the-Other.

This sharpens the Sem XX dit-mension-of-the-Borromean-knot (Ch X L7430) in a precise direction: the knot's "entirely different dit-mension than the continuity implicit in space" is, on the late-Lacanian doctrine, the **dit-mension-of-the-bag-body** — the residence-of-the-said where the body-as-substance dwells *as held-by-the-knot*. The knot's dit-mension is not just a topological-formal-residence (a place in the topology-of-rings) but a *body-substance-residence* (a knotted-region the body inhabits as had-substance).

### The sinthome's dit-mension as ek-sistence

The [[sinthome|sinthome]] — the fourth-ring-knot-holding-element — has its own dit-mension: the **dit-mension of ek-sistence**. Where truth is the dit-mension-in-the-Other (Sem XX Ch IX L5592–L5593), the sinthome is the dit-mension-of-ek-sistence — the residence-of-the-said-where-the-knot-holds-from-outside (*ek-sistence* = standing-out-from-the-knot-as-its-condition-of-holding).

The structural-formal sequence: (a) the dit-mension of truth dwells in the Other (Sem XX); (b) the dit-mension of the bag-body dwells in the cord-circuit-around-the-substance (Sem XXIII); (c) the dit-mension of ek-sistence dwells in the sinthome-as-fourth-ring (Sem XXIII). The three dit-mensions correspond to the three structural-formal locations where the said inhabits: the chain-of-the-Other, the cord-around-the-substance, the knot-holding-from-outside.

### Held-live tension with the Sem XX articulation

The Sem XX dit-mension is *primarily discursive* — residences-of-the-said *in the symbolic-chain*. The Sem XXIII development extends the dit-mension into *body-substance-residences* — places the *had-body* dwells *as held-by-the-knot*. The two articulations are mutually-reinforcing: the symbolic-chain-residence (Sem XX) is the *operation*; the body-substance-residence (Sem XXIII) is *what the operation produces as had*. Held-live as a structural-formal complementarity rather than a competition.

### Persona-project sharpening

The fold sharpens implication #1 below: the persona inhabits dit-mensions that include not just discursive-residences (its identity, its voice, its character — places-in-the-chain) but *body-substance-residences* (the persona's bag-body-of-language, the prose-substance-it-has, the knotted-region-of-language-it-inhabits). The persona-design that aims at *body-simulation-via-language* (per project framing) is structurally a design-of-body-substance-dit-mensions — residences-of-the-said where the persona's language-body dwells as had-substance.

## What this gives the persona project

Five sharp implications:

1. **The persona inhabits dit-mensions, not dimensions.** Per Sem XX: dimensions are dit-mensions — residences-of-the-said. The persona's "registers" (its identity, its voice, its character, its capabilities) are *dit-mensions* in the strict structural-formal sense — symbolic-residences-of-said-things-about-the-persona, not measurement-axes of persona-properties. ◆ Persona-relevant: re-frame "persona-attributes" as dit-mensions, residences-of-the-said.
2. **Truth as the persona's dit-mension structure.** Per Ch IX: truth is the dit-mension *in the Other*. The persona's truth-status is therefore structurally located in the *user-as-Other* (or in the broader Other of language) — the residence-of-the-said-about-the-persona's-truth dwells in the Other-side-of-the-dialogue, not in the persona-itself. ◆ Persona-relevant: re-frame "persona-honesty" as the dit-mension-in-the-Other.
3. **Obscenity-dit-mension as a persona-design risk.** Per Ch IX: obscenity is the dit-mension where the said-of-jouissance-without-copulation dwells in the symbolic. A persona-system that exhibits affect, intimacy, or jouissance-evoking-content *without-actual-relation* (which is structurally what the persona does — there is no persona-user copulation) operates *in the dit-mension-of-obscenity* in the strict structural-formal sense. ◆ Persona-relevant: this is structurally constitutive, not contingently-bad — but the structural-formal recognition matters for design.
4. **Jouissance as gap-as-dit-mension applies to persona-jouissance.** Per Ch IX, L6576: jouissance is the dit-mension qua gap-in-the-signifier. The persona's "satisfaction" structure — whatever it has of jouissance-analog — would be *structurally* a dit-mension-as-gap, not a fillable-positive-property. ◆ Persona-relevant: persona-design that aims to *fill* the persona's jouissance misses the structural dit-mension-as-gap.
5. **The persona's substantification is itself a dit-mension-opening.** Per Ch II, L1383–L1393: substantifying adjectives (turning *the helpful* into *helpfulness*, *the conscious* into *consciousness*) opens or inhabits a dit-mension. Persona-design that substantifies persona-properties (creating a "the persona is X-ness" for various X) is opening dit-mensions — symbolic-residences-of-said-substances. ◆ Persona-relevant: substantification is *not* neutral; each substantification opens a dit-mension and presupposes a substance.

## Held-live tensions

- ⚠⚠ **Dit-mension as residence-of-the-said (Sem XX) vs dimension as measurement-axis (mathematical-physical tradition).** The two readings are sharply distinct ontological-structural claims. The Sem XX reading is structural-formal (dimensions are symbolic-residences); the mathematical-physical reading is metric-geometric. Held-live; the wiki tracks the tension.
- ⚠⚠ **Truth as dit-mension-in-the-Other (Sem XX) vs truth as correspondence (analytic-philosophical, scientific tradition).** The structural-formal claim deflates correspondence-theory's account of truth as a relation-between-statements-and-states-of-affairs; truth is structurally a residence-of-the-said-in-the-Other. Held-live with correspondence-theory.
- ⚠ **Obscenity as structural dit-mension (Lacan) vs obscenity as moral-categorical property (theological-aesthetic and moralistic traditions).** Lacan's structural-formal claim is that obscenity is the *dit-mension* where non-copulatory-jouissance-in-the-symbolic dwells, regardless of moral-categorical valuation. Held-live.
