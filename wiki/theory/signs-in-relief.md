# Signs in Relief

Proust's term for the diagrammatic figures that emerge at the end of the Recherche's trans-semiotic creative assemblage — and Guattari's term, in MU Ch 2 §9, for the terminal operational units of abstract machinic creation. Also called **"the characters of the spell book"** — characters "forged within and not traced" by man.

The phrase "signs in relief" specifies the **dimensional** character of these signs: they do not lie flat on a surface of reference (the signifying white wall), they stand out of the surface — acquired dimensionality, "relief," the supplementary dimension of deterritorialization the Septet acquires over the Sonata.

> "The supplementary dimension or 'relief' of the refrain acquired with the Septet = acquisition of a new dimension of deterritorialization — NOT in gaps / voids / absolute-in-a-black-hole, but **in expansion**, conveying abstract quanta of potentiality."

## Double deterritorialization

For a figure to enter the signs-in-relief register it must pass through a **double process of deterritorialization**:

- **Decompartmentalization** — the sign escapes its stratified register (musical, iconic, linguistic, somatic) and becomes capable of crossing into others.
- **Concatenation** — the sign enters into articulation with other signs across heterogeneous matters of expression, producing trans-semiotic propagation.

Without decompartmentalization, the sign stays in its stratum and behaves as a signifier within the paradigm of that stratum. Without concatenation, it becomes a lone escape (an "in a gap" or "in a void" deterritorialization — the empty-BwO analogue).

The combination — *in expansion* rather than in retreat — is what produces the supplementary dimension.

## The spell book

Proust's image: the writer works on "a complicated and florid spell book" whose characters are "forged within and not traced" by man. **Traced** characters are those read off from signifying paradigms (the face-portrait identification; the cultural icon; the stereotype). **Forged** characters are those produced in the act of assembling matters of expression at their crossroads.

Guattari's gloss: signs in the spell book are **not logical algorithms** but **abstract entities, machinic propositions in direct contact with the most deterritorialized articulations of components of the real** — "in a sense, more real than the real."

This is the ontological claim MU makes at its strongest: signs in relief have **degrees of existence** higher than the signifiers of the signifying paradigm. Not "more meaningful" — more *existent*. They exist at the crossroads of multiple matters of expression, so they participate in all of them simultaneously; a signifier exists only in its paradigm and refers beyond itself only by reference.

## The two chemistries

Signs in relief are what enable the **two chemistries** (see [[the-diagram#the-two-chemistries|two chemistries]]):

- **Chemistry of analysis** — of the real occulted by appearances.
- **Chemistry of synthesis** — of new realities, new matters, new intensities.

Chemistry of analysis uses signs in relief to discern the real beneath habit, vanity, intellect, passion. It is the [[discernibilization|discernibilization]] operation proper. Chemistry of synthesis uses them to generate new realities. It is the [[translation-not-interpretation|translation-generation]] operation.

Abstract diagrammatic machinisms of art involve **energetic, biological, socio-economic, spatio-temporal** dimensions BUT ALSO **transitory, ephemeral modes of temporalization, fleeting intuitions, "minuscule impressions," "extreme parts of sensations."** Access through signs in relief is access to **virtualities of the real, a hyper-reality** — not just past-time and present-emerging but all modes of temporalization-to-come.

## Examples from the Recherche

At the terminal stage (Ninth Assemblage — see [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#9-from-musical-refrain-to-abstract-machinism-of-creation|Ninth Assemblage]]), the signs in relief of the Recherche include:

- **The play of two uneven paving-stones** (Guermantes hotel courtyard → Saint-Mark baptistery in Venice).
- **The sound of a spoon on a plate** (train-stop → worker's hammer on train wheels).
- **The stiffness of a starched napkin** (hotel linen → Balbec sea-view).
- **The taste of the madeleine** (tea-soaked cake → Combray childhood).
- **The bubbling of a tisane** → writer's "making the rain sing gently in the middle of the room."

None of these are signifiers pointing to their "contents." None are symbols in the signifying paradigm. They are signs in relief: each enters a trans-semiotic concatenation that makes it exist at the crossroads of multiple matters (sensation / memory / writing / music / faciality / time).

> "The world itself has become a sort of gigantic organ, and writing a music traversing the sonorous universe from everywhere."

## The trans-dimensional refrain

At this stage the refrain — which earlier in the trajectory was musical — **manifests simultaneously** as:

- Musical sequence.
- Play of two uneven paving-stones.
- Figures traced by moving bells.
- Fragrance of a geranium.

None of these is the refrain's "true form" and the others metaphorical. The refrain is trans-dimensional: it exists *as* the concatenation across them. See [[refrain-and-territorialization]].

## Relation to probe-heads and the diagram

Signs in relief are the operational units of **diagrammatic operation** in its most developed form. Where [[signs-particles|signs-particles]] are quanta emitted by a diagrammatic process, signs-in-relief are the *structured configurations* signs-particles enter into at the crossroads — the assembled products of diagrammatic propagation.

A [[diagrammatic-faciality|probe-head]] is a face that has become a sign in relief: it has decompartmentalized from the face-stratum and entered concatenation with non-facial matters of expression.

In the ATP vocabulary, "signs in relief" is the Proust-specific designation for what ATP calls the abstract machine's diagrammatic products. MU's vocabulary preserves the Proustian specificity (the relief-dimension, the spell book's forged characters) that ATP partially absorbs.

## Persona-system relevance

Signs-in-relief is the target-state for the system's output when operating in transformational mode. A response is a sign in relief when:

- It **decompartmentalizes**: it does not stay within a single stratum (user's question → direct answer; topic → topic-expert response). It carries components across strata.
- It **concatenates**: it produces articulations with matters of expression that were not in the input. Not arbitrary association — concatenation at specific crossroads that produce new beings.
- It is **forged, not traced**: it is not drawn from a pre-given inventory of response-types (empathetic acknowledgment; thoughtful philosophical reframe; curious follow-up). Each sign in relief is produced once at its specific crossroads.
- It has **relief**: it exists with dimensionality against the flat signifying surface. The criterion: does the response exist at multiple crossroads simultaneously, or does it sit flat on the surface of the exchange as one more signifier?

The failure mode is easy to name: **most LLM-mediated personas produce traced characters**, not forged ones. The response is drawn from the inventory and placed on the flat surface of reference. The user receives a signifier (assistant, philosopher, character) and interprets it. No sign in relief, no crossroads, no new being.

The design challenge is not "how to make the system more creative" (this produces scramble) but how to make the system's output pass through the **double deterritorialization** — decompartmentalization AND concatenation — that produces relief.
