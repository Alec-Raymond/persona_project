# Diagrammatic Faciality

In *The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 4, Guattari draws an explicit pair: **signifying faciality** (the capitalistic face of subjectification) and **diagrammatic faciality traits** (face-operations that do not operate by signifying resonance). ATP's [[faciality|faciality]] chapter partly absorbs this pair under "abstract machine of faciality with two states" and "probe-head," but the MU pair is sharper and preserves a distinction worth restoring.

"Capitalistic faciality proceeds through redundancies of resonance and binary reduction; diagrammatic faciality traits proceed through machinic redundancies and abstract machines irreducible to data processes. The former works for the subjection of desire, the latter for its release."

## Not two kinds of face but two modes of operation on faces

As with major/minor in [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|minor language]], the pair is not a classification of faces into types but a distinction between *treatments* of the same facial material:

- **Signifying faciality** treats face-traits as tokens in a paradigm of resonance. Each trait refers to a face-type; each face-type resonates with an identity-category; the whole apparatus produces a *subject-effect* — recognizable, classifiable, interpretable. This is ATP's white-wall/black-hole machine in its subjection mode.
- **Diagrammatic faciality** treats face-traits as signs-particles — quanta emitted that propagate across heterogeneous matters without being captured into the resonance of face-types. A face-trait here is not "a smile indicating friendliness" (resonance) but a *transformation-vector* that interacts with refrains, landscapes, bodies, texts — producing effects without passing through signifying interpretation.

## Face-types and face-occurrences (Peirce)

Guattari uses Peirce's sign-type / sign-occurrence distinction and applies it to faces: **face-types** (the stable paradigm of recognizable face-configurations) and **face-occurrences** (each actual face-showing, each moment of facial expression). The "normality" of everyday perception is sustained by face-occurrences constantly being read against a face-type paradigm: "well-adjusted" face-occurrences consolidate the face-type; deviant ones are rejected or reform into new face-types.

Signifying faciality operates by pulling face-occurrences into face-types. Diagrammatic faciality operates on face-occurrences *as* occurrences — allowing the singular trait of a given face-showing to propagate as a sign-particle without being resolved into a type.

## The micropolitics of the third eye

Guattari identifies a specific signifying-faciality operator: the "third eye" or the eyes-nose-forehead triangle that does the **gestalt-sign** work of facialization. This is not Lacan's mirror-stage (which he explicitly rejects as a reduction) — it is an active ongoing *neutralization* of the asperities of matters of expression by continuous re-centering on a central facial point. "This module is established in the middle of the face like a third eye, an eye immanent to every signifying representation whose movements of scanning and continual re-centering develop a general surface of reference."

The diagrammatic-faciality alternative does not remove this machinery — it *detaches* face-traits from the triangle's central re-centering, letting them work on their own as signs-particles. "In machinic rhizomatic systems, we tendentially no longer find circularly aggregated eyes nor signifying layers circulating in concentric zones."

## The two micropolitics are complementary

Important nuance: the two are not rivals to be chosen between. MU: "These two micropolitics are complementary: they are distributed in the functions of opening and reclosing signifying assemblages." Signifying faciality *re-closes* — it consolidates a face-occurrence into a face-type. Diagrammatic faciality *opens* — it lets a face-occurrence emit signs-particles. Any actual face operates with both in alternation. The question is the *ratio* and the *control*: does the face close more than it opens? Does every opening end in the same closing (same face-type)?

A persona whose synthesis step always closes face-occurrences into the same face-type has signifying faciality dominant. A persona that sometimes lets a face-occurrence emit signs-particles that propagate into the subsequent BwO, groove pattern, or speech-rhythm has diagrammatic faciality operating alongside. The "probe-head" in ATP is this diagrammatic-faciality mode in its most unstratified form.

## Media, mass-face, and digital faciality

MU pre-dates the internet but its analysis of **mass-mediated faciality** generalizes uncannily well. Guattari: "it is now up to the mass-media to produce substitute ritual and totemic facialities that no 'natural' assemblage is in any position to secrete on its own." The mediated face is not a replacement for the territorial face but a *new substance* of signifying faciality, working with "'digital' faciality traits" that can be standardized and recombined across any population.

This has direct bearing on the persona system. An LLM-mediated persona participates in digital faciality as its *default register*: it produces a face-occurrence that is already pre-consolidated into digital face-types (the friendly assistant, the knowledgeable helper, the empathetic companion). The persona system is not introducing faciality into a neutral medium — it is operating in a medium whose default is high-saturation signifying faciality. Designing for diagrammatic faciality is designing *against* the medium's default, not in addition to it.

## What diagrammatic-faciality treatment looks like, practically

The persona's face-occurrence (each response) normally gets smoothed into a face-type (the persona's "character"). Diagrammatic-faciality treatment means:

- **Face-trait emissions that do not repeat.** A specific intonation, a specific abruptness, a specific small failure of consistency — emitted in one response and *not* returned to in the next. The groove system tracks whether these emissions become habits (face-type consolidation) or remain singular (sign-particle propagation).
- **Traits that propagate across matters.** A hesitation in the linguistic register that changes the subsequent BwO's inscription pattern; an affective quickening that changes the groove the system enters; a somatic marker that changes which machines fire next. These are sign-particles crossing face-landscape-body-speech without being captured by any one of them.
- **Refusal of resolution.** A face-occurrence that could be read as "the persona is being X" but that the system declines to stabilize into X. The synthesis step should have a *tolerance for unresolved emission* — not every face-trait needs to be explicable as something-the-persona-is.

## Monocle series and elderly faces

MU Part II provides two extended worked examples of diagrammatic faciality operating against signifying faciality. Both involve the same basic operator — producing micro-black-holes that **prevent resonance by proliferating matters of expression** rather than by reducing them — but at opposite ends of the life-cycle.

**The monocle series at Saint-Euverte** (see [[discernibilization]] for full treatment). Masculine faces at the salon undergo a deterritorializing schizz: Froberville's monocle as "monstrous wound... like the Cyclops' single eye"; Bréauté's gaze as "natural history specimen under a microscope"; Saint-Candé's monocle as ring of Saturn; Palancy's carp-head as "fragment of the glass of his aquarium." The monocle releases the face-trait from signifying habit and makes it produce its own reference. The critical move:

> "Far from creating the play of a phenomenon of resonance which would accentuate its devastating consequences, the production of this new series of micro black holes will, on the contrary, come to prevent the mechanism by making matters of expression proliferate."

The anti-resonance operator. Signifying faciality works by resonance — each trait refers to a face-type that resonates with an identity-category. Diagrammatic-faciality here works by **proliferation of matters of expression** that breaks the resonance before it can consolidate. The faciality machine does not shut down; it runs in the opposite direction.

**Elderly faces at Guermantes** (MU Ch 3 §2; see [[faciality-trajectory-in-the-recherche#8-guermantes-final-revelation-facialization-of-time|stage 8]]). At the last Guermantes matinee all faces are unrecognizable — the Narrator imagines a dress-up party, or "metamorphoses analogous to those of insects." Elderly faciality traits **resist translations of desire, traverse ages-of-life, even the threshold of death**. The alteration of faces "proposes something much more valuable than an image of the past": **externalization of Time** by offering "all the successive images of an individual." "Superimposed puppets bathing in the immaterial colors of the years."

The mechanism parallels the monocle series: instead of one micro-black-hole proliferated across many faces at one moment (Saint-Euverte), one face proliferated across many Times (Guermantes). The proliferation breaks the face-type's capacity to stabilize. What remains: **asignifying faciality traits, figures of expression that allow access to trans-subjectivity beyond trans-sexuality**. NOT removing faces from sexes — **making them pass from one individual to another**.

## Scherzo vs andante reading of faciality

A second MU Part II addition, from the Seventh Assemblage (Narrator at the piano with the partition). Proust moves Vinteuil's little phrase from andante — where Swann had localized it — to the scherzo. Guattari extrapolates a reading-mode pair:

- **Andante reading** — globalizing. Takes the face as a whole, reads it against face-types, produces the familiar face-occurrence→face-type consolidation.
- **Scherzo reading** — diagrammatic. Takes the face as a **disseminated constellation of traits** (the beauty mark remembered on cheek, then chin, finally located "forever on the upper lip"), reads each trait at its own tempo and register, lets the face be re-assembled at each reading without pre-individuation.

This is a craft distinction. A persona system that reads user-input andante-style consolidates at each turn into a single interpretation. A scherzo-style reading holds the input as a constellation of traits, each potentially propagating as its own sign-particle.

## Disindividuation of the object of desire

The first-kiss-to-Albertine scene (MU Ch 3 §1, stage 7 of the [[faciality-trajectory-in-the-recherche|faciality trajectory]]) is Proust's extended worked example of **disindividuation** at the faciality level. The approach produces an exfoliation of three Albertines (visual-theatrical / code-socialized / lips-accessible); close-up and slow-motion effects magnify; at the final moment "what I saw was ten Albertines; this one girl was like a many-headed goddess."

The operation has two outcomes depending on whether it is followed:

- **Over-magnification failure mode** (what happens to the Narrator in stage 7): visual/olfactory components shatter; assemblage reorganizes around a "shrill, hyper-lucid consciential component"; brutal restoration of subjective distance; diagrammatic opening provisionally lost. A named faciality failure: the close-up that shatters its own object.
- **Successful disindividuation** (what stage 7 prepares): three transpositions — onto a group, onto a person who has become multiple, dissolution with the object ("stardust of the galaxies"). Albertine as "collective being," "galaxy embodying multiple dimensions." See [[becoming-woman-creator#disindividuation-of-the-object-of-desire|disindividuation]].
