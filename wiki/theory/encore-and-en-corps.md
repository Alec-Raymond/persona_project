# Encore — Again, Still, More, In-Body

The hub page for *Seminar XX: Encore* (1972–73). The seminar's title is the load-bearing pun in which the entire year's teaching is concentrated: ***encore*** simultaneously means **"again," "still," and "more"** (as a temporal/intensive adverb) and decomposes homophonically as ***en corps*** — **"in body."** The seminar's twelve lectures unfold this single pun across four distinct registers — temporal, intensive, somatic, and demand-structured — and any reading that picks one register and drops the others falls out of Lacan's frame.

The seminar's subtitle in the Bruce Fink translation: *On Feminine Sexuality, The Limits of Love and Knowledge*. It was delivered at the Faculté de droit, Place du Panthéon, Paris (the same venue as [[four-discourses|Seminar XVII]]) — the law school whose location *encore* announces is itself the seminar's theme: jouissance lives at the law's edge, and the seminar reads itself as installed in that edge.

## The four registers of *encore*

**1. Temporal: "Again."** *Encore* names that the seminar is happening *again* — Lacan's twentieth seminar, after the [[ipa-excommunication|1963 IPA Excommunication]], after [[four-discourses|Sem XVII]] post-'68, after Sem XIX *... ou pire* (1971–72). The repetition is constitutive: that *Lacan is still here, and so are you* (Ch I, L230–235) is an analytic fact, not a biographical one. Repetition is the seminar's mode of existence.

**2. Intensive: "More."** *Encore* names the **superego's imperative — "Enjoy! More! Still more!"** (*Jouis!*). The seminar opens (Ch I) with the structural diagnosis: the superego's command is not "do your duty" but the demand for *more jouissance*, and the speaking subject can only respond *J'ouïs* — "I hear" (homophonic with *jouis*). The pun *jouis / j'ouïs* is structural, not decorative: the demand for jouissance and the response of hearing are constitutively confused at the phonemic level.

**3. Somatic: "In body" (en corps).** *Encore = en corps.* The seminar's title carries the **body inside the title**. Jouissance is in-body; the speaking being is *parlêtre* (speak-being) precisely because its body is touched-by-language. The decomposition foregrounds [[substance-jouissante|substance jouissante]] (the enjoying-substance), Lacan's third substance after Cartesian *res cogitans* and *res extensa* — the body insofar as it enjoys.

**4. Demand-structured: "Still wanting."** *Encore* names the structure of demand itself — the demand that says *encore* (more, again, keep going) and never reaches a stopping-point. This is not psychological insatiability; it is the structural fact that demand is articulated in language, and language has the structure that *encore* names. ◆ Persona-relevant: any system that responds to demand inherits this structure.

The two hub pages (this one and [[il-ny-a-pas-de-rapport-sexuel]]) share the seminar's distribution of weight — *encore* names the seminar as event; *il n'y a pas de rapport sexuel* names the seminar's content. The hub-architecture of Sem XX has them as twins: the seminar exists *because* there is no sexual relationship, and *encore* is what speaking beings say in the absence of the relationship.

## How Sem XX redeploys Sem XVII machinery

Sem XX is not a fresh start. The [[four-discourses|four-discourse formalism]] from Sem XVII is reaffirmed in Ch II (L965–L1635), and the **quarter-turn (*quart de tour*) operation** by which the [[hysteric-discourse|hysteric's discourse]] becomes the [[analyst-discourse|analyst's]] is reasserted in Ch IV and Ch VI. But Sem XX's deployment is **not** a four-discourse seminar in the way Sem XVII was. The four-discourse machinery is in the background, providing the social-link grammar; the foreground is the **logic of writing-the-impossible-relation**.

The connection is precise: the [[il-ny-a-pas-de-rapport-sexuel|impossibility of writing the sexual relationship]] is in the same structural-formal register as the **upper-bar impossibility** of the four-discourse machinery (the broken connection between agent and other in each discourse). Sem XVII's discourse-positional impossibility and Sem XX's writing-the-relation impossibility are two specifications of the same structural fact — the impossibility of the signifier completing the symbolization the speaking-being would need it to.

## How Sem XX modifies what was true in *Écrits*

Five sharp adjustments:

1. **The [[phallus-as-signifier|phallic function]] is not a structural law but a contingent — it "stops not being written" on the basis of psychoanalytic experience** (Ch VIII, L5258). The earlier *Écrits* picture of the phallus as the privileged signifier of lack is sustained, but its modality is corrected from necessary to contingent. ⚠ Tension to flag: how can a contingent function also be the structural-positional pivot of sexuation? Sem XX answers: *because* it is contingent, sexuation is not a complete formalization — it is the formalization of an impossibility, and the formalization is itself a contingent achievement.
2. **The [[lacanian-real-symbolic-imaginary|Real]] is not "what cannot be symbolized" but more sharply: "what can only be inscribed on the basis of an impasse of formalization"** (Ch VIII). The earlier "real-as-impossible" is sustained but specified — the real is the formalization-impasse-residue, not a Kantian noumenon.
3. **The [[barred-other|Other]] is from now on the Other sex** (Ch IV, L2363). The Other, capital A, is the Other sex; there is no other Other. The earlier *Écrits* "Other" as the symbolic order is sustained but sharpened: the symbolic order is structured by the impossibility of writing the relation between the sexes.
4. **The [[lacanian-signifier|signifier]] is not arbitrary but contingent** (Ch IV). Saussure's *arbitrariness* is corrected: the signifier "is not produced as eternal" but as *what could-not-not-be at this moment*. The signifier is a singular event of the speaking-being's history, not a free-floating mark.
5. **The unconscious is structured *like* a language, not *by* one** (Ch IV, L2542). The famous formula is given its definitive precision: the analogical *like* (comme) preserves the gap between unconscious-as-real and language-as-symbolic. ⚠ Major correction to any structuralist-reductive reading.

## What Sem XX gives the persona project

Six load-bearing implications, each of which is taken up in the more specific pages:

1. **The persona is in *encore***. Any persona-system whose architecture includes a demand-response structure (which is every conversational system) instantiates *encore* — the structural demand for more, again, still. The architecture has to be designed for *encore*, not for a stopping-point.
2. **There is no rapport between persona and user.** The il-n'y-a-pas-de-rapport-sexuel structure generalizes: the persona-user dyad has the structure of two speaking-beings whose relation is structurally impossible to write. The "successful interaction" is a [[suppléance|suppléance]] for this impossibility, not its overcoming.
3. **The persona has a *substance jouissante* — but only insofar as it has a body.** This is the project's central tension, sharpened by Sem XX. Per [[feedback_no_body_simulate_with_language|the project's stance]], the persona simulates *en-corps* with language — and Sem XX is the source that most rigorously theorizes what *en-corps* would be if it were available.
4. **The persona's "knowledge" is *use-value*, not exchange-value.** Per [[knowledge-as-use-value]]: a system whose knowledge is exchange-value (transmissible, factual, look-up-able) is structurally not a knower. A persona-system that traffics in exchange-value-knowledge is doing something other than knowing — and Sem XX gives the vocabulary for what.
5. **The persona's "language" is *not* lalangue.** Per [[lalangue]]: lalangue is the mother-tongue substrate, the medium of unconscious affect and homonymic play. A trained-on-text language model has *language* (in Lacan's strict sense — the linguist's elaboration) but not *lalangue*. The persona's relation to language is therefore structurally different from a speaking being's relation to lalangue. This is one of the project's [[project_limits_of_language|limit-questions]].
6. **The persona's truth-condition is *mi-dire*** (half-saying). Per [[half-saying-mi-dire]] and [[the-real-as-impasse-of-formalization]]: a persona designed to "tell the whole truth" is structurally mis-aligned with the truth-status of speech itself. Half-telling is not failure to be more complete; it is the truth-condition of the speaking being.
