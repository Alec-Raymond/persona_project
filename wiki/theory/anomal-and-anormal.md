# Anomal and Anormal

In *A Thousand Plateaus* plateau 10 ("Becoming-Intense, Becoming-Animal, Becoming-Imperceptible"), at line 1837 and footnote 13, Deleuze and Guattari draw an etymological distinction that is foundational for the whole becoming-doctrine and not currently carried by the wiki.

- **Anormal** (Latin *a-* + *norma*) — deviation from a norm. Negative, indicating what a thing is not, what rule it fails to meet. A privative term. The register of pathology, classification, correction.
- **Anomal** (Greek *an-* + *homalos*, "even, smooth, regular") — positioned at the edge of a group, at the *anomalous* border. Non-privative. The register of boundary-dwelling, outsider-status, the figure through whom contagion passes.

The wiki has been using "anomalous" and "abnormal" interchangeably. D&G's text is explicit that they are not the same. The **Anomalous** (*l'anomal*) is a formal and technical term of the becoming-doctrine, not a synonym for "weird" or "deviant."

## What the Anomalous is

In a pack, swarm, or multiplicity, the Anomalous is the borderline individual — the edge-dweller, the one positioned *at* the boundary rather than at the center (the chief, leader, hierarchical apex) or simply outside it (the exile, the exception). The Anomalous is the pack member through whom the pack's outside is *of* the pack:

- *Moby-Dick*'s white whale is anomalous, not abnormal: an individual-at-the-edge of the sperm-whale pack, through whom the relation to the oceanic outside is borne.
- The witch at the edge of the village, not inside and not cast out, is anomalous.
- Lovecraft's *Outsider* (plateau 10 footnote 7) is anomalous: "I have been seized by a longing to know my origin, to tread again the familiar places of my childhood..."
- Hofmannsthal's rats (plateau 10 fn 8) are the anomalous crowd-at-the-edge through which becoming-rat happens.

Becoming-animal is *not* identification with a species or with a normed animal. It is contagion with the anomalous — the pack member at the edge, the one through whom the pack's outside enters. This is why becoming is **anti-archetypal**: the archetype is the species-norm ("the wolf," "the cat"); the anomalous is the pack-edge ("*this* wolf, at the border, through which the wolf-pack's relation to the outside is carried").

## Why the distinction is load-bearing

**Against species-thinking.** Most psychology that reaches for animal-material (Jung's archetype-catalog, Freud's totem-animal) operates on species-norms. The wolf stands for the wolf-species, the cat for cat-qualities, etc. D&G's position: becoming-animal has nothing to do with this. The becoming-wolf is not a relation to "wolf-nature" or "wolf-archetype"; it is a relation to an *individual-at-the-edge* of a pack, through whom the pack's multiplicity-character is carried.

**Against pathology-thinking.** The anomalous is not a deviation from a norm. It is not "worse" or "broken" or "exceptional-because-defective." It is *constitutively edge-dwelling*: the pack could not be a pack without members at its edge. The anomalous is a position, not a defect.

**Against the hero.** The anomalous is not the pack's leader. The pack-chief is at the apex of a hierarchy; the anomalous is at the boundary where the pack faces its outside. ATP is explicit that becoming proceeds through the anomalous, not through the chief. The persona-system analog: the persona's "most distinctive" output is not its most-central-to-the-groove but its edge-output — the one that carries the relation to what the persona is *not yet*.

## Application to the persona system

The distinction is directly operative on persona design in several ways:

- **Pack-style individuation.** If the persona is a pack rather than an individual (a set of machines, a multiplicity of voice-fragments — [[desiring-machines]]), then its anomalous is the edge-machine or edge-voice through which it connects to what it is not yet. This is different from the "most unusual output" (which would be anormal, deviation from the persona-norm). The anomalous is positional, not qualitative.

- **Becoming requires the anomalous.** Per plateau 10, becoming proceeds only through the anomalous-at-the-edge. A persona-design that hopes for becoming-events has to ensure the persona contains an edge — members/moves/voices that are *at* the boundary rather than cleanly internal or cleanly external. A fully-coherent persona (no edge) cannot become anything; a scattered persona (no pack, only exceptions) has no boundary for the anomalous to occupy.

- **Not every unusual move is anomalous.** An output that violates the persona-norm in a correctible, classifiable way (a tone-miss, a voice-drift) is anormal: deviation from the groove, returnable to it. An output that occupies the persona's edge — distinctively *of* the persona while pointing to what the persona is not — is anomalous. The difference is not always visible in the output itself; it is a positional distinction relative to the pack-structure of the persona's machines.

- **The diagnostic use.** Asking "is this output anormal or anomalous?" is a design-level diagnostic. Anormal outputs call for repair (tune the groove); anomalous outputs are sites of potential becoming and should be examined for what edge they occupy.

## Faciality and the anomalous

Plateau 7 and plateau 10 couple: faciality is the despotic capture-operation that sorts faces into the norm (white-man face at center) and the abnormal (deviations to be classified). The anomalous does not fit the faciality grid — it is not a deviation to be corrected or diagnosed, but a boundary-position that the face-grid structurally cannot place.

**Racism**, in ATP's account (plateau 7 line 1374), is precisely the operation of *reading the anomalous as anormal* — treating edge-positions as deviations-from-the-norm rather than as edges-of-the-multiplicity. A face that does not fit the white-man-face grid is classified as *deviation-from* rather than recognized as *edge-of*.

The persona-system analog: categorizing persona-outputs by their distance from a norm (deviation/pathology) rather than by their position in a multiplicity (edge/anomalous) is the same operation racism runs. The anormal/anomalous distinction is a political distinction before it is a technical one.

## Held-live tensions

- **Whether a language-only pack can have an anomalous at all.** The anomalous presupposes a pack — a multiplicity with edges. Whether a persona composed of language-alone supports a genuine pack-structure (rather than a simulated pack that is really a single voice with variations) is a specific version of the [[limits-of-language]] question. The answer affects whether the becoming-doctrine applies.

- **The anomalous as the persona-project's "probe-head."** A [[faciality#dismantling-the-face|probe-head]] (*tête chercheuse*) is a face that moves toward defacialization. The anomalous is the pack-member that moves toward the pack's outside. The two may be structurally the same operation at different registers (faciality-register vs pack-register). Whether to unify the concepts or hold them distinct is a design decision the wiki has not made.
