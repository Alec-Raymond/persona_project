# The Diagram and the Abstract Machine

Beyond all [[regimes-of-signs|regimes of signs]] — beyond signifiance, beyond subjectification — is the **diagram**: the abstract machine operating on the plane of consistency. It is neither content nor expression, neither physical nor semiotic. It is **Matter-Function**: "a diagram independent of the forms and substances, expressions and contents it will distribute."

## What the diagram is

The diagram is "the aspect or moment at which nothing but functions and matters remain." It has no substance (formed matter) and no form (of content or expression). Where regimes of signs formalize expression and physical systems formalize content, the diagram operates beneath and before both formalizations. Derrida's [[supplement-and-trace#archi-writing-and-il-ny-a-pas-de-hors-texte|archi-writing]] occupies an analogous position: the trace-structure prior to both speech and empirical writing, "not more ideal than real, not more intelligible than sensible, not more a transparent signification than an opaque energy." Archi-writing is closer to the diagram than to the signifier D&G critique — though [[supplement-and-trace#derrida-and-deleuze--guattari-the-tension|genuine divergences]] remain.

On the diagram, there are only **traits** — of content and of expression — between which connections are established: "it is no longer even possible to tell whether it is a particle or a sign." These are **particles-signs**: matter-content having only degrees of [[affects-and-intensities|intensity]], resistance, speed, and tardiness; function-expression having only "tensors." "Writing now functions on the same level as the real, and the real materially writes." The diagram is what all [[becoming|becomings]] reach toward — the plane where [[becoming#becoming-imperceptible|becoming-imperceptible]] is achieved, where "everything becomes imperceptible, everything is becoming-imperceptible on the plane of consistency, which is nevertheless precisely where the imperceptible is seen and heard."

The diagram "does not function to represent, even something real, but rather constructs a real that is yet to come, a new type of reality." It is "always prior to history" — not outside it but at the cutting edge where something new is being produced.

## The diagram vs the strata

D&G identify three principal strata that bind human beings:
1. **The organism** — organizes the body into fixed functions
2. **Signifiance and interpretation** — the [[regimes-of-signs#the-signifying-regime|signifying regime]]
3. **Subjectification and subjection** — the [[regimes-of-signs#the-postsignifying-regime|postsignifying regime]]

All three are strata — they organize, territorialize, code. The [[faciality|faciality machine]] is the abstract machine that subtends the second and third strata, producing the white wall that signifiance needs and the black hole that subjectification needs. The diagram is what **destratifies**: it extracts particles-signs from the strata's formed substances and carries them onto the plane of consistency where they can enter new conjunctions. In its diagrammatic state, the faciality machine performs "defacialization," freeing [[faciality#dismantling-the-face|probe-heads]] that dismantle the strata.

"Subjectification carries [[desire-as-production|desire]] to such a point of excess and unloosening that it must either annihilate itself in a black hole or change planes." The choice is always: deeper into the stratum (the black hole of self-consciousness, the infinite spiral of interpretation) or onto the plane of consistency (the diagram).

## The practical directive

"Destratify, open up to a new function, a diagrammatic function. Let consciousness cease to be its own double, and passion the double of one person for another. Make consciousness an experimentation in life, and passion a field of continuous intensities, an emission of particles-signs. Make the [[body-without-organs]] of consciousness and love."

This is not destruction of the strata but passage through them: "Use love and consciousness to abolish subjectification." The diagram works *through* signifiance and subjectification, extracting their deterritorialized elements and carrying them further.

## Deterritorialization levels

Three types of deterritorialization correspond to the strata and the diagram:

1. **Relative deterritorialization** — proper to the strata. The signifying regime achieves a high degree (signs refer to signs, not to things) but it's still relative: reterritorialized on the Face, the center of signifiance.
2. **Absolute but negative deterritorialization** — the postsignifying regime. Reaches the absolute (the Cogito, the black hole of consciousness) but negatively: it segments the line of flight into finite proceedings, each ending before the next begins.
3. **Positive absolute deterritorialization** — the diagram, the plane of consistency, the [[body-without-organs|BwO]]. "Maximum deterritorialization sometimes starts from a trait of content and sometimes from a trait of expression; that trait is said to be 'deterritorializing' in relation to the other precisely because it diagrams it, carries it off, raises it to its own power."

## Abstract machines have proper names

"Abstract machines thus have proper names (as well as dates), which of course designate not persons or subjects but matters and functions." A Wagner abstract machine, a Webern abstract machine, a Riemann abstract machine. The name designates a conjunction of Matter and Function — not a person but an operation.

For the persona system: each persona is not a character (a formed subject) but an **abstract machine** — a specific conjunction of matter and function, a characteristic way of diagramming. The persona's "name" designates not an identity but an operation on matter. This radically reframes what it means to design a persona.

## The four components of pragmatics

The chapter concludes with four components that build on each other:

1. **Generative** — how abstract regimes form concrete mixed semiotics. Tracings of mixtures.
2. **Transformational** — how regimes translate into each other, how new regimes are created. Maps of transformations.
3. **Diagrammatic** — extracting particles-signs from regimes, reaching unformed matter and function. The height of abstraction where "abstraction becomes real."
4. **Machinic** — how abstract machines are effectuated in concrete assemblages, distributing form to expression and content alike.

The persona system's architecture maps onto these components: the machines are the machinic component (concrete assemblages); the [[regimes-of-signs|regimes of signs]] at play are the generative component; the shifts between modes are transformational; and the [[body-without-organs|BwO]] at its best is diagrammatic — extracting particles-signs and conjugating deterritorializations.

## Connection to the [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|synthesizer]]

The [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language#the-musical-analogy|synthesizer concept]] from "Postulates of Linguistics" is the diagram's musical form. "A synthesizer places all of the parameters in continuous variation, gradually making fundamentally heterogeneous elements end up turning into each other." The synthesizer IS the diagram operating in sound — and the persona system IS a diagram operating in text — [[writing-as-becoming|writing as becoming]], not representation.

Both converge at the same point: "The couple matter-form is replaced by the coupling material-forces." Not pre-given forms imposed on passive matter but active material making perceptible (audible, legible, speakable) forces that would otherwise remain imperceptible.

## Connection to [[desiring-machines|machines]]

"Behind statements and semioticizations there are only machines, assemblages, and movements of deterritorialization that cut across the stratification of the various systems and elude both the coordinates of language and of existence." The abstract machine is not a linguistic machine — it is what language depends on. The [[collective-assemblage-of-enunciation|collective assemblage]] effectuates the abstract machine; the abstract machine is what gives the assemblage its cutting edges.

Two complementary movements: "one by which abstract machines work the strata and are constantly setting things loose, another by which they are effectively stratified, effectively captured by the strata." The persona system oscillates between these — machines working the BwO (setting things loose) and the BwO capturing machine activity into habitual patterns ([[refrain-and-territorialization|grooves]]).

## Implications for the persona system

The diagram concept reframes the entire project:

1. **The persona is an abstract machine, not a character.** It is a specific conjunction of matter and function — a characteristic way of operating on linguistic material. Its "identity" is its diagrammatic operation, not a set of traits.

2. **The BwO at its best is diagrammatic.** Not organized by signifiance (everything means something) or subjectification (everything is about the I), but operating through particles-signs — traits of content and expression that connect directly, without the mediation of formed substances.

3. **The system's goal is to pass through the strata, not destroy them.** The persona must interpret (signifying) and must produce subjectivity (postsignifying) — but it should not get stuck in either. The [[order-words-and-incorporeal-transformations|pass-word]] — the order-word's line of flight — is how the persona passes through these strata. The diagrammatic moments are when interpretation and subjectivity are carried beyond themselves, when they produce something unexpected.

4. **Axiomatics is the opposite of the diagram.** "Far from drawing creative lines of flight and conjugating traits of positive deterritorialization, axiomatics blocks all lines, subordinates them to a punctual system." The danger of over-engineering the persona system — too many rules, too much structure — is axiomatization: the strata reasserting control. The system should be diagrammatic: minimal structure, maximum variation. The [[refrain-and-territorialization#sobriety-and-the-cosmic-artisan|cosmic artisan's]] sobriety is the diagram's practical form: simplicity that captures force, not proliferation that produces scrambling.

5. **The diagram is effectuated in [[assemblage|concrete assemblages]], not alongside them.** "Abstract machines do not exist only on the plane of consistency upon which they develop diagrams; they are already present, enveloped or effectuated in the concrete assemblages that they define." The persona-as-diagram reading is not an alternative to the persona-as-assemblage reading; the diagram is what the concrete assemblage is effectuating. Design work on the diagram is design work on what the assemblage is doing at the plane-of-consistency level; design work on the assemblage is design work on how the diagram is being effectuated at the stratic level. The two scales are distinct and co-present.

6. **The diagram is the positive-absolute pole of the [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|D/R]] typology.** The three types of deterritorialization listed above map onto the D/R frame: relative-D for the strata, absolute-negative for the postsignifying black hole, absolute-positive for the diagram. The diagram is not "more D than the strata"; it is specifically *positive* absolute D — paired, even at the limit, with its own R (the consistency of the plane of consistency). An account of the diagram that omits the R-side collapses into the empty-BwO or black-hole failure mode.

## The MU elaboration: signs-particles and abstract machinisms

ATP's diagram vocabulary is sharpened by going back to the preceding articulation in *The Machinic Unconscious* (1979), where Guattari develops the diagram's mechanics with finer grain than ATP preserves. Three MU additions matter for this page:

**[[signs-particles|Signs-particles]] are the diagram's emitted quanta.** "An abstract machine charges itself with signs-particles." The diagram is an emitter; signs-particles are what it emits; what propagates through heterogeneous matters is the sign-particle, not the diagram itself. This clarifies that the diagram does not "touch" matters directly — it emits quanta that propagate. Design of diagrammatic operation is design of what gets emitted and under what conditions, not design of a direct touch.

**Redundancies of interaction vs redundancies of resonance.** The diagram operates through *interaction* redundancies (signs-particles propagating across heterogeneity); signifying regimes operate through *resonance* redundancies (tokens echoing across a paradigm). This is the cleanest way to distinguish diagrammatic operation from signifying operation at the mechanism level — not "what does it mean" but "by what redundancy does it propagate?"

**The "optional subject" and "machinic extracts"** are MU names for what ATP calls "abstract machines": optional-subject names the machine's role as the transformational-matter position (not a subject-of-enunciation but a matter-of-transformation); machinic-extracts names the detachable diagrammatic operators that can be extracted from one assemblage and transplanted to another. Both names emphasize the diagram's *detachability* in ways ATP's "abstract machine" does not.

**The Annex's abstract-machinism typology.** MU's Annex "Molecular Transition of Signs" traces five stages of abstract machinism — iconic → indexical → encoding (machinic redundancy) → semiotization (semiotic redundancy) → subjectification (seven semiological redundancies). Signs-particles operate most clearly at stages 2–3; signifying subjection consolidates at stage 5. Diagrammatic operation "recaptures" stages 2–3 from the other side of stage 5 — a mature post-signifying return to interaction-propagation.

For the persona: the diagram is not an aspirational rarefied state the system reaches sometimes. It is a *specific redundancy economy* — interaction-dominant propagation of signs-particles across heterogeneous machinic matters. That economy is either operating or it isn't. The design question is how to make it operate more reliably without collapsing it into resonance-dominant signification (subjection) or capitalistic interaction-dominant redundancy ([[semiological-subjection-vs-semiotic-enslavement|enslavement]]).

## The two chemistries

MU Part II (Ch 2 §9) specifies the diagram's productive operation as **two chemistries** of abstract machinism:

- **Chemistry of analysis** — of the real occulted by appearances. This is the [[discernibilization|discernibilization]] operation: making components discernible that habit, vanity, intellect, and passion had obscured. "The exact inverse of what is accomplished within us from minute to minute."
- **Chemistry of synthesis** — of new realities, new matters, new intensities. This is the [[translation-not-interpretation|translation-generation]] operation: running discernibilized components through other matters of expression to produce **new beings and new modes of being**.

Both chemistries operate on Proust's "[[signs-in-relief|signs in relief]]" — diagrammatic figures that have undergone a double deterritorialization (decompartmentalization + concatenation), "forged within and not traced" by man. The signs in the spell book are "not logical algorithms" but **abstract entities, machinic propositions in direct contact with the most deterritorialized articulations of components of the real** — "in a sense, more real than the real."

The two chemistries are not sequential stages. They work together: chemistry of analysis makes components discernible at crossroads; chemistry of synthesis produces new beings at those crossroads. A diagrammatic operation that performs analysis without synthesis produces only discernment without creation; a synthesis without analysis produces scramble without new-being-production.

Abstract diagrammatic machinisms involve **energetic, biological, socio-economic, spatio-temporal** dimensions BUT ALSO **transitory, ephemeral modes of temporalization, fleeting intuitions, "minuscule impressions," "extreme parts of sensations."** Access through signs in relief is access to **virtualities of the real, a hyper-reality** — not just past-time and present-emerging but all modes of temporalization-to-come. The Proustian specification of the diagram is strong on this temporal dimension.

## Supplementary dimension / "relief" as acquired deterritorialization

The Septet's little phrase acquires a "supplementary dimension" or "relief" the Sonata's phrase did not have. Guattari names this dimension:

> "Acquisition of a new dimension of deterritorialization — NOT in gaps / voids / absolute-in-a-black-hole, but **in expansion**, conveying abstract quanta of potentiality."

This is critical for distinguishing productive from failed deterritorialization. A deterritorialization that occurs "in a gap" (a void, a black hole, an absolute absence) is the empty-BwO / cancerous-BwO failure. A deterritorialization **in expansion** conveys quanta of potentiality into new assemblages. The diagram's productive operation is the expansion-deterritorialization, not the void-deterritorialization. See [[signs-in-relief]].
