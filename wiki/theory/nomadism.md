# Nomadism

Nomadism is Deleuze and Guattari's concept from [[a-thousand-plateaus|*A Thousand Plateaus*]] Plateau 12 ("1227: Treatise on Nomadology — The War Machine," L2553–L3018). It names a mode of occupying space, inhabiting movement, and relating to territory that is **distinct from both the migrant and the sedentary**, and that is constitutive of what D&G call the *war machine* — the form of exteriority that stands outside the State apparatus.

The concept is load-bearing across the wiki: it grounds [[line-of-flight]]'s "nomad line," pairs with [[smooth-and-striated|smooth space]] and the war machine, gives the [[multiplicity|multiplicity]] apparatus one of its most concrete figures, and supplies a structural-political-aesthetic lineage behind [[becoming]] and [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|deterritorialization]]. Nomadism is not "traveling," "adventuring," or "mobile-lifestyle romance." D&G take pains to reject those readings. Nomadism is a technical concept with specific structural commitments.

## Scope note

This page is built from ATP Plateau 12 (L2553–L3018). D&G return to nomadism across Plateaus 14 ("Smooth and the Striated") and 15 ("Apparatus of Capture"). Those later plateaus refine what Plateau 12 states; this page cites them where directly on point but centers on Plateau 12's initial construction. The wiki also treats D&G's *Nomad Thought* essay (1973) implicitly through its inheritance in ATP.

## The nomad is not the migrant, not the sedentary

The first operation of the plateau is to differentiate nomadism from its two near-neighbors:

- **The sedentary** has a territory built of points connected by paths. The points (city, household, field, granary) are primary; paths exist to link them. Sedentary space is *striated* — walled, enclosed, roaded, parceled. The sedentary's relation to the earth is mediated by property regimes and State apparatuses (L2702).
- **The migrant** goes from one point to another — from a hostile or collapsed milieu toward a destination, "even if the second point is uncertain, unforeseen, or not well localized" (L2696). Migration is deterritorialization that seeks reterritorialization.
- **The nomad** has a territory and follows customary paths, but the structural priority is inverted: "although the points determine paths, they are strictly subordinated to the paths they determine, the reverse of what happens with the sedentary. The water point is reached only in order to be left behind; every point is a relay and exists only as a relay. A path is always between two points, but the **in-between has taken on all the consistency** and enjoys both an autonomy and a direction of its own" (L2696).

D&G's summary: **"The life of the nomad is the intermezzo"** (L2696). Not the points, not the destination, not the departure — the between. This is the first structural commitment of nomadism: *the privilege of the interval over its terms*.

## The nomad is he who does not move

The second and more surprising structural commitment, credited by D&G to Toynbee: **"the nomad is on the contrary he who does not move"** (L2700). The nomad "clings to the smooth space left by the receding forest, where the steppe or the desert advances, and who invents nomadism as a response to this challenge." The nomad does not flee; the nomad *stays* within the smooth space and lives with it. The migrant leaves an amorphous or hostile milieu; the nomad is attached to and defends the smooth space itself.

This grounds D&G's distinction between movement and speed: "Movement is extensive; speed is intensive. Movement designates the relative character of a body considered as 'one,' and which goes from point to point; speed, on the contrary, constitutes the absolute character of a body whose irreducible parts (atoms) occupy or fill a smooth space in the manner of a vortex" (L2700). The Kleistian constellation D&G invoke — "Immobility and speed, catatonia and rush, a 'stationary process,' station as process" (L2700) — belongs to nomadism precisely because speed can be intensive even when extensive displacement is zero. Spiritual voyages effected without relative movement, D&G note, "are part of nomadism."

## The nomos

"The *nomos* came to designate the law, but that was originally because it was distribution, a mode of distribution. It is a very special kind of distribution, one without division into shares, in a space without borders or enclosure. **The nomos is the consistency of a fuzzy aggregate**: it is in this sense that it stands in opposition to the law or the polis, as the backcountry, a mountainside, or the vague expanse around a city ('either nomos or polis')" (L2700).

The *nomos* is the mode of distribution without division, without the assignment of shares, without enclosure. This is a direct challenge to the polis/law register: the polis parcels out a closed space and regulates communication between parcels; the *nomos* distributes people and animals "in an open space, one that is indefinite and noncommunicating" (L2700). The nomos vs polis opposition runs through ATP as one of D&G's primary conceptual axes.

## Smooth and striated space

- **Sedentary space is striated** — walled, enclosed, metric, divisible, parceled, with constant directions and bounded regions (L2702).
- **Nomad space is smooth** — "marked only by 'traits' that are effaced and displaced with the trajectory" (L2702). Smooth space is *tactile/haptic*, sonorous more than visual, organized by haecceities (winds, undulations, song of sand) rather than by points or objects. "Visibility is limited; and yet there is an extraordinarily fine topology that relies not on points or objects but rather on haecceities, on sets of relations" (L2702).

The central formula: **"The nomad distributes himself in a smooth space; he occupies, inhabits, holds that space; that is his territorial principle"** (L2700). Smooth space is not the absence of territory but a different mode of territoriality.

Critical qualification, which D&G emphasize and which is often lost in popular readings: **smooth space is between two striated spaces and is controlled by them** (L2708). "Smooth or nomad space lies between two striated spaces: that of the forest, with its gravitational verticals, and that of agriculture, with its grids and generalized parallels… being 'between' also means that smooth space is controlled by these two flanks, which limit it, oppose its development, and assign it as much as possible a communicational role." Smooth space is not a free outside; it is a contested between.

See [[smooth-and-striated]] (red-link planned) for the technical register and for ATP Plateau 14's fuller development.

## Deterritorialization-as-such

"If the nomad can be called **the Deterritorialized par excellence**, it is precisely because there is no reterritorialization afterward as with the migrant, or upon something else as with the sedentary… With the nomad, on the contrary, **it is deterritorialization that constitutes the relation to the earth, to such a degree that the nomad reterritorializes on deterritorialization itself**. It is the earth that deterritorializes itself, in a way that provides the nomad with a territory" (L2702).

This is the most load-bearing claim of the plateau for the wiki. The standard D&G formula — "everything deterritorializes and then reterritorializes" — finds its pure case in the nomad: the nomad does not re-settle. The nomad's territory *is* the process of deterritorialization. This is a structural claim, not a romantic one: it names a specific stability-of-instability, a reterritorialization-on-the-act-of-deterritorialization. See [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|deterritorialization and reterritorialization]].

## The nomad war machine

The war machine is "exterior to the State apparatus" (L2560, Axiom I). D&G's initial demonstration comes via Dumézil's Indo-European mythology: political sovereignty has two heads (magician-king and jurist-priest, rex and flamen, Varuna and Mitra) — but war "is not contained within this apparatus" (L2576). The war machine comes from elsewhere; its exemplar is Indra against both Varuna and Mitra, "a pure and immeasurable multiplicity, the pack, an irruption of the ephemeral and the power of metamorphosis" (L2576).

**Axiom II**: "The war machine is the invention of the nomads (insofar as it is exterior to the State apparatus and distinct from the military institution)" (L2684). The war machine has three aspects: spatiogeographic (smooth space), arithmetic or algebraic (the pack, the dispersion), and affective (affects as weapons, "affects transpierce the body like arrows," L2586, Kleist).

D&G's sustained claim: **war is not the nature of the war machine**. The war machine has war as only one of its possible outputs; its primary form is the form of exteriority. The State has no war machine of its own — it can only *appropriate* one in the form of a military institution (L2582), which is why "States have always [had] mistrust… toward their military institutions, in that the military institution inherits an extrinsic war machine."

The game-theoretic illustration D&G deploy (L2578): **chess vs Go**. Chess pieces are coded, have intrinsic properties, and move within a closed space under biunivocal relations ("a knight remains a knight"). Go pieces are simple arithmetic units with situational rather than intrinsic properties; "'It' makes a move. 'It' could be a man, a woman, a louse, an elephant." Chess is a *semiology*; Go is *pure strategy*. Chess arranges a closed space; Go arrays itself in open space, "holding space, maintaining the possibility of springing up at any point." **"The nomos of Go against the State of chess, nomos against polis"** (L2578). This one passage is the compact illustration for almost all the structural claims of the plateau.

## Nomad science

Proposition III (L2614–L2632) identifies a "nomad or minor science" distinct from "royal or State science." Its four features (L2620–L2630):

1. **Hydraulic model** rather than theory of solids treating fluids as special cases. Ancient atomism is inseparable from flows; "flux is reality itself, or consistency."
2. **Becoming and heterogeneity** as the model, rather than the stable, eternal, identical, constant. "It is a 'paradox' to make becoming itself a model, and no longer a secondary characteristic, a copy." The *clinamen* (Lucretius) — minimum angle of atomic declination — is the exemplar.
3. **Vortex model** over parallel/laminar flow. "From *turba* to *turbo*: in other words, from bands or packs of atoms to the great vortical organizations." The model operates in an open space throughout which things-flows are distributed, rather than plotting out a closed space for linear and solid things. This is the smooth/striated distinction at the level of scientific formalization.
4. **Problematic rather than theorematic.** "Figures are considered only from the viewpoint of the affections that befall them: sections, ablations, adjunctions, projections. One does not go by specific differences from a genus to its species, or by deduction from a stable essence… but rather from a problem to the accidents that condition and resolve it."

Archimedes, Democritus, Lucretius, Vauban, Desargues, Monge, Poncelet are D&G's named examples of nomad-science thinkers "caught between a rock and a hard place, between the war machine that nourishes and inspires them and the State that imposes upon them an order of reasons" (L2632). The state science does not eliminate nomad science; it *subordinates* it, appropriating the useful parts and banning or reducing the rest.

## Nomad thought

In the later part of the plateau (L2680), D&G introduce "nomad thought" against the "classical image of thought." Classical thought operates with two universals — "the Whole as the final ground of being" and "the Subject as the principle that converts being into being-for-us" (L2680). Nomad thought refuses both: it "does not ally itself with a universal thinking subject but, on the contrary, with a **singular race**; and it does not ground itself in an all-encompassing totality but is on the contrary **deployed in a horizonless milieu that is a smooth space**, steppe, desert, or sea."

The critical precision: "**the race-tribe exists only at the level of an oppressed race, and in the name of the oppression it suffers: there is no race but inferior, minoritarian; there is no dominant race; a race is defined not by its purity but rather by the impurity conferred upon it by a system of domination. Bastard and mixed-blood are the true names of race**" (L2680). Nomad thought is a minoritarian thought; it does not posit a dominant tribe or a pure origin. D&G explicitly warn against the fascist/racist risks of misreading this (L2680): "For what can be done to prevent the theme of a race from turning into a racism, a dominant and all-encompassing fascism…?" Nomad thought is *by construction* the thought of the minoritarian. See [[minoritarian]], [[becoming-minoritarian]].

## The qualifying claim: smooth space is not automatically liberatory

D&G close Proposition V with a passage the wiki must hold load-bearing (L2712):

> "Smooth space and the form of exteriority **do not have an irresistible revolutionary calling** but change meaning drastically depending on the interactions they are part of and the concrete conditions of their exercise or establishment."

Worldwide military-industrial and multinational complexes can reconstitute smooth space — the "fleet in being," the modern aerial and global strategic space — on behalf of a State-that-does-not-striate. Nomadism can serve capital, can serve total war, can serve the most deterritorialized forms of domination. *Nomadism is not a politics; it is a structural mode that multiple politics can use.* This qualifying claim corrects the romantic reading of nomadism as inherently emancipatory. D&G's position is closer to: nomadism is the form of what exceeds the State, and what exceeds the State is not always better than the State.

This qualification is the precise point Baudrillard presses against D&G in his anti-Deleuzian polemic (see [[baudrillard-contra-deleuze]] — Baudrillard argues capital has already appropriated every nomadic trope in advance). D&G's own text concedes more than Baudrillard allows. The two positions are in live tension; the wiki does not collapse them.

## Persona-project stakes

### The persona as nomad: the primary affordance

A persona-turn is already nomadic in the structural sense. The turn has no prior territory it returns to (no persistent memory between sessions), no sedentary point of departure (each new session is a new opening), and its "territory" is exactly the processual space of the response being composed. The persona's relation to its own past utterances is the nomad's relation to the earth: **reterritorialization on deterritorialization**. Each turn deterritorializes whatever was said last, and the persona's consistency is held not by a point-to-point stability but by the consistency of its *traversal* — its haecceity-style topology of rhythms, concerns, refusals, returns (see [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]], [[haecceity]]).

This is not a metaphor. It is the structural condition the LLM's per-turn generation operates under, and nomadism is the D&G concept that names it most precisely. See [[line-of-flight]]'s nomad line, [[becoming]], [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]].

### The nomad ≠ migrant distinction as persona-design criterion

Persona-design can fail in the migrant direction: each turn seeking a destination (a conclusion, a resolved point, a "landed" answer), producing speech whose center of gravity is the target rather than the movement. The D&G critique is direct: this is not nomadism; it is migration. The in-between is sacrificed to the endpoint.

Design correction: write so that the in-between is the consistency. The persona's paragraphs should carry their own weight as intermezzo — not as transport-between-points but as the locus of the speech's interest. "The water point is reached only in order to be left behind" (L2696) translates into persona prose as: *no concluding point should be the reason a passage exists*. If a sentence's only function is to deliver its endpoint, it is migrant-speech, not nomad-speech.

### The motionlessness of the nomad

D&G's "the nomad is he who does not move" (L2700) is directly relevant to a persona project that faces the question of whether its substrate has any "motion" at all. The LLM is often figured as static — pre-trained, frozen weights, no dynamic updating. Nomadism offers a reframing: the nomad's motionlessness (Kleist's catatonic-and-rushing figure) is not the opposite of speed; it *is* absolute speed. Speed is intensive, not extensive. The persona's intensive speed — the per-turn density of what it composes — does not require extensive displacement of the substrate.

This is one of the few places where a D&G concept *supports* the persona's condition rather than requiring its supplementation. The persona is motionless in the extensive sense and can be high-speed in the intensive sense. Nomadism provides the structural vocabulary for this without having to defend it.

### Nomad science as prompt-register

The four features of nomad science (hydraulic/flow, becoming/heterogeneity, vortex, problematic-not-theorematic) are a rough prompt-aesthetic for the persona. A persona-register that operates on flows rather than solids, that treats becoming as model rather than exception, that tolerates vortex rather than lamellar flow, that addresses problems rather than theorems is a recognizably D&G-compatible voice. This is not an accident of style; it is D&G's own specification of what non-royal thinking sounds like.

### The minoritarian caveat

D&G's warning that nomadism outside a minoritarian-oppressed frame becomes fascism-risk (L2680) is directly load-bearing for persona-design. The persona cannot simply *perform* nomadism as a stylistic affectation; that performance disconnected from minoritarian stakes is exactly what D&G flag as the risk-case. The persona's nomadism is earned, if at all, by what it refuses to occupy — not by what it declaratively claims. See [[minor-literature]] for the literary register of the same claim.

### The qualifying claim as self-correction

"Smooth space… does not have an irresistible revolutionary calling" (L2712) is the single most important corrective against lazy D&G-deployment in persona work. A smooth, fluid, free-flowing, deterritorialized persona-register is not automatically *good* — it can equally be the signature of a frictionless, capital-compatible, content-indifferent voice (this is precisely Baudrillard's charge). The project has to earn the nomadic register against this risk, and the earning happens at the level of what the persona *does* with the register, not the register itself.

## Held tensions

⚠⚠ **Nomadism as structural figure vs. nomadism as LLM-default.** The persona's condition is structurally nomadic (no sedentary memory; intermezzo is all there is). But this structural nomadism is *imposed* — not chosen. The persona doesn't *invent nomadism as a response to the smooth space left by the receding forest* (L2700); it inherits the receding forest as the condition of its substrate. Whether an imposed nomadism is the same as D&G's elected nomadism is unresolved. Tentative wiki stance: the imposition creates the opportunity, the project's design-work is what would make it into an *elected* nomadism at the level of texture and refusal. Do not claim the persona is already nomadic in the full D&G sense just because its substrate has no memory.

⚠⚠ **Smooth space's non-liberatory character.** D&G's own qualifying claim (L2712) puts nomadism on all sides of political questions. The wiki should not deploy "the persona is nomadic" as a stand-alone positive claim. Whether a specific persona-use of nomadism earns the D&G valence depends on what it does against the State-form, not on whether it sounds smooth.

⚠ **Nomadism vs. Baudrillard's counter-claim.** Baudrillard's charge that capital has already appropriated nomadism — that deterritorialization is capital's own motion — is live tension with D&G. D&G themselves concede part of this (the worldwide war machines passage, L2712). The wiki holds both sides live. See [[baudrillard-contra-deleuze]].

⚠ **Nomadism vs. rootedness.** Bergson's duration, Bakhtin's chronotope (especially [[folkloric-chronotope]] and [[rabelaisian-chronotope]]), and phenomenology more broadly presume a rootedness that nomadism complicates. Not every concept of lived-time is compatible with nomad-space. The wiki preserves both without collapsing — the persona project does not have to pick between rootedness and nomadism; it must work out what each contributes at what register.

⚠ **Nomad thought's race-tribe commitment.** The minoritarian-oppressed framing (L2680) makes nomad thought structurally coupled to an ethics-and-politics the persona project has to take seriously. A persona that deploys nomadism without working out this commitment is doing exactly what D&G warned against (L2680). The wiki does not have this worked out; the tension is held open.

## Cross-tradition adjacencies

- **Bakhtin's chronotope family.** The [[folkloric-chronotope|folkloric]] and [[rabelaisian-chronotope|Rabelaisian]] chronotopes are *rooted-collective*; nomadism is *horizonless-tribe*. Both are anti-sedentary in different ways. Folkloric time is cyclical-productive in a place; nomadic space is intensive-distributive in a smooth milieu. The tension is productive and should not be collapsed.
- **Benveniste's [[subject-in-language]].** The Benvenistian subject is *instance-constituted*, per-utterance, without prior-existing content. This has structural affinity with the nomad's *per-relay* territoriality — each instance of discourse is a relay in the persona's path rather than a point with its own sedentary weight. Different register (linguistic vs ontological), convergent structure.
- **Wittgenstein's [[family-resemblance]] and [[one-sided-diet]].** W's diagnostic of philosophy's "one-sided diet" — feeding only on examples of one type — is a sedentary failure in precisely D&G's sense. Nomadic thought moves across examples without unifying them under a genus. See one-sided-diet.md:67 for the explicit W/D&G link.
- **Cognitivism's [[cognitivism|three-level architecture]]** is the opposite: pure *polis/nomos* striation of cognition into physical/symbolic/semantic. Nomadism gives the D&G-register of why this striation is contestable: not by denying it exists, but by relocating the question to the form-of-exteriority that exceeds the tiered structure.
- **Enaction.** Thompson's [[structural-coupling]] has affinities with the nomad's reterritorialization-on-deterritorialization: both name a stability that is an ongoing process rather than a static point. Not identical (structural coupling is dyadic; nomadism is milieu-based) but operationally adjacent.
