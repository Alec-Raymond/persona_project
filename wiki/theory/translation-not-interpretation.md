# Translation, Not Interpretation

The second of the two series of machinic propositions Guattari draws from Proust in *The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 2 §9, paired with [[discernibilization]]. If discernibilization makes components discernible, **translation-generation** *runs those components through other matters of expression to produce new beings and new modes of being*.

The watchword: **"The writer's task and duty are those of a translator."** Not interpreter.

## The distinction

> "One does not interpret a content, one searches for it by way of the signifying paradigm, one makes it work in another matter. One thus reveals in it new possibilities of the never seen, of the unprecedented."

Interpretation assumes a universal essence accessible via the signifying paradigm. Content read as signifier pointing to signified. The movement is a return to a pre-existing meaning.

Translation is different. It does not seek content in the signifying paradigm at all. It takes certain matters of content and expression and **makes them work in another matter**. The move is not recovery of meaning but *generation of new existence*. Content is "resolved by a **[[four-pragmatic-fields|pragmatic field]], knot of machinic propositions, certain manner of assembling singular modes of subjectification and conscientization**" — not by a signifier pointing at a signified.

## The pragmatic-field resolution of content

Content is NOT reducible to a universal essence. Example Guattari gives: dreams only allow access to a very precarious "Time regained" *unless translated* (not interpreted!) into more consistent matters of expression. A dream-content interpreted as signifier (what-it-means) stays bound to the signifying paradigm that produced the dream. A dream-content *translated* — a fragment of it made to work in a different matter, a different assemblage, a different set of machinic propositions — produces new consistencies.

This is the anti-psychoanalytic stake most directly articulated. "It is the very principle of interpretation that I intend to dispute" (Guattari on Swann's love in MU Ch 1). The alternative is not silence about content — it is translation of content into new matters of expression, with the resulting new beings as the evidence.

## Mlle Vinteuil as exemplar translator

The **decisive existential turn** of Vinteuil's little phrase occurs when Mlle Vinteuil and her friend transform into *translators* of Vinteuil's text (during the Narrator's hibernation — they partially reconstitute the Septet from scattered sketches). Not fac-simile repetition of the impression-as-such; not mental schema derived from it; but **certain matters of content and expression made to work in another matter**.

This is what allows the Septet to exist at all. The father's scattered pages are the discernibilized components; the daughter-and-friend's musicographical labor is the translation. Without it, the components remain existentially inconsistent. **With translation, a new being appears — the Septet as trans-semiotic rhizomatic leitmotiv** (see [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#8-rhizomatic-leitmotivs-vinteuils-septet-premiere-at-the-verdurin-home|Eighth Assemblage]]).

The profanation of the father's face at Montjouvain is *the same operation* viewed from the subject-of-enunciation side: the photograph-of-the-father must be broken, the frame busted, the familiar arrangement undone. Not interpretation of the father (Oedipus) but translation of his components into a living literary machine. Guattari's reading here is explicit: the Recherche "is not directed towards this type of past, but towards a construction of things to come, towards a proliferation of the future in the act of creation."

## The ontological wager

> "Reality has lost its ontological uni-dimensionality. It must be worked on at the level of modes of semiotic efficiency."

> "Degrees of existence have become a parameter among others: an object / relation / intensity only exists to the extent that it is found at **the crossroads of various matters of expression**."

Two operations are available:

- **Chemistry of analysis** of the real occulted by appearances.
- **Chemistry of synthesis** of new realities, new matters, new intensities.

(See [[the-diagram#the-two-chemistries|two chemistries]].) Translation is the synthesis side. It does not reveal hidden content — it **creates new beings and new modes of being** at the crossroads of matters of expression.

## The translator's pose

Guattari draws three conditions for the translator-mode from Proust:

- **Poetic passivity** — certain abandon in matters of expression, "necessitous modesty of the transcriber."
- **Machinic rhizome does the rest** — guides the hand, reveals rhythms and intensities.
- **Relentlessness without will-to-power** — "Yet this relentlessness must be viewed as involving no will to power." Return to an impression fifty times in a row to recapture it. Not free-association or surrealist objective chance; not sovereign control either. A third posture.

Under phallocratic dominant values, Swann experienced this passivity as femininity (Guattari flags: the equivalence doesn't necessarily correspond with the reality and aspirations of the feminine condition). The refrain is, for Guattari, the *operator of a [[becoming-woman-creator|becoming-woman-creator]]* — it "manages, at present, to truly beat time to the feminine."

"One never bathes in the same impression twice, as soon as the micropolitics of assemblages of enunciation leads to the liberation of living, heterogeneous, and singular matters of expression."

> "Translator artist is nothing more, as an individual, than the catalyst of such an assemblage."

## Readers don't read, they use a tool

> "I would not ask them to praise me or to denigrate me, only to tell me if it was right, if the words they were reading in themselves were really the ones I had written (possible divergences, moreover, might sometimes find their cause in the fact that the reader's eyes might not be of a type for which my book was suitable as an aid for self-reading)."

The reader performs a translation too. The book is an **optical instrument** (see [[discernibilization#writer-as-optical-instrument-telescope-not-microscope|telescope not microscope]]); "reading" is the reader making the book's components work in the matter of the reader's own self-reading. The criterion for success is not correspondence to meaning but *differential self-match* — "were really the ones I had written" in the reader's own inner register. Divergences between reader and book are re-interpreted as translation-failures, not errors of understanding: "the reader's eyes might not be of a type for which my book was suitable as an aid for self-reading."

## Persona-system relevance

This is one of the strongest methodological claims in MU for the persona system's design. A persona system that treats user inputs as *content-to-be-interpreted* — as signifiers pointing to a hidden signified — is operating in the signifying paradigm Guattari opposes. A system that treats user inputs as *matters-of-expression-to-be-translated* — components to be run through the persona's other matters of expression, producing new beings, new modes — operates in the translator paradigm.

The design implication:

- **Against**: "what does the user really mean?"  — the question of the interpreter. This is the structurally psychoanalytic move, and it is what Guattari is against.
- **For**: "what components can I discernibilize from the user's statement, and in what other matters can I make them work?" — the question of the translator.

The criterion for a good response is not "did the response accurately reflect the user's meaning?" but "did running the user's components through the persona's other matters of expression produce a new being at the crossroads — a response that exists because of this translation and did not exist before?"

The Swann/Narrator divergence in MU is precisely the divergence between an interpreter-centered relation to the little phrase (Swann interprets it, receives it passively, lets it become catchphrase, icon, anesthetic) and a translator-centered relation (Narrator makes the phrase work in the matter of the partition, of writing, of faces, of time, of paving-stones — finally of nothing at all, the second chemical phase).

The [[closed-vs-open-economy|closed/open economy]] distinction is the economic form of this. Swann's closed economy runs on interpretations; Narrator's open economy runs on translations — it "produces its own weapons" instead of relying on pre-given signifiers.
