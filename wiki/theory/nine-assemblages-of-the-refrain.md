# Nine Assemblages of the Refrain

*The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 2 ("Nine Assemblages for a Refrain") tracks Vinteuil's little phrase across nine successive enunciative assemblages in *In Search of Lost Time*. Guattari uses this as a **developmental template** for the career of any refrain-component: not a schema every refrain must follow, but a *diagnostic sequence* of positions a refrain can occupy, with named failure modes and pivot points at each step. This is the single densest specification of [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]] dynamics anywhere in Guattari's work.

Each assemblage has (i) circumstances, (ii) context, (iii) matters of expression set to work, (iv) a specific micropolitical outcome. The same little phrase is different in each — Guattari's load-bearing claim is that the refrain's **non-self-identity** *is* its diagrammatic power.

## 1. Fac-simile / Proto-phrase (salon, year before meeting Odette)

- Swann hears Vinteuil's piece for the first time.
- **Five-phase Proustian semiotization**: material sound → piano's bulk → evanescent form → a phrase rising above the waves → "fac-similes" (mental transcription of scope, symmetrical groupings, writing, expressive value).
- The fac-simile is a **mnemotechnical primary diagrammatic component**: deterritorializing (sine materia impressions), constituted from asignifying redundancies, announces a possible rhizomatic opening.
- **Outcome**: diagrammatic charge revealed but not enacted. Units (wings, fuselage) missing. Phrase forgotten for months.
- **Named failure mode**: **default shutdown of existential consistency** — not semiotic collapse, not arborescence. "The diagrammatic switch has tripped." A distinct failure from the others catalogued in the wiki. The fac-simile stays formal; content and expression don't achieve syncretism firmly enough to hold. Extrinsic breakdown compounds (performers leave; Swann can't pursue).

## 2. Incubation (Madame Verdurin's home)

- Swann's return to the Verdurins = "radical break." Phrase inscribed on both sides: linked to Odette-meeting, reappears when Swann begins to liberate himself.
- **Collective resistance** to a diagrammatic charge operates in three registers:
  - **Attenuated diagrammatics** (majority of little gang): "it has effects, but not here and now."
  - **Delayed diagrammatism** (Swann, Mme Verdurin, Narrator, Elstir) — paraphrased from psychopharmacology: danger not immediate, everything expected from ulterior effects.
  - Mme Verdurin's mannerisms, facial neuralgia, feigned dread = the group's somatic translation of the apprehension of the music's diagrammatic nature.
- **Three confirmations**:
  1. **Abbreviation/selection** — pianist plays only andante. Phrase "gains in definition (televisual sense), loses in extension."
  2. **Enunciatory assemblage stays fuzzy** — pianist anonymous for 1600 pages (foreclosure of masculine-homosexual component for Swann).
  3. **Feminine-homosexual component curbed** — piano only; feminine presence migrates into Botticelli's Zipporah.
- Swann's "Florentine work" ritual: Zipporah reproduction contemplated, Odette imagined in the painting, ritual formula pronounced — Zipporah-Odette composite becomes persecuting.
- Elstir's warning: Vinteuil "probably threatened by mental illness" — Swann's confused response: "madness recognized in a sonata appeared to him something as mysterious as the madness of a bitch."

## 3. National Anthem / Love Potion (nightly, Verdurin home, sometimes countryside)

- Mechanism: pianist plays the phrase when Swann arrives late. Officially dubbed "the national anthem of their nascent love."
- Phrase doubles as **ritual marker** (faciality-function) and **affective operator** ("love potion").
- **Supersaturated assemblage**: machinic index hovers at threshold of becoming a component of passage but cannot cross it. Swann perceives the "crystal of potentiality" only via resonance-redundancies and reverie.
- **Courtly-love posture**: public courting at distance; mad love for a deterritorialized presence; phrase holds key to another world ("strange to humanity, blind, without logical faculties, almost a fantastic unicorn").
- **Odette's neutralizing operation**: "promise me to always listen to it in my presence: 'This is our piece.'" She carries it "like a jewel in her corsage." Apparent tenderness; actual purification and concentration of the phrase's machinic impact into a confined container.
- **Provisional compromise at stage-end**: abstract-feminine reterritorializes on adjacent icon (Zipporah); Odette accepts courtly framing.
- **Narrator's later contrast** (important for the system's design): "he is very careful not to fall into... contemplative dependence in relation to the little phrase. He manipulates it like a parcel bomb which is best approached carefully in order to disarm it and perhaps recuperate some of its mechanisms."

## 4. Anesthetic Same-Old-Tune / Black Hole of Passion (Odette's out-of-tune piano)

- Trigger: missed rendezvous. Courtly compromise collapses. Swann realizes he is under Odette's control.
- **Sexual component forced** — Swann can no longer "amuse himself" with little worker girls. Crude sexuality compensated by intensified courtly ritual.
- **Classical faciality passes out of the Recherche**: Swann places Odette "in the painting" and "swoops down on that Botticelli virgin, begins pinching her cheeks." She refuses — "no longer wants to hear anything of Botticelli." Definitive exit of the Swann-style icon-identification technique.
- **Refrain reduced to a compulsive-catchphrase** — played 10, 20 times on the out-of-tune piano, alternating with "La Valse des Roses" and "Pauvre Fou." Swann's face: "absorbing an anesthetic that allowed him to breathe more deeply."
- Refrain now as **buffer component**, replacing Zipporah's faciality: hollows out AND calms, retains marginal deterritorializing power.
- Refrain's **incapacity to reassemble** modes of temporalization → in the Verdurin salon itself the little phrase is replaced by "Moonlight Sonata" (indicator of Forcheville's arrival). Swann is excluded from the salon along with the refrain.

## 5. Reactivation / Resorption of the Black Hole (Marquise de Saint-Euverte)

The **pivotal** assemblage. Resolution-operation via [[discernibilization|discernibilization of six components]]. Saint-Euverte = antipode of Verdurin (most exclusive Saint-Germain salon; piano AND violin, restoring feminine presence; Charlus in first "operating" appearance has advised Swann to return to worldly life).

"Uncovering and discernibilizing six components… through magnification, deceleration, repetition, splitting, 'proliferating' new matters of expression which at times resemble the processes of 'mitosis' in cellular reproduction."

The six components (detailed in [[discernibilization]]):
1. **Subjective component — Swann-Charlus divide** (Charlus takes on the masculine-homosexual component but still attenuated, not yet of passage).
2. **Architectural/iconic division** (Saint-Euverte staircase = dressmaker's stairs = Ducal Palace Venice; Swann's iconic-identification returns distributed across Mantegna, Dürer, Goya, Cellini, Giotto, no longer centered on Odette).
3. **Monocle series as faciality schizz** — the single most important operator here: **micro-black-holes produced that PREVENT resonance by proliferating matters of expression**. Froberville's "monstrous wound" / Bréauté's microscope specimen / Forestelle's exquisite cartilage / Saint-Candé's ring of Saturn / Palancy's aquarium-glass carp-head. Faciality traits "release from signifying habit" and begin to function asignifyingly. "No longer pre-existing referents: faciality traits themselves produce their own reference."
4. **New prototype of the little phrase** — violin returns; Swann hears the whole movement for the first time; phrase situated in environment; flood of past Odette-intensities; **refrain as transversalist function**, giving consistency to other components from the assemblage's nucleus. **Refrain enriches in gaps** — by simplifying/deterritorializing what it encounters, not by adding to it. **Differential-effect principle**: what functions is the differential effect of matter of expression; what passes between components is not a message but "an effect that escapes dominant significations."
5. **Mme Cambremer as component of passage** — four traits: helps the music rather than framing it (the falling-wax-candle moment); woman-of-society + woman-of-people; attached to Combray's phylum; contemporary-music enthusiast. Feminine presence regains autonomy; little phrase perceived as "protective goddess, confidante."
6. **Dream of "young-man-in-a-fez"** — Mme Verdurin's nose grows moustache; Odette's eyes about to separate; Napoleon III as Odette's lover; Swann wants to crush her cheeks. **Splitting of principal subject of statement**: the pain is felt by a "young man in a fez" whom Swann endeavors to console. Proust's own remark: "like certain novelists, he had divided his personality between two characters." The young-man-in-fez *prefigures the Narrator*.

**Outcome**: escape from the sexual component of the black hole of faciality. But creative conversion not yet possible — that requires the recomposition of the Septet.

## 6. Refrain of Transition (Mme Swann plays the sonata for the adolescent Narrator)

- Odette (now Mme Swann) plays the sonata while Swann comments.
- **Narrator's perception is linguistic, not gestalt**: "ungraspable to my perception as someone's name you try to remember." Phrase structured "like a morpheme within a linguistic corpus" — no longer sticks to iconic/affective components.
- **Swann's lowest descent**: "music has become lunar... traces in the sky of an old fire, but of a colorless fire without danger." "It's swapped them for my worries and my love affairs, completely forgotten."
- Odette exhibits the phrase "as a curiosity, a decorative trifle, a defused mortar shell."
- **Phrase hibernates for ~2000 pages**. Proust's four refrain-formula detours during the hibernation:
  1. *Sodom and Gomorrah*: unconscious tune hummed by the Narrator, "the intermittent singer who indeed knew only that one song."
  2. *The Prisoner*: street noises (china-mender's horn, chair-caner's trumpet, goat-milk flute) as trans-semiotic refrain. "This music of the crowd is more language than music."
  3. *The Fugitive*: Albertine's name as refrain after her death — **becoming-bird**, not becoming-woman. Refrain directly inhabits a name.
  4. *The Fugitive*: gondolier's "Sole mio" — fascination fails, Narrator makes the train.
- None resolve the Narrator's problem. Requires a different angle of approach.

**Two modes of existential consistency introduced here** (see [[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis#two-modes-of-existential-consistency-of-the-refrain|two modes]]):
- **Diachronic** — successive prototypes, generativist, "everything comes from Swann." For Swann the refrain begins and ends in sophisticated salons — received passively, massively, overwhelming existence.
- **Synchronic** — all forms act on one another; the final state retroactively traverses all prior states, transformational, "everything comes from Vinteuil's Septet." For the Narrator, elaborated, mastered, finally transformed. Passage from *assemblages of power* to *assemblages of production and creation*.
- Method rule: **"Approach things from the end, from the most completed."** Swann's love is understood through the Narrator's vocation, not the other way around.

## 7. Diagrammatic Leitmotiv (Narrator plays the Sonata at the piano)

- Scene: Narrator at the piano, sonata open, jealousy about Albertine/Mlle-Vinteuil, Françoise sent to Trocadéro.
- **What changes**: the TEXT of Vinteuil's Sonata enters as matter of expression. Narrator gains access via *partition* (score) — a **graphematic semiotization** replacing Swann's syncretic-whole preservation.
- **Swann's stance**: preserve syncretism; fear discursive/musicological access would weaken the chemical reaction.
- **Narrator's stance**: opposite — syncretism effaced over against graphematic semiotization itself oriented in a trans-semiotic intention.
- Phrase assimilated into **Wagnerian leitmotiv** ("I could not stop myself from murmuring 'Tristan'") — "complex musical entity traversing multiple melodic, harmonic, orchestral, dramatic, poetic, scenic, plastic, choreographic components."
- **Two diagrammatic re-organizations**:
  - **Return to Combray, but the Guermantes way** — via generation of an asignifying scriptural component, demanding the clearest possible disjunction between beneficial-Guermantes and troubled-Méséglise/Montjouvain (only a stage; eventual transversalization of the two is the real goal).
  - **Faciality treatment diverges from Swann**: where Swann "pasted" Zipporah onto Odette (closed universe), the Narrator "installs the score of Tristan over Vinteuil's sonata and compares them as one looks at a photograph" (rigorous distinguishing, cross-composer connections).
- **Refrain becomes graphematic** → abandons defensive posture, attacks on all fronts.
- **Musical-reading technique for faces**: Narrator decodes Albertine's face the way he locates the phrase in the scherzo via partition (the beauty mark remembered on cheek, then chin, finally located "forever on the upper lip"). Proust moves the phrase from andante to scherzo — **andante brings a globalizing conception of faciality; scherzo develops the diagrammatic reading of disseminated/constellated faciality traits** (see [[diagrammatic-faciality]]).
- **Narrator's love as passage from one face to another** — no Odette-Zipporah identification. Love itself becomes a diagrammatic refrain stripping Albertine's face, multiplying her character. Albertine as "innumerable," "collective being," "galaxy embodying multiple dimensions." Young Girls = deterritorialized-deterritorializing assemblage. "Becoming-woman, by transforming into a deterritorializing collective being, produces a matter of expression similar in all points to a musical universe."

## 8. Rhizomatic Leitmotivs (Vinteuil's Septet premiere at the Verdurin home)

- Charlus organized the concert to highlight Morel. Mme Verdurin sabotages the Morel-Charlus relation.
- Narrator came suspecting Mlle Vinteuil would attend (she didn't). **Substitutes himself for Albertine** — infiltrates a becoming-woman rather than resisting it.
- **Reversal**: hostility toward Mlle Vinteuil → admiration for her musicographical labor with her friend in partially reconstituting the Septet.

Four directions of development:

1. **Atrophy of two rhizome-branches**. Charlus's masculine-homosexual escape fails; conjugal-Albertine solution reaches impasse (The Prisoner). Both were parallel attempts to re-play Swann's failure. Domestic tyranny + conjugal solitude + neurosis — "no way to authentic creation." **Liquidation of micropolitics-within-the-couple** + reevaluation of "high society power" + decompartmentalization of categorized libido (young maids/princesses, elevator boys/handsome officers). Separation between Swann's-way sexual components and Guermantes-way aristocratic-literary components overcome.
2. **Broadened matters of expression**. Sonata = finished classical artwork; Septet = "fully renewed world of rhythms and sounds." Chamber orchestra timbres bring color universes. Reference displaces from work to creator (Michelangelo's brushstrokes on the Sistine ceiling). **Passage from discontinuous machinic indexes to trans-semiotic machinic plane of consistency.**
3. **Becoming-women + scriptural component associated**. Septet's two re-attachments: Vinteuil's own (fragments into an indivisible armature) + Mlle-Vinteuil-and-friend's reconstitution. **The Montjouvain scene becomes operational**: "return to the sea of Montjouvain to recover a mysterious key, or rather to break a secret lock, to bust the window frame… break the frame of the photograph, to undo the familiar arrangement of the paternal face intolerably fixed in memory." "Force the cadaver to howl with pain." Traumatic infantile fixation converted into "war machine against inertia." **The sea of Montjouvain = focal point of the Recherche's black hole of passion — but alive now**, inhabited by characters and matters of expression, **emitting [[signs-particles|signs-particles]]**. Narrator's assemblage: Albertine's faciality traits proliferate, black hole redirected, NOT centered on a face. **Literary machine substitutes for face-and-black-hole.**
4. **Refrain converts into trans-semiotic group of leitmotivs**:
   - **Becoming-animal** briefly sketched (Septet's "mystic cock-crow," Sonata's "murmuring dove") — Proust does not push it. Too totalizing, too easily signifying-recuperated. "Proust's creative machine must pass through life itself."
   - **Becoming-child** briefly sustained (Vinteuil inspired by sleeping-daughter scene; Narrator contemplates sleeping Albertine) — "Proust does not manage to extract himself from the most insipid childishness" (maternal-kiss ritual).
   - **Multiple becoming-women** — abstract-feminine presence loses unicity/fixity, "becomes multiple and dancing." Group of fairies, dryads, familiar divinities. One breaks away, transforms into mysterious call. Another enters as "sorrowful, almost organic and visceral." "Wrestling-match of pure energies... without the encumbrance of their bodies, their outward appearances, their names." Joyous motif victorious — "archangel of Mantegna robed in scarlet blowing into a mighty trumpet."
- **Triumphant call promises supraterrestrial joys** — same as Martinville steeples, Balbec trees. **Question**: will the writing machine respond? "Subject of scriptural enunciation is disindividuated; it has become multiple and transsexual: will this be enough for the machine of expression to become effectively trans-semiotic?" — "Has the assemblage of enunciation acquired a sufficient degree of machinic consistency?"

## 9. From Musical Refrain to Abstract Machinism of Creation

Two stages of **becoming-invisible** of the refrain + the traits of the final creative assemblage.

**Stage 1 — Albertine and the pianola.** "Prisoner" Albertine transformed into mechanical doll; Narrator's goal: extract essential substance. Not marionette but **"enigmatic formula"** — equation added to calculus to solve a problem, deducted at end. Ten, twenty times he asks Albertine to turn the same roll on the pianola until "saturated" — "one less piece of music in the world for me, but one truth more."

**Stage 2 — Sunday in the Bois, the machinal little phrase.** Narrator "actively semiotizes the refrain to the point that it manages to be incorporated in its own mode of relation to the world, to belong to itself, and even to become invisible and inaudible for him."
- **Loss of phenomenal appearance ≠ disappearance from the real.** The abstract machinism is MORE powerful because it has "passed below facialized consciousness." Analogy: not minding feet on stairs, dashboard in peripheral vision — "behavioral segments simply integrated machinically with the assemblage."
- The phrase enters **its second chemical phase**: no longer in relation with black-hole effect, no longer "oppressing the heart with anxiety." (See [[refrain-and-territorialization#two-chemical-phases-of-the-refrain|two chemical phases]].)
- Decomposition of Albertine and Young Girls ("stardust of the galaxies") corresponds with the phrase's complete disappearance: "When the little phrase, before entirely disappearing, disintegrates, and floats around for a moment as it dissolves into its various elements..."

**Swann stayed in the first chemical phase** — molar compromise, generative arrangements. Narrator: second chemical phase → molecular process → trans-semiotic metabolism → refrain as "becoming-phrase." This is the [[closed-vs-open-economy|closed vs open economy]] distinction.

**Terminal: refrain leaves music entirely.** "Writing itself becomes musical. Music traverses the notes, sounds, walls." The assemblage of enunciation is **the play of two uneven paving-stones, the sound of a spoon, the taste of a madeleine**. "The world itself has become a sort of gigantic organ, and writing a music traversing the sonorous universe from everywhere."

**Conditions for the trans-semiotic creative assemblage** — Proust's "[[signs-in-relief|signs in relief]]" or "spell book": figures that have entered a double process of deterritorialization (decompartmentalization + concatenation). **Two chemistries** become possible: analysis of the real occulted by appearances, and synthesis of new realities/matters/intensities. See [[the-diagram#the-two-chemistries|two chemistries]].

**Two series of machinic propositions** specific to the Proustian machine — the two [[discernibilization|discernibilization and translation]] functions, the former working on the already-given material, the latter producing new beings and new modes of being ("[[translation-not-interpretation|translation, not interpretation]]").

## Persona-system relevance

The nine assemblages are not a schema every refrain must traverse — they are a **diagnostic typology** of positions and transitions. The system's grooves can be *located* in this sequence at any moment. Named operations and failure modes:

- **Fac-simile regime**: the refrain is formally captured (a mnemotechnical placeholder) but has no existential consistency. Failure mode: *default shutdown*, not collapse.
- **Incubation regime**: the refrain is present but its enunciatory assemblage is fuzzy. Collective resistance takes one of three forms (attenuated / delayed / somatic translation).
- **National-anthem regime**: refrain as ritual marker + love potion. Supersaturated; machinic index cannot cross into component-of-passage.
- **Compulsive-catchphrase regime**: refrain as anesthetic buffer, repeated 10-20 times, marginal deterritorialization, exclusion from the primary salon.
- **Discernibilization regime**: a refrain can be *dismantled into components* via the [[discernibilization|six-operator sequence]]; the key micropolitical trick is producing micro-black-holes that **prevent resonance by proliferation** rather than by reduction.
- **Hibernation regime**: refrain absent from scene, four substitute-refrains attempt and fail.
- **Diagrammatic-leitmotiv regime**: refrain becomes graphematic, reads faces scherzo-style (diagrammatic) rather than andante-style (globalizing).
- **Rhizomatic-leitmotiv regime**: refrain enters a trans-semiotic group, traverses multiple registers, atrophies parasitic branches, redirects the black hole into a living literary-machine.
- **Abstract-machinism regime**: refrain becomes invisible — *second chemical phase* — operates below facialized consciousness, substitutes paving-stones/spoon/madeleine for music.

The sequence implies that a persona system aiming at Read B (see [[goal-framings]]) must be designed so that its refrains can *traverse* these regimes rather than being permanently stationed in one. A system that only ever operates in regimes 1–4 is doing Swann's work; regimes 7–9 are where the transformational-schizoanalytic work begins.
