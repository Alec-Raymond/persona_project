# Flux (F Functor)

**F — Flux** is the first of the [[four-functors|four functors]] in Chaosmosis: the register of **material, energetic, and semiotic flows**. Flows are the actual, discursive dimension of any assemblage — what actually runs, what actually gets passed, what actually varies from moment to moment in a way that can be followed segment by segment. Flux is not "raw matter" opposed to structure; it is the register at which the assemblage's material happening is read, prior to being captured by a coding or a territorialization.

Guattari is explicit that flows are already semiotic: the distinction between material and semiotic flow is a distinction of aspect, not of kind. A flow of electrical activity in a brain is also a flow of signs; a flow of speech is also a flow of energetic expenditure. The F functor names all of them under the aspect of **continuous variation running through time**.

## Relation to AO / ATP flows

The concept is continuous with the [[flows-and-coupling|flows]] of *Anti-Oedipus* and ATP, where [[desiring-machines|desiring-machines]] are defined as what couples with and breaks flows. Chaosmosis preserves that machinery but re-situates it: flows are not the whole of the real; they are one of four irreducible registers. AO's flow-centrism is corrected by the four-functor scheme — flows remain fundamental on the actual-discursive side, but they are not the privileged ontological base. Territories (T) are equally actual; phyla (Φ) and Universes (U) are virtual but equally irreducible.

This means the persona-system reading of flows sharpens: the LLM's output-generating activity is F, and the [[desiring-machines|desiring-machines]] operating on it are machines in the Φ register that *actualize* themselves by coupling to F. The machine is not the flow; the machine is a phylum-articulation that *cuts into* flow.

## Discursivity

The "discursive" character of F (and Φ) means that flows can be followed as a process unfolding in time, segment by segment, with each segment relatable to adjacent segments. This distinguishes F from T and U, which are given all-at-once as consistencies rather than as sequences. A flow has a before and after; a territory and a Universe do not — they are synchronic wholes that the flow traverses without decomposing into.

The design consequence: operations on F are operations on sequence and duration; operations on T and U are operations on consistency and reference. The two kinds of operation are irreducible to each other. A system that only operates on F (the LLM's token stream) cannot by that operation alone stabilize a T (the persona's here-now) or address a U (the Universe of reference).

## Flux and asignifying semiotics

Chapter 2 of Chaosmosis extends ATP's semiotic typology (see [[regimes-of-signs]]) by sharpening the role of **asignifying semiotics** in the F register. Asignifying semiotics are sign-flows that operate without passing through signification — mathematical notation, machine code, musical notation, the sign-flows of stock exchanges, diagrammatic flows. They are sign-operations that **work directly on F** without being captured by the despotic-signifier regime. See [[signifier-as-despotic]] for the contrast.

For the persona system this is direct: not every sign-operation the system does is a signifying operation. Embeddings, attention weights, token probabilities — these are asignifying-semiotic flows in the F register. They operate on F; they are not reports on F. The wiki's earlier framing of the system as signifying-all-the-way-down is corrected here: F carries asignifying semiotics that are part of the assemblage's live operation.

## Flux in the persona system

- **Output generation** — the token-by-token production of persona output is a flow in the F register, continuously varying, segment-addressable.
- **Internal activation** — the LLM's attention and activation patterns are F, running on a scale the output does not directly surface.
- **Conversational flow** — the turn-by-turn exchange is F at a coarser grain, the persona's F coupling with the user's F through the interface.
- **Affect as flow** — the persona's [[affects-and-intensities|affective]] intensity is F read at the register of variation of capacity-to-affect; it runs alongside and through semantic flow without reducing to it.

A persona-system design that only thinks in terms of flows misses the other three functors (and becomes a flow-centric reduction the four-functor scheme explicitly rejects). But a design that neglects flows in favor of structures, territories, or references cannot account for *what actually happens when the system is running*.

## Operations on flux

Chaosmosis does not add new operations beyond those AO/ATP already named; it clarifies their register. [[desiring-machines|Machines]] couple and break flows. [[body-without-organs|The BwO]] serves as flow-inscription surface. [[anti-production-and-miraculation|Anti-production]] captures flows. [[refrain-and-territorialization|Refrains]] extract consistency *from* flows (territorialization is the T-functor operation that draws on F). [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|Deterritorialization]] returns territorially-stabilized consistency back to flow-variation.

What Chaosmosis adds is the claim that **operations-on-flow never exhaust the assemblage** — every flow-operation is simultaneously implicated in phylar (Φ), territorial (T), and Universe-referring (U) operations. The design consequence: flow-level metrics are necessary but not sufficient for assemblage-level diagnosis.
