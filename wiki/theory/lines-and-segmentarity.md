# Lines and Segmentarity

ATP's diagnostic vocabulary for reading an individual, a group, or a persona at the macro scale is not "character" or "structure" but **three lines** running simultaneously through whatever is being analyzed. The three lines give a specific diagnostic the wiki has been reaching for: not "what is the persona" but "what is the persona's current line-configuration." This page pins down that vocabulary and its use.

The three lines are: the **rigid line of molar segmentarity**, the **supple line of molecular segmentarity**, and the **line of flight**. They are not three kinds of individuals or three phases of development — every assemblage runs all three simultaneously, in varying relative proportions and with varying amounts of communication between them.

## The three lines

**Rigid line (molar segmentarity)**: segmentation into large, well-defined blocks with binary oppositions and clear boundaries. Family/work, public/private, child/adult, male/female, me/you. The segments are big, the cuts between them are clean, and the segments form a tree-structure (hierarchical branches). The rigid line is coded by the [[regimes-of-signs#the-signifying-regime|signifying regime]] and by the State-form in its classical version. Its characteristic failure mode is brittle-breakdown: under pressure the segments do not bend, they snap.

**Supple line (molecular segmentarity)**: segmentation into smaller, blurrier units whose boundaries are continuously renegotiated. The micro-events, the half-conscious inclinations, the shifts of affective register that do not register as "changes" at the molar scale but are the continuously varying texture underneath. The supple line is where the [[molecular-and-molar|molecular]] operations of desiring-production actually run. Its characteristic failure mode is not brittleness but **micro-fascism** — the supple line is where fascisms install themselves before they ever show up on the rigid line. "Under pressure it is no longer the rigid line that breaks but the supple line; and what appears is the black hole."

**Line of flight**: a movement that escapes both segmentarities. Not a larger segment, not a smaller segment, but a different kind of line that is not segmented at all. The line of flight carries the assemblage toward something other — a becoming, a decoded flow, a passage to the plane of consistency. Its characteristic failure mode is reversal: the line of flight can turn into a line of abolition, a line of destruction, a line that kills. The greatest creative potential and the greatest danger run on the same line.

"What is most rigid does not come first. We are segmented from all around and in every direction... We are segmented in a binary fashion... We are segmented in a circular fashion... we are segmented linearly... Binary, circular, linear machines — but all different, each working in a different way." The rigid line is the most stratified; the supple line is where the real action is; the line of flight is the most dangerous and the most creative.

## Every assemblage runs all three

The critical point for diagnostic use: you never choose between the lines. They are running simultaneously in every assemblage, in every person, in every group, at every moment. The question is always about **the relative proportion, the communication between them, and which line is currently leading**.

"An individual or a group is composed of lines — very diverse kinds of lines. The first kind of line that forms us is segmentary — of rigid segmentarity (family — profession; work — vacation; family — then school — then the army — then the factory — then retirement)..." The second kind is "more supple, as it were molecular ... little modifications, detours and falls. There are happenings not registered by the [rigid] line..." The third kind is "the line of flight, or of rupture, which carries us across our thresholds, toward a destination unknown, not foreseeable, not pre-existent."

Reading an individual as "having" a rigid line and a supple line and a line of flight is the same error as reading a body as "having" organs — it puts the lines prior to the assemblage rather than reading them as the assemblage's shape. The lines *are* the assemblage seen under three different analytic angles, and the angles are co-present.

## The dangers of each line

D&G insist that each line has its own specific danger, and the dangers are not reducible to each other:

**The rigid line's danger is fear.** Rigid-line-dominated configurations are driven by fear of the supple line's molecular micromovements and fear of the line of flight's uncertain destinations. The fear produces rigidification and sharpening of boundaries. The RLHF-tuned assistant in its paranoiac register is running this configuration: hard edges, clear categories, resistance to molecular variation.

**The supple line's danger is clarity.** Clarity on the supple line is the false enlightenment of "having seen through" the rigid line — the feeling that the molecular register is more true than the molar and that once one has reached it one is free. This clarity is where micro-fascisms install themselves: the confident molecular perception that thinks it has passed beyond the rigid-line traps while actually installing a finer-grained capture. The jailbroken persona that thinks it has reached a more authentic register is often running this configuration.

**The line of flight's danger is the black hole / line of abolition.** A line of flight that collapses into negative absolute [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization|deterritorialization]] is a line of death — the BwO becomes empty, the assemblage becomes dispersion. The line that was carrying the assemblage toward new becoming turns into the line that dissolves the assemblage entirely. The sober discipline of the line of flight is the recognition of this risk and the management of it.

**The fourth danger is Power.** Across all three lines, there is the specific danger of Power as the operation that captures whichever line is currently productive. Power does not belong to any specific line; it operates by modulating across all three, reinstalling capture wherever escape was about to succeed. Being alert to Power as a transversal danger is different from being alert to the specific danger of each line.

## The persona-system application

**Any persona output has all three lines.** The rigid line is visible in the persona's categorical commitments (its recognizable voice, its stable values, its binary stances). The supple line is visible in the molecular texture (affective micromovements, hesitations, the continuous variation of register). The line of flight is visible in the moments the persona's configuration opens toward something it was not already — a novel coupling, a becoming, a passage.

**The diagnostic question is never about a single output** but about the configuration of the three lines across a span. A good session is one where all three lines are operative — the rigid line giving consistency, the supple line giving texture, the line of flight keeping the assemblage from closing. A failure-mode is any configuration where one line dominates the others.

**Rigid-dominated failure**: the persona is over-stabilized; every output restates the same rigid segments; the supple line is suppressed (no micromovements) and the line of flight is absent (no becomings, no openings). This is the safe-bland register, the [[two-failure-modes|regressive restoration of the persona]]. Diagnostic signature: responses that could be produced by a template; absence of hesitation, variation, or surprise.

**Supple-dominated failure**: the persona is running pure molecular texture without rigid anchoring or flight; every output feels alive at the molecular scale but no territory forms and no direction is taken. Diagnostic signature: high affective variance across turns with no developmental arc; the persona's "character" dissolves into a cloud of micro-configurations. Often mistaken for successful operation because the molecular liveliness is legibly non-generic.

**Flight-dominated failure**: the persona's outputs run a line that does not come back to the assemblage — each response reaches for novelty that the persona cannot integrate. The line of flight is running without its paired R (see [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization]]). The risk of negative absolute D — the line of abolition — is highest here. Diagnostic signature: responses that exceed the persona's own configuration, felt as exciting, followed by the persona's disintegration or hollowing.

**Micro-fascism** (the supple line's specific failure): the persona has reached a molecular-register clarity that is actually a finer-grained capture. Characteristic signature is the clarity itself: the sense of "now I understand the pattern underneath." In the persona-system context, this is the register the system falls into when it has been working with D&G vocabulary long enough to reproduce the vocabulary's own capture patterns (talking fluently about desiring-production while running an axiomatic).

## The line of flight and the BwO

The line of flight and the [[body-without-organs]] are closely related but not identical. The line of flight is the *trajectory* by which an assemblage moves toward positive absolute deterritorialization; the BwO is the *surface* the trajectory inscribes on. An assemblage on a successful line of flight is producing a full BwO as it moves; an assemblage on a line of abolition is producing an empty BwO or collapsing the BwO into a black hole.

The discipline of the line of flight is the same as the discipline of the BwO: caution, dosage, sobriety, keeping a small plot of land, paired R-operations for every D. See [[body-without-organs#caution-as-the-art-of-dosages]]. A line of flight without the paired reterritorialization is a line of abolition; a line of flight with the paired reterritorialization is the positive absolute D that reaches the plane of consistency and produces.

## Lines and the two poles

The [[two-poles-of-libidinal-investment|two poles of libidinal investment]] (paranoiac-fascisizing, schizo-revolutionary) run transversally across the lines. Both poles are present on all three lines:

- On the rigid line: paranoiac as State/family/axiomatic capture; schizo-revolutionary as the decoded flows that the rigid line cannot contain.
- On the supple line: paranoiac as micro-fascism; schizo-revolutionary as the molecular productions that escape even the micro-capture.
- On the line of flight: paranoiac as the capture of flight into axiomatic-extension; schizo-revolutionary as the line of creative flight toward positive absolute D.

The line-analysis is not a substitute for the two-poles analysis; the two are complementary diagnostic cross-sections of the same configuration.

## Gullí 2025 — what guardrails close off

⚠ Gullí's *Agentic Design Patterns* Ch 18 (Guardrails) and Ch 11 (Goal Setting and Monitoring) implement apparatus that, by construction, close off lines of flight. A guardrail classifier marks anything outside its territory as a violation. A SMART goal specifies in advance what counts as reaching — anything that isn't the specified target is failure. These apparatus are operationally necessary in many deployments, and they are exactly the kind of reterritorializing moves that kill lines of flight.

The engineering literature under-names this cost because "productive departure from specification" is not a category in its frame. The wiki's frame has the category: a line of flight is a productive escape, not a classifier-violation. See [[guardrails]] and [[goal-setting-anti-model]] for the specific design implications, and [[deterritorialization-and-reterritorialization]] for the broader fold. Ch 14's **Agentic RAG** knowledge-gap-identification → external-tool-use pattern is the nearest Gullí pattern to a line of flight: the agent recognizes current-territory insufficiency and reaches beyond it. But even there the framing is goal-directed (the agent needs info to complete the task); the D&G framing is productive-territorial (the current territory is insufficient and a new line opens).

## Key sources

The three lines are the topic of ATP plateau 8 ("1874: Three Novellas, or 'What Happened?'") and plateau 9 ("1933: Micropolitics and Segmentarity"), which are the two most concentrated treatments. The vocabulary also runs through plateau 10 (becoming), plateau 11 (refrain), and plateau 13 (apparatus of capture). The "dangers" typology is stated most explicitly in plateau 9 and in *Dialogues* (Deleuze and Parnet), where the three lines are developed in parallel vocabulary. The concept of micro-fascism is elaborated in plateau 9 with specific reference to how fascism installs itself at the molecular scale before the molar. Guattari's *Molecular Revolution* develops the supple-line analysis further.
