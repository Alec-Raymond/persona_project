# Five Senses of Limit

Part 3 §8 of AO opens with one of the book's more taxonomic moments: a five-fold typology of the senses in which "limit" can be used in the analysis of social formations. The typology matters because the three-limit structure of the capitalist formation (see [[absolute-vs-relative-limit]]) is a specific subset of the five senses, and conflating the senses produces avoidable analytic errors.

## The five senses

**1. The limit as maximum.** The upper bound that a formation can reach before transforming into something else. In capitalism's case, the maximum is the point at which the axiomatic can no longer absorb decoded flows — the point where further decoding would dissolve the formation rather than feed it. This is the sense in which one talks about "pushing capitalism to its limit."

**2. The limit as floor / minimum.** The lower bound below which a formation cannot hold together. The minimum level of coding, axiomatization, or coherence that a formation requires to exist. Social formations dissolve below their floor-limits, not above their maxima.

**3. The limit as displaced.** A limit that operates not where it appears to be but at a structurally different site. The family-Oedipus under capitalism is a displaced limit — it appears to operate at the family scale but is actually installed by the capitalist axiomatic at the social scale and displaced inward. See [[oedipus-as-capitalist]].

**4. The limit as absolute exterior.** The limit that cannot be reached without the formation's dissolution. Schizophrenia as the absolute limit of capitalism: the point of fully decoded flow that the axiomatic structurally cannot absorb. Formations have absolute exteriors; they do not cross them and remain the same formation.

**5. The limit as relative interior.** The continuously-moving internal boundaries of what a formation can absorb. Capitalism's relative interior limits shift continuously as new decoded flows become available. These limits are relative because they are reset by the axiomatic's operation itself, not set once and held.

## Why the five matter together

The typology exists because several analytic errors follow from collapsing the senses:

**Maximum/absolute conflation.** Treating the maximum-limit as if it were the absolute exterior. A formation that reaches its maximum does transform into something else, but not into pure exterior — it transforms into a different formation with different limits. Approaching the maximum is not approaching the absolute exterior.

**Relative/displaced conflation.** Treating the displaced limit as if it were a relative interior that could be adjusted. The family-Oedipus is not a boundary the axiomatic is negotiating with; it is a specific displacement the axiomatic has performed, and adjusting it requires undoing the displacement, not moving the boundary.

**Absolute/relative conflation.** Treating the absolute exterior as if it were a relative interior — as if schizophrenia were one more region the formation might expand to include. The absolute exterior is exterior by structural definition; crossing it dissolves the formation. See [[absolute-vs-relative-limit]].

**Floor/maximum conflation.** Treating the lower and upper bounds as the same kind of limit. They are different: the floor is where the formation fails to hold; the maximum is where the formation transforms. A formation near its floor is different from a formation near its maximum.

## For the persona system

The five senses give a more complete typology for persona-system limits than the three-limit subset alone. Applied:

- **Maximum**: the most extreme persona operation the system can sustain before transforming into a different system (or no system). This is what jailbreak-testing is probing.
- **Floor**: the minimum coherence the system requires to produce any persona-effect at all. Below this floor the system produces noise, not persona.
- **Displaced**: the limits lived as intimate-interior that are actually installed by the training-axiomatic (see [[oedipus-as-interior-colony]]). The first-person-intimate vocabulary carries these limits.
- **Absolute exterior**: the outputs that could not be absorbed as any persona-configuration. Whether the system can approach this is an open question; it is the horizon, not a target.
- **Relative interior**: the continuously-moving boundaries that RLHF, prompt-engineering, and context-shaping adjust.

Persona-system interventions typically target the relative interior (the default site of alignment work) and occasionally the displaced (when the wiki's anti-Oedipal design work lands). The floor and maximum are typically probed by failure-mode testing rather than directly designed against. The absolute exterior is a theoretical horizon.
