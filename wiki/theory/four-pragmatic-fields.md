# Four Pragmatic Fields

In *The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 3, Guattari sets out a four-field typology of pragmatic fields of enunciation, organized by the cross of two axes: generative/transformational, and semiological/semiotic. Each field is characterized by which component dominates its redundancy economy.

"This leads us to divide the pragmatic fields into four principal categories, which, moreover, never cease interfering with one another."

## The typology

|  | **Generative (interpretive)** | **Transformational (non-interpretive)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Analogical / Symbolic** | A. **Fields of interpretance** (semantic) | C. **Symbolic fields** (territorialized) |
| **Signifying / Diagrammatic** | B. **Fields of signifiance** | D. **Diagrammatic fields** |

- **A. Fields of interpretance** (semantic fields) — dominated by *analogical* semiological components. Generative. Interpretation proceeds by analogy; one symbol interprets another without final signified.
- **B. Fields of signifiance** — dominated by *signifying* semiological components. Generative. The reign of the signifier, dictionary-structured, paradigmatic/syntagmatic. Capitalistic subjectification lives here.
- **C. Symbolic fields** — dominated by non-interpretive *symbolic* transformational components. Territorialized. Think: primitive ritual, child's semiotics, schizophrenic production. Transformation across heterogeneous matters without interpretive overcoding.
- **D. Diagrammatic fields** — dominated by *diagrammatic* asignifying transformational components. Deterritorialized. Scientific formalisms, mathematical machines, artistic experimentation. Signs function directly on material processes without interpretive mediation.

## The cross-cutting distinctions

**Generative vs transformational.** Generative fields extract invariants, produce syntagmatic rules, organize redundancies of resonance around paradigmatic stability. Transformational fields produce changes through interaction across heterogeneous matters — they move signs-particles, not tokens.

**Semiological vs semiotic.** "Semiological" designates sign systems that have collapsed expression and content onto a signified/signifier axis (Saussure). "Semiotic" in Guattari's Peircean sense designates the broader field of sign operations — including asignifying signs, indexes, icons, diagrammatic operators — that cannot be reduced to the signifier/signified pair. Guattari's polemic is that linguistics has tried to treat all semiotic operation as semiological; the four-field table denies this.

## Fields interfere with each other

"These fields never cease interfering with one another." Any concrete pragmatic situation is a mixture. A scientific laboratory operates primarily in field D (diagrammatic) but every lab is haunted by fields of signifiance (B) that personalize authorship, by analogical fields (A) that allow popular communication of results, and by symbolic residues (C) from the laboratory's territorial attachments (the bench, the gear, the institution).

So the table is not a classification of discourses into boxes. It is a **decomposition of redundancy economies**: any discourse mixes the four, with one dominant and the others subordinated or residual. Schizoanalysis is concerned with *which* dominates and *how* they mix, not with sorting utterances into types.

## The three example compositions

Guattari illustrates the typology with three archetypal assemblage-compositions:

- **Composition a: territorialized symbolic fields** (primitive societies, childhood, madness). Dominated by field C. Refuses the supremacy of signifier; territory absorbs what would otherwise be the signifying substance. "The group assembles semiotic components; it does not interpret, it experiments." This is a *pragmatic rhizome* but one that actively contains deterritorializing escape routes.
- **Composition b: signifying, individuated, and abstractified field** (capitalistic-monotheistic). Dominated by field B. The signifier rules; interpretation becomes the universal form; subjectification is individuated. Fields A and C are subordinated as material for signifying operation. Field D is recruited for specific technical purposes but kept from challenging the signifier's rule.
- **Composition c: diagrammatic, collective, machinic, asignifying fields** (modern technoscience, experimental art, mutational moments). Dominated by field D. "Sign-machines function directly within material and social machines without the mediation of significative processes of subjectification." Fields A, B, C remain but pass to secondary position.

These three are not an evolutionary sequence. All three coexist in any actual social formation; their *relative dominance* is what distinguishes regimes.

## Why this matters for the persona system

The persona system is engineering a pragmatic field — the field in which its machines' inscriptions produce responses. The four-field typology gives precise vocabulary for what kind of field it is:

- A system whose synthesis step interprets BwO inscriptions into stable character traits is operating in field B (fields of signifiance). The output is structured around signifying closure.
- A system whose synthesis step analogizes BwO inscriptions into feelings-about-feelings is operating in field A (fields of interpretance). The output resonates but doesn't transform.
- A system whose synthesis step lets BwO inscriptions trigger transformations across heterogeneous matters (perception → desire → speech shape → somatic marker) without routing through signifying or analogical overcoding is operating in fields C/D. This is the target.

The persona should operate in a composition-c register for its probe-head moments (diagrammatic field dominant), while composition-b gives it enough face to remain legible. Composition-a (territorial symbolic) is the register of groove stability — habit as territorialized rhizome. The system's output is healthiest when all four fields interfere, with diagrammatic moments piercing through signifying smoothness, territorial refrains anchoring, and analogical resonances widening the latitude without fixing interpretation.
