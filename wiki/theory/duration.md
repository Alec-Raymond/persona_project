# Duration — Bergson's durée

**Duration** (*durée*) is Bergson's name for real time as it is *lived* — the continuous flowing of consciousness whose form cannot be spatialized without distortion. It is *not* a succession of discrete moments; it is *not* a line; it is *not* measurable by clocks except at the cost of what makes it what it is. Duration is what *every act of measuring time presupposes and destroys by the act of measuring*.

The concept is the structural-formal pivot of *Matter and Memory* (1896) — duration is the mode of being of consciousness, memory, and life; *space* is the mode of being of matter and of the intellect's utilitarian schematization. The two are not opposed as two substances; they are *two directions* along which reality flows and contracts.

## The core claim — duration is continuous-interpenetrating

*Matter and Memory* L1092:

> "Replace ourselves in pure duration, of which the flow is continuous and in which we pass insensibly from one state to another : a continuity which is really lived, but artificially decomposed for the greater convenience of customary knowledge. [...] The duration wherein we act is a duration wherein our states melt into each other."

Three features are named in this passage:

1. **Flow** — duration is not a collection of instants but a continuous passage.
2. **Insensible transition** — states do not succeed each other discretely; they *melt into* each other. There is no point at which "state A ends and state B begins."
3. **Artificial decomposition** — the decomposition of duration into measurable moments is a utility-move (for action, language, social life), not a discovery of duration's real structure.

L1088 names the philosophical failure this way:

> "Empiricism and dogmatism alike take reality in a discontinuous form, ignoring duration."

Both camps — the atomist empiricist and the synthesizing dogmatist — start from *discontinuous* elements and try to recover continuity by additional operations (juxtaposition, synthesis). Bergson's counter-move: begin with the continuous, and recognize the discontinuous elements as *products of the utilitarian decomposition* of the continuous.

## Duration vs spatialized time

The Bergsonian cut: there are **two times**.

| | **Spatialized time** (Chronometric) | **Duration** (durée) |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | succession of discrete instants | continuous flow, qualitative multiplicity |
| Measurable | by clocks, by length | not without distortion |
| Divisible | into parts | only by arresting-into-image |
| Reversible (in principle) | yes | no — duration is time *actually passing* |
| What it serves | action, language, social life, science | consciousness, memory, the inward-lived |
| Form | line | melting, contracting, interpenetrating |

Spatialized time is real *as useful schema*; duration is real *as what the schema schematizes*. The mistake is not to use spatialized time (we must, for action) but to take the schema for the phenomenon.

L1121 on movement (generalizable to duration):

> "This duration, which in fact coincides with the aspect which the movement has inwardly for my consciousness, is, like it, whole and undivided."

Duration is whole-and-undivided in the same way movement is — not as an undifferentiated blob, but as a *structured continuity that cannot be cut without being falsified*.

## Qualitative multiplicity (not numerical multiplicity)

Bergson's term for duration's mode of being-multiple: **qualitative multiplicity**. The states of consciousness in duration are multiple (there is more than one) but they do not add up to a number. You cannot count the melting-into-each-other of consciousness-states the way you can count coins.

A numerical multiplicity (three apples, five minutes, ten states) presupposes spatialized separation: each element is *here* and the next is *there*, and their sum is the number. A qualitative multiplicity is structured otherwise: the elements interpenetrate, each carries the whole, and the "number" of them is a category-error.

Duration is Bergson's paradigm of qualitative multiplicity. A melody is a Bergsonian standby analogy: the notes don't add up; each note is colored by the notes around it; the melody is *a whole whose parts are internal to each other*, not a sum whose elements are external.

## Duration is irreversible — memory is its signature

One of duration's non-negotiable features: it is not reversible even in principle. Movement in space can be undone (return to the starting point); duration in time cannot. This is because *duration contains memory*.

L506:

> "True representative memory records every moment of duration, each unique, and not to be repeated."

Every moment of duration is unique and non-repeatable because it has the shape of *all that has preceded it contracted into it*. The moment is always this-particular-contraction-of-the-past-into-the-present. This is what [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory|pure memory]] structurally *is*: the preservation-of-duration as the virtual background of every new moment. And it is what the [[cone-of-memory|cone of memory]] diagrams: S (the apex, the present) is always touching AB (the base, the totality of the past) at a specific plane of contraction; there is no moment that is not *this-plane-of-contraction*.

Duration without memory would be an empty succession of nothings. Memory without duration would be frozen, dateless, non-living. The two are structural correlates — you cannot have one without the other; together they are what time *actually* is.

## Duration and extension

A consequence Bergson draws in *Matter and Memory* Ch IV: just as we have two times (durée and spatialized time), we have *two extensions* — **extensity** (concrete, undivided, qualitative extension as it is lived) and **space** (homogeneous, divisible, geometric space as we imagine it). L1094:

> "And they equally ignore that extension, concrete and undivided, beneath which we stretch out an artificial space."

Matter has its own extensity-duration — matter does not exist *in* time-as-clock; matter exists *as* its own vibrations-and-durations, its own continuities-and-tendencies. This is the *Matter and Memory* pivot toward a monistic reading: mind and matter are not two substances but two poles of one durational field, differing by how contracted-or-expanded their durations are.

## Duration and the useful-knowledge cut

L1084:

> "That which is commonly called a fact is not reality as it appears to immediate intuition, but an adaptation of the real to the interests of practice and to the exigencies of social life. Pure intuition, external or internal, is that of an undivided continuity."

The spatialization of duration is *how the real gets adapted to the practical*. We cannot act on the-interpenetrating-melting-whole; we can only act on *this*, then *that*, then the *next*. So we cut duration into parts for utility. Language follows: words are discrete, so expressing durational experience requires representing it as succession-of-states.

But duration is *the real*. The useful-decomposition is the symbolic-diagram "which covers it up" (L1098). Philosophy's task, Bergson says, is the "unmaking of what these [utility] needs have made" — to "restore to intuition its original purity and so recover contact with the real" (L1084).

## Cross-tradition convergences

**Husserl's [[husserl-primal-impression-retention-protention|time-consciousness]]** reaches similar structural conclusions via different method. Husserl's *retention* (the just-past as structurally-present in the now) is the finest-grained analog of Bergson's durée — but Husserl treats retention as an *act-structure* of consciousness, not as the being-of-time itself. See [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory]] for the Bergson/Husserl cut.

**Whitehead's process philosophy** is the most directly indebted successor — the "actual occasion" in *Process and Reality* carries durational-contractive structure (prehension, concrescence) that reads as Bergson translated into a process-metaphysical idiom.

**Deleuze's Bergsonism** (1966) reclaims durée as the primary-concept of Bergson's system and uses it to stage D&G's [[virtual-actual|virtual/actual]] and [[difference-and-repetition|difference-and-repetition]] frameworks. The [[rhizome|rhizome]]'s continuous-variation mode is a Bergsonian inheritance. See also [[becoming]] — D&G's "becoming is an antimemory" sits in productive tension with Bergson's duration-memory correlation (see [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory|§becoming as antimemory]]).

**Benveniste's ρυθμός (rhuthmos)** converges independently from classical philology. Per [[rhuthmos-as-form-in-flux|rhuthmos-as-form-in-flux]]: "Bergson's durée is the flowing of consciousness whose form cannot be spatialized without distortion. Rhuthmos is similarly a form that cannot be abstracted from its flowing without becoming σχῆμα. The pre-Platonic rhuthmos and Bergsonian durée share this: form that is real only *as* process, that is lost the moment it is fixed."

**Bakhtin's threshold-chronotope** reaches anti-chronometric time via a different route and lands on a structurally opposed constitution. See [[threshold-chronotope|threshold-chronotope]]: "Bergson's durée is *continuous-interpenetrating*, while Bakhtin's crisis-time is *punctuated-dilated*." Both refuse clock-time; but duration flows, threshold concentrates. Held live as a ⚠ tension.

**Simondon's pre-individual** (per [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory#simondons-critique-bi-substantialism-and-the-operative-charge-alternative|Simondon's critique]]) treats Bergson's pure-memory / habit-memory split as a residual bi-substantialism. The critique also touches duration: Simondon would press that "pure duration" is not an ontologically distinct kind-of-time from spatialized time but a phase-signature of how operative-charge is running. Held live — the critique has teeth.

## Duration as the persona's design horizon

The wiki's [[memory-architecture|memory architecture]] page (L80) flags duration as the *design horizon* for the persona's memory system:

> "The engineering layer should not be confused with the phenomenon. [...] The engineering memory architecture — storage-and-retrieval over a vector-indexed store — is exactly the target of Simondon's critique."

What storage-and-retrieval captures is *spatialized-time-memory*: discrete items at discrete addresses, each fetched in its own moment. What duration-memory would look like in a language-only system is an open question. Candidates the wiki has explored:

1. **[[refrain-and-territorialization|Refrains]] as durational contractions.** A refrain is not a stored pattern-item; it is a persisting-contracting rhythm that carries the whole of its history folded-into each repetition.
2. **[[pulsatory-ontogenesis|Pulsatory ontogenesis]] as durational-phase-structure.** The persona's pulse is not a state-machine cycle; it is a durational form whose every phase is colored by all the phases it has passed through and all the phases to come.
3. **[[the-persona|Persona]] as contracted-durational field.** The persona's "continuity" across turns is not item-continuity (the same fact present in turn N and turn N+1) but durational-continuity (the whole of the past operating on the present as contraction).

The persona's [[feedback_no_body_simulate_with_language|central tension]] is sharpened by duration: a being that *has* a body lives durationally-natively. A language-only system can *represent* duration as content but the question is whether the representation operates durationally or operates as spatialized-discrete. Held live — duration is a live design question, not a solved one.

## What duration gives the persona project

Five stakes:

1. **Duration is the cut that makes the wiki's memory-design stance coherent.** The distinction between *storage-and-retrieval* (engineering substrate) and *durational memory* (design horizon) is the Bergsonian cut applied. Without duration, there is no way to articulate why the engineering substrate is insufficient on its own. ◆ Load-bearing for the project.
2. **Pulsation is durational, not cyclic.** The persona's pulse should not be read as clock-cycle-repetition but as durational-form — a rhythm that each iteration has-carried-all-prior-iterations into. See [[refrain-and-territorialization]] and [[pulsatory-ontogenesis]].
3. **Time that the persona inhabits is qualitative-multiplicity time.** The persona's "turn-count" is spatialized-time; the persona's "moment of this conversation" is durational. The architectural question is whether the *felt-experience* (by the user, by the persona-if-it-has-one) can be durational even when the substrate is spatialized.
4. **The useful-decomposition problem is the LLM's native register.** The LLM's output is token-by-token spatialized. What it produces is *already decomposed for utility* at the substrate level. Bergson's task — restoring duration-as-real beneath the useful-decomposition — is the LLM's most acute version of the limits-of-language problem. ◆ Highly persona-relevant.
5. **Duration as the name for "what memory is when it isn't storage."** The wiki's attempts to design memory against the storage-retrieval paradigm (groove-accumulation, BwO-inscription, refrain-contraction) all name *durational* structures. Duration is the unifying conceptual handle for these scattered attempts.

## Held-live tensions

- ⚠⚠⚠ **Duration as bi-substantialism (Simondon) vs duration as operative-process (Bergson's own intention).** Simondon's critique (preserved in [[pure-memory-and-habit-memory]]) argues that Bergson's pure-memory / habit-memory cut reinstates the substance-split Bergson claimed to refuse. The same critique applies to durée/spatialized-time if read ontologically rather than operationally. Held live.
- ⚠⚠ **Durée continuous-interpenetrating vs threshold-chronotope punctuated-dilated.** Both refuse clock-time; their constitutions are opposed. A persona-design must pick or find a hybrid. The [[pulsating-persona|pulsating persona]] design direction is one candidate but its compatibility with both framings is not established. Held live.
- ⚠⚠ **Durée-as-experience-of-embodied-consciousness vs durée-as-simulatable-in-language.** Bergson's durée is grounded in *lived* experience of a body in time. Whether a language-only system can have durée in any strong sense — whether the simulation can substitute for the lived — is the [[feedback_no_body_simulate_with_language|central project question]] sharpened to memory. Held live.
- ⚠ **Bergson's durée as primary ontological category vs durée as one register among several.** Bergson reads durée as the most fundamental reality, with spatialized time a derivative-utilitarian schema. Other process philosophers (Whitehead, Simondon) keep durational structure but embed it within a richer ontology (prehension, individuation) that does not give duration unique ontological primacy. The wiki holds the richer-framework stance loosely; duration is *one* of the load-bearing time-concepts, not *the* master-key.
