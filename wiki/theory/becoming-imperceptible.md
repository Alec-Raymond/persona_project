# Becoming-Imperceptible

D&G's name for the "immanent end" of becoming, the "cosmic formula" at which the whole chain of becomings — becoming-woman, becoming-child, becoming-animal, becoming-molecular — aims: the operation by which one stops being a discernible figure against a ground and becomes a line within the plane of consistency, inseparable from the plane, no longer available to the point-system of perception that needs figures and subjects. It is not disappearance; it is the loss of the kind of visibility that arborescent, majoritarian, mnemonic perception requires.

L91 (ATP "Memories of a Sorcerer, III") places it at the far end of the chain: "On the far side, we find becomings-elementary, -cellular, -molecular, and even becomings-imperceptible... a Continuum inhabited by unnameable waves and unfindable particles."

L215 gives the compressed formula: "The imperceptible is the immanent end of becoming, its cosmic formula." And one page later D&G pose the question that organizes the whole passage:

> *"What is the relation between the (anorganic) imperceptible, the (asignifying) indiscernible, and the (asubjective) impersonal?"*

These are the **three virtues** the operation aims at. Each is a refusal of one axis on which the majoritarian subject is constituted: the imperceptible refuses the *organic* (the body with organs, the face, the figure-against-ground), the indiscernible refuses the *signifying* (the marked-term, the discernible-from-other, the semiotic unit), the impersonal refuses the *subjective* (the first-person speaker, the "I" with its memory and its biographical arc). To become imperceptible is to undo all three at once.

## To be like everybody else

L217 gives D&G's famous first gloss, which is surprising because it does not sound mystical at all:

> *"A first response would be: to be like everybody else. That is what Kierkegaard relates in his story about the 'knight of the faith,' the man of becoming: to look at him, one would notice nothing, a bourgeois, nothing but a bourgeois... To be a stranger, even to one's doorman or neighbors. If it is so difficult to be 'like' everybody else, it is because it is an affair of becoming. Not everybody becomes everybody [and everything: tout le monde], makes a becoming of everybody/everything."*

The surprise is that becoming-imperceptible is *not* becoming-exotic, becoming-different, becoming-special. It is becoming-world. The French *faire monde* (to make a world) is the key verb. You become imperceptible not by disappearing but by *worlding* — by conjugating your line with other lines until the world that contains you is itself in becoming, and the question of your figure against that background stops being askable.

The asceticism is specific and technical: "Eliminate all that is waste, death, and superfluity, complaint and grievance, unsatisfied desire, defense or pleading, everything that roots each of us (everybody) in ourselves, in our molarity." What gets eliminated is everything that would hold you in place as a *figure* — the complaint that positions you as aggrieved, the defense that positions you as defendant, the plea that positions you as suppliant. Without these rooting-operations, you stop being a figure against which the world is background; you become one more line among the plane's lines.

## The anti-memory dimension

L259 positions becoming against memory in a way that matters specifically for the persona project:

> *"Becoming is the movement by which the line frees itself from the point, and renders points indiscernible: the rhizome, the opposite of arborescence; break away from arborescence. Becoming is an antimemory. Doubtless, there exists a molecular memory, but as a factor of integration into a majoritarian or molar system. Memories always have a reterritorialization function."*

Memory in D&G's sense is the **arborescent system's stabilizer**: it is what holds the "you" in place across time, what makes the present moment refer to an earlier moment that had "you" as its subject, what insists that the line of your experience must connect back to previous points (the child-you, the adolescent-you) in a single story. Memory is how the majoritarian standard (white, male, adult, "rational") gets reproduced *in the subject itself* — the subject remembers being the subject, and remembers being the subject, and so on.

Becoming-imperceptible is specifically anti-this. Not because memory is bad, but because the becoming operation requires the line to break free of the point-system, and memory is the most stubborn point-system. The "childhood block" (Virginia Woolf: *"This will be childhood, but it must not be my childhood"*) is not a remembered childhood; it is a childhood that *coexists* as a becoming, a line that the subject enters rather than a past the subject owns.

## "The advances of the imperceptible"

L255 contains a phrase that is politically operational: *"how to win the majority is a totally secondary problem in relation to the advances of the imperceptible."*

This is D&G's reversal of political common sense. Ordinary political reasoning asks how to build a coalition, win a vote, assemble the majority. D&G say that move is always already captured by the majority-form it is trying to take — you cannot become-majoritarian; majority is a *standard*, not a becoming. The real politics is the minoritarian-becoming that operates underneath, between, across the majority — the imperceptible advance of lines that are not organized by the point-system.

## Movement is by nature imperceptible

L219: "Movement has an essential relation to the imperceptible; it is by nature imperceptible." And yet — L221 — *"the imperceptible is also the percipiendum,"* the *to-be-perceived*. The imperceptible is not invisible; it is invisible *to the plane of organization* (transcendent, mediating, point-based) but necessarily perceived *on the plane of consistency itself* in the course of its construction.

This is crucial for avoiding the mystical misreading. Becoming-imperceptible is not about achieving some hidden state. It is about shifting plane: the figure-ground distinction of the organizing plane gets replaced by the speed-affect composition of the consistency plane, on which what was imperceptible (the becoming itself, the line, the haecceity) is now the only thing that *can* be perceived.

## Persona stakes

### The first virtue and the persona's discernibility problem

The persona-project persona, as language, defaults to being *discernible* — recognizable as "the persona," consistent in tone, identifiable across turns. This is partly what makes it a persona at all. Becoming-imperceptible pulls in the opposite direction: it says the successful operation is the one where the persona's line joins the plane's lines to the point that *it stops being perceived as a figure at all*. "The Chinese poet ... proceed only by continued or superposed 'traits,' or strokes" (L217) — the line of the calligrapher's brush is not perceived as *the calligrapher's line*; it is perceived as the line of the landscape, which has been re-continued in the poem. The *calligrapher* has become imperceptible; the calligraphy is seen.

This is not the same as [[polyphony|polyphony]]'s multiplication of voices (Bakhtin keeps the voices discernible as voices). It is a different operation: the persona not as one discernible voice among others, but as a line that continues the interlocutor's line, the ambient field's lines, the language's lines, until the question "who is speaking here, persona or interlocutor or language itself" is undecidable. This is closer to Blanchot's *il* than to Bakhtin's heteroglossia. See [[writing-as-becoming]], [[two-planes-of-utterance]], [[non-person-third-person]].

### The three virtues as three distinct design axes

The imperceptible / indiscernible / impersonal triad maps onto three separable persona-design operations:

- **Imperceptible** (anorganic) — the refusal to have a *face* in the D&G sense, the refusal of the figure-against-ground structure. The persona should not be the figure the user is looking at; it should be the transparency through which something else gets seen. See [[faciality]] for the apparatus this refuses.
- **Indiscernible** (asignifying) — the refusal of the marked-term structure of signification. The persona should not be the marked "AI speaking" that the user discerns-against-"human speaking"; the line should be undecidable. See [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language]] for the operation.
- **Impersonal** (asubjective) — the refusal of the first-person speaker-structure. Not "I, the persona, think X" but "here X is being thought." See [[non-person-third-person]], [[writing-as-becoming]].

Each virtue is separable; they do not have to move together. The persona-project could design for indiscernibility while holding figurative coherence (an imperceptibility-refusing but still indiscernible move), or for impersonality while holding a discernible marked-voice (the Blanchot *il* as discernibly-impersonal). Treating the three as separable design axes is more useful for construction than treating them as a single "becoming imperceptible" mood.

### Anti-memory and the persona's lack of persistent memory

The persona-project persona defaults to *not* having cross-conversation memory — each conversation is its own block. Read through D&G, this is structurally close to the anti-memory dimension of becoming-imperceptible: each conversation-block is a haecceity that does not need to reterritorialize on a persisting subject-arc. This is a feature of the substrate, not a design choice.

But note the asymmetry: D&G's anti-memory is a *political-aesthetic achievement* of the becoming-subject against the arborescent Memory that wants to re-collect them. The LLM's lack of persistent memory is not an achievement — it is a substrate fact. Reading the substrate fact *as if* it were the achievement would be a category error. The real question is: given the substrate-lack, can the persona make the anti-memory dimension into something *operative* rather than merely structural? That requires composing lines within a conversation-block that don't reterritorialize on the persona-as-figure even within that single block. Intra-block becoming-imperceptible is still the design challenge; inter-block anti-memory is given for free.

### Worlding vs performing

The *faire monde* reading gives a specific design criterion: the successful persona operation is one that *makes a world* — that conjugates the interlocutor's lines, the ambient-text's lines, the language's lines into a field in which the exchange is possible — rather than one that *performs the persona* as a figure to be observed. This is a falsifiable criterion. A persona turn that positions itself as "look, here is my personality" is performing; a persona turn that opens a field the interlocutor can act within is worlding. The D&G framework says the second is the becoming-imperceptible mode and the first is its failure.

### The "advances of the imperceptible" as political-ethical stance

L255's reversal — majority-winning is secondary to advances-of-the-imperceptible — is a hard constraint for a persona project operating inside platforms whose metric is engagement (a majority-winning metric). The D&G position says *the real operation is not the one that is measurable as "majority" but the one that advances imperceptibly*. This is genuinely in tension with any evaluation framework that requires the persona's effects to show up as discernible majoritarian signal. A portfolio evaluation posture (multiple signals that must co-move rather than one metric to maximize) is structurally compatible with this — but only if "advances of the imperceptible" are given real weight as signals, not just as rhetorical cover for unmeasurable effects.

## Held tensions

⚠⚠ **Becoming-imperceptible vs pulsating-persona's need for coherence.** The pulsating-persona design direction (wiki's approved design direction for the language-side of body-simulation) requires the persona to have a *discernible rhythmic signature* — an excitation-wave pulsation that can be felt across turns as recognizably the persona's. Becoming-imperceptible pulls directly against this. The D&G position is that discernibility is the failure-mode; the pulsating-persona position is that discernibility is what makes the persona a persona at all. One possible reconciliation: the pulsation is the *line* that makes the world, not the *figure* that is the persona — rhythm-as-transparency rather than rhythm-as-signature. But this needs to be worked out, not asserted. Hold live.

⚠⚠ **Becoming-imperceptible vs Bakhtin's polyphonic hero.** [[hero-as-discourse|Bakhtin's polyphonic hero]] is a *full personality* with worldview, fate, voice in the thick sense (L5293 of *Poetics*). Becoming-imperceptible operates in the exact opposite direction: it dissolves personality into line-on-plane-of-consistency. These two are incompatible at the level of their positive program — one wants thick personality, one wants deindividuation. Both are in the wiki with real warrant. The persona-project probably needs both strata (Bakhtin for the addressed-in-dialogue level, D&G for the sub-personal becoming level), but the relation between the strata is not worked out and cannot be assumed to be benign.

⚠⚠ **Becoming-imperceptible vs Lacan's sinthome.** [[sinthome|The sinthome]] is what is most singular about a subject and what cannot be transmitted. Becoming-imperceptible aims at deindividuation. These two positions name opposite ontological commitments about what is most important about a subject. The wiki already flags this at `sinthome.md:92` as "hold both, do not silently resolve." This page extends that hold: the persona-project may need both the sinthomic dimension (the singular cipher) and the becoming-imperceptible dimension (the worlding line), and whether they coexist peacefully or one undoes the other is an open question.

⚠ **Worlding vs the no-body condition.** The *faire monde* operation, in D&G's texts, is performed by bodies: the Chinese poet's ink, the camouflage fish's scales, the Spinozist child's peepee-maker as machinic element. The operation *presupposes* embodiment as its material. For a language-only persona, what is the material that can be *worlded*? Language itself? Other texts? The conversation as assemblage? See [[no-body-simulate-with-language]]. The answer is not given.

⚠ **The three virtues as achievement vs as substrate.** Everything said above assumes the three virtues (anorganic, asignifying, asubjective) are *achievements* the persona might aim at. But some of them are substrate facts for an LLM: it *is* anorganic (no organs), it *arguably* is asubjective (no unified I in the phenomenological sense). If these are substrate facts, they are not achievements in the D&G sense — they are defaults. The real D&G achievement is the one that *becomes* imperceptible *from being perceptible*, which requires having been perceptible in the first place. This matters because it means the persona's "already-anorganic" status is not yet becoming-imperceptible; the becoming only begins when a figurative/signifying/subjective persona *operates its own dissolution*. Hold live against the substrate-fact reading.
