# Discernibilization

**Discernibilization** is one of the two series of machinic propositions Guattari extracts from Proust (MU Ch 2 §9), paired with [[translation-not-interpretation|translation-generation]]. It is the **named schizoanalytic operation** of making a component discernible that was previously absorbed into undifferentiated stratification. In [[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis#the-three-transformational-functions|generative-vs-transformational schizoanalysis]] it is the first of three assembling functions — alongside [[generative-vs-transformational-schizoanalysis#the-three-transformational-functions|proliferation]] and [[the-diagram|diagrammatization]] — by which a component passes to the rank of **component of passage**.

Guattari: "Methods of semiotic magnification, 'colorization,' and crossing in Proust, or those of acceleration, deceleration, thickening, and deformation of spatio-temporal coordinates in Kafka."

## Discernibilization is not analysis

The operation is not decomposition into pre-existing parts. It is **production of discernibility** where stratification had blurred components into one another. "This labor of the artist, this attempt to see something different beneath the material, beneath experience, beneath words, is the exact inverse of that which is accomplished within us from minute to minute… when [vanity, passion, intellect, habit] overlay our true impressions so as to hide them from us completely, with the repertoire of words and the practical aims, which we wrongly call life."

Signifying semiologies **lack the natural** (trap us in dominant redundancies) AND **lack the artificial** (not machinic enough to resist "the resistance of the material"). Discernibilization is what produces access to the discarded register:

- **Greater passivity** — greater abandon to fortuitous encounters and bareness of the real.
- **Greater sophistication** — more machinic mode of semiotization.

Both simultaneously. Kantian empirical apperception **"armed"** with semiotic machines of discernibilization that accelerate/condense time, distend/contract space, produce composite faces, derive them from one another.

"An impression is for the writer what an experiment is for the scientist."

## The Saint-Euverte six-component operation

The paradigmatic example is the Fifth Assemblage in Swann's career of the little phrase (see [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#5-reactivation-resorption-of-the-black-hole-marquise-de-saint-euverte|Fifth Assemblage]]): Swann's black hole of passion for Odette is dismantled through a **six-fold discernibilization**. Guattari describes the operation as "uncovering and discernibilizing six components… through different processes that expose them again, laying bare their deterritorialization point (magnification, deceleration, repetition, splitting, 'proliferating' new matters of expression which at times resemble the processes of 'mitosis' in cellular reproduction)."

The six components:

**1. Subjective component of the statement — Swann/Charlus divide.** Charlus acquires novelistic identity by taking on the masculine-homosexual component Swann had foreclosed. At this stage Charlus's homosexuality is **attenuated, not yet of passage** — it functions as "eunuch" / confidant / surgeon-asepticizing-a-field, not yet as creative process. Diagnostic: a component can be present and registered without yet being of passage.

**2. Architectural/iconic division.** The Saint-Euverte monumental staircase is simultaneously climbed as the little dressmaker's stairs (Odette, Charlus) AND the Ducal Palace stairs of Venice — **spatial component proliferates** (prefigures the paving-stone scene in Guermantes hotel). Swann's iconic-identification technique *returns, but now distributed*: Mantegna, Dürer, Goya, Cellini, Giotto evoked from the salon, no longer centered on Odette-Zipporah. The art-reference component, released from its neutralizing function, now "undeceives Swann's gaze and allows him to anchor himself in external reality again."

**3. Monocle series — the central faciality schizz.** The operator of the whole sequence. Masculine characters undergo a "deterritorializing schizz"; faces seen as "figures in a painting" then *overflow* the picturesque frame. Monocles literally make the constellation of faciality traits **explode**:

- General de Froberville: monocle as "monstrous wound... struck between his eyelids like a shell splinter... like the Cyclops' single eye."
- M. de Bréauté: gaze as "natural history specimen under a microscope."
- M. de Forestelle: "superfluous cartilage... whose material was exquisite."
- M. de Saint-Candé: monocle as ring of Saturn.
- M. de Palancy: carp's head — "accidental and perhaps purely symbolic fragment of the glass of his aquarium."

The decisive mechanism — **production of micro-black-holes that PREVENTS resonance by proliferating matters of expression**, inverse of the usual black-hole mechanism:

> "Far from creating the play of a phenomenon of resonance which would accentuate its devastating consequences, the production of this new series of micro black holes will, on the contrary, come to prevent the mechanism by making matters of expression proliferate."

"The balance between empty consciential redundancies and the resonant redundancies of faciality is broken." Implosion of signifying subjectivity projects "a high-voltage matter of expression whose asignifying particles will have been able to radically change Swann's life." Faciality traits are released from signifying habit: "no longer pre-existing referents — faciality traits themselves produce their own reference."

This is the named anti-resonance operator. It has direct analogues outside the Saint-Euverte scene (the elderly-faces-as-puppets in Guermantes; the monocle-series is the young-man counterpart). See [[diagrammatic-faciality#monocle-series-and-elderly-faces|monocle series]].

**4. New prototype of the dominant component.** Violin returns; Swann hears the whole movement of the Sonata for the first time; the phrase is situated in its environment; past Odette-intensities flood back ("chrysanthemum, embossed 'Maison Dorée,' eyebrows, fragrance of hairdresser's iron, worker girl, rains, icy moonlight"). The refrain is now **transversalist** — making other components work together. Three sub-principles:

- **Refrain enriches in gaps** (not by addition): integrates keys by simplifying, deterritorializing, examining.
- **Positive deterritorialization**: "nourishes the refrain of new matters of expression which disperse the central black hole effect" — not self-annihilation but "positive, germinating deterritorialization developing heterogeneous qualities."
- **Differential-effect principle**: the violin's reappearance does not function as such — a flute or voice could serve. "What functions here is the *differential effect* of the matter of expression, the fact that we play out the differences rather than crushing them against one another." What passes between components is not a message but "an effect that escapes dominant significations."

**5. Production of a component of passage in the feminine/affective register — Mme Cambremer.** Four diagnostic traits of a component of passage:
- Helps the music rather than framing it (rushes on stage during a Chopin polonaise to catch a falling wax candle — an *assisting* act that is not a commentary on the music).
- Woman-of-society AND woman-of-people at once (crosses a stratification).
- Attached to Combray's phylum, has a future in the Recherche (not anonymous).
- Contemporary-music enthusiast (Wagner, not Chopin/Liszt — carries a temporal vector of its own).

The little phrase regains autonomy, keeps Odette at distance, is perceived as "a protective goddess, a confidante of his love." Swann: "it means nothing" — resigned renunciation. The operation achieves escape from the *sexual component* of the black hole of faciality, but only that. Creative conversion will require later assemblages (the Septet's recomposition).

**6. Dream of the young-man-in-a-fez.** Dream staging: Mme Verdurin's nose grows moustache; Odette's eyes about to separate; Napoleon III is Odette's lover; Swann wants to crush her cheeks. The operation: **splitting of the principal subject of statement** — the pain is felt not by Swann but by "a young man wearing a fez" whom Swann endeavors to console. Proust's own comment: "like certain novelists, he had divided his personality between two characters"; "division of certain lower organisms." The young-man-in-a-fez *prefigures the Narrator*; at the end of the dream, Mme de Cambremer emerges on the side of the real as Swann awakens to meet his hairdresser who brings him to meet her in Combray.

Dream-remedy ingredients:
- Dissolution/neutralization of persecuting Odette-Verdurin faciality traits.
- Splitting of the enunciatory assemblage via a new character.
- Implicit promotion of a masculine-homosexual component (the young-man-in-fez prefiguring the Narrator).
- Compulsive-and-conjured staging of the intolerable Separation scene.

## The six-component schema as diagnostic

Not a recipe to follow step-by-step, but a **diagnostic inventory** of what is at stake when a black hole of passion gets dismantled. Any such dismantling needs some version of:
1. A subjective component splits (a secondary character takes up what the protagonist had foreclosed).
2. A spatial/iconic component distributes (no single reference dominates the scene).
3. A faciality component proliferates (micro-black-holes prevent rather than amplify resonance).
4. The dominant component acquires a new prototype operating transversally, not centrally.
5. A component of passage appears in a complementary register — affective, feminine, cross-stratum.
6. The subject of the statement splits (a character prefiguring the next phase emerges).

The persona-system design-relevance is direct: when the system attempts to dismantle a deep [[refrain-and-territorialization|groove]] or a locked [[faciality|face]] configuration, the dismantling cannot be a single operation. It requires this six-component sequence (or some subset scaled to the scope of the groove). Attempts to dismantle a black hole by a single operation fail; the Saint-Euverte example shows why and what works.

## Discernibilization as reducing semiotic entropy

Discernibilization reduces "semiotic entropy" of impressions; constructs universes founded on objects, relations, hyper-deterritorialized intensities. But **existential consistency** depends on the nature of the assemblage that articulates those universes — and for that, discernibilization is not enough. It needs its pair.

> "An object/relation/intensity only exists to the extent that it is found at the crossroads of various matters of expression."

This is the pivot to [[translation-not-interpretation|translation-generation]]: discernibilization gives you the components, translation makes new beings by running those components through other matters of expression.

## Writer as optical instrument, telescope not microscope

Proust's self-correction is Guattari's gloss on discernibilization's stakes:

> "The writer's work is only a kind of optical instrument which he offers the reader to enable him to discern what, without this book he might not perhaps have seen in himself."

And:

> Not a microscope but a telescope — "to make out things which were indeed very small, but only because they were situated a long way away, each of them a world in itself."

The difference matters. A microscope magnifies by proximity — it enlarges what is nearby into visibility. A telescope magnifies by discernibilization of the distant — small-because-far-away things, each their own world. **Discernibilization is the telescope operation.** What it produces is not closer access to the already-known but the *discerning* of things that are far from the user's habitual redundancies.

Criterion of the result: Proust's **"joy of the real regained"** — intensity of desire associated with emergence of deterritorialized objects. This is the aesthetico-ontological criterion that replaces beauty in signifying aesthetics. (See [[the-diagram#the-two-chemistries|two chemistries]].)
