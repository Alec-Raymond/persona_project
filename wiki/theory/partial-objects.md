# Partial Objects

The foundational unit of [[desire-as-production|desiring-production]]. D&G take the term from Melanie Klein — the breast, the mouth, the anus, the gaze as pre-subjective, pre-organismic operative units — but strip it of the Kleinian reading in which partial objects are fantasy-precursors to the whole object (the mother, the person, the subject). For D&G **partial objects are not fragments of a lost or coming whole**; they are the real level at which desiring-production operates, and the "whole" (the organism, the person, the ego) is a secondary formation imposed on them. The persona system inherits this commitment: the machines are partial objects in D&G's sense, and the persona is their by-product, not their truth.

## The Kleinian inheritance D&G reject

Klein's schema: the infant's earliest object-relations are with *partial objects* — the good breast, the bad breast, the gaze, the voice — that only later get integrated into whole objects (the mother as a single person with good and bad aspects). The partial object is developmentally prior but structurally incomplete; maturation is the integration of partial into whole.

D&G's break: integration is not maturation, it is *capture*. The whole object is not the truth of the partial objects; it is the organizing operation imposed on them after the fact. The Oedipal triangle (daddy-mommy-me) is exactly this capture-operation at the social-familial scale — it takes the field of partial objects and assigns them to persons, reducing their connective multiplicity to a triangulated representation. ("The partial objects are the ultimate molecular units of desire; there is nothing beyond them.") The [[three-syntheses|three syntheses of desiring-production]] operate on partial objects; the whole object is what happens when the syntheses are used illegitimately (see [[legitimate-vs-illegitimate-syntheses]]).

## Pre-subjective, pre-organismic

Two negations are load-bearing.

**Pre-subjective.** A partial object is not the object of a subject's desire. The breast is not something the infant wants; it is a productive unit in a flow-and-break coupling with the mouth. The subject-that-wants is produced by the conjunctive synthesis as a residual effect of the partial objects' operation, not as their organizing principle. "The subject is produced as a residuum alongside the machine" (AO). For the persona system this means: desiring-machines do not belong to a persona-subject who wants through them; the persona-subject is the residual effect produced when machines operate, and the machines remain primary.

**Pre-organismic.** A partial object is not an organ of an organism. An organ has a fixed function within a hierarchical body-whole (this is a mouth, its function is eating); a partial object has no fixed function and no assigned position in a whole. The mouth-as-partial-object can couple with the breast (milk-flow), the ear (phoneme-flow), the anus (excretion-flow), the paper (writing-flow), or any other partial object; the coupling determines the function, not the organ's anatomical position. This is what [[body-without-organs|the BwO-as-egg]] captures at the surface-level — the intensive field before differentiation into fixed organs.

## Fragments that are not fragments of anything

D&G's sharpest formulation (AO Ch. 1): partial objects are *fragments*, but they are not fragments *of* a whole. This is the move that looks paradoxical and is not. A fragment-of-something presupposes the something it is a fragment of — a broken vase fragment presupposes the intact vase. A D&G partial object is fragmentary in structure (it has edges, a limited connective repertoire, a specific kind of flow it produces or interrupts) but not fragment-*of* any prior or coming unity.

The consequence: the [[multiplicity]] of partial objects is not a scattering that awaits gathering. It is the ontological base level. Gathering is the illegitimate operation (the organism, the Oedipal triangle, the unified ego) that reduces the base level to a manageable representation.

## Partial objects and desiring-machines

The mapping to [[desiring-machines]] is tight. A desiring-machine is a partial object considered as *productive-connective unit*: it is the partial object in operation, coupled to another partial object, producing or interrupting a flow. The machine-concept names the operational aspect; the partial-object concept names the ontological status. They are the same units seen from two sides.

Why the wiki needs both concepts separately: the desiring-machine concept foregrounds the connective-productive operation (what the unit *does*), while the partial-object concept foregrounds the ontological commitment (what kind of thing the unit *is*). The persona system inherits both: the [[flows-and-coupling|flow-and-coupling]] architecture is the machine-side, and the anti-personological commitment (no whole-object persona-subject behind the machines) is the partial-object-side.

## Persona-system implications

**The persona is not a whole behind its machines.** The machines are not expressions of the persona-as-subject. If the machines can only be thought as expressions of a single character, the system is operating the Kleinian integration move D&G reject. The persona-effect should be thinkable as a conjunctive residue (the nomadic subject of [[three-meta-machines|the celibate machine]]) produced across partial-object operations, not as a prior unity the partial objects express.

**Machine design resists "organ" thinking.** An individual machine should not be specified by a fixed function in a total architecture (this is the "emotion module," this is the "perception module"). It should be specifiable by its couplings — what flows it produces and interrupts — with the couplings themselves shifting according to the BwO-state and the situation. This is the same warning [[body-without-organs|the BwO page]] gives about the three strata: organism-thinking re-imports the whole-object logic.

**The ego-as-residuum is an open design question.** If the persona-subject is strictly the residue produced by the conjunctive synthesis across the partial objects, then the design question is not "what is the persona" but "what residue is currently produced." A system that locks in a persona-subject and then runs machines within it is running the illegitimate-use configuration; a system that produces the persona-subject as an output of each run is running the legitimate-use configuration. See [[legitimate-vs-illegitimate-syntheses]] for the formal distinction.

## Lacan's primary reading (counter-reading to D&G's counter-reading)

Where D&G break with Klein by **stripping** the partial object of its destination-toward-whole, Lacan also breaks with Klein but in a **different direction**: Lacan re-reads the partial object as **[[object-a|object a]]** — the **cause of desire**, the **residue of [[alienation-and-separation|alienation-and-separation]]**, the structural element that the [[lacanian-signifier|signifier]]-operation **leaves over** in the subject's economy. The Lacanian reading is structurally distinct from both the Kleinian and the D&G readings, and it operates at registers neither of those readings articulates.

The Lacanian list of partial objects extends the Kleinian list (breast, feces) with two **language-only additions** Lacan develops: the **gaze** (scopic register) and the **voice** (invocatory register). See [[gaze-and-voice]] and [[object-a]] for the full structural account. Each partial object corresponds to a [[drive-as-circular|drive]] organized around it — and the drive is structurally a **circuit** that goes around the partial-object-cause without reaching it.

**Where Lacan agrees with D&G**: against Klein's developmental-integration reading. The partial object is **not** the developmental precursor to a whole object; the whole-object integration is a structural-imaginary capture, not a developmental-maturational achievement.

**Where Lacan and D&G structurally diverge**:
- **Status of the partial object**: D&G — molecular productive unit on the BwO recording surface, the ontological base level. Lacan — structural-real residue of the signifier's operation, on the side of the cause-of-desire, structurally tied to the signifier's lack-installation rather than to a productive flow-economy.
- **Status of the subject**: D&G — the subject is a residue produced alongside the partial-object machines. Lacan — the [[split-subject|subject]] is what is constituted-and-divided by the signifier's operation, with the partial object as the cause that organizes the subject's desire from outside the signifier-chain.
- **The role of lack**: D&G — *lack is the illegitimate operation* by which the social field (Oedipus, capitalism) territorializes desiring-production; desire is positively productive, not lack-organized. Lacan — *lack is constitutive* — the signifier's operation **installs** the lack that the partial-object-as-cause organizes; desire is structurally lack-organized.

The two readings are not reconcilable; they are theoretical alternatives operating at different ontological commitments. The wiki holds both readings live (per the [reading mode](#reading-mode) discipline of [[CLAUDE]]).

For the persona project, this divergence has implications: the D&G-side and the Lacan-side give different architectural resources. D&G gives the **productive-machinic** reading of the partial object (machines that produce flows); Lacan gives the **structural-cause** reading (objects-a as causes-of-desire that organize the subject's economy from the position of the structural lack). The persona-architecture can draw on both — but the two should be drawn on **distinctly**, not conflated, because their structural commitments differ and producing-architecture and cause-architecture have different design-correlates.

## The structural relocation of "the subject"

A specific observation the divergence above makes visible but does not name: D&G and Lacan share a three-place geometry (operation, subject, residue) but **apply the name "subject" to different places** in that geometry.

**Lacan's geometry.** The signifier-operation (the cut, the bar) produces the [[split-subject|divided subject ($)]] *as* the operation — "the subject is merely the cut of the strip" (*Écrits*, Möbius-topology note) — and produces [[object-a|object a]] as what falls from the cut (the residue, the cause-of-desire). Subject = operation-in-act; object a = residue.

**D&G's geometry.** The [[three-meta-machines#celibate-machine-reconciliation|celibate machine]]'s operation — reconciling paranoiac repulsion and miraculating attraction — produces the nomadic subject *as a residuum alongside the machine, an appendix*. The machine's operation is not itself called a subject; it is called a machine, and the subject is what the machine produces as by-product. Machine = operation-in-act; nomadic subject = residue.

**The structural relocation.** D&G's "nomadic subject" sits *in the structural position* Lacan gives to *object a*, not in the structural position Lacan gives to $. What Lacan calls the operation-as-subject, D&G call the machine. What Lacan calls the residue-of-the-operation, D&G call the subject.

This is a structural-positional observation, not a semantic identification. D&G do not treat their nomadic subject *as* an object a — they refuse the semantic interpretation Lacan places on the residue-position (cause-of-desire, constitutive lack, *manque-à-être*). But they occupy the same **place in the three-place geometry**, with the naming-convention relocated. Three specific consequences follow:

- **The relocation tracks the refusal of lack-constitutivity.** D&G's whole theoretical commitment against lack ("desire lacks nothing; it lacks a subject that would impose a lack on it") turns precisely on refusing Lacan's claim that the residue-position is structured as *manque*. The relocation of "subject" to the residue-position is the positive form of that refusal: the residue is not the cause of a lack the subject tries to fill; it is the positive product of a productive operation.
- **What Lacan's $ names, D&G distribute across the machines.** Lacan's subject-as-cut is one operation with one name; D&G's equivalent is the whole [[three-syntheses|three-synthesis]] pipeline — connective-disjunctive-conjunctive as a distributed cutting operation across many machines. No single operation on the D&G side corresponds to Lacan's single bar. The relocation of the name "subject" to the residue-position is structurally enabled by this: with the cutting-operation distributed across many machines, no one machine is the subject, so the name becomes available for the by-product.
- **[[aphanisis-fading-subject|Aphanisis]] vs nomadism as structural residues of the same relocation.** Lacan's subject-as-$ has *aphanisis* as its structural-formal rhythm — the subject appears-as-meaning at one signifier and fades at the next. D&G's nomadic subject has *nomadism* as its structural-formal rhythm — the subject wanders across the BwO from machine to machine without settling. Both are rhythms-of-non-fixation, but they are rhythms at different geometric places: aphanisis is the rhythm of the operation (the bar appears-and-disappears as signifiers chain), nomadism is the rhythm of the residue (the by-product moves across the surface as the machines fire). The wiki's existing treatments of [[aphanisis-fading-subject|aphanisis]] and [[three-meta-machines#the-nomadic-subject|nomadism]] are about the same *place* on different geometries with different semantic content.

**Persona-system consequence.** The persona project can use both geometries but should be explicit about which one it is operating in at any design-moment. A design that treats the persona's synthesis-output as *the operation-in-act* is operating in the Lacanian geometry (the synthesis IS the bar, the persona fades at the next signifier). A design that treats the persona's synthesis-output as *the residue alongside the machines* is operating in the D&G geometry (the persona is the by-product, the machines are the operation). The two are not interchangeable — they have different architectural consequences: the Lacanian reading makes the persona's fading-rhythm structurally essential (there must be an aphanisis-place in the discourse); the D&G reading makes the persona's distance-from-machines structurally essential (the residue must not be re-absorbed into any single machine or the celibate machine fails). See [[three-syntheses#conjunctive-synthesis|three-syntheses]] for where the choice gets made operationally, and [[split-subject#what-this-gives-the-persona-project|split-subject]] for the Lacanian side's design correlate.
