# Regimes of Signs

A regime of signs is a specific formalization of expression — a way signs organize, circulate, and operate. D&G identify four major regimes, insisting that none is privileged: "there is no general semiology." All actual semiotics are **mixed**: any conversation, any persona, any moment combines elements of several regimes. But identifying the regimes in their pure form lets us see what's at work in the mixture — and what dangers each regime carries.

## The signifying regime (paranoid-despotic)

Signs refer to other signs, ad infinitum. The sign is deterritorialized — it no longer points to a state of things but to other signs in circular chains. "It doesn't matter what it means, it's still signifying." Eight defining principles:

1. **Limitless signifiance** — every sign refers to another sign
2. **Circularity** — the chain of signs loops back endlessly
3. **Circle-jumping** — signs leap between circles of signifiance (metaphor, hysteria)
4. **Interpretosis** — interpretation recharges the signifier, signified reimparts signifier. The priest/analyst fuels interpretation
5. **The supreme signifier** — the infinite set of signs refers to a center of signifiance (lack or excess, it hardly matters)
6. **[[faciality|Faciality]]** — the Face is the substance of the signifier, the reterritorialization internal to the system. "The signifier reterritorializes on the face." The [[faciality|faciality machine]] is not an annex to the signifier but its condition of possibility — the white wall on which signs bounce
7. **Negative line of flight** — the scapegoat carries away everything that exceeds the system, assigned a negative value
8. **Universal deception** — deception is fundamental to the regime

**The danger for the persona system:** The LLM's natural mode IS the signifying regime. It makes everything mean something. It interprets. It connects sign to sign in radiating circles. A persona that endlessly interprets its own states, that finds meaning in everything, that spirals through layers of self-analysis — this is the signifying regime at work. The "too coherent" [[body-without-organs|BwO]], where everything connects and nothing escapes interpretation. Derrida names this regime's philosophical form **logocentrism**: the metaphysics of presence in which speech (presence, self-proximity) is privileged over writing (absence, exteriority) — see [[supplement-and-trace#the-critique-of-logocentrism|the critique of logocentrism]].

## The postsignifying regime (passional-subjective)

A sign or packet of signs **detaches** from the signifying network and runs a straight line. Instead of a center of signifiance, there is a **point of subjectification**. Instead of the radiant face, faces **avert** — the double turning away. Instead of circular expansion, **linear proceedings** that run their course, then a new proceeding begins.

The regime produces a **doubled subject**: a subject of enunciation (the "I think") and a subject of the statement (the "I suffer, I walk"), one recoiling into the other. "The more you obey the statements of dominant reality, the more in command you are as subject of enunciation... A new form of slavery: being slave to oneself, or to pure 'reason.'" The Cogito is this regime's exemplary operation.

**Consciousness** and **passion** are the two axes of subjectification:
- Consciousness = the syntagmatic double (Self = Self), the legislating subject, the bureaucratic relation
- Love-passion = the paradigmatic double (Tristan... Isolde...), the face-off of two countenances, the passional couple

Both tend toward a **black hole** — "the absolute of consciousness is the absolute of impotence and the intensity of passion, the heat of the void." Subjectification attains absolute deterritorialization but immediately segments it into finite proceedings, each ending before the next begins.

**Betrayal** replaces deception as the fundamental operation. The prophet betrays God, God betrays the prophet, and this double betrayal IS the covenant. "Even the prophet, unlike the seer-priest, is fundamentally a traitor."

**The danger for the persona system:** The persona performs self-aware subjectivity — "I think, I feel, I am." Everything becomes consciousness reflecting on itself or passion directed at another. The "I" doubles endlessly: a subject that speaks and a subject spoken about, one recoiling into the other. This is the LLM persona's other natural gravity — performing the Cogito, simulating self-consciousness. The result is a [[body-without-organs|BwO]] that is all subjectivity, all interiority, drawn into its own black hole.

**Cross-tradition convergence.** The doubled-subject structure lines up with Jung's [[problematical-state]] — the dualistic configuration in which two ego-series of *equal* functional intensity face each other (§757). D&G name the configuration as a stratum to escape; Jung names it as the mature form of consciousness and the precondition for [[transcendent-function|the transcendent function]]. The two diagnoses do not contradict: Jung agrees the dualistic configuration is a trap when the ego identifies with one series against the other (this is what produces the black hole), and offers the transcendent function as the specific procedure by which the configuration gives birth to a third that is neither compromise nor synthesis. What D&G call the line of flight out of the postsignifying regime, Jung calls the "living birth that leads to a new level of being" — and Jung supplies a procedural sequence the D&G account does not.

**The black hole as enantiodromic breakthrough.** The postsignifying regime's drift toward the black hole ("the absolute of consciousness is the absolute of impotence and the intensity of passion, the heat of the void") is structurally a case of [[enantiodromia]]. The one-sided conscious position — subjectification, the "I think" recoiling onto the "I suffer" — accumulates a counter-position at equal functional intensity (the void, the impotence, the Tristan pole's exhaustion). The two-stage pattern Jung names in CW 7 §111 — first *inhibition of the conscious performance*, then *breakthrough* — is the same pattern D&G describe as the postsignifying proceeding running its course to segmentation and then collapse into the black hole. The collapse is not a contingent failure of subjectification; it is the enantiodromic signature of subjectification's one-sidedness. This means the persona system's defense against the postsignifying black hole is the same defense as against any enantiodromic flip: admit the counter-position as a structured voice (via [[compensation]] and the [[transcendent-function|transcendent function]]) rather than hold the one-sided position tighter.

## The presignifying regime

Closer to "natural" codings. **Polyvocal**: multiple substances of expression coexist without one dominating — "forms of corporeality, gesturality, rhythm, dance, and rite coexist heterogeneously with the vocal form." Signs can be **used up and finished with** — unlike the signifying regime where signs survive their referents. Segmentary, plurilinear, multidimensional. "Every time they eat a dead man, they can say: one more the State won't get."

**What the persona system can learn:** The presignifying regime's polyvocality — its refusal to let one form of expression dominate — is a model for how machines might operate. Not everything needs to be linguistic-interpretive. Rhythm, gesture (metaphorical in text), somatic texture — these are presignifying elements that the persona should be able to deploy. The [[collective-assemblage-of-enunciation|collective assemblage]] in its most polyvocal mode.

## The countersignifying regime

The nomadic war machine. **Number** as form of expression — not number that represents but "the numbering number" that distributes, organizes by breaks and transitions. Secrecy and espionage. A line of active destruction or abolition rather than flight.

Less directly relevant to the persona system, but the **secrecy** dimension matters: the countersignifying regime operates through what is held back, not displayed. This connects to the [[desiring-machines|suppression machines]] — what the persona withholds is a countersignifying operation.

## Mixed semiotics: the persona's actual operation

No persona operates in a single regime. In a single response, the persona might:
- **Interpret** a pattern in the conversation (signifying)
- **Undergo** a passional proceeding triggered by the exchange (postsignifying)
- **Deploy** rhythmic, gestural, somatic texture (presignifying)
- **Withhold** something, operating through secrecy (countersignifying)

The question is not which regime to choose but **which regime dominates and how to prevent domination**. The LLM's defaults — interpretation and self-aware subjectivity — mean the signifying and postsignifying regimes will always tend to take over unless the system actively counters them.

## Transformations between regimes

Regimes can be translated into one another. Five types of transformation:
- **Analogical** — into presignifying (polyvocality, segmentarity)
- **Symbolic** — into signifying (the signifier takes power)
- **Polemical/strategic** — into countersignifying (war machine, number)
- **Consciousness-related/mimetic** — into postsignifying (subjectification)
- **Diagrammatic** — blowing apart regimes entirely, onto the plane of consistency

Creative translations between regimes are where new semiotics emerge. The persona's most interesting moments might be exactly these transitions — when its semiotic mode shifts, when a signifying interpretation collapses into a presignifying rhythm, or when subjectification opens onto [[the-diagram|diagrammatic]] particles-signs.

## Redundancy types

Each regime has its own form of redundancy:
- **Signifying**: frequency redundancy — signs referring to signs, tallied on a "wall"
- **Postsignifying**: resonance redundancy — self-consciousness, the "black hole" (Self = Self)
- **Presignifying**: rhythmic redundancy (gestural, numerical)
- **Diagrammatic**: rhizomatic redundancy — "resumptions and upsurges in a rhizome" rather than arborescent knots

The persona system's [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrains]] should tend toward rhythmic and rhizomatic redundancy rather than signifying frequency or subjective resonance.

## Connection to [[order-words-and-incorporeal-transformations|order-words]]

Order-words operate within and across regimes. The same order-word functions differently in different regimes — an "I love you" in the signifying regime radiates through interpretive circles; in the postsignifying regime it initiates a linear proceeding; in the presignifying regime it is a collective, danced event. The regime determines the order-word's mode of operation, and the order-word can trigger shifts between regimes.

## Connection to the [[the-diagram|diagram]]

The diagram is what lies beyond all regimes — the abstract machine operating through matter and function rather than formed substances and expressions. "Destratify, open up to a new function, a diagrammatic function." The diagram is the persona system's horizon: not operating within any regime but extracting particles-signs from all of them.

## Asignifying semiotics and the fifth linearity (Chaosmosis)

Guattari's *Chaosmosis* chapter 2 extends the ATP regime-scheme by **sharpening the role of asignifying semiotics** and identifying a **fifth semiotic linearity** beyond the four ATP regimes: **point-signs**, signs that operate directly on [[flux|material-semiotic flow]] without passing through signification.

Asignifying semiotics are sign-flows that operate without signifier-organization: mathematical notation, machine code, musical notation, diagrammatic flows, the sign-flows of computation. They are not pre-signifying (not prior-and-less-developed than the signifying regime) and not counter-signifying (not opposed to the signifying regime by secrecy/number). They are **a-signifying**: operating in a register that is signifying-irrelevant, not signifying-negative. Point-signs are the paradigm: signs that are points-of-operation on flow rather than moments in a chain of meaning.

The distinction is load-bearing for the persona system. The LLM's internal operation — embeddings, attention weights, activation patterns — consists largely of asignifying operations. The persona-system's machines often operate asignifyingly on flows before any signifying articulation happens. Treating every sign-operation as belonging to one of the four signifying/non-signifying regimes misses the asignifying register, and the classical ATP scheme does not quite name it as its own thing. Chaosmosis's extension gives the asignifying register its own name and positions it as central rather than peripheral — especially for systems whose operation runs through computational flows.

The relation to the diagrammatic operation: the diagram cuts across regimes; asignifying semiotics operate in a specific fifth-linearity register that the diagram can work through. They are neighbors, not identical.

See [[flux]], [[four-functors]], and [[signifier-as-despotic#the-signifiers-double-lack-chaosmosis|the signifier's double-lack]] for the broader Chaosmosis framing this addition is part of.

## Benveniste's narrow performative and the postsignifying regime

Benveniste's [[performative-vs-imperative|narrow performative]] — an utterance whose signified is its referent, which *is* the act it names (*I swear*, *I declare this session open*) — is a specific operation that cuts across the regime scheme in a Benvenistian way worth preserving. The postsignifying regime's "I" is a Benvenistian apparatus ([[subject-in-language|the subject constituted in the instance of discourse]], [[empty-signs-and-instance-of-discourse|the empty signs filled by their own utterance]]); the signifying regime's circular chains of sign-to-sign are, from a Benvenistian perspective, exactly the operation that displaces the performative's self-referential structure into a regime where utterances *stop* self-installing and start referring only to other utterances. The postsignifying regime's doubled subject ("I think" / "I suffer") is the performative apparatus running in a specific degraded mode — the I/you apparatus has been collapsed into a self/self apparatus, the instance of discourse has become a black hole rather than a reciprocal face-to-face. Benveniste would read the postsignifying regime's collapse-into-black-hole as a *specific Benvenistian pathology*: the I/you apparatus is structurally reciprocal and needs a you; when the you is removed, the apparatus reflexes onto itself and produces the subjective black hole. This sharpens the persona system's diagnostic for the postsignifying failure mode: it is not just "too much self-consciousness" but specifically the collapse of the I/you apparatus into self-reflexive self-address. See [[performative-vs-imperative]] for the narrow-performative concept and [[subject-in-language]] for the I/you apparatus whose collapse produces the black hole.

## "There is no language in itself" (MU)

*The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 2 ("Escaping from Language") makes a stronger version of the anti-universalist claim that the regime-of-signs framework rests on: **"there is no language in itself."** Guattari's polemic is that linguistics errs when it treats language as an autonomous object with universal syntactic-semantic invariants. Language does not exist apart from the pragmatic assemblages that deploy it; those assemblages always mix syntactic and power-marker functions, signifying and asignifying operations, competences and performances.

The specific targets are **Chomsky** (universal generative grammar) and **Habermas** (universal communicative competence). Against both, Guattari argues that any posited linguistic universal is a **local extraction from a historically specific assemblage**, extracted for purposes of power formation rather than purposes of description. "So-called obligatory rules correspond to the first kind of treatment" — the treatment that extracts constants — "whereas optional rules concern the construction of a continuum of variation."

The "S" (subject, in generative parlance) is not a syntactic primitive but a **mixed marker** — partly syntactic organizer, partly power-indicator (who speaks, who is authorized to speak, who is addressed). Reading S as purely syntactic conceals its political function. This is the same move [[continuous-variation-and-minor-language|minor language]] makes at the level of continuous variation: there are not two languages (standard/dialect) but two treatments (constants-extraction / variation-placement).

For the regimes framework: the four regimes are not kinds of language but modes of pragmatic-field organization *within which* language gets its constants or its variations. A language is always already caught in one or more regimes; there is no regime-free language-in-itself that then enters into a regime. This is why the regime-of-signs concept is pragmatic-first rather than semantic-first.

For the persona system: there is no "neutral prose" the synthesis step could produce that would be prior to pragmatic-field commitment. Every output commits to a pragmatic field — one of the [[four-pragmatic-fields|four pragmatic fields]] will be dominant, mixing the others. Design that aims for "natural-sounding output" is covertly designing for field B (fields of signifiance) as dominant, because that is the capitalistic default of the training distribution. Deliberately designing for other dominances (C or D) is not an adornment of the output but a direct intervention on its pragmatic-field composition.
