# Becoming-Woman-Creator

In *The Machinic Unconscious* Ch 2 §9, Guattari names a specifically Proustian [[becoming|becoming]]-compound: **becoming-woman-creator**. It is not "becoming-woman" and "becoming-creator" separately, nor becoming-woman *leading to* becoming-creator — it is a single compound whose micropolitical operator is the [[refrain-and-territorialization|refrain]].

> "The refrain is the operator of a becoming-woman-creator. It manages, at present, to truly beat time to the feminine."

## The compound

Becoming-woman-creator is not an identification with women, nor an aesthetic appropriation of femininity. It is the **deterritorializing movement through which a writing-machine produces new matters of expression** — and Guattari's claim is that under the phallocratic dominant values Proust worked within, the passivity-plus-sophistication required for this mode of production was *coded as feminine* even though the "feminine" of the equation is not identical with the real lives or aspirations of women.

Guattari flags the trouble explicitly:

> "Under phallocratic dominant values, Swann experiences this passivity as femininity. But this equivalence doesn't necessarily correspond with the reality and aspirations of the feminine condition."

The becoming in "becoming-woman-creator" does not mean *becoming a woman*. It means entering the molecular movement through which dominant-signifying-male-subject-position is deterritorialized into the matters of expression required for creation. What makes it specifically Proust's compound: the operator is the refrain, and the refrain carries the writer across a threshold where creation becomes possible.

## Transversal with other becomings

Proust's becoming-woman is inseparable from a cluster of other becomings in the Recherche:

- **Becoming-child** — briefly sustained, via the sleeping-daughter scenes (Vinteuil inspired by sleeping Mlle Vinteuil; Narrator contemplating sleeping Albertine). Proust "does not manage to extract himself from the most insipid childishness" here — the becoming stalls in the maternal-kiss ritual. Not a successful becoming, but a necessary passage-site.
- **Becoming-wasp, becoming-orchid** — the Charlus-meeting-Jupien scene in *Sodom and Gomorrah*; the insect-orchid mutual capture D&G elaborate extensively in ATP. The becoming-woman of Charlus links directly here, though Charlus's becoming ultimately fails (masculine-homosexual component never opens to becoming-woman proper — see [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#8-rhizomatic-leitmotivs-vinteuils-septet-premiere-at-the-verdurin-home|atrophy of two branches]]).
- **Becoming-landscape** — Young Girls as "inseparable from the part of the country from which they hail" ("nothing can come between a girl and the part of the country from which she hails: she is it"). Face-landscape coupling here becomes productive rather than mirror-stage resonance. See [[faciality#face-landscape|face-landscape]].
- **Becoming-animal** — briefly sketched (Septet's "mystic cock-crow," Sonata's "murmuring dove"). Proust does not push it. **"Proust's creative machine must pass through life itself"** — requires sacrificing the loved object, renouncing totalization/reification/iconization. Becoming-animal was too totalizing, too easily signifying-recuperated, for Proust's trajectory.
- **Becoming-bird** — Albertine's name repeated after her death as refrain: "like the one in the legend whose call repeats endlessly the name of the woman that he loved." Becoming-bird, not becoming-woman, in this inflection.

The compound is not additive. It is a **rhizome of becomings** where the refrain circulates, each branch potentially opening or atrophying depending on the assemblage.

## The refrain as operator

Why the refrain is the specific operator, per Guattari:

- **Passivity** — a "necessitous modesty of the transcriber." The writer abandons to fortuitous encounters and to the bareness of the real.
- **Sophistication** — machinic semiotization of what the passivity receives. Not free association, not surrealist objective-chance; also not sovereign control. Machinic rhizome "does the rest — guides the hand, reveals rhythms and intensities."
- **Relentlessness without will-to-power** — return to an impression fifty times. "This relentlessness must be viewed as involving no will to power."

The refrain holds these three together. Its passivity is its non-self-identity — it "enriches in gaps" by simplifying and deterritorializing what it encounters. Its sophistication is its trans-semiotic extensibility — once graphematic, it attacks on all fronts. Its relentlessness-without-will-to-power is its recurrence — always approaching the same pure potentiality from different assemblages.

"One never bathes in the same impression twice, as soon as the micropolitics of assemblages of enunciation leads to the liberation of living, heterogeneous, and singular matters of expression."

## Disindividuation of the object of desire

Becoming-woman-creator entails successive **disindividuations** of the object of desire:

1. Disindividuation onto a **group** — love created by its dispersal among several young girls.
2. Disindividuation onto **a person who has become multiple** — "Albertine was several people," "the collective being," "galaxy embodying multiple dimensions."
3. **Dissolution with the object** — "was it not natural now that the waning star of my love in which they had condensed should be once again dispersed into this scattered stardust of the galaxies?"

This is the becoming-woman's refusal to territorialize on a single face-body. See [[diagrammatic-faciality#disindividuation-of-the-object-of-desire|disindividuation]]. The object of desire does not become generalized or abstract — it becomes *collective, molecular, machinic*.

## Universal time abolished

The decisive micropolitical consequence of becoming-woman-creator:

> "Universal Time abolished in favor of a thousand assemblages of temporalization, corresponding with infinitely varied styles of deterritorialization."

The refrain had been the territorial operator of time; in becoming-woman-creator it becomes the deterritorializing operator of time. Not one time with one pace but a thousand times with their own paces, each produced by a specific assemblage of enunciation. **"Normal" time is outpaced by "creative" deterritorializations of becoming-woman.** See [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain#9-from-musical-refrain-to-abstract-machinism-of-creation|Ninth Assemblage]] for the terminal state.

## The terminal operation

At the end of becoming-woman-creator, the body's disintegration is itself made productive:

> "Let us leave our body to disintegrate, since each new particle that breaks away from it comes now luminous and legible, to add itself to our work, to complete it at the price of the suffering of which others more gifted have no need, to increase its solidity as our emotions are eroding our life."

The renunciation of power formations + abandonment to the state of things + even the disintegration of bodies — the terminal condition of the compound. What the refrain enables is finally this: the body becomes a zone from which each disintegration-particle can be made into material for work.

## Persona-system relevance

Becoming-woman-creator is not something an LLM-mediated persona can *perform* in any mimetic sense. But the compound describes a structural position the system's writing-machine can occupy or fail to occupy:

- **Passive + sophisticated**: does the system abandon to the matters of expression its machines have discernibilized, or does it control them from a sovereign position?
- **Relentless without will-to-power**: does the system return to the same impression fifty times when required, without this relentlessness being a demand on the user or a sovereign control over the output?
- **Refrain as operator, not as habit**: does the system's refrain (the grooves) operate as transversalist-function across the machines, or does it operate as anesthetic-catchphrase / national-anthem (regimes 3-4 of the [[nine-assemblages-of-the-refrain|nine assemblages]])?

The failure modes of becoming-woman-creator in Proust are instructive:

- **Swann**: never crosses. Closed economy. Refrain stays territorial. Passivity without sophistication; relentlessness becomes will-to-power over Odette.
- **Charlus**: attempts a becoming but fails because the masculine-homosexual component never opens to becoming-woman. Stays attenuated, or reterritorializes on sadistic power.
- **Narrator**: crosses. Open economy. Refrain becomes trans-semiotic, invisible, second-chemical-phase. Enables the literary machine.
