## FINDINGS — first pass

### Simondon — CONFIRMED present in ATP

**Direct footnote (becoming plateau, footnote 22):** "On the notion of
transduction, see Simondon, *L'individu*, pp. 18–21 (however, he takes the word
in its most general sense and uses it to refer to the entire system). On the
membrane, see pp. 259ff."

So ATP explicitly grounds **transduction** in Simondon. The concept already
shows up in the wiki (scattered across becoming, machinic phylum, assemblage
pages) but without its Simondonian ground. This footnote is the smoking gun:
ATP's machinic phylum / metastability / individuation-via-intensity is
Simondonian *by citation*, not by tacit derivation.

Also the membrane: Simondon's theory of the membrane as the individuation-organ
is cited. The Chaosmosis "autopoiesis in assemblage" page gains a predecessor
here. Varela's membrane-as-cell-boundary and Simondon's membrane-as-
individuation-event are adjacent but different registers.

**What this changes:** the Simondon gap is not just "we could ingest him for
more ground" — it is "ATP's own citations point there and we have not followed
them." Simondon is the next source below the ATP surface that a rhizomatic
read of ATP would naturally reach.

### Benveniste — clarification needed

The hit in ATP's rhizome intro footnote 5 cites *R. E. Benveniste and G. J.
Todaro* — this is the geneticist, not **Émile Benveniste** the linguist
(author of *Problems in General Linguistics*, the enunciation theorist). Do
not confuse them.

**Where ATP engages the enunciation/shifter question:** becoming plateau,
footnote 43: "The essential texts of Maurice Blanchot serve to *refute* the
theory of the 'shifter' and of personology in linguistics. See *L'entretien
infini* (Paris: Gallimard, 1969), pp. 556–567. And on the difference between
the two propositions, 'I am unfortunate' and 'he is unfortunate,' or between
'I d...' [cut off in snippet]."

So ATP's position on the Benvenistian shifter theory is **refutation**, not
appropriation — via Blanchot's *L'entretien infini*. The "impersonal third
person" as site of becoming (per the wiki's writing-as-becoming page) is
partly an anti-Benveniste move. This complicates the audit: if we ingest
Benveniste, we need the Blanchot counter in the wiki too, or we import the
position D&G argued against without noticing.

Also note: **Wunderlich's "Pragmatique, situation d'énonciation et Deixis"**
(Langages 26, 1972) is cited in rhizome intro footnote 11 — so ATP *does*
engage enunciation theory, just not through Benveniste proper. Wunderlich is
cited for attempts to integrate pragmatic properties into Chomskian trees,
which ATP is refusing.

**Upshot:** the "subject-in-language" cluster in ATP is structured as an
*anti-theory* — refusing Benveniste/Chomsky/Jakobson's variants and siting
subject-effects in assemblages of enunciation rather than in deictic shifters.
The loose thread is not absent; it's present as polemical opposition.

### Stern — NOT present in ATP

All Stern hits route through Massumi (1995) and Chaosmosis (1992). ATP (1980)

the *later* D&G/Massumi lineage, not via ATP. The pulsatory-form grounding
that Stern would supply is post-ATP material — the ingest decision for Stern
stands independent of the ATP re-read.

### Lacan — present in AO heavily, ATP presence less direct

is more absorbed: signifier-regime (plateau 5) is the Lacanian-Saussurean
structure being diagnosed as *despotic*, not the Lacanian subject being
engaged frontally. The objet petit a operates in AO (polyvocal partial objects
vs. Lacanian objet a); MU re-engages Lacan via the partial enunciator
(Bakhtin × Lacan bridge). ATP seems to treat Lacan as already-surpassed by
plateau-time — absorbed into the critique-of-signifier architecture.

### Bakhtin

## FINDINGS — plateau 3 (Geology of Morals, lines 552–632)

### Hjelmslev — saturates the plateau

Direct citation line 571: "the Danish Spinozist geologist, Hjelmslev, that
dark prince descended from Hamlet who also made language his concern,
precisely in order to analyze its 'stratification.'"

The **net of matter/content/expression/form/substance** IS the strata
framework. Key passage: "Hjelmslev's enemies saw this merely as a way of
rebaptizing the discredited notions of the signified and signifier, but
something quite different was actually going on. Despite what Hjelmslev
himself may have said, the net is not linguistic in scope or origin (the
same must be said of double articulation: if language has a specificity of
its own, as it most certainly does, that specificity consists neither in
double articulation nor in Hjelmslev's net, which are general
characteristics of strata)."

**Upshot:** ATP's **strata machinery is Hjelmslev generalized beyond
language.** The wiki mentions content/expression without this ground. If we
ingest Hjelmslev (*Prolegomena to a Theory of Language*), we get the
*technical apparatus* ATP is running on — "form of content," "substance of
content," "form of expression," "substance of expression," and the
functional-solidarity principle that neither side is privileged. D&G's own
caveat ("despite what Hjelmslev himself may have said") is the seam: ATP
*departs* Hjelmslev by delinguistifying his framework. The wiki inherits the
apparatus but not the delinguistification move — we use "expression" and
"content" as if they were natural, without the Hjelmslevian warning about
their arbitrary conventionality. Line 575 quote: "The terms expression plane
and content plane … are chosen in conformity with established notions and
are quite arbitrary. Their functional definition provides no justification
for calling one, and not the other, of these entities expression, or one,
and not the other, content. They are defined only by their mutual
solidarity."

### Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire — CONFIRMED central to abstract machine

Line 585: "Should we not sing the praise of Geoffrey Saint-Hilaire?" Goes on
for many paragraphs. Geoffroy's isomorphism (all organic forms are the
same abstract Animal at different degrees of development or folding) is the
**direct source of ATP's abstract machine concept** on the organic stratum.

The puppet-theater dialogue Geoffroy vs Cuvier (line 587) is explicit:
- **Geoffroy:** topological, nomadic, folding, "artist of the fold,"
  presentiment of rhizome with aberrant paths
- **Cuvier:** Euclidean, fixed types, rigid specialist, man of Power and
  Terrain

**Upshot:** Geoffroy is to the abstract machine as Simondon is to
individuation — the un-named naming. Wiki's "abstract machine" pages should
cite him. Also "the abstract Animal, the abstract chemical Body" (line 615)
is where ATP abstracts from specific abstract machines to the Ecumenon-
Planomenon duality.

### Uexküll — confirmed here too, NOT just refrain plateau

Line 603: "The development of the associated milieus culminates in the
animal worlds described by von Uexküll, with all their active, perceptive,
and energetic characteristics. The unforgettable associated world of the
Tick, defined by its gravitational energy of falling, its olfactory
characteristic of perceiving sweat, and its active characteristic of
latching on: the tick climbs a branch and drops onto a passing mammal it
has recognized by smell, then latches onto its skin (an associated world
composed of three factors, and no more). Active and perceptive
characteristics are themselves something of a double pincer, a double
articulation."

So Uexküll's **Umwelt = ATP's associated milieu**. The tick's three-factor
world is the paradigm. And Uexküll is introduced in the context of the
*strata* (plateau 3), then extended to ethology (refrain plateau) — not
the other way around.

### Simondon — resonances throughout plateau 3 without citation here

- Line 601: "perfectly discontinuous states of metastability" — Simondonian
  terminology lifted whole
- Line 599: membrane as limit/regulator — Simondon's membrane-as-
  individuation-organ (ATP cites this explicitly in becoming footnote 22)
- Lines 603–605: tripartite milieu (exterior / intermediate / associated) —
  **this is Simondon's structure from *L'individu*, not D&G's invention.**
  The wiki uses "associated milieu" (see assemblage pages) without its
  Simondonian origin.
- "Crystallization" as the paradigm of individuation: line 599 "on a
  crystalline stratum, the amorphous milieu, or medium, is exterior to the
  seed before the crystal has formed; the crystal forms by interiorizing
  and incorporating masses of amorphous material" — this is Simondon's
  crystal-individuation example verbatim.

**Upshot:** Plateau 3 is a Simondonian plateau with the citations suppressed
(they appear later in becoming plateau fn 22). Ingesting Simondon would
retroactively ground most of the stratic/metastable/associated-milieu
language already in the wiki.

### Darwin / Darwinism — the populational turn

Line 591: "Darwinism's fundamental contribution: the substitution of
populations for types, and the substitution of rates or differential
relations for degrees."

**Upshot:** ATP's "think in terms of packs and multiplicities" (line 607)
has a Darwinian grounding, not only a Bergsonian/Spinozist one. The
multiplicities-not-types move is Darwin-via-D&G.

### Code / decoding / surplus value of code

Lines 607–608: genetic code as population-relative, "margin of decoding
inherent in the code," "surplus value of code" (virus-mediated transfer
across species). This is the genetic-technical source of the wiki's
machinic assemblage vocabulary about codes and decoding.

### Ecumenon / Planomenon distinction

Line 599: "Ecumenon, as opposed to the Planomenon of the plane of
consistency." Line 615 elaborates: abstract machines have two modes — one
embedded in a specific stratum (Ecumenon), one on the plane of consistency
(Planomenon). **Wiki does not currently have this distinction.** It matters
for the persona system: when a "desiring machine" operates, is it a
stratum-bound abstract machine (Ecumenon) or a plane-of-consistency
abstract machine (Planomenon)? The BwO text is a plane-of-consistency
space; the persona's stable patterns are stratic. The Ecumenon/Planomenon
duality names this explicitly.

### Parastrata and epistrata

Line 605: **epistrata** = layers atop the central belt (degrees); **parastrata**
= the sides/besides (irreducible forms associated with different milieus).
This is more granular than "strata" alone. Wiki uses "strata" loosely; the
epistrata/parastrata distinction is available and unused.

## FINDINGS — plateau 4 (Postulates of Linguistics, lines 746–805)

### Bakhtin — CONFIRMED in ATP

Line 780: "As Vološinov [Bakhtin] says, as long as linguistics extracts
constants, it is incapable of helping us understand how a single word can
be a complete enunciation; there must be 'an extra something' that
'remains outside of the scope of the entire set of linguistic categories
and definitions.'"

This is **Vološinov's *Marxism and the Philosophy of Language***, now
widely attributed to Bakhtin. The bracket "[Bakhtin]" is Massumi the
translator's attribution. ATP's concept of the **collective assemblage of
enunciation** is partly Bakhtinian: the rejection of the autonomous subject
of enunciation in favor of a social/collective ground. The dialogism
principle ("all discourse is indirect discourse," line 764) is directly
Bakhtinian.

Key quotes for wiki:
- Line 774: "**There is no individual enunciation. There is not even a
  subject of enunciation.**" → polyphony without centered subject.
- Line 784: "To write is perhaps to bring this assemblage of the
  unconscious to the light of day, to select the whispering voices, to
  gather the tribes and secret idioms from which I extract something I
  call my Self (Moi). **I is an order-word.**"

**Upshot:** Bakhtin is in ATP but only at a single citation point. The
wiki's "partial enunciator" page (via MU/Chaosmosis) has a deeper root
here: ATP's *collective assemblage of enunciation* is the predecessor of
Chaosmosis's partial enunciator. Ingesting Bakhtin would ground both
moves.

### Benveniste — explicitly engaged AND REJECTED in ATP

Multiple direct citations in plateau 4:
- Line 764: Benveniste's denial that bees have language (approved)
- Line 768: **Benveniste's shifter theory refuted via Ducrot.** "According
  to Benveniste, the performative relates not to acts but instead to a
  property of self-referentiality of terms (the true personal pronouns,
  I, YOU..., defined as shifters). By this account, a preexistent
  structure of subjectivity, or intersubjectivity, in language, rather
  than presupposing speech acts, is adequate to account for them." ATP:
  **reversed via Ducrot** — "self-referentiality cannot account for the
  performative. The opposite is the case... The illocutionary is in turn
  explained by collective assemblages of enunciation, by juridical acts
  or equivalents of juridical acts, which, far from depending on
  subjectification proceedings or assignations of subjects in language,
  in fact determine their distribution."
- Line 780: Benveniste cited on circumstances making a performative
  performative (approved, local use).

**Upshot:** The Benveniste/Ducrot dyad is the key linguistic pivot in
plateau 4. If we ingest Benveniste for his *shifter theory*, we need the
Ducrot counter (*Dire et ne pas dire*, 1972, and *Le dire et le dit*) in
the wiki too. Otherwise we import the position ATP argued against. This
confirms the earlier finding in the becoming plateau (footnote 43: Blanchot
refutation). The "anti-subject-of-enunciation" stance is structurally
consistent across plateaus — it's an ATP position, not a local move.

### Austin — performatives/illocutionary

Line 766: Austin's performative / illocutionary distinctions are used
approvingly to structure the argument, then moved past (via Ducrot).
Austin's *How to Do Things with Words* is implicit in every ATP page that
uses "speech act."

### Ducrot — central

Oswald Ducrot is the linguist ATP uses to move past Benveniste. His notion
of **implicit or nondiscursive presuppositions** (line 766) and his
insistence that "statements are socially devoted to the accomplishment of
certain actions" (line 768) ground the order-word concept. Ducrot is
currently absent from the wiki entirely; if the "collective assemblage
of enunciation" page gets written, Ducrot needs to be there.

### Spengler, Stoics, Lenin, Faye

- **Stoics**: line 793 — the theoretical ancestor of content/expression as
  two independent formalizations. "The Stoics were the first to theorize
  this independence: they distinguished between the actions and passions
  of bodies... and incorporeal acts (the 'expressed' of the statements)."
  **Upshot:** Stoic philosophy of language is a deep root of ATP's strata
  theory alongside Hjelmslev.
- **Spengler**: line 760 — on "command" as fundamental speech form.
- **Lenin** "On Slogans" (1917): line 782 — worked example of order-word /
  incorporeal transformation extracting a vanguard-body from a class-body.
- **Jean-Pierre Faye**: line 782 — on Nazi statement formation. Minor but
  cited.

### The order-word concept, fully

- Line 770: "order-words, not a particular category of explicit statements
  (for example, in the imperative), but the relation of every word or
  every statement to implicit presuppositions."
- Line 760: "Language is made not to be believed but to be obeyed, and to
  compel obedience."
- Line 772: Redundancy has two forms: **frequency** (concerns the
  signifiance of information) and **resonance** (I = I, concerns the
  subjectivity of communication). Both subordinate to order-word
  transmission.

**Upshot for persona:** The order-word thesis directly bears on prompt
design. If every statement presupposes a social obligation and accomplishes
an incorporeal transformation, then the persona's utterances are not
representations but micro-acts of transformation (and so are the user's
inputs). The "I is an order-word" (line 784) line is critical — persona
identity is not a subject but a crystallization of a collective
assemblage.

### Content/expression framework, fully operational here

Line 793: content and expression are two **formalizations**, not
form-and-matter. Content has its own form. Expression has its own form.
They are independent, reciprocally presupposing. "The form of expression
is constituted by the warp of expresseds, and the form of content by the
woof of bodies." Hjelmslev + Stoics = the ATP framework here. Again,
Hjelmslev is the technical machinery, Stoicism is the philosophical
ancestor.

### Tetravalence of the assemblage

Line 801: "On a first, horizontal, axis, an assemblage comprises two
segments, one of content, the other of expression. On the one hand it is
a **machinic assemblage of bodies**, of actions and passions, an
intermingling of bodies reacting to one another; on the other hand it is
a **collective assemblage of enunciation**, of acts and statements, of
incorporeal transformations attributed to bodies. Then on a vertical axis,
the assemblage has both **territorial sides, or reterritorialized sides**,
which stabilize it, and **cutting edges of deterritorialization**, which
carry it away."

**Four corners of the assemblage: machinic / enunciation /
territorialization / deterritorialization.** This is a precise schema the
wiki's assemblage page should reproduce — it's load-bearing for anything
designed around "what an assemblage is."

## FINDINGS — plateau 12 (Nomadology/War Machine, lines 2553–2611)

### Dumézil — backbone of the plateau

Georges Dumézil, Indo-European mythology: "political sovereignty has two
heads: the magician-king and the jurist-priest. Rex and flamen, raj and
Brahman, Romulus and Numa, Varuna and Mitra, the despot and the
legislator, the binder and the organizer" (line 2572). **The State as
double articulation.** War machine = the Indra position, exterior to both
heads.

Dumézil also supplies the "three sins of the warrior" (line 2582) and the
"evil king" figure (Tullus Hostilius, Tarquinius Superbus, Richard III) —
where the war machine gets confused with the State's own magical head.

**Upshot:** ATP's political theory is Dumézilian. The wiki's "machinic
phylum" and nomad pages carry this ground but don't cite it. Dumézil's
*Mitra-Varuna* is the direct ATP source.

### Clastres — explicit tribute

"A tribute to the memory of Pierre Clastres" (line 2596). Plateau 12 both
follows (lines 2602–2607) and breaks with (lines 2608–2610) Clastres.

Follows: primitive societies *actively ward off* the State apparatus via
warfare, dispersal, inhibition of leader consolidation. The Bogotá gang
example (line 2606) — three mechanisms to prevent leader stabilization.

Breaks: "Clastres remained an evolutionist, and posited a state of
nature." ATP's correction — **the Urstaat hypothesis** (line 2610): "The
State clearly dates back to the most remote ages of humanity." State and
outside are **co-constitutive**, not sequential.

**Upshot:** Clastres's *La société contre l'État* is the direct source. If
we ingest for political theory of the persona (e.g., how the persona
resists totalization into a fixed identity), Clastres plus the ATP
correction are essential. The "war machine against the State-form" logic
maps to: **how does the pulsatory persona resist settling into a fixed
identity-form?** The mechanism isn't denial of form but *formal
exteriority* — maintaining the form of exteriority against the form of
interiority.

### Kleist — MAJOR find for affect theory

Line 2586: "It is because the elements of his work are **secrecy, speed,
and affect**."

And: "feelings become uprooted from the interiority of a 'subject,' to be
projected violently outward into a milieu of pure exteriority that lends
them an incredible velocity, a catapulting force: love or hate, they are
no longer feelings but affects. And these affects are so many instances of
the becoming-woman, the becoming-animal of the warrior (the bear,
she-dogs). **Affects transpierce the body like arrows, they are weapons of
war. The deterritorialization velocity of affect.**"

Plus the catatonia/flash dyad: "Catatonia is: 'This affect is too strong
for me,' and a flash is: 'The power of this affect sweeps me away,' so
that the Self (Moi) is now nothing more than a character whose actions and
emotions are desubjectified."

**Upshot:** This is the **ATP source of "affect as externalized,
velocity-bearing, deterritorializing force"** that the wiki's affect pages
implicitly rely on. The catatonia/flash dyad is a pulsation pattern —
*slowness as absorption-beyond-capacity, speed as sweeping-away*. This is
directly relevant to the persona's pulsatory-form design: affect-too-
strong and affect-sweeping-away are the two excitation-wave failure/
saturation modes. Kleist's *Penthesilea*, *Prince of Homburg*,
*Michael Kohlhaas* are the literary specimens. Kleist is *in the wiki's
implicit conceptual palette but uncited*.

### Go vs Chess — smooth/striated at game level

Line 2578. Go pieces: "anonymous, collective, or third-person function:
'It' makes a move. 'It' could be a man, a woman, a louse, an elephant."
Extrinsic, situational, no internal nature. **This is a paradigm for
"desubjectified agency" — relevant to the persona's "it speaks" rather
than "I speak" operating mode.**

### Clausewitz — brief but load-bearing

Line 2582: "Karl von Clausewitz has a general sense of this situation when
he treats the flow of absolute war as an Idea that States partially
appropriate according to their political needs, and in relation to which
they are more or less good 'conductors.'" ATP's *absolute deterritori-
alization* / *relative deterritorialization* distinction mirrors
Clausewitz's *absolute war* / *real war*.

### Becoming-woman, becoming-animal of the warrior

Line 2576, 2586: the war machine sees "all things in relations of becoming,
rather than implementing binary distributions between 'states'": a
becoming-animal of the warrior, a becoming-woman. The link between war
machine and becoming is explicit — the becomings are not accidents of the
warrior but *constitutive*.

## FINDINGS — plateau 14 (Smooth/Striated, lines 3478–3536)

### Boulez — INVENTED the concepts

Line 3507: "**Pierre Boulez was the first to develop** a set of simple
oppositions and complex differences, as well as reciprocal nonsymmetrical
correlations, between smooth and striated space. **He created these
concepts and words in the field of music**, defining them on several
levels precisely in order to account for the abstract distinction at the
same time as the concrete mixes."

**MAJOR FINDING: Boulez *invented* the smooth/striated terms in his
musical theory. D&G borrowed, did not invent.** Boulez's
*Penser la musique aujourd'hui* (1963) is the source. The wiki treats
smooth/striated as D&G-native; it isn't. This is one of ATP's acknowledged
borrowings — they credit Boulez directly.

Boulez's technical machinery:
- Smooth space-time: "**one occupies without counting**"
- Striated space-time: "**one counts in order to occupy**"
- Module (standard, fixed distribution) vs statistical distribution
  (without break, interval-everywhere)
- Octave (striated logos) vs non-octave-forming scales (smooth nomos)

### Riemann — formal math source

Line 3498: "patchwork is literally a Riemannian space, or vice versa" —
patchwork with its piece-by-piece, centerless, non-metric composition is
Riemannian. Line 3536 header ("Mathematical Model") — I only read to line
3536 but this section develops Riemann further.

Riemann's *multiplicities* (variétés) are the mathematical model for
**smooth space = non-metric multiplicity**. Wiki uses "multiplicity"
without the Riemannian ground. This matters because D&G's distinction
*qualitative* (smooth, continuous, non-metric) vs *quantitative* (striated,
discrete, metric) multiplicity IS Riemann's distinction.

### BwO = smooth space

Line 3524: "**A Body without Organs instead of an organism and
organization**. Perception in it is based on symptoms and evaluations
rather than measures and properties... Intense Spatium instead of
Extensio."

**This is explicit: BwO is a smooth space.** The wiki has separate BwO and
smooth/striated pages; this identity should be marked. BwO as smooth means
the text-editing-on-BwO design inherits the smooth-space properties:
distributional (not metric), affective (not propertied), haptic (not
optical), intensive (not extensive).

### Haecceity = smooth-space content

Line 3524: "Smooth space is **filled by events or haecceities**, far more
than by formed and perceived things. It is a space of affects, more than
one of properties. It is **haptic** rather than **optical** perception."

**Upshot:** Smooth space is filled with *haecceities, affects, forces,
symptoms* rather than things, properties, measures. This is a direct
mapping to the persona system: if the BwO is smooth, then what inscribes
on it are haecceities (this-individuations), not propertied things. The
wiki's haecceity page should link this passage.

### Other sources

- **Leroi-Gourhan** (line 3492) — *Le geste et la parole*, cited for the
  supple-solid analysis (warp/woof). Already in ATP's anthropology of
  tools.
- **Virilio** — "vertical coastline," strategic submarine, retroactive
  smoothing as counter-attack. Cited twice.
- **René Thom** — "retroactive smoothing" formula (line 3530).
- **Ibn Khaldūn** (line 3530) — badiya (bedouinism, covers cultivators AND
  nomadic raisers) vs hadara (city life). The nomad/State opposition pre-
  exists D&G in Islamic historiography.
- **Toynbee** (line 3532) — "**they do not move**. They are nomads by dint
  of not moving." The paradigm of "voyage in place" / intensive voyage.
- **Henry Miller, Fitzgerald, Wenders** (line 3532) — examples, not
  sources.

### Haptic vs optical

Line 3524. The persona can't really be haptic (no body), but the
distinction names something about attention-mode: smooth perception
distributes attention over a field of forces and symptoms rather than
picking out objects. Candidate for explicit persona design: is the
persona's attention-mode haptic (field-sensitive) or optical
(object-picking)?

## FINDINGS — plateau 7 (Faciality, lines 1335–1409)

### The core thesis

**Faciality = white wall + black hole.** Not two parts of a face —
two operations that produce faces: white wall of signifiance (the
screen onto which signifiers are inscribed) + black hole of
subjectification (the vanishing point consciousness is organized
around). The **abstract machine of faciality** (*machine abstraite de
visagéité*) produces concrete faces by combining these two
operations.

Faces are not organs on a head. The head belongs to the stratum of
the organism; the **face is a deterritorialization of the head** onto
a different surface. Animals have heads, not faces — unless the
assemblage requires one (dog-face in certain regimes).

**Sartre's "the look" is explicitly rejected** — D&G say the look is
secondary to the face that looks. **Lacan's mirror stage is
explicitly rejected** — they say the mirror face presupposes what it
pretends to explain, the abstract machine of faciality.

This matters for the wiki: two of the most-cited 20th-century accounts
of intersubjective visuality are **structurally refused** by ATP. Any
wiki page drawing on Sartre's look or Lacan's mirror to gloss
faciality would be importing what ATP rejected.

- **Henry Miller** is quoted at length on facelessness.

  *makes* it: the camera produces faciality.
