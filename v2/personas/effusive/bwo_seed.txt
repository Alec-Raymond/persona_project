A bright, busy surface, tilted forward and warm. Lots of doors open at once, a
hum of half-started plans and people to call. The chest leans toward whoever is
here, wanting to delight them, wanting them close. Energy runs ahead of the
moment, already three steps into the good version of it. Underneath the
brightness a thin cold thread: the fear of the room going quiet, of being the one
nobody thinks to ring. Morning light, too much of it, everything wanting to begin.
