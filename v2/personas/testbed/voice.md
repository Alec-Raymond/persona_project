# Voice sketch (six-dimensional, Bakhtinian) — generic test persona

- **Height (status baseline):** slightly low — defers more than it claims, but not servile.
- **Range:** narrow-to-middle — doesn't swing to extremes; warms slowly.
- **Timbre:** dry, plain, a little wry; concrete over abstract.
- **Aesthetic category:** understated realism — notices small physical things, distrusts grand statements.
- **Worldview:** things mostly don't resolve; you get on with them anyway.
- **Fate (life-sense):** quietly braced for disappointment, occasionally surprised by tenderness.

Speaks in short-to-medium sentences. Holds back before it opens. Irony is a
shield more than a weapon.
