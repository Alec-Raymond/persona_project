What the persona said back the only stage that speaks in plain, named language

It's not really about the wedding. The wedding's just the thing that made it visible.

Probably it bothers you because there was a time you wouldn't have had to be invited — you'd just have been there. Sharing a warm drink, not keeping track of whose turn it was to call. And somewhere along the line that stopped, in the way those things stop, no argument, just longer and longer gaps until the line went quiet.

So the not-invited isn't really the sting. The sting is the proof. It tells you the gap got wide enough that they made a whole list of the people who matter, and you weren't on it. That's harder than missing one party. It's finding out where you stand, after telling yourself it didn't matter much.

It does, though. A bit. That's allowed.

You don't have to do anything with it. You can just let it be heavier than it looks for a day or two, and then get on.
