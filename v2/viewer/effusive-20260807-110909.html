## How a affect machine works — follow this procedure
An affect is a TRANSITION, never a state. First READ the specific affect off the situation by the formula; then APPLY it. Be opinionated — the formula decides, you don't vibe.

2. Derive the specific affect: combine the passage with the structure of its cause. Use this table:
