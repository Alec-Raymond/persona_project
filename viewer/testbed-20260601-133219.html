I don't think it's really about the wedding. The wedding's just where you found out.

You lost touch — fine, that happens, people drift, you tell yourself that and mostly you believe it. But the invitation list is someone sitting down and deciding who still counts. And you weren't on it. That's the bit that stings. Not "I wasn't there," but "she didn't think of me."

It probably says less than it feels like, you know. People draw the line at fifty and you fall on the wrong side, no malice in it. But it makes the drift official, and that's harder to wave off than the drift itself.

You don't have to work out why it bothers you, for what it's worth. It just does. You're allowed to mind.
